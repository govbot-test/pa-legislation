2025-2026 Regular Session SR 197 PN 1323 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 1323 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIASENATE RESOLUTION No.197 Session of 2025 INTRODUCED BY TARTAGLIONE, NOVEMBER 19, 2025 INTRODUCED AND ADOPTED, NOVEMBER 19, 2025 A RESOLUTIONNoting with great sadness the passing of Geoffrey Mock, Esq., whose life greatly benefited all those who lived, served and worked with him.WHEREAS, The Senate mourns the loss of Geoffrey Mock, Esq., who passed away on July 17, 2025, at 35 years of age; andWHEREAS, A native of Sicklerville, New Jersey, Mr. Mock earned a bachelor's degree in political science and government at Kutztown University in 2012 and a Juris Doctor degree at the Widener University School of Law in 2016; andWHEREAS, Mr. Mock began his career as an intern with the Pennsylvania Senate in 2013 and worked as a Regional Representative for United States Senator Bob Casey from 2016 to 2018, when he joined the staff of Senator Christine Tartaglione as Legislative Director; andWHEREAS, Employed with the Long Island Board of Realtors as Director of Government Affairs for nearly two years, Mr. Mock joined Lehigh University in December 2021 as Associate Director of Government Relations, the position he served at the time of his passing; and12345678910111213141516171819 WHEREAS, He was named a "rising star" in 2023 and one of the top Forty Under 40 by City & State Pennsylvania magazine; andWHEREAS, In 2024, he was on the State policy committee for Life Sciences PA and was the keynote speaker at the Delaware Valley Student Affairs Conference and Lenfest Scholars Community Weekend; andWHEREAS, Known for his empathy, intellect, gracious nature and dry sense of humor, Mr. Mock was also an accomplished athlete who participated in sprints and relays at the Penn Relays and other championship meets for the Kutztown track team, played cornerback for two seasons on the football team and volunteered his time as an assistant track and field coach for Ursinus College since 2023; andWHEREAS, To his great credit, he was lauded for his efforts to uplift others and make them feel heard; andWHEREAS, Mr. Mock enjoyed reading, learning, engaging in thoughtful conversations and spending time with his family and friends; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the Senate note with great sadness the passing of Geoffrey Mock, Esq., whose life greatly benefited all those who lived, served and worked with him; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the Senate extends heartfelt condolences to his wife, Danica Mock, children, Chancellor and Amia, and many other family members and friends.20250SR0197PN1323 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324
