2025-2026 Regular Session HR 419 PN 2932 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 2932 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.419 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY HARKINS, HANBIDGE, VITALI, PROBST, HOWARD, McNEILL, RIVERA, GREINER, WAXMAN, BRENNAN, SAPPEY, DOUGHERTY, SANCHEZ, CIRESI, NEILSON, MAYES AND SHUSTERMAN, FEBRUARY 19, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON AGRICULTURE AND RURAL AFFAIRS, FEBRUARY 20, 2026 A RESOLUTIONDesignating April 14, 2026, as "Animal Control Officer and Animal Cruelty Officer Day" in Pennsylvania; and recognizing the week of April 12 through 18, 2026, as "National Animal Control Appreciation Week" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, Animal control officers and animal cruelty officers help pets, animals and people in our communities every day across this Commonwealth and the United States; andWHEREAS, There are 8,300 animal control officers and animal cruelty officers across the United States; andWHEREAS, Officers conduct humane investigations and seek justice in cases of abuse, neglect and abandoned animals; andWHEREAS, Officers protect animals from abusers as well as pick up stray and helpless animals; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives designate April 14, 2026, as "Animal Control Officer and Animal Cruelty Officer Day" in Pennsylvania and recognize the week of April 12 through 18, 2026, as "National Animal Control Appreciation Week" in 1234567891011121314151617 Pennsylvania; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives, in conjunction with national and State efforts, recognize the contributions and importance of animal control officers and animal cruelty officers in this Commonwealth and across the United States.20260HR0419PN2932 - 2 - 12345
