andWHEREAS, The Commonwealth's hunting, fishing and boating programs administered through the Pennsylvania Game Commission and the Pennsylvania Fish and Boat Commission receive no State General Fund tax revenue, but are supported by Pennsylvania hunters, anglers, boaters and trappers through licenses, permits, registrations, fees and related Federal excise tax revenues; andWHEREAS, These user-based dollars support wildlife and fisheries management, habitat conservation, public access, education, enforcement and sporting traditions; andWHEREAS, Hunting, fishing, boating and trapping are traditions shared among families, neighbors, friends and 123456789101112131415161718 generations of Pennsylvanians; andWHEREAS, Generations of hunters have learned safety, patience, responsibility, respect for wildlife and respect for private and public lands while hunting alongside parents, grandparents, relatives, mentors and friends; andWHEREAS, The future of hunting depends upon families, mentors and hunting groups passing along knowledge, ethics of responsible conservation, skills and traditions to younger generations; andWHEREAS, For many Pennsylvanians, gathering at deer camp before the opening of firearms deer season is a cherished tradition rooted in family, fellowship, storytelling, preparation and shared respect for the outdoors; andWHEREAS, The opening of firearms deer season, historically observed for many years on the Monday after Thanksgiving, has long been meaningful to families, rural communities, schools, workplaces and local businesses across this Commonwealth; andWHEREAS, As the timing of that opening has changed in recent years, the importance of preserving hunting traditions, deer camp and multigenerational participation remains clear; andWHEREAS, The traditions of hunting, fishing, boating and trapping support small businesses throughout Pennsylvania, including outfitters, guides, sporting goods stores, campgrounds, restaurants and lodging establishments;
