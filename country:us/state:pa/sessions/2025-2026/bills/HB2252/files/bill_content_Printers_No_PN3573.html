AN ACT
Amending Title 18 (Crimes and Offenses) of the Pennsylvania
Consolidated Statutes, in sexual offenses, further providing
for the offense of unlawful dissemination of intimate image;
AND, IN MINORS, FURTHER PROVIDING FOR THE OFFENSE OF SEXUAL
ABUSE OF CHILDREN.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Section 3131(a), (b), (c) and (g) of Title 18 of
the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes, amended or added October
29, 2024 (P.L.1095, No.125), are amended and the section is
amended by adding subsections to read:
