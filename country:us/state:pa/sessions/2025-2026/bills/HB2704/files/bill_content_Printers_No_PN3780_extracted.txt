Definitions.The following words and phrases when used in this chapter shall have the meanings given to them in this section unless the context clearly indicates otherwise:"Affiliate." Waiting period for purchases by covered entities.(a) Waiting period.--A covered entity may not engage in any of the following with respect to a property zoned for single-family residential use or low-density residential use unless the property has been listed for sale to the general public for at least 90 days:(1) Acquire ownership of, purchase or obtain an interest in the property.(2) Submit an offer to acquire ownership of, purchase or obtain an interest in the property.(b) Reset of waiting period.--If the listed price of a property zoned for single-family residential use or low-density residential use changes by at least 5% from the original listing price, the 90-day period under subsection (a) shall begin again on the date the price change is publicly reflected.(c) Penalty.--The department may assess a fine of not more than $100,000 for each violation of this section. A covered entity that violates this section shall forfeit the right to acquire ownership of, purchase or obtain an interest in the property that is the subject of the violation for an additional 90-day period. The additional 90-day period shall commence on the date the covered entity pays the fine or, if the department does not assess a fine, on the date of the violation.(d) Seller nonliability.--A seller that completes a transaction with a covered entity in violation of this section is not liable in a civil or criminal proceeding in this Commonwealth for the violation.(e) Completed transaction.--If a covered entity completes a transaction in violation of this section, the covered entity shall sell or otherwise relinquish the property acquired in 20260HB2704PN3780 - 10 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 violation of this section.
