§ 7601. Definitions.
The following words and phrases when used in this chapter
shall have the meanings given to them in this section unless the
context clearly indicates otherwise:
"Affiliate." A person or entity that directly or indirectly,
through one or more intermediaries, controls, is controlled by

parcels of land zoned for single-family residential use or low-
density residential use.

§ 7604. Waiting period for purchases by covered entities.
(a) Waiting period.--A covered entity may not engage in any
of the following with respect to a property zoned for single-
family residential use or low-density residential use unless the
property has been listed for sale to the general public for at
least 90 days:
(1) Acquire ownership of, purchase or obtain an interest
in the property.
(2) Submit an offer to acquire ownership of, purchase or
obtain an interest in the property.
(b) Reset of waiting period.--If the listed price of a
property zoned for single-family residential use or low-density
residential use changes by at least 5% from the original listing
price, the 90-day period under subsection (a) shall begin again
on the date the price change is publicly reflected.
(c) Penalty.--The department may assess a fine of not more
than $100,000 for each violation of this section. A covered
entity that violates this section shall forfeit the right to
acquire ownership of, purchase or obtain an interest in the
property that is the subject of the violation for an additional
90-day period. The additional 90-day period shall commence on
the date the covered entity pays the fine or, if the department
does not assess a fine, on the date of the violation.
(d) Seller nonliability.--A seller that completes a
transaction with a covered entity in violation of this section
is not liable in a civil or criminal proceeding in this
Commonwealth for the violation.
(e) Completed transaction.--If a covered entity completes a
transaction in violation of this section, the covered entity
shall sell or otherwise relinquish the property acquired in

§ 7606. Notice to seller and alert to department.
(a) Declaration to seller.--When a covered entity submits an
offer to acquire ownership of, purchase or obtain an interest in
a property zoned for single-family residential use or low-
density residential use, the covered entity shall provide to the
seller, or an agent of the seller, a notarized form signed by
the covered entity or an authorized agent stating that the
covered entity is subject to this chapter. The form shall inform
the seller of the requirement to alert the department under
subsection (b) and the potential penalty for failing to comply.
This subsection shall apply to a person or entity that will
become a covered entity if the transaction is completed.
(b) Alert to department.--The department shall promulgate
regulations establishing the means by which a seller shall alert
the department of an offer submitted by a covered entity.
(c) Penalties.--The department may assess a fine of not more
than $500 against a seller that fails to alert the department
under subsection (b), unless the seller demonstrates the failure
resulted from the act or omission of the seller's authorized
agent, in which case the department may assess the fine against
the authorized agent. The department may assess a fine of not
more than $5,000 against a covered entity that fails to provide
the form required under subsection (a).

§ 7611. Planned communities, cooperatives and condominiums.
A planned community as defined in section 5103 (relating to
definitions), cooperative as defined in section 4103 (relating
to definitions) and condominium as defined in section 3103
(relating to definitions) shall be exempt from the provisions of
this chapter if the residents and members own the residents' or
members' units or maintain at least partial ownership of the
real estate owned by the planned community, cooperative or
condominium.
§ 7612. Other exemptions.
This chapter shall not apply to any of the following:
(1) A developer, including a developer of a planned
community, condominium or cooperative, that intends to build
and sell single-family or low-density residential housing to
an owner-occupant.
(2) A developer that purchases a property with existing
distressed residential housing to repair, renovate or
otherwise make the housing habitable for resale to an owner-
occupant.
(3) A nonprofit corporation exempt from tax under 26
U.S.C. § 501(a) and (c)(3) (relating to exemption from tax on
corporations, certain trusts, etc.).
(4) A person or entity that operates a group home for
individuals with a disability or other special health care
need.
(5) An employer that rents a home to an employee.

§ 7613. Affordable housing incentive.
(a) Application.--A person or entity subject to this chapter
may apply to the department for a permit under subsection (b).
(b) Permit.--The department may issue a permit that
increases the ownership threshold in the definition of
"prohibited entity" from 50 properties or parcels of land to 100
properties or parcels of land. A permit holder shall not be a
prohibited entity until the permit holder, through itself or
collectively with its affiliate, owns or holds an interest in
more than 100 properties or parcels of land zoned for single-
family residential use or low-density residential use.
(c) Eligibility.--
(1) The department may issue a permit under subsection
(b) only if the person or entity meets all of the following:
(i) At least 20% of the person's or entity's total
portfolio of residential rental units located in this
Commonwealth is offered for rent at a rate tied to the
tenant's adjusted gross income, with the rental rate not
exceeding any of the following:
(A) 25% of the tenant's adjusted gross income if
the income is less than $40,000 annually.
(B) 24% of the tenant's adjusted gross income if

§ 7615. Collection.
A fine, penalty or forfeited profit that is finally due and
payable under this chapter shall constitute a debt due the
Commonwealth. Upon request of the department, the Office of
Attorney General may bring an action to recover the debt and may
engage in any other lawful collection remedy.
