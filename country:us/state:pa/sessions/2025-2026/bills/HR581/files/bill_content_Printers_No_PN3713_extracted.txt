2025-2026 Regular Session HR 581 PN 3713 Bill Text (HTM) PRINTER'S NO. 3713 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.581 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY WEBSTER, SANCHEZ, D. WILLIAMS AND PASHINSKI, JUNE 25, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON VETERANS AFFAIRS AND EMERGENCY PREPAREDNESS, JUNE 26, 2026 A RESOLUTIONDesignating the week of January 24 through 30, 2027, as "Medicolegal Death Investigation Professionals Week" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, In cases of unexpected, unexplained or suspicious death, medicolegal death investigation professionals are responsible for determining the cause of death and whether a criminal investigation is warranted; andWHEREAS, Medicolegal death investigation professionals have expertise in medicine and law to investigate cases under the jurisdiction of a medical examiner or coroner; andWHEREAS, Medicolegal death investigation professionals work toward justice for victims who can no longer advocate for themselves and represent the interests of decedents, families and communities; andWHEREAS, Medicolegal death investigation professionals work with medical examiners and coroners to analyze the scene of a death and the body of the decedent; andWHEREAS, Medicolegal death investigation professionals 123456789101112131415161718 determine whether criminal activity is involved in a death and collaborate with local law enforcement and forensic teams to share findings; andWHEREAS, Approximately 3,100 medicolegal death investigation professionals work across the United States, representing a specialized but essential workforce; andWHEREAS, These professionals are highly trained to promote public health and safety by ensuring that suspicious, violent, unattended, unexplained and unexpected deaths are investigated; andWHEREAS, Medicolegal death investigation professionals work with Federal, State and local agencies to ensure public safety; andWHEREAS, Medicolegal death investigation professionals contribute to local and national public health databases regarding injury, illness and disease; andWHEREAS, The International Association of Coroners and Medical Examiners recognizes "Medicolegal Death Investigation Professionals Week" in January to honor these professionals and their role in supporting public health and safety; andWHEREAS, On March 23, 2024, the United States Senate agreed to Senate Resolution 532 of the 118th Congress, recognizing and supporting the goals and ideals of "National Medicolegal Death Investigation Professionals Week"; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives designate the week of January 24 through 30, 2027, as "Medicolegal Death Investigation Professionals Week" in Pennsylvania; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives recognize that this observance provides an opportunity to promote community 20260HR0581PN3713 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 understanding of this indispensable workforce; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives honor the commitment of medicolegal death investigation professionals to provide skilled, comprehensive and compassionate investigations of deaths; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives acknowledge the work of medicolegal death investigation professionals and their contributions to public health, safety and local law enforcement in this Commonwealth.20260HR0581PN3713 - 3 - 123456789
