2025-2026 Regular Session HR 417 PN 2900 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 2900 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.417 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY STRUZZI, COOPER, FREEMAN, GUENST, J. HARRIS, JAMES, KHAN, McANDREW, NEILSON, E. NELSON, ROAE, ROWE, SMITH AND WARREN, FEBRUARY 5, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON EDUCATION, FEBRUARY 9, 2026 A RESOLUTIONDesignating the month of November 2025 as "Indiana University of Pennsylvania Appreciation Month" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, Indiana University of Pennsylvania (IUP) was founded in 1875 as Indiana Normal School; andWHEREAS, The institution was later purchased by the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania in 1927, becoming Indiana State Teachers College; andWHEREAS, The institution was subsequently renamed Indiana State College in 1959; andWHEREAS, The act of December 16, 1965 (P.L.1113, No.430), known as the Indiana University of Pennsylvania Act, redesignated the institution as a university; andWHEREAS, IUP is now a thriving institution and part of the Pennsylvania State System of Higher Education; andWHEREAS, IUP is celebrating its 150th anniversary in 2025; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives designate the 1234567891011121314151617 month of November 2025 as "Indiana University of Pennsylvania Appreciation Month" in Pennsylvania; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives extend its congratulations to IUP on achieving 150 years of academic excellence and service to our Commonwealth and its students.20260HR0417PN2900 - 2 - 12345
