2025-2026 Regular Session SR 282 PN 1619 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 1619 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIASENATE RESOLUTION No.282 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY DUSH, BROWN AND VOGEL, APRIL 17, 2026 REFERRED TO RULES AND EXECUTIVE NOMINATIONS, APRIL 17, 2026 A RESOLUTIONRecognizing the week of April 20 through 24, 2026, as "National Home Visiting Week" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, The early childhood years are the most active period for the developing brain, and a stable, secure relationship with a nurturing, caring adult is a key factor in the development of young children; andWHEREAS, Many parents begin the difficult lifetime job of raising a child feeling unprepared for the challenges ahead and children have a better, healthier start when parents have the support and skills needed to raise them; andWHEREAS, Home visiting can include a variety of different programs and models, including early childhood home visiting programs and parenting education programs; andWHEREAS, Home visiting programs help parents meet the unique needs of their children, promote healthy development, strengthen family relationships, reduce the risk of abuse and neglect and promote equity by providing resources to families who are furthest from opportunity; and123456789101112131415161718 WHEREAS, Well-trained professionals implement early childhood home visitation so that all families have the opportunity to access information in ways that respect their unique beliefs, traditions and customs; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the Senate recognize the week of April 20 through 24, 2026, as "National Home Visiting Week" in Pennsylvania; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the Senate urge individuals and organizations across our Commonwealth to utilize home visiting resources as needed to support the health and well-being of our children and families.20260SR0282PN1619 - 2 - 1234567891011
