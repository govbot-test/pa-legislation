2025-2026 Regular Session HR 531 PN 3421 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 3421 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.531 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY DELOZIER, RAPP, GALLAGHER, McNEILL, NEILSON, PUGH, RIVERA, STEHR AND VENKAT, MAY 15, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON HEALTH, MAY 20, 2026 A RESOLUTIONDesignating the month of June 2026 as "Scoliosis Awareness Month" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, Scoliosis is a condition characterized by an abnormal side to side curvature of the spine, often appearing as an "S" or "C" shape rather than a straight line; andWHEREAS, An estimated 6 million to 9 million people in the United States are affected by scoliosis, with idiopathic scoliosis, the most common form, typically developing in preteens and teenagers; andWHEREAS, Early detection through school screenings and regular physical examinations is critical to preventing severe spinal deformity and reducing the need for invasive surgical procedures; andWHEREAS, Organizations such as the Scoliosis Research Society and the National Scoliosis Foundation work tirelessly to unite patients, families and clinicians in a collaborative effort to advocate for enhanced patient care, awareness and protection; and123456789101112131415161718 WHEREAS, Young advocates in this Commonwealth, like Ella from the 88th Legislative District, are leading initiatives to increase public understanding, provide peer support and partner with medical providers to expand access to resources across this Commonwealth; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives designate the month of June 2026 as "Scoliosis Awareness Month" in Pennsylvania; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives urge all Pennsylvanians to learn more about this condition and support early screening efforts to improve the quality of life for individuals affected by scoliosis.20260HR0531PN3421 - 2 - 123456789101112
