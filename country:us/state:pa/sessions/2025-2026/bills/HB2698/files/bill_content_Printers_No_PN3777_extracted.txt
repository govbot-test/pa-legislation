Section 1526 of the act of March 10, 1949 (P.L.30, No.14), known as the Public School Code of 1949, is amended by adding a subsection to read:Section 1526. Youth Suicide Awareness and Prevention.--* * * (c.1) The following shall apply to student suicide awareness and prevention education:(1) Beginning with the first school year that commences within twenty-four (24) months of the effective date of this subsection, each school entity serving students in grades six through twelve shall provide students at least one (1) hour or 1234567891011121314151617181920 one (1) standard class period of evidence-based, age-appropriate suicide awareness and prevention education each school year. The following shall apply:(i) A school entity may deliver the education in person or by electronic means.(ii) A school entity may use a combined program that satisfies this subsection and section 1526.1(a) to meet the requirement under this paragraph.(2) Within one (1) year of the effective date of this subsection, the department shall develop a list of evidence-based, age-appropriate educational materials, including no-cost programming, if any, to fulfill the requirements of this subsection. At a minimum, the educational materials or curriculum shall:(i) teach students how to identify signs and signals of depression, suicide risk and self-injury in themselves and their peers;(ii) emphasize the importance of seeking help for themselves and their peers; and(iii) describe the process for seeking help, including how and where to report concerns and the resources available within the school entity and the community.(3) The department shall post the list under paragraph (2) on the department's publicly accessible Internet website.(4) As used in this subsection, the term "evidence-based" shall have the meaning given to it in section 1526.1(e).* * *Section 2.
