2025-2026 Regular Session SR 250 PN 1525 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 1525 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIASENATE RESOLUTION No.250 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY STREET, SANTARSIERO, HUGHES, SAVAL, KEARNEY, HAYWOOD, SCHWANK, TARTAGLIONE, CAPPELLETTI, VOGEL, ROBINSON AND PICOZZI, MARCH 20, 2026 REFERRED TO RULES AND EXECUTIVE NOMINATIONS, MARCH 20, 2026 A RESOLUTIONObserving March 20, 2026, as "Eid al-Fitr Day" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, Muslims observe two major annual religious observances, Ramadan and Hajj, which correspond to the holidays of Eid al-Fitr and Eid al-Adha; andWHEREAS, Eid al-Fitr and Eid al-Adha are observed according to the lunar-based Islamic calendar; andWHEREAS, Eid al-Fitr is celebrated on the first day of the Islamic month of Shawwal, marking the end of Ramadan, a month dedicated to fasting and prayer and a period of spiritual reflection and renewal; andWHEREAS, In addition to celebrating the end of fasting with family gatherings and feasts, many Muslims pay Zakat al-Fitr, an obligatory charitable giving, attend congregational prayers and listen to a communal sermon known as a khutbah, seeking Allah's forgiveness and continued blessings; andWHEREAS, Eid al-Fitr is also commonly celebrated by wearing new clothing, visiting relatives and neighbors and sharing food 1234567891011121314151617 or providing gifts of food or money to those in need, reflecting gratitude for the blessings received; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the Senate observe March 20, 2026, as "Eid al-Fitr Day" in Pennsylvania.20260SR0250PN1525 - 2 - 1234
