"Fund." The Residential Economic Development District Fund
established under section 6304(a) (relating to Residential

"Major economic development project." A project in this
Commonwealth that meets both of the following criteria:
(1) the project is reasonably expected to create, retain
and attract jobs or otherwise improve the economic well-being
of the area surrounding the project site; and
(2) at least $250,000,000 in private investments are
committed to establish, expand, renovate or occupy a facility
as part of a single project at a designated project site,
including investment in new buildings, additions or
improvements to existing buildings, machinery, equipment,
furniture, fixtures and inventory.
"Major workforce housing project." A project that reserves
at least 50 units designed for residential occupancy by at least
50 individuals or families living independently from each other.
"Program." The Residential Economic Development District
Grant Program established under section 6302 (relating to
Residential Economic Development District Grant Program).

developers.

§ 6302. Residential Economic Development District Grant
Program.
(a) Establishment.--The Residential Economic Development
District Grant Program is established within the department for
the purpose of encouraging major workforce housing projects in
areas of this Commonwealth that otherwise would not attract such
developments and to increase home ownership among residents of
this Commonwealth.
(b) Applicants.--A county or municipal corporation that is
fully or partially located within a residential economic
development district may apply for a grant under the program.
(c) Duties of department.--The department shall establish
guidelines necessary to implement and administer this chapter.
The guidelines shall address application procedures, scoring
metrics and grant distribution.
§ 6303. Submission to department.
(a) Application.--A county or municipal corporation may
submit an application independently or in collaboration with a
housing developer or one or more other counties or municipal
corporations. The application shall, at minimum, include
documentation or other evidence that proves, to the satisfaction
of the department, that the applicant has done or has imminent
plans to do both of the following within the district:
(1) adopt and implement pro-housing-development
policies; and
(2) approve a major workforce housing project.
(b) Award of grants.--The department shall review
applications and award grants under the program on a rolling
basis, to the extent that funds are available.

(d) Revocation.--If a county or municipal corporation is

to adopt and implement pro-housing-development policies and

confirm that the county or municipal corporation follows through
with the plans, as described in the grant application, before
disbursing grant funds. The department may revoke approval if
the applicant fails to meet minimum requirements set by the
department.
(e) Limitations.--A grant recipient shall use the funds only
for the following purposes:
(1) Providing capital for housing development through
grants or loans.
(2) Acquiring and readying sites for development.
(3) Providing financial assistance for housing-related
or other infrastructure projects, including road improvements
and water or sewer connections.
(4) Addressing additional service or public safety needs
due to increases in population, except that expenditures
under this paragraph may not exceed 30% of the grant amount.
(5) Any other purpose deemed appropriate by the
department.
