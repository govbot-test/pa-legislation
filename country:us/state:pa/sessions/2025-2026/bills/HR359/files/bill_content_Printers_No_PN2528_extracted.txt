2025-2026 Regular Session HR 359 PN 2528 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 2528 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.359 Session of 2025 INTRODUCED BY BRENNAN, BARTON AND TWARDZIK, OCTOBER 27, 2025 TO UNCONTESTED CALENDAR UNDER RULE 35 OCTOBER 27, 2025 A RESOLUTIONExpressing condolences on the death of the Honorable Edward J. Lucyk of Barnesville, Schuylkill County, former 11-term Member from the 123rd District of the House of Representatives.WHEREAS, Born July 29, 1942, in Mahanoy City, Edward J. Lucyk died July 7, 2025, at 82 years of age; andWHEREAS, Elected to the House of Representatives in 1980, Mr. Lucyk faithfully represented the 123rd District from 1981 through 2002; andWHEREAS, The House of Representatives notes with respect Mr. Lucyk's distinguished pursuits and achievements on behalf of Schuylkill and Columbia Counties, this Commonwealth and our nation; andWHEREAS, A graduate of Mahanoy Area High School, Mr. Lucyk received a bachelor of science degree from the United States Military Academy at West Point in 1964; andWHEREAS, Mr. Lucyk served two tours of duty in Vietnam, achieving the rank of Captain and earning a Bronze Star with two oak leaf clusters and the Air Medal, Army Commendation Medal and 12345678910111213141516171819 Vietnam Service Medal; andWHEREAS, While enlisted, Mr. Lucyk graduated with a master's degree in business administration from George Washington University; andWHEREAS, As a legislator, Mr. Lucyk dedicated his energies to securing services and assistance for veterans and seniors and advancing environmental and labor initiatives; andWHEREAS, Mr. Lucyk chaired the House Military and Veterans Affairs Committee and the Tourism and Recreational Development Committee; andWHEREAS, To Mr. Lucyk, "You've Got a Friend in Pennsylvania" was the most meaningful of the changing State slogans; andWHEREAS, Mr. Lucyk further contributed to the important work of the House Appropriations Committee and the Legislative Coal Caucus; andWHEREAS, Mr. Lucyk's parents, Edward and Lucy Lucyk, and his wife, Patricia Guinan Lucyk, preceded him in death; andWHEREAS, His children, Matthew and Tara, and his grandchildren, Lucy and Margaux, survive him; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives express condolences on the death of the Honorable Edward J. Lucyk of Barnesville, Schuylkill County, former 11-term Member from the 123rd District of the House of Representatives; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That a copy of this resolution be transmitted to Mr. Lucyk's family in care of Tara Lucyk Gensler.20250HR0359PN2528 - 2 - 1234567891011121314151617181920212223242526
