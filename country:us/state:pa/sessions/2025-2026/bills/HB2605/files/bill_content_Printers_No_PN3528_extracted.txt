2025-2026 Regular Session HB 2605 PN 3528 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 3528 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE BILL No.2605 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY DALEY, PROBST, SHUSTERMAN, MADDEN, CURRY, HANBIDGE, HOHENSTEIN, OTTEN, PASHINSKI AND INGLIS, JUNE 4, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON HEALTH, JUNE 5, 2026 AN ACTAmending Title 18 (Crimes and Offenses) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes, in abortion, further providing for Commonwealth interference prohibited.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Section 3208.1 of Title 18 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is amended to read:§ 3208.1. Commonwealth interference prohibited.(a) Prohibition.--The Commonwealth shall not interfere with the use of medically appropriate methods of contraception or fertility treatment or the manner in which medically appropriate methods of contraception or fertility treatment are provided.(b) Definitions.--As used in this section, the following words and phrases shall have the meanings given to them in this subsection unless the context clearly indicates otherwise:"Fertility treatment." Includes:(1) The preservation of human oocytes, sperm or embryos for later reproductive use.123456789101112131415161718 (2) Artificial insemination, including intravaginal insemination, intracervical insemination and intrauterine insemination.(3) Assisted reproductive technology, including in vitro fertilization and other treatments or procedures in which oocytes, sperm, fertilized eggs, embryos and other reproductive genetic material are handled.(4) Genetic testing of embryos.(5) Medication prescribed or obtained over-the-counter, as indicated for fertility.(6) Gamete donation.(7) Other information, referrals, treatments, procedures, medications, laboratory testing, technologies or services relating to fertility.Section 2. This act shall take effect in 60 days.20260HB2605PN3528 - 2 - 123456789101112131415
