AN ACT
Amending Title 42 (Judiciary and Judicial Procedure) of the
Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes, IN BUDGET AND FINANCE,
FURTHER PROVIDING FOR EXPENSES; AND, in facilities and
supplies, further providing for DEPOSITS INTO ACCOUNT AND FOR
surcharge.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Section 3733.1(a) introductory paragraph and (b)
of Title 42 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes are
amended to read:

CONSOLIDATED STATUTES IS AMENDED TO READ:

SECTION 2. SECTION 3733(A) OF TITLE 42 IS AMENDED BY ADDING
A PARAGRAPH TO READ:
§ 3733. DEPOSITS INTO ACCOUNT.
(A) GENERAL RULE.--
* * *
(3) BEGINNING JULY 1, 2025, AND NOTWITHSTANDING SECTION
3571 (RELATING TO COMMONWEALTH PORTION OF FINES, ETC) OR THIS
SECTION, ANY COSTS CHARGED BY MAGISTERIAL DISTRICT JUDGES
UNDER SECTION 1725.1 (RELATING TO COSTS) OTHERWISE PAID TO
THE COMMONWEALTH SHALL BE DEPOSITED INTO THE RESTRICTED
ACCOUNT ESTABLISHED IN SECTION 3731 (RELATING TO
ESTABLISHMENT OF RESTRICTED RECEIPT ACCOUNT). NOTHING IN THIS
PARAGRAPH SHALL BE CONSTRUED AS AFFECTING THE AMOUNT PAYABLE
TO COUNTIES UNDER SECTION 3572 (RELATING TO COUNTY PORTION OF
FINES, ETC).
