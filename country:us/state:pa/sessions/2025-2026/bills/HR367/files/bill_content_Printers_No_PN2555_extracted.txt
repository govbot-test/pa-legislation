2025-2026 Regular Session HR 367 PN 2555 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 2555 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.367 Session of 2025 INTRODUCED BY OTTEN, McNEILL, HOHENSTEIN, VITALI, HOWARD, GUZMAN, SAPPEY, MAYES, PASHINSKI, WAXMAN, CEPEDA-FREYTIZ, CURRY, SANCHEZ, RIVERA AND GALLAGHER, NOVEMBER 5, 2025 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON CHILDREN AND YOUTH, NOVEMBER 6, 2025 A RESOLUTIONDesignating the month of November 2025 as "Children's Grief Awareness Month" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, Children who experience the loss of a loved one, especially a close family member, can carry that loss with them forever and the loss sometimes causes confusion as to why their loved one was taken away; andWHEREAS, Thousands of children across this Commonwealth experience the death of a parent, sibling or other close loved one; andWHEREAS, One in 11 children in this Commonwealth will lose a parent or sibling by the age of 18; andWHEREAS, Grief is the physical, mental and emotional response to the loss of someone or something important; andWHEREAS, Grief can be a lifelong experience for children and symptoms can vary in duration and intensity; andWHEREAS, Children grieve differently from adults and each child responds differently to grief depending on age, 1234567891011121314151617 developmental stage and level of understanding of death; andWHEREAS, Young children may struggle with understanding the finality or meaning of death; andWHEREAS, Grieving children often feel different and alone, and their grief can impact their relationships, academic performance and overall emotional well-being; andWHEREAS, Common symptoms of grief in children and youth can include depression or loss of interest in daily activities, problems sleeping, loss of appetite, fear of being alone, maturity regression, believing that they can see or speak to the deceased person, decline in academic performance, withdrawal from peers and social activities, irritability, anger and emotional outbursts; andWHEREAS, Associated mental and behavioral health problems include depression, posttraumatic stress reactions, substance use, suicide-related behaviors and developmental impairments; andWHEREAS, The death of a parent, caregiver, sibling or loved one is a form of childhood trauma; andWHEREAS, Childhood traumatic grief is a condition that may occur when a child has a trauma response to the death of an attachment figure or loved one and traumatic stress reactions interfere with the ability to grieve; andWHEREAS, Childhood traumatic grief can cause intrusive reactions, physical or physiological distress, avoidance reactions and negative mood or beliefs; andWHEREAS, Grieving children rely on the adults in their lives to help process their grief and navigate bereavement; andWHEREAS, With caring, support and understanding from family members, educators, peers and community organizations, children 20250HR0367PN2555 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 can learn healthy ways to cope with loss and build resilience; andWHEREAS, "Children's Grief Awareness Month," observed each November, was created to raise awareness of the needs of grieving children and to encourage open conversations about grief, loss and healing; andWHEREAS, Nonprofit organizations across this Commonwealth, including A Haven in Chester County, provide essential grief support services for children, teens, families and schools helping to find hope and connection in the midst of loss; andWHEREAS, It is vital that our communities recognize and respond to the emotional needs of grieving children, ensuring that no child grieves alone; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives designate the month of November 2025 as "Children's Grief Awareness Month" in Pennsylvania; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives honor the children and families who have experienced profound loss and express its gratitude to the counselors, educators, volunteers and community organizations who walk alongside them on their journey of healing.20250HR0367PN2555 - 3 - 123456789101112131415161718192021
