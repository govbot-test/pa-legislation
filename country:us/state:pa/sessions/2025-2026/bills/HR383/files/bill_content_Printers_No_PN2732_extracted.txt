2025-2026 Regular Session HR 383 PN 2732 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 2732 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.383 Session of 2025 INTRODUCED BY ECKER, DECEMBER 29, 2025 TO UNCONTESTED CALENDAR UNDER RULE 35 JANUARY 5, 2026 A RESOLUTIONHonoring the life and extending condolences to the family of Detective Sergeant Cody Michael Becker.WHEREAS, Detective Sergeant Cody Michael Becker gave his life in the line of duty on September 17, 2025, at 39 years of age; andWHEREAS, Born in Hanover on September 19, 1985, Detective Sergeant Becker was the son of Jody L. (Hartlaub) Becker and Thomas Becker; andWHEREAS, Detective Sergeant Becker was a devoted husband to Jordan L. (Mitzel) Becker and a loving father to their children, Canton and Wrynn; andWHEREAS, Detective Sergeant Becker was a 2004 graduate of Spring Grove Area High School, where he excelled as a three-sport athlete and lead the baseball, football and wrestling teams as team captain; andWHEREAS, Detective Sergeant Becker continued his academic and athletic career at Millersville University, where he became an NCAA Division I East Regional Champion in wrestling and a two-123456789101112131415161718 time national qualifier in 2006 and 2007; andWHEREAS, After graduation from college in 2008, Detective Sergeant Becker dedicated his life to public service with the Northern York County Regional Police Department; andWHEREAS, On June 8, 2010, Detective Sergeant Becker heroically responded to a house fire, saving a family of four; andWHEREAS, Throughout his distinguished career with the Northern York Regional Police Department, Detective Sergeant Becker received numerous commendations and awards for excellence during his career in law enforcement; andWHEREAS, Detective Sergeant Becker's reputation for humility, dedication and selfless service left a lasting impact on those that had the privilege of knowing him; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives honor the life and extend deepest condolences to the family of Detective Sergeant Cody Michael Becker.20250HR0383PN2732 - 2 - 1234567891011121314151617
