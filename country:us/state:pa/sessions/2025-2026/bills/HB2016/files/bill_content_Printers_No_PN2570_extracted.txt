2025-2026 Regular Session HB 2016 PN 2570 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 2570 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE BILL No.2016 Session of 2025 INTRODUCED BY MADSEN, HILL-EVANS AND BRENNAN, NOVEMBER 5, 2025 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON HOUSING AND COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT, NOVEMBER 6, 2025 AN ACTAmending the act of October 17, 2008 (P.L.1645, No.132), entitled "An act providing for the regulation of home improvement contracts and for the registration of certain contractors; prohibiting certain acts; and providing for penalties," further providing for home improvement contracts.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Section 7(e)(8) of the act of October 17, 2008 (P.L.1645, No.132), known as the Home Improvement Consumer Protection Act, is amended to read:Section 7. Home improvement contracts.* * *(e) Voidable clauses.--If a home improvement contract contains any of the following clauses, the home improvement contract shall be voidable by the owner:* * *(8) A provision that [the contractor shall be awarded attorney fees and costs.] awards attorney fees and costs to a prevailing party in an action or arbitration unless the 12345678910111213141516171819 provision applies equally to both parties.* * *Section 2. The amendment of section 7(e)(8) of the act shall apply to home improvement contracts executed on or after the effective date of this section.Section 3. This act shall take effect in 60 days.20250HB2016PN2570 - 2 - 123456
