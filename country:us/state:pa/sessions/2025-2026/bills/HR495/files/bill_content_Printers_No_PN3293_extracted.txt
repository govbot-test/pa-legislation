2025-2026 Regular Session HR 495 PN 3293 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 3293 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.495 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY COOK, HAMM, WENTLING, WALLEN, NEILSON, GREINER, BANTA, KRUPA, RAPP, STAMBAUGH, PICKETT, HADDOCK, MALONEY, CAUSER, PASHINSKI, SMITH AND ANDERSON, APRIL 28, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON AGRICULTURE AND RURAL AFFAIRS, APRIL 28, 2026 A RESOLUTIONDesignating May 5, 2026, as "The Blessing of the Balers Day" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, The Blessing of the Balers started in Greene County when a local farmer, Joseph Krampy, was having trouble with his baler one day; andWHEREAS, Just when Mr. Krampy was about to give up, a Catholic priest who was passing by stopped to inquire about the baling process; andWHEREAS, Mr. Krampy spoke to the priest for a while and mentioned the issues he was having on that day; andWHEREAS, As the conversation wrapped up, the priest suggested the two men pray over the baler that had been giving Mr. Krampy problems; andWHEREAS, After the prayer, Mr. Krampy decided to go back to the baler and give it one more shot before he called it a day; andWHEREAS, Much to Mr. Krampy's surprise, the baler started up 1234567891011121314151617 and went back to dropping bales again like nothing ever happened; andWHEREAS, After that, Mr. Krampy was able to finish the field without missing a beat; andWHEREAS, In 2026, The Blessing of the Balers will be held in Harrisburg; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives designate May 5, 2026, as "The Blessing of the Balers Day" in Pennsylvania; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives wish good fortune to all farmers across this Commonwealth for their upcoming growing season and harvest.20260HR0495PN3293 - 2 - 123456789101112
