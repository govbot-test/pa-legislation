A RESOLUTION
Designating May 5, 2026, as "The Blessing of the Balers Day" in
Pennsylvania.
WHEREAS, The Blessing of the Balers started in Greene County
when a local farmer, Joseph Krampy, was having trouble with his
baler one day; and
WHEREAS, Just when Mr. Krampy was about to give up, a
Catholic priest who was passing by stopped to inquire about the
baling process; and
WHEREAS, Mr. Krampy spoke to the priest for a while and
mentioned the issues he was having on that day; and
WHEREAS, As the conversation wrapped up, the priest suggested
the two men pray over the baler that had been giving Mr. Krampy
problems; and
WHEREAS, After the prayer, Mr. Krampy decided to go back to
the baler and give it one more shot before he called it a day;
and
WHEREAS, Much to Mr. Krampy's surprise, the baler started up
