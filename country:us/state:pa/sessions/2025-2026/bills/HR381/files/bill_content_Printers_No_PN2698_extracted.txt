2025-2026 Regular Session HR 381 PN 2698 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 2698 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.381 Session of 2025 INTRODUCED BY BELLMON, MADDEN, PARKER, WAXMAN, BRENNAN, VENKAT, HILL-EVANS, RABB, RIVERA, HOHENSTEIN AND SANCHEZ, DECEMBER 10, 2025 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON TOURISM, RECREATION AND ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT, DECEMBER 11, 2025 A RESOLUTIONRecognizing January 1, 2026, as "Haitian Independence Day" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, On January 1, 1804, Haiti became a free republic, ending colonial rule and the practice of slavery; andWHEREAS, Haiti's independence was the culmination of the Haitian Revolution, which began in 1791 in what was then the French colony of Saint-Domingue, the western third of the island Hispaniola; andWHEREAS, The Haitian Revolution consisted of a complex series of conflicts that persisted for over a decade, involving enslaved Haitians, colonists, British armies and French colonizers, among other parties; andWHEREAS, As the world's first Black Republic, Haiti secured its independence through extraordinary courage and persistence; andWHEREAS, Despite the success of the Haitian Revolution, it came at the cost of more than 345,000 lives between 1790 and 1234567891011121314151617 1804, with most of these deaths being those of formerly enslaved individuals; andWHEREAS, The Haitian Revolution helped to shape world history, including the history of Pennsylvania; andWHEREAS, Widespread effects of the Haitian Revolution led to acts of resistance and protests against slavery and racial injustice in Pennsylvania; andWHEREAS, Black Philadelphians, inspired by the success of the Haitian Revolution, used it as a model for their own local liberation efforts, demonstrating the city's long-established roots in Black resistance; andWHEREAS, Today more than 27,000 Haitians reside in this Commonwealth, contributing to Pennsylvania culture and society in diverse ways; andWHEREAS, By commemorating "Haitian Independence Day," we honor the extraordinary courage, endurance and fight for freedom that defined the Haitian Revolution, while paying tribute to those who lost their lives for this cause and recognizing the contributions of the Haitian population in this Commonwealth; andWHEREAS, Recognizing Haiti's Independence Day not only celebrates the enduring legacy of the world's first Black Republic, but also the universal human right to be free from oppression; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives recognize January 1, 2026, as "Haitian Independence Day" in Pennsylvania.20250HR0381PN2698 - 2 - 1234567891011121314151617181920212223242526
