2025-2026 Regular Session HB 2232 PN 2925 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 2925 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE BILL No.2232 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY MAKO AND KAUFFMAN, FEBRUARY 19, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON LABOR AND INDUSTRY, FEBRUARY 20, 2026 AN ACTAmending Title 44 (Law and Justice) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes, in law enforcement background investigations and employment information, further providing for maintenance of records.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Section 7308(c) of Title 44 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is amended by adding a paragraph to read:§ 7308. Maintenance of records.* * *(c) Disagreement with record accuracy.--* * *(1.1) If the law enforcement agency refuses to correct or remove a portion of the separation record under paragraph (1), the law enforcement officer may file an appeal of the law enforcement agency's determination in accordance with 2 Pa.C.S. Ch. 7 (relating to judicial review).* * *Section 2. This act shall take effect in 60 days.12345678910111213141516171819
