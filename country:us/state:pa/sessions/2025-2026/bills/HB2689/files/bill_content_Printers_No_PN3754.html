ambulatory surgical center data collection, for the Joint

and all receipts of appropriations from the Commonwealth,

section 7(a) of Article VIII of the Constitution of
Pennsylvania authorizing and restricting the incurring of
certain debt and imposing penalties; affecting every
department, board, commission, and officer of the State
government, every political subdivision of the State, and
certain officers of such subdivisions, every person,

The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania

Section 1. Section 1718-E(a) of the act of April 9, 1929
(P.L.343, No.176), known as The Fiscal Code, is amended by
adding a paragraph to read:
Section 1718-E. Department of Agriculture.
(a) Appropriations.--The following shall apply to
appropriations for the Department of Agriculture:
* * *
(6) Notwithstanding any other provision of law, from
remaining money allocated for highly pathogenic avian
influenza prior to fiscal year 2024-2025, the following shall
apply:
(i) No more than $20,000,000 shall be used for a
disaster recovery grant program to assist fruit and
vegetable growers who experienced losses exceeding 30% of
expected 2026 production of eligible Pennsylvania-grown
specialty crops due to freeze events that occurred
between April 1 and April 21, 2026.
(ii) Pennsylvania-grown specialty crops include all
pome and stone fruits, grapes, berries and vegetables.
(iii) Losses or costs that are eligible for or
covered by Federal funding, insurance, contracts or other
funding sources are not eligible for recovery grant
funding under this paragraph.
(iv) Within 90 days of the effective date of this
paragraph, the Department of Agriculture shall submit
