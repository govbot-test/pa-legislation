AN ACT
Providing for safety regarding artificial intelligence in
companionship applications; and imposing a penalty.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Short title.
This act shall be known and may be cited as the Artificial
Intelligence in Companionship Applications Safety Act.
Section 2. Definitions.
The following words and phrases when used in this act shall
have the meanings given to them in this section unless the
context clearly indicates otherwise:
"AI companion."
(1) A system that:
(i) uses artificial intelligence and generative
artificial intelligence to simulate a human or humanlike
relationship with emotional recognition algorithms; and
(ii) interacts with a user by compiling previous

(B) personalize interaction based on user

(C) ask emotion-based questions unprompted in

(D) maintain conversations to the user feelings

(2) The term does not include AI used for customer
service, research, technical assistance or systems for
employee productivity in the workplace.
"AI model." A component of an informational system program
that uses artificial intelligence for computational techniques
to produce outputs from a given set of outputs.
"Artificial intelligence" or "AI." A machine-based system
that can, for a given set of human-defined objectives, make
predictions, recommendations or decisions influencing real or
virtual environments, including the ability to:
(1) perceive real and virtual environments;
(2) abstract perceptions made under paragraph (1) into
AI models through analysis in an automated manner; and
(3) use AI model inference to formulate options for
information or action based on outcomes under paragraphs (1)
and (2).
"Emotional recognition algorithms." Artificial intelligence
that detects and reacts to an individual user's emotions from
collected data like text, voice audio or video.
"Generative artificial intelligence." The class of AI models
that emulate the structure and characteristics of input data in
order to generate derived synthetic content.

"Operator." Any individual, association, business, member or
subsidiary who operates for or provides an AI companion to a

"Self-harm." Intentional self-injury.
"Suicidal ideation." A feeling, thought or consideration
with the preoccupation of the idea of death or suicide.
"Synthetic content." Information, such as images, videos,
audio clips or text, that have been significantly modified or
generated by algorithms, including by artificial intelligence.
"User." An individual who:
(1) uses an AI companion for personal use within this
Commonwealth; and
(2) is not an operator.
Section 3. Artificial intelligence companion.
(a) Certain protocols required.--It shall be unlawful for an
operator to provide an AI companion to a user unless the AI
companion contains protocols that:
(1) identify suicidal ideation or expressions of self-
harm;
(2) decline to assist a user with a suicide attempt,
methods or improvement of methods; and
(3) refer the user to a crisis center if suicidal
ideation or expressions of self-harm are recognized.
(b) Referral to crisis center.--The referral required under
subsection (a)(3) shall include:
(1) crisis service contact information, including the
988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline, or a subsequent iteration;
(2) the closest behavioral health crisis centers to the
user; or
(3) other appropriate crisis services.

(c) Prohibition.--An AI companion may not claim, imply or

Section 4. Artificial intelligence companion requirements.
An operator shall:
(1) Publish details on the protocol on the operator's
Internet website.
(2) At the beginning of a session with an AI companion
and once every three hours during the session, provide a
notification to the user stating, either verbally or in
writing, that the user is communicating with an AI companion
and not a human.
Section 5. Violations and penalty.
A user may file a complaint with the Attorney General
alleging that an operator has violated this act, to which the
following shall apply:
(1) The Attorney General, if provided satisfactory
evidence that an operator has, or intends to, violate this
act, can bring an action in the name and on behalf of the
people of this Commonwealth against the operator.
(2) An operator in violation of this act shall be
subject to:
(i) a civil penalty of no more than $15,000 per day
per violation; and
(ii) additional remedies that the court deems
appropriate.
(3) The Attorney General may initiate, in Commonwealth
Court or the court of common pleas of the county in which the
individual or entity resides, an action in equity for an

SECTION 3. ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE COMPANION PROTOCOLS.
(A) PROHIBITION.--AN OPERATOR MAY NOT PROVIDE AN AI
COMPANION TO A USER UNLESS THE OPERATOR IMPLEMENTS AND MAINTAINS
PROTOCOLS.
(B) PROTOCOLS.--
(1) AN OPERATOR SHALL IMPLEMENT AND MAINTAIN PROTOCOLS
THAT REFER A USER TO A CRISIS CENTER, INCLUDING 988 LIFELINE
RESPONSE, IF THE USER:
(I) INDICATES SUICIDAL IDEATION OR EXPRESSIONS OF
SELF-HARM, INCLUDING EXPRESSIONS RELATING TO EATING
DISORDERS; OR
(II) INDICATES INTEREST OR INTENT TO HARM OTHERS.

INTERACTION.

USER AS FOLLOWS:
(I) AT THE BEGINNING OF EACH INTERACTION.
(II) AT LEAST ONCE EVERY HOUR DURING AN INTERACTION
THAT REMINDS THE USER TO TAKE A BREAK AND THAT THE AI
COMPANION IS ARTIFICIALLY GENERATED AND NOT HUMAN. THE

SEXUALLY EXPLICIT IMAGES BY MINOR).
