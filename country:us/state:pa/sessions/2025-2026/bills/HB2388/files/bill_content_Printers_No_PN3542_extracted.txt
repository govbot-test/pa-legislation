Short title.This act shall be known and may be cited as the Pennsylvania Common Cents Act.Section 2. Definitions.The following words and phrases when used in this act shall have the meanings given to them in this section unless the context clearly indicates otherwise:"Commonwealth agency." A department, bureau, board or other body created by the laws of this Commonwealth."Municipal authority." A body or board authorized to enact ordinances or adopt resolutions on behalf of a municipality and that:(1) provides natural or artificial gas, electricity or <--1234567891011121314151617 steam for the production of light, heat or power to or for the public for compensation;(2) diverts, develops, pumps, impounds, distributes or furnishes water to or for the public for compensation; or(3) provides wastewater collection, treatment or disposal for the public for compensation."Physical legal tender." Coins, banknotes and other currency recognized as legal tender under 31 U.S.C. § 5103 (relating to legal tender)."Political subdivision." A county, city, borough, township, town or school district with taxing jurisdiction in a defined geographic area within this Commonwealth."Public utility." A person that owns or operates in this Commonwealth equipment or facilities for:(1) producing, generating, transmitting, distributing or furnishing natural or artificial gas, electricity or steam for the production of light, heat or power to or for the public for compensation;(2) diverting, developing, pumping, impounding, distributing or furnishing water to or for the public for compensation; or(3) wastewater collection, treatment or disposal for the public for compensation.Section 3. Physical legal tender transactions rounding.(a) Rounding required.--A political subdivision, Commonwealth agency, municipal authority or public utility that accepts face-to-face, in-person sales or collections of physical legal tender in this Commonwealth shall determine the total price of collections in the following manner:(1) Collections shall be totaled with any discount or 20260HB2388PN3542 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 deduction.(2) If one cent, two cents, six cents or seven cents is at the end of the total sum, the sum shall be rounded down to the nearest amount divisible by five for those individuals paying with physical legal tender.(3) If three cents, four cents, eight cents or nine cents is at the end of the total sum, the sum shall be rounded up to the nearest amount divisible by five for those individuals paying with physical legal tender.(b) Exception.--Subsection (a) shall not apply to a payment method utilizing a payment other than physical legal tender, such as a check, debit card, credit card or electronic or digital payment.Section 4. Effective date.This act shall take effect immediately.SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.THIS ACT SHALL BE KNOWN AND MAY BE CITED AS THE PENNSYLVANIA COMMON CENTS ACT.SECTION 2. COINS, BANKNOTES AND OTHER CURRENCY RECOGNIZED AS LEGAL TENDER UNDER 31 U.S.C. § 5103 (RELATING TO LEGAL TENDER)."POLITICAL SUBDIVISION." PHYSICAL LEGAL TENDER TRANSACTIONS ROUNDING.A COMMONWEALTH AGENCY, ENTITY, MUNICIPAL AUTHORITY OR POLITICAL SUBDIVISION THAT ACCEPTS FACE-TO-FACE, IN-PERSON SALES OR COLLECTIONS OF PHYSICAL LEGAL TENDER IN THIS COMMONWEALTH SHALL DETERMINE THE TOTAL DUE AT THE TIME OF COLLECTION BY A CUSTOMER OR RATEPAYER.
