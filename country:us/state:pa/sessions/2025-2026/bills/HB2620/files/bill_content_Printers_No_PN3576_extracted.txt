Biennial Educational Requirements.--Each holder of a license to practice veterinary medicine under this act shall be required to complete thirty clock hours of educational programs in the twenty-four months preceding each renewal date, and each holder of a certificate to practice veterinary technology as a certified veterinary technician or certified veterinary nurse under this act shall be required to complete sixteen clock hours of educational programs in the twenty-four months preceding each renewal date.
