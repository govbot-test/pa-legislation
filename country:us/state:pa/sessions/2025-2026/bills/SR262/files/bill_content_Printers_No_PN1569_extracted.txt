2025-2026 Regular Session SR 262 PN 1569 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 1569 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIASENATE RESOLUTION No.262 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY CAPPELLETTI, HUGHES, STREET, KEARNEY, KANE, HAYWOOD, TARTAGLIONE, COMITTA AND COSTA, APRIL 8, 2026 REFERRED TO RULES AND EXECUTIVE NOMINATIONS, APRIL 8, 2026 A RESOLUTIONRecognizing the 56th celebration of "Earth Day" on April 22, 2026, in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, On April 22, 1970, United States Senator Gaylord Nelson of Wisconsin created the concept of "Earth Day" to increase public awareness of the world's environmental problems; andWHEREAS, Increasingly, more individuals and communities recognize and participate in "Earth Day" each year; andWHEREAS, "Earth Day" is now observed in more than 190 countries worldwide, serving as a powerful reminder that environmental stewardship is a shared global responsibility; andWHEREAS, The necessity for individuals, communities and governments to protect the planet is more essential now than ever, particularly for the people of this Commonwealth who recognize that environmental protection is vital to public health, economic stability and the well-being of all living things; andWHEREAS, All living things have intrinsic value and each 123456789101112131415161718 plays a unique role in the complex web of life; andWHEREAS, The theme of "Earth Day" 2026 is "Our Power, Our Planet," which highlights the collective strength of individuals, communities and governments to drive meaningful environmental change and accelerate the transition to a more sustainable and resilient future; andWHEREAS, "Earth Day" 2026 emphasizes that the power to protect our planet lies in our choices and public policies, including advancing renewable energy, reducing pollution, preserving natural habitats and investing in sustainable infrastructure; andWHEREAS, "Earth Day" continues to promote environmentally responsible practices, including recycling, energy efficiency, reducing waste and the use of environmentally friendly products; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the Senate recognize the 56th celebration of "Earth Day" on April 22, 2026, in Pennsylvania; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the Senate encourage the residents of this Commonwealth to take meaningful action to protect the environment and support the goals of "Earth Day" 2026 to ensure a sustainable future for generations to come.20260SR0262PN1569 - 2 - 12345678910111213141516171819202122
