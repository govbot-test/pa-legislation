2025-2026 Regular Session HB 2075 PN 2658 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 2658 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE BILL No.2075 Session of 2025 INTRODUCED BY CAUSER, RAPP, PICKETT, JAMES, BERNSTINE, RIVERA, KUZMA, GILLEN, ROWE, ARMANINI AND OWLETT, DECEMBER 1, 2025 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON JUDICIARY, DECEMBER 3, 2025 AN ACTAmending Title 61 (Prisons and Parole) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes, in Pennsylvania Board of Probation and Parole, further providing for reentry supervision.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Section 6137.2(a) of Title 61 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is amended and the section is amended by adding a subsection to read:§ 6137.2. Reentry supervision.(a) General rule.--This section applies to persons committed to the department:(1) with an aggregate minimum sentence of total confinement under 42 Pa.C.S. § 9756(b) (relating to sentence of total confinement) of 4 years or more[.]; or(2) except as provided in 42 Pa.C.S. § 9718.5 (relating to mandatory period of probation for certain sexual offenders), with an aggregate minimum sentence of total confinement under 42 Pa.C.S. § 9756(b) of one year or more if 123456789101112131415161718 the offense involved a victim under 18 years of age.(a.1) Applicability.--Regardless of the sentence imposed, this section does not apply to persons sentenced to death, life imprisonment, persons otherwise ineligible for parole or persons subject to 42 Pa.C.S. § 9718.5 [(relating to mandatory period of probation for certain sexual offenders)].* * *Section 2. This act shall take effect in 60 days.20250HB2075PN2658 - 2 - 12345678
