2025-2026 Regular Session HB 2168 PN 2810 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 2810 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE BILL No.2168 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY N. NELSON, JANUARY 28, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON EDUCATION, JANUARY 28, 2026 AN ACTAmending the act of December 19, 1990 (P.L.1372, No.212), entitled "An act providing for early intervention services for infants, toddlers and preschool children who qualify; establishing the Interagency Coordinating Council and providing for its powers and duties; and conferring powers and duties upon the Department of Education and the State Board of Education, the Department of Health and the Department of Public Welfare," in Statewide system for provision of early intervention services, further providing for administration by Department of Education.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Section 304(a)(4) of the act of December 19, 1990 (P.L.1372, No.212), known as the Early Intervention Services System Act, is amended to read:Section 304. Administration by Department of Education.(a) Overall responsibility.--* * *(4) The department shall provide for the programs of eligible young children who are not eligible for services provided in school district kindergarten programs and who are placed in approved [private] special education schools or 12345678910111213141516171819202122 chartered schools pursuant to the provisions of section 1376 or 1376.1 of the Public School Code of 1949 from the State funds appropriated to the department for special education in approved [private] special education schools. The department shall provide only for the excess costs for eligible young children who are eligible for services provided in school district kindergarten programs and who are placed in approved [private] special education schools or chartered schools pursuant to the provisions of section 1376 or 1376.1 of the Public School Code of 1949 from the State funds appropriated to the department for special education in approved [private] special education schools.* * *Section 2. This act shall take effect in 60 days.20260HB2168PN2810 - 2 - 1234567891011121314
