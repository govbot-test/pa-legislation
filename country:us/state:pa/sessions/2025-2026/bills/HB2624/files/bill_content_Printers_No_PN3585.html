AN ACT
Amending Title 75 (Vehicles) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated
Statutes, in size, weight and load, further providing for
limits on number of towed vehicles and for length of
vehicles, providing for application of width, height and
length provisions to tow trucks and further providing for
application of maximum weights of vehicle provisions to tow
trucks.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:

Section 3. Title 75 is amended by adding a section to read:
§ 4926. Application to tow trucks.
(a) Size exemption.--Except as provided in subsection (b),
the size restrictions specified in this subchapter do not apply
to a tow truck towing a wrecked or disabled motor vehicle or
combination that meets all of the following requirements:
(1) The tow truck is towing the wrecked or disabled
motor vehicle or combination:
(i) from the scene of an accident or other location
where the motor vehicle or combination is disabled;
(ii) to a close and practical place of repairs or
other place of safety within 75 miles; and
(iii) by a safe and practical route.
(2) The tow truck and the wrecked or disabled motor
vehicle or combination comply with the size restrictions
specified in this subchapter when each vehicle is measured
separately.
(b) Restricted bridges and highways.--A tow truck towing a
wrecked or disabled motor vehicle or combination shall comply
with any size restrictions imposed by the department or a local
authority on a bridge or portion of a highway under section 4902
(relating to restrictions on use of highways and bridges).
