2025-2026 Regular Session HB 2624 PN 3585 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 3585 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE BILL No.2624 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY STENDER, ROWE AND SMITH, JUNE 10, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON TRANSPORTATION, JUNE 10, 2026 AN ACTAmending Title 75 (Vehicles) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes, in size, weight and load, further providing for limits on number of towed vehicles and for length of vehicles, providing for application of width, height and length provisions to tow trucks and further providing for application of maximum weights of vehicle provisions to tow trucks.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Section 4904(c) of Title 75 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is amended and the section is amended by adding subsections to read:§ 4904. Limits on number of towed vehicles.* * *(c) Towing vehicles requiring service.--(1) A dolly not exceeding ten feet in length may be towed by a motor vehicle for the purpose of towing another vehicle requiring service.[(2) A combination requiring emergency service may be towed to a nearby garage or other place of safety.](c.1) Towing disabled combinations.--Subsection (a) shall 123456789101112131415161718192021 not apply to a wrecked or disabled combination being towed by a tow truck on a highway if the wrecked or disabled combination meets all of the following requirements:(1) The tow truck is towing the wrecked or disabled combination:(i) from the scene of an accident or other location where the combination is disabled;(ii) to a close and practical place of repairs or other place of safety within 75 miles; and(iii) by a safe and practical route.(2) The wrecked or disabled combination, including the tow truck, consists of no more than three vehicles, unless:(i) the wrecked or disabled combination needs to be towed in an emergency situation; and(ii) the tow truck operator is directed by emergency personnel or a qualified Commonwealth employee to move the wrecked or disabled combination exceeding three vehicles to the closest and practical place of safety.(c.2) Restricted bridges and highways.--A tow truck towing a wrecked or disabled combination shall comply with any weight or size restrictions imposed by the department or a local authority on a bridge or portion of a highway under section 4902 (relating to restrictions on use of highways and bridges).* * *Section 2. Section 4923(b.1)(7) of Title 75 is amended to read:§ 4923. Title 75 is amended by adding a section to read:§ 4926. Application to tow trucks. (a) Size exemption.--Except as provided in subsection (b), the size restrictions specified in this subchapter do not apply to a tow truck towing a wrecked or disabled motor vehicle or combination that meets all of the following requirements:(1) The tow truck is towing the wrecked or disabled motor vehicle or combination:(i) from the scene of an accident or other location where the motor vehicle or combination is disabled;(ii) to a close and practical place of repairs or other place of safety within 75 miles; and(iii) by a safe and practical route.(2) The tow truck and the wrecked or disabled motor vehicle or combination comply with the size restrictions specified in this subchapter when each vehicle is measured separately.(b) Restricted bridges and highways.--A tow truck towing a wrecked or disabled motor vehicle or combination shall comply with any size restrictions imposed by the department or a local authority on a bridge or portion of a highway under section 4902 (relating to restrictions on use of highways and bridges).Section 4.
