2025-2026 Regular Session HR 433 PN 2991 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 2991 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.433 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY SMITH-WADE-EL, CARROLL, BENHAM, MAYES, SALISBURY, SCOTT, KENYATTA, TAKAC, HOHENSTEIN, GUENST, RIVERA, SHUSTERMAN, WAXMAN, McNEILL, FRANKEL, HILL-EVANS, SANCHEZ, POWELL, BURGOS, MERSKI, FLEMING, BOYD AND CEPEDA-FREYTIZ, MARCH 11, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON STATE GOVERNMENT, MARCH 12, 2026 A RESOLUTIONRecognizing March 31, 2026, as "Transgender Day of Visibility" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, Begun in 2009, "Transgender Day of Visibility" is recognized annually on March 31 to celebrate the accomplishments and contributions of the transgender community; andWHEREAS, The day celebrates transgender lives while also recognizing that, because of discrimination, not every transgender individual can, or chooses to, be visible; andWHEREAS, Today, more than 2.8 million individuals identify as transgender in the United States; andWHEREAS, Transgender Americans are part of the fabric of the United States and play a vital role in helping our nation succeed, from serving our communities and the military to raising families and leading businesses; andWHEREAS, In the past year, there has been escalating hostility and attacks against the transgender community, 12345678910111213141516 including more than 450 anti-LGBTQ bills introduced in state legislatures across the country; andWHEREAS, Alongside these legislative attacks, the transgender community continues to face direct physical violence, a crisis that has been declared an ongoing epidemic since 2019 by the American Medical Association; andWHEREAS, Transgender individuals deserve the freedom and safety to live openly and authentically as themselves; andWHEREAS, That is why transgender visibility matters, and it is important that we celebrate the authentic, diverse and beautiful stories that reflect the real lived experiences of every transgender person; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives recognize March 31, 2026, as "Transgender Day of Visibility" in Pennsylvania.20260HR0433PN2991 - 2 - 1234567891011121314
