A RESOLUTION
Recognizing March 31, 2026, as "Transgender Day of Visibility"
in Pennsylvania.
WHEREAS, Begun in 2009, "Transgender Day of Visibility" is
recognized annually on March 31 to celebrate the accomplishments
and contributions of the transgender community; and
WHEREAS, The day celebrates transgender lives while also
recognizing that, because of discrimination, not every
transgender individual can, or chooses to, be visible; and
WHEREAS, Today, more than 2.8 million individuals identify as
transgender in the United States; and
WHEREAS, Transgender Americans are part of the fabric of the
United States and play a vital role in helping our nation
succeed, from serving our communities and the military to
raising families and leading businesses; and
WHEREAS, In the past year, there has been escalating
hostility and attacks against the transgender community,

31, 2026, as "Transgender Day of Visibility" in Pennsylvania.
