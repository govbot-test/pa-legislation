2025-2026 Regular Session HR 496 PN 3307 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 3307 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.496 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY HOHENSTEIN, WAXMAN, HARKINS, NEILSON, CONKLIN, KULIK, HILL-EVANS, SANCHEZ, McNEILL, DOUGHERTY, GUENST, FREEMAN, GALLAGHER, RIVERA, CEPEDA-FREYTIZ, PASHINSKI, MALAGARI, D. WILLIAMS AND HADDOCK, APRIL 28, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON GAME AND FISHERIES, APRIL 29, 2026 A RESOLUTIONRecognizing the week of May 16 through 22, 2026, as "National Safe Boating Week" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, Boating is a fun and healthy recreational activity, from canoeing and kayaking on Pennsylvania's numerous creeks and ponds to deepwater boating on large rivers and lakes; andWHEREAS, Safety is the most important consideration in any boating activity; andWHEREAS, Basic knowledge, skills and safety equipment are required to ensure that boat operators, whether first-time participants or seasoned operators, understand the exemplary benefits of being out on the water as well as potential risks and dangers; andWHEREAS, The Pennsylvania Fish and Boat Commission, in partnership with other organizations and entities, provides basic boating courses, paddling safety and educational opportunities for the public; andWHEREAS, Pennsylvania law requires an individual to obtain a 1234567891011121314151617 boating safety education certificate if the individual was born after January 1, 1982, and operates a powerboat with a motor greater than 25 horsepower or if the individual operates a personal watercraft; andWHEREAS, United States Coast Guard-approved, properly sized and fitted life jackets are required for each person aboard a watercraft; andWHEREAS, Life jackets are the most important piece of equipment on a boat, as nearly 80% of all boating fatalities in this Commonwealth occur because boaters were not wearing a life jacket at the time of the incident; andWHEREAS, Lives can be saved each year by the simple act of wearing life jackets while boating; andWHEREAS, Safe boating practices and the responsible, legal use of prescription drugs or alcohol are everyone's responsibility; andWHEREAS, Recognizing and promoting safe boating practices and wearing a life jacket while enjoying Pennsylvania's waterways are essential; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives recognize the week of May 16 through 22, 2026, as "National Safe Boating Week" in Pennsylvania.20260HR0496PN3307 - 2 - 12345678910111213141516171819202122
