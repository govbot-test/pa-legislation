A RESOLUTION
Recognizing the week of May 16 through 22, 2026, as "National
Safe Boating Week" in Pennsylvania.
WHEREAS, Boating is a fun and healthy recreational activity,
from canoeing and kayaking on Pennsylvania's numerous creeks and
ponds to deepwater boating on large rivers and lakes; and
WHEREAS, Safety is the most important consideration in any
boating activity; and
WHEREAS, Basic knowledge, skills and safety equipment are
required to ensure that boat operators, whether first-time
participants or seasoned operators, understand the exemplary
benefits of being out on the water as well as potential risks
and dangers; and
WHEREAS, The Pennsylvania Fish and Boat Commission, in
partnership with other organizations and entities, provides
basic boating courses, paddling safety and educational
opportunities for the public; and
WHEREAS, Pennsylvania law requires an individual to obtain a

WHEREAS, United States Coast Guard-approved, properly sized
and fitted life jackets are required for each person aboard a
watercraft; and
WHEREAS, Life jackets are the most important piece of
equipment on a boat, as nearly 80% of all boating fatalities in
this Commonwealth occur because boaters were not wearing a life
jacket at the time of the incident; and
WHEREAS, Lives can be saved each year by the simple act of
wearing life jackets while boating; and
WHEREAS, Safe boating practices and the responsible, legal
use of prescription drugs or alcohol are everyone's
responsibility; and
WHEREAS, Recognizing and promoting safe boating practices and
wearing a life jacket while enjoying Pennsylvania's waterways
are essential; therefore be it
RESOLVED, That the House of Representatives recognize the
week of May 16 through 22, 2026, as "National Safe Boating Week"
in Pennsylvania.
