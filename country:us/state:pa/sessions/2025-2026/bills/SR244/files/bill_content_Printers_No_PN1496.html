A RESOLUTION
Directing the Joint State Government Commission to establish the
Task Force on Child Protection in the Digital Age.
WHEREAS, Technology is evolving at a pace that is leaving
gaps in existing statutory framework to protect children online;
and
WHEREAS, Advances in artificial intelligence, social media
platforms, algorithm-driven content and digital manipulation
have created new and increasingly sophisticated risks to minors,
including exploitation, exposure to harmful content and
psychological harm; and
WHEREAS, The General Assembly has taken meaningful steps in
recent legislative sessions to address issues such as deepfakes,
child sexual abuse material and online safety; and
WHEREAS, There is a need to continually reevaluate our laws
to ensure that they are updated to protect children; and
WHEREAS, Establishing a reoccurring Task Force on Child
Protection in the Digital Age ensures that Pennsylvania's laws
