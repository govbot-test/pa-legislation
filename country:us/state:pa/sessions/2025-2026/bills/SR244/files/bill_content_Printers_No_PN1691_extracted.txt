WARD, VOGEL, SCHWANK, PICOZZI, MALONE, BROOKS, BAKER AND FARRY, MARCH 12, 2026 SENATOR PENNYCUICK, COMMUNICATIONS AND TECHNOLOGY, AS AMENDED, MAY 6, 2026A RESOLUTIONDirecting the Joint State Government Commission to establish the Task Force on Child Protection in the Digital Age.WHEREAS, Technology is evolving at a pace that is leaving gaps in existing statutory framework to protect children online; andWHEREAS, Advances in artificial intelligence, social media platforms, algorithm-driven content and digital manipulation have created new and increasingly sophisticated risks to minors, including exploitation, exposure to harmful content and psychological harm; andWHEREAS, The General Assembly has taken meaningful steps in recent legislative sessions to address issues such as deepfakes, child sexual abuse material and online safety; andWHEREAS, There is a need to continually reevaluate our laws to ensure that they are updated to protect children; andWHEREAS, Establishing a reoccurring Task Force on Child Protection in the Digital Age ensures that Pennsylvania's laws 1234567891011121314151617 keep pace with the digital age and that our statutes and policies are continually reevaluated, updated and strengthened to protect children; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the Senate direct the Joint State Government Commission to establish the Task Force on Child Protection in the Digital Age; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the Task Force on Child Protection in the Digital Age consist of:(1) the Attorney General of Pennsylvania or a designee, who shall serve as chairperson;(2) the Commissioner of Pennsylvania State Police or a designee who must be an employee of the Pennsylvania State Police;(3) the Chairperson EXECUTIVE DIRECTOR of the Pennsylvania Commission on Crime and Delinquency or a designee who must be an employee of the Pennsylvania Commission on Crime and Delinquency;(4) the Victim Advocate from the Office of Victim Advocate or a designee who must be an employee of the Office of Victim Advocate;(5) a member of law enforcement who has experience in investigations of child sexual abuse material or sexual abuse of children, appointed by the Attorney General;(6) a district attorney in this Commonwealth who has experience in prosecutions related to child sexual abuse material or sexual abuse of children, appointed by the Attorney General;(7) a member of the Pennsylvania Internet Crimes Against Children Task Force, appointed by the Attorney General;(8) the director of a rape crisis center in this 20260SR0244PN1691 - 2 abuse, appointed by the Attorney General;(11) three members appointed by the Attorney General who have experience in investigations or prosecutions of child abuse or sexual abuse of children, have PENNSYLVANIA COMMISSION ON SENTENCING OR A DESIGNEE WHO MUST BE AN EMPLOYEE OF THE PENNSYLVANIA COMMISSION ON SENTENCING; AND(12) (16) additional members necessary to carry out the 20260SR0244PN1691 - by the Attorney General shall serve until submission of the next comprehensive report following appointment, unless reappointed by the Attorney General; and BE IT FURTHERRESOLVED, That the Task Force on Child Protection in the Digital Age conduct comprehensive reviews to ascertain inadequacies in State statutes relating to advancements in technology, including, but not limited to, artificial intelligence that affects efforts relating to child protection, including, but not limited to, the offense of child sexual abuse material in 18 Pa.C.S. § 6312 (relating to sexual abuse of children); and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the Task Force on Child Protection in the Digital Age recommend changes in State statutes and practices, policies and procedures related to:(1) Child protection measures, as provided or required by applicable Federal or State law, as technology advances, including, but not limited to, artificial intelligence.(2) The investigation and prosecution of child sexual abuse material as defined in 18 Pa.C.S. § 6312.(3) The recognition, prevention or prosecution of child sexual abuse material as defined in 18 Pa.C.S. § 6312.(4) Efforts to protect and assist victims of child sexual abuse material or sexual abuse of children as defined in 18 Pa.C.S. § 6312;and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the Joint State Government Commission:20260SR0244PN1691 - 4 - recommendations no later than November 30, 2027, and submit a comprehensive report by November 30 of each odd-numbered year thereafter following the release of the initial report; and(3) reconstitute the Task Force on Child Protection in the Digital Age, in coordination with the Attorney General, for each comprehensive report;and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the report be published on the Joint State Government Commission's publicly accessible Internet website and that copies of the report be transmitted to:(1) the Governor;(2) the President pro tempore of the Senate; and(3) the Speaker of
