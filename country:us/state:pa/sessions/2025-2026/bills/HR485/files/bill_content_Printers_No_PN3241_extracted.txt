2025-2026 Regular Session HR 485 PN 3241 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 3241 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.485 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY MERSKI, HARKINS, CONKLIN, HOHENSTEIN, KHAN, PASHINSKI, ISAACSON, HOWARD, SANCHEZ, FREEMAN, RIVERA, WAXMAN, HILL-EVANS, GALLAGHER, CEPEDA-FREYTIZ, NEILSON, CIRESI AND CURRY, APRIL 20, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON EDUCATION, APRIL 21, 2026 A RESOLUTIONDesignating the month of September 2026 as "Healthy Hunger-Free Kids Month" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, Hunger is a reality for many students, with one in six children in this Commonwealth experiencing severe hunger; andWHEREAS, In 2024, nearly 7.3 million children in the United States lived in food-insecure homes, and 18.3 million households were food insecure at some point; andWHEREAS, In Pennsylvania, one in six children experienced food insecurity, and food insecurity was a daily part of life for nearly 1.7 million Pennsylvanians; andWHEREAS, We are fighting not only hunger in our children, but also millions of tons of food waste; andWHEREAS, Nearly 30 million school lunches are served daily across the nation; andWHEREAS, An estimated 530,000 tons of food, about 39 pounds per student, and 45 million gallons of milk, about 29 cartons 1234567891011121314151617 per student, are wasted per year in school cafeterias; andWHEREAS, The Healthy, Hunger-Free Kids Act went into effect in December 2010; andWHEREAS, Under the act, students must take a certain number of items, including a fruit or vegetable; andWHEREAS, Schools must serve the same size meals to kindergarteners, for whom the lunch might be too much, and older students, for whom it might not be enough; andWHEREAS, Research shows that many students are throwing the required healthier options into the trash; andWHEREAS, Share tables are a place where students can return unopened food and drinks that they choose not to consume so that other students may take additional helpings at no cost; andWHEREAS, Share tables are an excellent way for child nutrition programs to reduce food waste and provide nutrition for hungry kids; andWHEREAS, Share tables are an innovative strategy to encourage the consumption of nutritious foods and reduce food waste; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives designate the month of September 2026 as "Healthy Hunger-Free Kids Month" in Pennsylvania.20260HR0485PN3241 - 2 - 12345678910111213141516171819202122
