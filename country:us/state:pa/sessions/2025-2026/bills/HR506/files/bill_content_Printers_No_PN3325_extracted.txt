2025-2026 Regular Session HR 506 PN 3325 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 3325 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.506 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY MALONEY, ANDERSON, GLEIM, GREINER, HAMM, KEPHART, KLUNK, M. MACKENZIE, ROWE, SCHEUREN, STEHR, STENDER AND ZIMMERMAN, APRIL 29, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON LABOR AND INDUSTRY, APRIL 29, 2026 A RESOLUTIONRecognizing June 16, 2026, as "Merit Shop Day" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, The construction industry is one of our nation's largest industries, employing more than 5 million individuals in the United States; andWHEREAS, This Commonwealth relies on more than 200,000 construction workers and has the fifth largest construction workforce in the nation; andWHEREAS, Merit shop construction contractors operate on the principles of free enterprise, open competition and the selection of the most qualified and responsible contractors based on merit, performance and value; andWHEREAS, Merit shop construction contractors that are nonaffiliated with a labor organization represent approximately 90% of the industry; andWHEREAS, Merit shop registered apprenticeship programs have graduated thousands of apprentices into skilled craft professionals across the construction industry; and1234567891011121314151617 WHEREAS, Merit shop construction contractors significantly invest in their employee career development through registered apprenticeship programs, OSHA training, suicide prevention and re-entry opportunities for individuals returning from the criminal justice system in an effort to develop a highly skilled, diverse and safe construction workforce; andWHEREAS, Merit shop construction employees report rewarding careers in construction and high satisfaction as craft professionals; andWHEREAS, Merit shop construction contractors play a vital role in strengthening national, State and local economies by undertaking projects that build and maintain essential infrastructure, including schools, hospitals, transportation systems, utilities, commercial facilities and housing; andWHEREAS, Merit shops foster innovation, efficiency and cost-effectiveness by encouraging competition, adopting innovative construction technologies and implementing best practices in project planning, safety and workforce development; andWHEREAS, Merit shop construction contractors place a strong emphasis on workplace safety, recognizing that protecting the health and well-being of all workers is essential to project success, community trust and long-term industry sustainability; andWHEREAS, Through their commitment to excellence, integrity and accountability, merit shop construction contractors positively impact communities by delivering projects that enhance quality of life, promote economic development and support public services; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives recognize June 16, 2026, as "Merit Shop Day" in Pennsylvania.20260HR0506PN3325 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930
