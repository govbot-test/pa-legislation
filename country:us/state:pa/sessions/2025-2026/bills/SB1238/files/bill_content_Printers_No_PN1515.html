AN ACT
Amending Title 34 (Game) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated
Statutes, in hunting and furtaking, further providing for
unlawful devices and methods and for prohibited devices and
methods.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Sections 2308(b.1) and 2322(a)(2) of Title 34 of
the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes are amended to read:
§ 2308. Unlawful devices and methods.
* * *
(b.1) Additional exception.--[A semiautomatic rifle may be
used to hunt game in accordance with regulations promulgated by
the commission.] A semiautomatic centerfire rifle that fires
.223 caliber or larger ammunition may be used to hunt big game
if the rifle is limited to a capacity of not more than three
cartridges .
* * *
§ 2322. Prohibited devices and methods.
(a) General rule.--Except as otherwise provided in this

(2) Any automatic or semiautomatic firearm, except [that
