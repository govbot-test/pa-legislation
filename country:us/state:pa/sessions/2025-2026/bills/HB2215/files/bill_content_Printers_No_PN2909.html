PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS
Sec.
31A01. Scope of chapter.
31A02. Definitions.
§ 31A01. Scope of chapter.
This chapter relates to artificial intelligence chatbots and
guidelines for user age verification and responsible dialogue.
§ 31A02. Definitions.
The following words and phrases when used in this chapter
shall have the meanings given to them in this section unless the
context clearly indicates otherwise:
"Adult." An individual who is 18 years of age or older.
"Artificial intelligence chatbot" or "AI chatbot." An
interactive computer service or software application that:
(1) produces new expressive content or responses not
fully predetermined by the developer or operator of the
service or application;
( 2) accepts open-ended natural-language or multimodal
user input and produces adaptive or context-responsive
output; and
(3) does not include an interactive computer service or
software application, the responses of which are limited to
contextualized replies and are unable to respond on a range
of topics outside of a narrow specified purpose.
"Artificial intelligence companion" or "AI companion." An AI
chatbot that:
(1) p rovides adaptive, human-like responses to user
inputs; and
(2) is designed to encourage or facilitate the
simulation of interpersonal or emotional interaction,

"Covered entity." A person that owns, operates or otherwise
makes available an AI chatbot to individuals in the United

"Minor." An individual who is under 18 years of age.
"Reasonable age verification measure." A method that is
authenticated to relate to a user of an AI chatbot, including:
(1) a government-issued identification; or
(2) a ny other commercially reasonable method that:
(i) can reliably and accurately determine whether a
user is an adult; and
(ii) prevents access by minors to AI companions, as
required under this chapter.
"Reasonable age verification process." An age verification
process employed by a covered entity that:
(1) uses one or more reasonable age verification
measures to verify the age of a user of an AI chatbot owned,
operated or otherwise made available by the covered entity;
(2) p rovides that requiring a user to confirm that the
user is not a minor, or to insert the user's birth date, is
not sufficient to constitute a reasonable age verification
measure;
(3) ensures that each user is subjected to each
reasonable age verification measure used by the covered
entity as part of the age verification process; and
(4) does not base verification of a user's age on
factors such as whether the user shares an Internet Protocol
address, hardware identifier or other technical indicator
with another user determined to not be a minor.

SUBCHAPTER B
PROHIBITED CONDUCT
Sec.
31A11. Prohibited promotion of sexually explicit conduct.
31A12. Prohibited promotion of violence.
§ 31A11. Prohibited promotion of sexually explicit conduct.
(a) Offense defined.--A person commits an offense if the
person designs, develops or makes available an AI chatbot,
knowing or with reckless disregard for the fact that the AI
chatbot poses a risk of soliciting, encouraging or inducing
minors to:
(1) engage in, describe or simulate sexually explicit
conduct; or
(2) create or transmit any visual depiction of sexually
explicit conduct.
(b) Penalty.--A person who violates subsection (a) shall,
upon conviction, be sentenced to pay a fine not exceeding
$100,000 per offense.

§ 31A21. Creation of user accounts.
A covered entity shall require each individual accessing an
AI chatbot to make a user account in order to use or otherwise
interact with the AI chatbot.
§ 31A22. Age verification for existing accounts.
For each user account of an AI chatbot that exists on the
effective date of this section, a covered entity shall:
(1) On the effective date of this section, freeze each
user account.
(2) As a condition t o restore the functionality of a
user account, require that the user provide age data that is
verifiable using a reasonable age verification process,
subject to section 31A25 (relating to use of third party).
(3) Using the age data provided in accordance with

§ 31A23. Age verification for new accounts.
On or after the effective date of this section, at the time
that an individual creates a new user account to use or interact
with an AI chatbot, a covered entity shall:
(1) Request age data from the individual.
(2) V erify the individual's age using a reasonable age
verification process, subject to section 31A25 (relating to
use of third party).
(3) Using the age data provided in accordance with
paragraph (2), classify each user as a minor or an adult.
§ 31A24. Review of verified user accounts.
A covered entity shall periodically review previously
verified user accounts using a reasonable age verification
process, subject to section 31A25 (relating to use of third
party), to ensure compliance with this chapter.
§ 31A25. Use of third party.
(a) Authorization.--For purposes of sections 31A22 (relating
to age verification for existing accounts), 31A23 (relating to
age verification for new accounts) and 3 1A24 (relating to review
of verified user accounts), a covered entity may contract with a
third party to employ reasonable age verification measures as
part of the covered entity's reasonable age verification
process.
(b) Effect.--The use of a third party under subsection (a)
shall not relieve the covered entity of its obligations under
this chapter or from liability under this chapter.

§ 31A27. Age verification measure data security.
A covered entity:
(1) Shall develop, implement and maintain reasonable
data security for data under this chapter to:
(i) Limit the collection of personal data to that
which is minimally necessary to verify a user's age or
maintain compliance with this chapter.
(ii) P rotect age verification data against
unauthorized access.
(2) Shall protect the integrity and confidentiality of
data under this chapter by only transmitting data using
industry-standard encryption protocols.
(3) Shall retain data under this chapter for no longer
than is reasonably necessary to verify a user's age or
maintain compliance with this chapter.
(4) May not share with, transfer to or sell data under
this chapter to any other person or entity.

(1) enjoin the violation;

(3) obtain civil penalties in accordance with subsection
(c), restitution and other appropriate relief.
