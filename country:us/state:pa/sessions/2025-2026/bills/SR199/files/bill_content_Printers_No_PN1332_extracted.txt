andWHEREAS, On December 2, 1957, the Shippingport Atomic Power Station in Beaver County began operating as the first commercial nuclear power plant in the world dedicated exclusively to peaceful applications; andWHEREAS, Nuclear energy produces nearly 20% of the electricity generated in the United States; andWHEREAS, Nuclear energy produces nearly 50% of the carbon-free electricity generated in the United States; andWHEREAS, The nuclear sector of the United States directly employs nearly 70,000 people in high-quality jobs that last for decades; andWHEREAS, This sector is responsible for more than 250,000 123456789101112131415161718 secondary jobs; andWHEREAS, For every 100 nuclear power plant jobs, another 66 jobs are created in local communities; andWHEREAS, Nuclear energy plays a key role in the national security of the United States; andWHEREAS, The United States Navy, the largest nuclear-powered navy in the world, is supported by the commercial nuclear energy industry and a shared nuclear supply chain; andWHEREAS, Advanced nuclear technologies are vital to powering space exploration; andWHEREAS, Nuclear energy promotes grid security and reliability as one of the least expensive sources of baseload power generation worldwide; andWHEREAS, Nuclear power plants are largely resilient to adverse weather events, providing electricity 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year to distressed areas when other sources of electricity shut down; andWHEREAS, Nuclear energy contributes an estimated $60 billion per year to the gross domestic product of the United States; andWHEREAS, Pennsylvania is the second largest producer of nuclear energy in the United States, behind only the State of Illinois; andWHEREAS, There are more nuclear operators in this Commonwealth than in any other state; andWHEREAS, These nuclear operators include Constellation, Talen and Vistra; andWHEREAS, Nuclear energy comprises 30% of all electricity generated in this Commonwealth; andWHEREAS, Currently eight reactors operating at four sites in this Commonwealth provide nearly 9,500 megawatts of emission-free, clean electricity; andWHEREAS, These sites are Beaver Valley Power Station, Limerick Generating Station, Peach Bottom Atomic Power Station and Susquehanna Nuclear Plant; andWHEREAS, The Crane Clean Energy Center (formerly TMI Unit 1) is set to resume operations by 2027, adding an additional 837 megawatts of energy to the PJM electric grid; andWHEREAS, Nuclear energy contributes approximately $2 billion to our State gross domestic product;
