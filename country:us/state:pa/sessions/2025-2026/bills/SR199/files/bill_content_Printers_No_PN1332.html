A RESOLUTION
Recognizing December 2, 2025, as "World Nuclear Energy Day" in
Pennsylvania.
WHEREAS, On December 2, 1942, Enrico Fermi and his team
achieved a historic milestone by demonstrating the first self-
sustaining, controlled nuclear chain reaction in the world at
Chicago Pile-1; and
WHEREAS, On December 2, 1957, the Shippingport Atomic Power
Station in Beaver County began operating as the first commercial
nuclear power plant in the world dedicated exclusively to
peaceful applications; and
WHEREAS, Nuclear energy produces nearly 20% of the
electricity generated in the United States; and
WHEREAS, Nuclear energy produces nearly 50% of the carbon-
free electricity generated in the United States; and
WHEREAS, The nuclear sector of the United States directly
employs nearly 70,000 people in high-quality jobs that last for
decades; and
WHEREAS, This sector is responsible for more than 250,000

WHEREAS, Nuclear energy plays a key role in the national

WHEREAS, The United States Navy, the largest nuclear-powered

adverse weather events, providing electricity 24 hours a day, 7
days a week, 365 days a year to distressed areas when other
sources of electricity shut down; and
WHEREAS, Nuclear energy contributes an estimated $60 billion
per year to the gross domestic product of the United States; and
WHEREAS, Pennsylvania is the second largest producer of
nuclear energy in the United States, behind only the State of
Illinois; and
WHEREAS, There are more nuclear operators in this

and Vistra; and

this Commonwealth provide nearly 9,500 megawatts of emission-

WHEREAS, These sites are Beaver Valley Power Station,

WHEREAS, The United States collaborates with other countries
