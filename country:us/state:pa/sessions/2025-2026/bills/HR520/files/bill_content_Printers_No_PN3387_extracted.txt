2025-2026 Regular Session HR 520 PN 3387 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 3387 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.520 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY KERWIN, VENKAT, SMITH, STAMBAUGH, POWELL, ANDERSON, BRENNAN, HARKINS, M. MACKENZIE, KUTZ, GUENST, COOK, JAMES, STAATS, GREINER, PICKETT, GAYDOS, BOROWSKI, WATRO, KHAN, LEADBETER, BANTA, STEELE, DELOZIER, HAMM, MALAGARI, KAUFFMAN AND MENTZER, MAY 8, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON VETERANS AFFAIRS AND EMERGENCY PREPAREDNESS, MAY 8, 2026 A RESOLUTIONRecognizing September 27, 2026, as "Gold Star Mother's Day" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, Throughout this nation's history, the men and women of the United States Armed Forces have placed the nation's security before their own, fulfilling their duty in the face of grave danger; andWHEREAS, During World War I, Service Flags were displayed in homes, businesses, schools and churches to indicate, by the use of a blue star, each active service member in the United States military; andWHEREAS, A gold star placed over a blue star came to signify those service members who had given their lives for their country and the devotion and sacrifice of the families they left behind; andWHEREAS, In 1928, the Gold Star tradition was formalized in Washington, DC, when mothers who had lost sons and daughters in 12345678910111213141516 military service formed American Gold Star Mothers, Inc., a nondenominational, nonprofit and nonpolitical organization; andWHEREAS, In 1936, the Congress of the United States designated the last Sunday in September as "Gold Star Mother's Day," a national observance that continues each year; andWHEREAS, Pennsylvania is home to many Gold Star Mothers whose sons and daughters made the ultimate sacrifice in service to the United States; andWHEREAS, Pennsylvania's Gold Star Mothers are remarkable patriots who continue to serve their communities by promoting good citizenship, supporting members of the United States Armed Forces and veterans and providing comfort to families of fallen service members; andWHEREAS, Though they carry a profound burden of grief, Gold Star Mothers demonstrate extraordinary courage and resilience, ensuring that the legacy of this nation's fallen heroes endures; andWHEREAS, The service members of Pennsylvania who gave their lives in times of war and armed conflict are remembered with the deepest respect, gratitude and honor; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania recognize September 27, 2026, as "Gold Star Mother's Day" in Pennsylvania; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania acknowledge Pennsylvania's Gold Star Mothers and all Gold Star Families for their sacrifice, courage and enduring contributions to their communities and this nation; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That a copy of this resolution be transmitted to the American Gold Star Mother's, Inc., 2128 Leroy Place NW, 20260HR0520PN3387 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 Washington, DC, 20008.20260HR0520PN3387 - 3 - 1
