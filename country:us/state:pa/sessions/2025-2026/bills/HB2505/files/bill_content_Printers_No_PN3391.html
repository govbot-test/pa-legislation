AN ACT
Amending the act of July 28, 1953 (P.L.723, No.230), entitled
"An act relating to counties of the second class and second
class A; amending, revising, consolidating and changing the
laws relating thereto," in second class county charter law,
further providing for charter limitations.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Section 3107-C(k) of the act of July 28, 1953
(P.L.723, No.230), known as the Second Class County Code, is
amended to read:
Section 3107-C. Charter Limitations.--* * *
[(k) No county shall enact any ordinance or take any other
action dealing with the regulation of the transfer, ownership,
transportation or possession of firearms.]
* * *
Section 2. This act shall take effect in 60 days.
