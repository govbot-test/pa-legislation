2025-2026 Regular Session HB 2505 PN 3391 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 3391 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE BILL No.2505 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY FRANKEL, RABB, HOWARD, KHAN, SANCHEZ, VENKAT, PIELLI, HILL-EVANS, KINKEAD, DONAHUE, SCHLOSSBERG, HOHENSTEIN, FIEDLER, WARREN, OTTEN, BRIGGS, GREEN, WAXMAN, VITALI, SALISBURY, SHUSTERMAN, KRAJEWSKI, ABNEY, HANBIDGE, MAYES, GUZMAN, SAPPEY, BOROWSKI AND CEPEDA-FREYTIZ, MAY 11, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON JUDICIARY, MAY 11, 2026 AN ACTAmending the act of July 28, 1953 (P.L.723, No.230), entitled "An act relating to counties of the second class and second class A; amending, revising, consolidating and changing the laws relating thereto," in second class county charter law, further providing for charter limitations.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Section 3107-C(k) of the act of July 28, 1953 (P.L.723, No.230), known as the Second Class County Code, is amended to read:Section 3107-C. Charter Limitations.--* * *[(k) No county shall enact any ordinance or take any other action dealing with the regulation of the transfer, ownership, transportation or possession of firearms.]* * *Section 2. This act shall take effect in 60 days.12345678910111213141516
