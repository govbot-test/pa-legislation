2025-2026 Regular Session HR 552 PN 3534 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 3534 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.552 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY DAVIDSON, DELOZIER, GREINER, HILL-EVANS, GUZMAN, GUENST AND CEPEDA-FREYTIZ, JUNE 4, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON TOURISM, RECREATION AND ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT, JUNE 5, 2026 A RESOLUTIONRecognizing July 17, 2026, as "717 Day" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, The 717 area code for phone calls has existed in central Pennsylvania since the 1940s; andWHEREAS, Pennsylvania had only four area codes until 1997; andWHEREAS, The demand for more telephone lines increased when cell phone usage became prevalent, and now there are 15 area codes throughout the state; andWHEREAS, The 717 area code serves multiple central Pennsylvania counties, including Dauphin, Cumberland, Lancaster, Lebanon and York Counties; andWHEREAS, The region serves as a cornerstone of our state's identity and economy; andWHEREAS, From the halls of the Capitol to the farms of Lancaster and the manufacturing plants of York, this region represents a diverse and industrious workforce; andWHEREAS, This region is also a primary driver of 1234567891011121314151617 Pennsylvania's tourism industry, drawing millions of visitors annually to its world class heritage sites, scenic valleys and historic landmarks; andWHEREAS, The shared 717 area code is an iconic cultural identifier for many Pennsylvanians; andWHEREAS, This designation celebrates the South-Central Pennsylvania region that includes Harrisburg, Hershey, York, Lancaster and many vital communities that connect them; andWHEREAS, Considering America's 250th anniversary this year, acknowledging the historical significance of the 717 region is especially fitting; andWHEREAS, More people are moving to these areas, especially as Cumberland County is the fastest growing county in Pennsylvania; andWHEREAS, This resolution is a call to action for residents and visitors alike to support local restaurants, farmers, artisans and Main Street businesses that make this region a unique and wonderful place to live, work and raise a family; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives recognize July 17, 2026, as "717 Day" in Pennsylvania.20260HR0552PN3534 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718192021
