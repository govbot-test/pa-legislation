A RESOLUTION
Recognizing July 17, 2026, as "717 Day" in Pennsylvania.
WHEREAS, The 717 area code for phone calls has existed in
central Pennsylvania since the 1940s; and
WHEREAS, Pennsylvania had only four area codes until 1997;
and
WHEREAS, The demand for more telephone lines increased when
cell phone usage became prevalent, and now there are 15 area
codes throughout the state; and
WHEREAS, The 717 area code serves multiple central
Pennsylvania counties, including Dauphin, Cumberland, Lancaster,
Lebanon and York ADAMS, BERKS, CHESTER, CUMBERLAND, DAUPHIN,
FRANKLIN, FULTON, HUNTINGDON, JUNIATA, LANCASTER, LEBANON,
MIFFLIN, PERRY, SCHUYLKILL, SNYDER AND YORK Counties; and
WHEREAS, The region serves as a cornerstone of our state's
identity and economy; and
WHEREAS, From the halls of the Capitol to the farms of

WHEREAS, This region is also a primary driver of

WHEREAS, The shared 717 area code is an iconic cultural

WHEREAS, This designation celebrates the South-Central
Pennsylvania region that includes Harrisburg, Hershey, York,

especially fitting; and
WHEREAS, More people are moving to these areas, especially as
Cumberland County is the fastest growing county in Pennsylvania;
and
WHEREAS, This resolution is a call to action for residents
and visitors alike to support local restaurants, farmers,
artisans and Main Street businesses that make this region a
