Section 2702 of Title 34 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is amended by adding a subsection to read:§ 2702. Residents.* * *(a.1) Native Pennsylvanian Annual License.--The commission shall issue annual Pennsylvania hunting licenses known as the Native Pennsylvanian Annual License. The issuance of a license shall be subject to the following: (1) The license may be issued to a nonresident who meets either of the following:(i) The nonresident was born in this Commonwealth.(ii) The nonresident has a living parent or former guardian who resides in this Commonwealth.<--123456789101112131415161718 (2) The license shall cost the same as a resident license under section 2709 (relating to license costs and fees).(3) An applicant must provide a current photo identification card and the applicable documentation under one of the following:(i) For an applicant who qualifies under paragraph (1)(i), either of the following:(A) A certified copy of the applicant's original birth certificate showing that the applicant was born in this Commonwealth or that the applicant's parent's address was in this Commonwealth at the time of the applicant's birth.(B) A written affirmation that the applicant was born in this Commonwealth.(ii) For an applicant who qualifies under paragraph (1)(ii), proof of the living parent or former guardian's address within this Commonwealth.(4) A first-time applicant for a Native Pennsylvanian annual license must submit the application to the commission.(5) A Native Pennsylvanian annual license shall be identical to an annual resident license sold by the commission and shall be properly designated "Native Pennsylvanian."(6) All regular annual licenses offered to residents shall be made available to an applicant who qualifies as a Native Pennsylvanian. THE ADDITION OF 34 PA.C.S. § 2703.1 SHALL APPLY TO THE 2027-2028 DEER REGULAR FIREARMS SEASON AND EACH DEER REGULAR FIREARMS SEASON THEREAFTER.SECTION 3.
