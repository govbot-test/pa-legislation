Section 2702 of Title 34 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is amended by adding a subsection to read:§ 2702. Residents.* * *(a.1) Native Pennsylvanian Annual License.--The commission shall issue annual Pennsylvania hunting licenses known as the Native Pennsylvanian Annual License. The license shall be in accordance with THE ISSUANCE OF A LICENSE SHALL BE SUBJECT TO the following:(1) The license may be issued to either:(i) Nonresidents who were born in Pennsylvania and do not currently reside in this Commonwealth.(ii) Nonresidents who currently have a living parent or former guardian residing in this Commonwealth.<--<--<--12345678910111213141516171819 (2) The license shall cost the same as a resident license under subsection (a).(3) Applicants must meet the following criteria:(i) An applicant must provide:(A) For an applicant born in Pennsylvania:(I) A certified copy of the original birth certificate showing the applicant was born in the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania or that the applicant's parent's address was in the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania at the time of the birth as shown on the birth certificate.(II) A valid current photo identification card.(B) For an applicant with a living parent or guardian residing in this Commonwealth:(I) Proof of the living parent or guardian's address within this Commonwealth.(II) A valid current photo identification card.(ii) The applicant may hold status as a service member, or a family member who resides in the household of the service member, domiciled in this Commonwealth who resides outside this Commonwealth while serving on active duty in the armed forces of the United States.(4) First-time applicants for the Native Pennsylvanian Annual License must submit the application to the commission.(5) The Native Pennsylvanian Annual License shall be identical to annual resident licenses sold by the commission and shall be properly designated "Native Pennsylvanian."(6) All regular annual licenses currently offered to 20260SB1313PN1795 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 residents shall be made available to applicants who qualify as Native Pennsylvanians. Lifetime licenses shall not be included in the Native Pennsylvanian license program.(7) Nothing in this subsection shall be construed to limit eligibility for a resident hunter's license under paragraph (3)(ii).(1) THE LICENSE MAY BE ISSUED TO A NONRESIDENT WHO MEETS EITHER OF THE FOLLOWING:(I) THE NONRESIDENT WAS BORN IN THIS COMMONWEALTH.(II) THE NONRESIDENT HAS A LIVING PARENT OR FORMER GUARDIAN WHO RESIDES IN THIS COMMONWEALTH.(2) THE LICENSE SHALL COST THE SAME AS A RESIDENT LICENSE UNDER SECTION 2709 (RELATING TO LICENSE COSTS AND FEES).(3) AN APPLICANT MUST PROVIDE A CURRENT PHOTO IDENTIFICATION CARD AND THE APPLICABLE DOCUMENTATION UNDER ONE OF THE FOLLOWING:(I) FOR AN APPLICANT WHO QUALIFIES UNDER PARAGRAPH (1)(I), EITHER OF THE FOLLOWING:(A) A CERTIFIED COPY OF THE APPLICANT'S ORIGINAL BIRTH CERTIFICATE SHOWING THAT THE APPLICANT WAS BORN IN THIS COMMONWEALTH OR THAT THE APPLICANT'S PARENT'S ADDRESS WAS IN THIS COMMONWEALTH AT THE TIME OF THE APPLICANT'S BIRTH.(B) A WRITTEN AFFIRMATION THAT THE APPLICANT WAS BORN IN THIS COMMONWEALTH.(II) FOR AN APPLICANT WHO QUALIFIES UNDER PARAGRAPH (1)(II), PROOF OF THE LIVING PARENT OR FORMER GUARDIAN'S ADDRESS WITHIN THIS COMMONWEALTH.(4) A FIRST-TIME APPLICANT FOR A NATIVE PENNSYLVANIAN 20260SB1313PN1795 - 3 - <--123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 ANNUAL LICENSE MUST SUBMIT THE APPLICATION TO THE COMMISSION.(5) A NATIVE PENNSYLVANIAN ANNUAL LICENSE SHALL BE IDENTICAL TO AN ANNUAL RESIDENT LICENSE SOLD BY THE COMMISSION AND SHALL BE PROPERLY DESIGNATED "NATIVE PENNSYLVANIAN."(6) ALL REGULAR ANNUAL LICENSES OFFERED TO RESIDENTS SHALL BE MADE AVAILABLE TO AN APPLICANT WHO QUALIFIES AS A NATIVE PENNSYLVANIAN. THIS PARAGRAPH SHALL NOT APPLY TO A LIFETIME LICENSE.(7) NOTHING IN THIS SUBSECTION SHALL BE CONSTRUED TO LIMIT ELIGIBILITY FOR A RESIDENT HUNTER'S LICENSE UNDER SUBSECTION (B).(8) AN APPLICANT WHO PROVIDES A FALSE WRITTEN AFFIRMATION UNDER PARAGRAPH (3)(I)(B) SHALL BE SUBJECT TO SECTION 2711(A)(2) AND (B)(3) (RELATING TO UNLAWFUL ACTS CONCERNING LICENSES) AND 18 PA.C.S. § 4904 (RELATING TO UNSWORN FALSIFICATION TO AUTHORITIES).* * *Section 2.
