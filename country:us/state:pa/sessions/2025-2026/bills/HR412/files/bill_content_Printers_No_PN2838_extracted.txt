2025-2026 Regular Session HR 412 PN 2838 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 2838 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.412 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY DOUGHERTY, JANUARY 30, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON LOCAL GOVERNMENT, JANUARY 30, 2026 A RESOLUTIONDesignating January 31, 2026, as "Heroes of the Northeast Philadelphia Plane Crash Day" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, On January 31, 2025, a medical jet carrying six people crashed along Cottman Avenue in Philadelphia, near the Roosevelt Mall, shortly after taking off from Northeast Philadelphia Airport; andWHEREAS, All six people on board and two people on the ground died as a result of the crash; andWHEREAS, At least 20 additional people were injured, with at least four people requiring hospitalization; andWHEREAS, The crash negatively impacted more than 340 homes, including four homes that were destroyed, six homes that sustained major damage and 11 homes that sustained other damage; andWHEREAS, In response to the crash and ensuing fire, an immediate multiagency response was deployed to the scene by the Philadelphia Fire Department, the Philadelphia Police Department, the Office of Emergency Management, the Managing 123456789101112131415161718 Director's Office, PECO, Philadelphia Gas Works and other Federal and State partners to assist victims, enforce a public safety zone and bring the situation under control; andWHEREAS, Police officers, firefighters, emergency medical services personnel and hospital staff demonstrated extraordinary courage and professionalism while residents of Northeast Philadelphia also worked to aid their neighbors and emergency personnel in the aftermath of the crash; andWHEREAS, On the anniversary of this deadly crash, it is fitting that the Commonwealth honor the valor and effort of all those who came to the aid of their neighbors during this tragedy and the memory of those who sadly passed away; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives designate January 31, 2026, as "Heroes of the Northeast Philadelphia Plane Crash Day" in Pennsylvania; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives offer its appreciation for the police officers, firefighters, emergency medical services personnel, hospital staff, public employees and local residents who worked to help those affected by this tragedy and to rebuild the Northeast Philadelphia community.20260HR0412PN2838 - 2 - 1234567891011121314151617181920
