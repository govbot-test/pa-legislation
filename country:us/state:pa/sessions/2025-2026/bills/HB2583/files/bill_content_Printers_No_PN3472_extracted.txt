2025-2026 Regular Session HB 2583 PN 3472 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 3472 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE BILL No.2583 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY KOSIEROWSKI, ISAACSON, FREEMAN, RIVERA, HILL-EVANS, STEELE, BOROWSKI, SANCHEZ, CURRY, SHUSTERMAN, MERSKI, DELLOSO, GUZMAN, OTTEN, MADDEN, KAZEEM, MAYES AND FRITZ, JUNE 1, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON HUMAN SERVICES, JUNE 1, 2026 AN ACTAmending the act of April 9, 1929 (P.L.343, No.176), entitled "An act relating to the finances of the State government; providing for cancer control, prevention and research, for ambulatory surgical center data collection, for the Joint Underwriting Association, for entertainment business financial management firms, for private dam financial assurance and for reinstatement of item vetoes; providing for the settlement, assessment, collection, and lien of taxes, bonus, and all other accounts due the Commonwealth, the collection and recovery of fees and other money or property due or belonging to the Commonwealth, or any agency thereof, including escheated property and the proceeds of its sale, the custody and disbursement or other disposition of funds and securities belonging to or in the possession of the Commonwealth, and the settlement of claims against the Commonwealth, the resettlement of accounts and appeals to the courts, refunds of moneys erroneously paid to the Commonwealth, auditing the accounts of the Commonwealth and all agencies thereof, of all public officers collecting moneys payable to the Commonwealth, or any agency thereof, and all receipts of appropriations from the Commonwealth, authorizing the Commonwealth to issue tax anticipation notes to defray current expenses, implementing the provisions of section 7(a) of Article VIII of the Constitution of Pennsylvania authorizing and restricting the incurring of certain debt and imposing penalties; affecting every department, board, commission, and officer of the State government, every political subdivision of the State, and certain officers of such subdivisions, every person, association, and corporation required to pay, assess, or collect taxes, or to make returns or reports under the laws imposing taxes for State purposes, or to pay license fees or 1234567891011121314151617181920212223242526272829303132 other moneys to the Commonwealth, or any agency thereof, every State depository and every debtor or creditor of the Commonwealth," in human services, providing for medical assistance payments for medical equipment and supplies.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. The act of April 9, 1929 (P.L.343, No.176), known as The Fiscal Code, is amended by adding a section to read:Section 1607-T. Medical assistance payments for medical equipment and supplies.(a) Payment.--To the extent money is appropriated for this purpose, the Department of Human Services shall make a one-time payment to a medical assistance managed care organization for the purpose of increasing payments to participating medical assistance providers for medical equipment and supplies. The Department of Human Services shall make the payment no later than December 31, 2027.(b) Amount.--The amount of a payment under subsection (a) shall be determined by multiplying the amount paid during calendar year 2024 by the medical assistance managed care organization to participating medical assistance providers for medical equipment and supplies by 10%.(c) Managed care organization payment.--No later than 30 days after receiving a payment under subsection (a), a medical assistance managed care organization shall make a payment to each participating medical assistance provider for medical equipment and supplies.(d) Amount of participating medical assistance provider payment.--The amount of a payment under subsection (c) to a participating medical assistance provider shall be determined by multiplying the amount paid during calendar year 2024 by the medical assistance managed care organization to the 20260HB2583PN3472 - 2 - 1234567891011121314151617181920212223242526272829303132 participating medical assistance provider for medical equipment and supplies by 10%.(e) Definitions.--As used in this section, the following words and phrases shall have the meanings given to them in this subsection unless the context clearly indicates otherwise:"Medical assistance provider." A person or entity enrolled by the Department of Human Services as a provider of services in the medical assistance program."Medical equipment and supplies." An item specified by Federal or State law or regulation as compensable under the medical assistance program."Participating medical assistance provider." A medical assistance provider that participates in the provider network of a medical assistance managed care organization.Section 2. This act shall take effect in 60 days.20260HB2583PN3472 - 3 - 123456789101112131415
