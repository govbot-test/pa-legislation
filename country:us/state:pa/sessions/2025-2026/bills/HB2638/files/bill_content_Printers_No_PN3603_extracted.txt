2025-2026 Regular Session HB 2638 PN 3603 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 3603 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE BILL No.2638 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY RADER, STAATS, OLSOMMER, KAUFFMAN, VEROBISH, PUGH, PICKETT, STAMBAUGH, M. MACKENZIE AND KUZMA, JUNE 15, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON CONSUMER PROTECTION, TECHNOLOGY AND UTILITIES, JUNE 15, 2026 AN ACTAmending Title 66 (Public Utilities) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes, in alternative form of regulation of telecommunications services, providing for telecommunications line repair and tree limb removal.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Title 66 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is amended by adding a section to read:§ 3020. Telecommunications line repair and tree limb removal.(a) Duty.--Upon receiving notice from a municipality or Commonwealth agency that a telecommunications line has been damaged, broken, cut or is in direct contact with a tree limb and the condition interferes with or endangers vehicle or pedestrian traffic, a telecommunications carrier shall, as soon as practicable based on the severity of the condition, take any of the following actions as applicable:(1) Remove the tree limb.(2) Remediate or repair the telecommunications line.123456789101112131415161718 (b) Cost recovery.--A telecommunications carrier may not recover the cost of removal, remediation or repair under subsection (a) from a customer or rate class that does not utilize the specific type of infrastructure that necessitated the removal, remediation or repair.(c) Enforcement.--A telecommunications carrier that violates this section shall be subject to Chapter 33 (relating to violations and penalties).(d) Regulations.--The commission shall adopt regulations as necessary to implement and enforce this section.Section 2. This act shall take effect in 60 days.20260HB2638PN3603 - 2 - 1234567891011
