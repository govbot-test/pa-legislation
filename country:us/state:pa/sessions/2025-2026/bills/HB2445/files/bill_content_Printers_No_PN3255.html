AN ACT
Amending the act of June 27, 1996 (P.L.403, No.58), entitled "An
act to enhance community and economic development in this
Commonwealth by restructuring certain administrative
functions and entities; changing the name of the Department
of Commerce to the Department of Community and Economic
Development; transferring functions of the Department of
Community Affairs into the Department of Community and
Economic Development and other agencies; providing for a
Deputy Secretary for Community Affairs and Development in the
Department of Community and Economic Development;
establishing the Center for Local Government Services and the
Local Government Advisory Committee; establishing the Small
Business Advocacy Council; conferring powers and duties on
the Legislative Reference Bureau; and making repeals,"
providing for Office of Housing Opportunity.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. The act of June 27, 1996 (P.L.403, No.58), known
as the Community and Economic Development Enhancement Act, is
amended by adding a chapter to read:
CHAPTER 17
OFFICE OF HOUSING OPPORTUNITY

The following words and phrases when used in this chapter
shall have the meanings given to them in this section unless the
context clearly indicates otherwise:
"Advisory committee." The Housing Opportunity Advisory
Committee established in section 1705.
"Agency." The Pennsylvania Housing Finance Agency.
"Housing production targets." The housing production targets
under section 1706.
"OTO." The Pennsylvania Office of Transformation and
Opportunity established by Executive Order 2023-05.
"Office." The Office of Housing Opportunity established
under section 1702.
Section 1702. Office of Housing Opportunity.
The Office of Housing Opportunity is established in the
department.
Section 1703. Deputy secretary.
The Governor shall appoint a Deputy Secretary for Housing
Opportunity, who shall be responsible for the administration of
the office.
Section 1704. Powers and duties.
The office shall have the following powers and duties:
(1) Administering the functions specified in section
301(a)(6), (9) and (14).
(2) Chairing and making certain appointments to the
advisory committee as approved under section 1705.
(3) Adopting and publishing housing production targets
every 10 years based on the recommendations of the advisory
committee and implementing policies to meet the housing
production targets.
(4) Publishing an annual report detailing progress

Section 1705. Advisory committee.
(a) Establishment.--The Housing Opportunity Advisory
Committee is established within the office.
(b) Membership.--The committee shall consist of the
following members:
(1) The Deputy Secretary of Housing Opportunity or a
designee, who shall serve as chairperson of the committee.
(2) The secretary or a designee.
(3) The Secretary of Environmental Protection or a
designee.
(4) The Secretary of Policy and Planning in the
Governor's Policy Office or a designee.
(5) The Secretary of Health or a designee.
(6) The Secretary of Labor and Industry or a designee.
(7) Four members of the General Assembly, including:
(i) One member of the Senate, appointed by the
President pro tempore of the Senate.
(ii) One member of the Senate, appointed by the
Minority Leader of the Senate.
(iii) One member of the House of Representatives,
appointed by the Speaker of the House of Representatives.

(d) Compensation and expenses.--Members of the advisory
committee shall receive no compensation for their services but
shall receive reimbursement for necessary and proper expenses
for attendance at meetings.
(e) Meetings and quorum.--
(1) The advisory committee shall meet at least quarterly
and upon the call of the chairperson at other times as deemed
necessary.
(2) Twelve members of the advisory committee shall
constitute a quorum.
(f) Declared vacancies.--The Deputy Secretary of Housing
Opportunity shall declare a vacancy to exist on the advisory
committee whenever any member appointed by the Deputy Secretary
fails to attend three consecutive meetings without good cause. A
declared vacancy shall be filled for the unexpired term.
(g) Staff.--The office shall provide staff for the advisory
committee.
(h) Duties.--The advisory committee shall:
(1) Study and make recommendations on ways that the
Commonwealth and local governments can improve housing supply
and housing affordability.
(2) Identify and assess factors that impact housing
supply and housing affordability, including:
(i) Labor and material costs.
(ii) Taxes and fees.
(iii) Access to financing and capital investment.
(iv) Planning, zoning and land use regulations.
(v) State building code standards.
(vi) Appeals and other legal barriers.
(vii) State and local permitting and approval

(i) Report.--On or before March 30, 2027, and every two

Section 1706. Housing production targets.
(a) Target report.--The office shall publish a report, on or
before July 1, 2027, and every 10 years thereafter, establishing
housing production targets. The following shall apply:
(1) The housing production targets shall be established
for:
(i) The Commonwealth.
(ii) Each local jurisdiction, which shall include:
(A) Each county.
(B) Each municipality that exercises zoning or
planning authority.
(2) In establishing the housing production targets, the
office shall:
(i) Analyze data and forecasts related to:
(A) Employment demand.
(B) Housing demand.
(C) Population growth.
(D) The ratio of median household income wage
