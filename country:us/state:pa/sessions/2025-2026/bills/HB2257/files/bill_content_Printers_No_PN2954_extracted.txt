2025-2026 Regular Session HB 2257 PN 2954 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 2954 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE BILL No.2257 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY WEBSTER, SANCHEZ, KINKEAD, HILL-EVANS, KHAN, FREEMAN, DOUGHERTY, MERSKI, STEELE, HANBIDGE AND DELLOSO, MARCH 2, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON HUMAN SERVICES, MARCH 3, 2026 AN ACTAmending the act of June 13, 1967 (P.L.31, No.21), entitled "An act to consolidate, editorially revise, and codify the public welfare laws of the Commonwealth," in children and youth, providing for screening requirements.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. The act of June 13, 1967 (P.L.31, No.21), known as the Human Services Code, is amended by adding a section to read:Section 710. Screening Requirements.--(a) The department, in consultation with each county agency as defined in 67 Pa.C.S. § 7101 (relating to definitions), shall develop a trauma-informed screening to identify a child who is a victim of, or at risk of becoming a victim of, human trafficking involving sexual servitude as defined in 18 Pa.C.S. § 3001 (relating to definitions).(b) A county agency shall administer the screening under subsection (a) to a child alleged to be a dependent child as 123456789101112131415161718 defined in 42 Pa.C.S. § 6302 (relating to definitions) with whom the county agency has contact.(c) If a county agency identifies a child under subsection (a) as a victim of, or at risk of becoming a victim of, human trafficking involving sexual servitude, the county agency shall connect the child with specialized programs and services available under 18 Pa.C.S. § 3062 (relating to specialized services for sexually exploited children).Section 2. This act shall take effect in 90 days.20260HB2257PN2954 - 2 - 123456789
