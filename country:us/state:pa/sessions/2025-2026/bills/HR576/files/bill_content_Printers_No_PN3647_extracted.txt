andWHEREAS, Years of unpopular and oppressive British colonial rule and strict British laws in colonial America fueled outrage; andWHEREAS, The frustration over taxation without colonial representation in Parliament erupted in historic violence in colonial Massachusetts in 1770, known as the "Boston Massacre"; andWHEREAS, As colonial resistance grew, in 1774, the First Continental Congress was held in Philadelphia; andWHEREAS, In 1775, the first fighting of the Revolutionary War began in Massachusetts; andWHEREAS, The idea of war was not popular among American colonists at first; andWHEREAS, Britain's continued strong military presence in the American colonies inspired the sense of American colonial independence; andWHEREAS, On June 7, 1776, Richard Henry Lee submitted a resolution to the Continental Congress, urging Congress to declare independence from Britain; andWHEREAS, The Continental Congress appointed Thomas Jefferson, John Adams, Benjamin Franklin, Roger Sherman and Robert R. Livingston, known as the "Committee of Five," to draft the Declaration of Independence; andWHEREAS, On June 11, 1776, a first draft of the declaration was submitted; andWHEREAS, From July 1 through 4, 1776, the Continental Congress debated and revised the declaration, and the document was adopted on the morning of July 4, 1776;
