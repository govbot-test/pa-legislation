A RESOLUTION
Authorizing the sale of property known as the Juniata Gap
Weekend Training Site, located at Good Shepherd Road,
Altoona, Blair County; the Polk Center, located at 1 Lakewood
Circle, Polk Borough, Venango County; SCI Retreat, located at
660 State Route 11, Glen Lyon, Luzerne County; the White
Haven Center, located at 827 Oley Valley Road, White Haven,
Luzerne County; and the Woodhaven Center, located at 2900
Southampton Road, Philadelphia, Philadelphia County.
