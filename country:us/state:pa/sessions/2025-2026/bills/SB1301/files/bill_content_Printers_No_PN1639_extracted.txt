2025-2026 Regular Session SB 1301 PN 1639 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 1639 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIASENATE BILL No.1301 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY COMITTA, CULVER, FONTANA, SCHWANK, KEARNEY AND VOGEL, APRIL 23, 2026 REFERRED TO LABOR AND INDUSTRY, APRIL 23, 2026 AN ACTAmending the act of December 5, 1936 (2nd Sp.Sess., 1937 P.L.2897, No.1), entitled "An act establishing a system of unemployment compensation to be administered by the Department of Labor and Industry and its existing and newly created agencies with personnel (with certain exceptions) selected on a civil service basis; requiring employers to keep records and make reports, and certain employers to pay contributions based on payrolls to provide moneys for the payment of compensation to certain unemployed persons; providing procedure and administrative details for the determination, payment and collection of such contributions and the payment of such compensation; providing for cooperation with the Federal Government and its agencies; creating certain special funds in the custody of the State Treasurer; and prescribing penalties," in preliminary provisions, further providing for definitions.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Section 4(l)(4) of the act of December 5, 1936 (2nd Sp.Sess., 1937 P.L.2897, No.1), known as the Unemployment Compensation Law, is amended by adding a subparagraph to read:Section 4. Definitions.--The following words and phrases, as used in this act, shall have the following meanings, unless the context clearly requires otherwise.* * *12345678910111213141516171819202122232425 (l) * * *(4) The word "employment" shall not include--* * *(22) Services performed by an alien who is admitted to the United States to perform temporary, seasonal labor pursuant to 8 U.S.C. §§ 1101(a)(15)(H)(i)(b) (relating to definitions) and 1184(c) (relating to admission of nonimmigrants) of the Immigration and Nationality Act.* * *Section 2. This act shall apply to services performed on or after the effective date of this section.Section 3. This act shall take effect in 60 days.20260SB1301PN1639 - 2 - 123456789101112
