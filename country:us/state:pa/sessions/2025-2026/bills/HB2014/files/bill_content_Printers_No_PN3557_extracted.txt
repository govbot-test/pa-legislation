2025-2026 Regular Session HB 2014 PN 3557 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRIOR PRINTER'S NO. 2568PRINTER'S NO. 3557THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE BILL No.2014 Session of 2025 INTRODUCED BY SCOTT, HILL-EVANS, SANCHEZ, K. HARRIS, RIVERA, GILLEN, HAMM, ROWE, NEILSON, FLEMING, STENDER AND D. WILLIAMS, NOVEMBER 3, 2025 AS REPORTED FROM COMMITTEE ON LABOR AND INDUSTRY, HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, AS AMENDED, JUNE 9, 2026AN ACTAmending the act of October 24, 2012 (P.L.1209, No.151), entitled "An act regulating child labor; conferring powers and duties on the Department of Labor and Industry and the Department of Education; imposing penalties; and making a repeal," further providing for minors serving in volunteer emergency service organizations.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Section 7(d)(3) of the act of October 24, 2012 (P.L.1209, No.151), known as the Child Labor Act, is amended AND THE SUBSECTION IS AMENDED BY ADDING A PARAGRAPH to read:Section 7. Minors serving in volunteer emergency service organizations.* * *(d) Other provisions.--* * *(3) An individual who is 14 or 15 years of age may engage in training or [firefighting activities permitted under this section until 10 p.m. before a school day] other <--12345678910111213141516171819 activities permitted under subsection (b)(1) until 10 p.m. if the minor:(i) is a member of a volunteer fire company; and(ii) has the written consent of the minor's parent or legal guardian.(4) AN INDIVIDUAL WHO IS AT LEAST 14 YEARS OF AGE MAY RIDE AN OFFICIAL VEHICLE TO THE SCENE OF A FIRE OR OTHER EMERGENCY RESPONSE FOR THE PURPOSE OF ENGAGING IN ACTIVITIES AUTHORIZED UNDER THIS SECTION IF TRANSPORTATION OF THE MINOR COMPLIES WITH FEDERAL REGULATIONS FOR THE TRANSPORTATION OF MINORS ENGAGED IN EMPLOYMENT.Section 2. This act shall take effect in 60 days.20250HB2014PN3557 - 2 - <--123456789101112
