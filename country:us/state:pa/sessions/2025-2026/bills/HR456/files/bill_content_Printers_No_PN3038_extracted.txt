2025-2026 Regular Session HR 456 PN 3038 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 3038 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.456 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY DAVIDSON, VENKAT, HILL-EVANS, VITALI, MATZIE, MERSKI, FREEMAN, GUENST, RIVERA AND OLSOMMER, MARCH 20, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON STATE GOVERNMENT, MARCH 20, 2026 A RESOLUTIONDesignating July 1, 2026, as "Canada Day" in Pennsylvania to celebrate the enduring relationship between the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania and Canada.WHEREAS, The Commonwealth of Pennsylvania and Canada share a longstanding and productive relationship rooted in geographic proximity, shared democratic values and a commitment to economic growth and regional stability; andWHEREAS, Canada is Pennsylvania's largest international trading partner, supporting tens of thousands of jobs across Pennsylvania through robust cross-border trade in energy, manufacturing, agriculture, technology and natural resources; andWHEREAS, The Great Lakes-St. Lawrence region, encompassing Pennsylvania and multiple Canadian provinces, represents one of the largest integrated economic regions in the world, supporting advanced manufacturing, energy production, agriculture, transportation and innovation; andWHEREAS, Pennsylvania's access to the Great Lakes Basin, 123456789101112131415161718 particularly through Lake Erie, strengthens its connection to Canadian markets and reinforces shared stewardship of vital freshwater resources; andWHEREAS, Cross-border supply chains between Pennsylvania and Canada are deeply intertwined, particularly in industries such as steel, automotive manufacturing, energy, life sciences and agriculture, enhancing competitiveness and resilience on both sides of the border; andWHEREAS, Pennsylvania and Canada maintain strong collaboration in energy development and reliability, including nuclear energy, hydropower, natural gas and emerging clean energy technologies; andWHEREAS, Academic institutions, research organizations and workforce development programs in Pennsylvania and Canada regularly partner to advance innovation, scientific discovery and talent exchange; andWHEREAS, Cultural and people-to-people ties, including tourism, education, sports, arts and shared heritage, continue to strengthen mutual understanding and cooperation between Pennsylvanians and Canadians; andWHEREAS, The Council of State Governments Eastern Regional Conference and its Canada-United States Relations Committee serve as important forums for fostering dialogue, collaboration and policy alignment between Pennsylvania and Canadian provinces; andWHEREAS, Continued cooperation between Pennsylvania and Canada is essential to addressing shared challenges, including economic competitiveness, infrastructure modernization, environmental sustainability and workforce development; therefore be it20260HR0456PN3038 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 RESOLVED, That the House of Representatives of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania designate July 1, 2026, as "Canada Day" in Pennsylvania to celebrate the enduring relationship between the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania and Canada; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania encourage residents, businesses and institutions throughout Pennsylvania to observe this day in recognition of the close ties between Pennsylvania and Canada; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That a copy of this resolution be transmitted to the Consulate General of Canada and to appropriate governmental and trade partners to reaffirm Pennsylvania's commitment to continued collaboration and friendship.20260HR0456PN3038 - 3 - 1234567891011121314
