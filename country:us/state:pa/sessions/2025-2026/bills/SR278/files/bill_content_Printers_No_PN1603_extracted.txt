2025-2026 Regular Session SR 278 PN 1603 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 1603 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIASENATE RESOLUTION No.278 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY STREET, TARTAGLIONE, CAPPELLETTI, LAUGHLIN, HAYWOOD, COMITTA, VOGEL, COSTA AND KANE, APRIL 17, 2026 REFERRED TO RULES AND EXECUTIVE NOMINATIONS, APRIL 17, 2026 A RESOLUTIONRecognizing the month of April 2026 as "Fair Housing Month" in Pennsylvania and designating April 30, 2026, as "Affordable Homeownership Day" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, April is recognized nationally as "Fair Housing Month" to commemorate the passage of the Fair Housing Act of 1968, landmark Federal legislation enacted in the aftermath of the assassination of the Reverend Dr. Martin Luther King, Jr., in Memphis, Tennessee, which brought new urgency to the unfinished work of equal access and justice in the United States; andWHEREAS, The legacy of Dr. King's sacrifice in Memphis carries a particular and solemn weight in this Commonwealth, where the work of expanding access to safe, affordable and equitable housing remains both a moral imperative and an economic priority; andWHEREAS, The National Association of Real Estate Brokers (NAREB), founded in 1947 and the oldest minority professional real estate trade association in the United States, has served 123456789101112131415161718 for more than seven decades as the premier network of Black real estate professionals committed to the principle of democracy in housing; andWHEREAS, Under the national leadership of President Ashley Thomas III, NAREB launched the strategic initiative "Closing the Gap: Advocacy, Affordability, and Access," with a focused agenda targeting underwriting inequities, affordability barriers and the persistent gap in Black homeownership as critical challenges to closing the racial wealth divide; andWHEREAS, President Thomas has called for a return to "optimistic underwriting" in the mortgage and lending industries, advocating for systems that presume creditworthiness rather than demand that prospective homebuyers prove their worth, and has led active engagement with the United States Department of Housing and Urban Development and the Federal Housing Administration on the community property fairness initiative and other policy reforms; andWHEREAS, The national homeownership rate for Black households stands at approximately 44.7%, compared to approximately 72.4% for white households, a gap of nearly 28% that reflects the compounding effects of historical redlining, discriminatory lending practices and systemic disinvestment in communities of color; andWHEREAS, The median net worth of Black families remains approximately $44,100, compared to $282,310 for white families, a disparity directly tied to homeownership attainment, making the expansion of affordable homeownership one of the most consequential tools available for building intergenerational wealth in Black communities; andWHEREAS, In 2026, NAREB launched a national eight-city 20260SR0278PN1603 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 Affordable Homeownership Bus Tour as a grassroots outreach initiative to connect families in underserved communities with real estate professionals, lending resources, homebuyer education and policy advocacy across the country; andWHEREAS, The recognition of "Affordable Homeownership Day" in this Commonwealth aligns with the State's commitment to expand economic opportunity, support working families and invest in the stability of communities across all regions of this Commonwealth; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the Senate of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania recognize the month of April 2026 as "Fair Housing Month" in Pennsylvania and designate April 30, 2026, as "Affordable Homeownership Day" in Pennsylvania; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the Senate of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania commend the National Association of Real Estate Brokers, under the national leadership of President Ashley Thomas III, for its unwavering commitment to expanding homeownership access for Black families and underserved communities across the nation through advocacy, affordability initiatives and community-centered outreach; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the Senate of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania encourage all Pennsylvanians, financial institutions, community organizations and local governments to join in the effort to remove barriers to homeownership, support equitable lending practices and invest in the communities that have long been denied fair access to housing and the generational wealth it creates;
