2025-2026 Regular Session SR 270 PN 1592 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 1592 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIASENATE RESOLUTION No.270 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY TARTAGLIONE, KANE, CAPPELLETTI, KEARNEY, MALONE, HUGHES, FONTANA, COSTA, HAYWOOD, BROWN, COMITTA AND VOGEL, APRIL 16, 2026 REFERRED TO RULES AND EXECUTIVE NOMINATIONS, APRIL 16, 2026 A RESOLUTIONRecognizing the month of April 2026 as "Alcohol Awareness Month" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, Most adults in the United States who drink alcohol do so moderately and without complications, yet alcohol-related problems are among the most significant public health issues in the nation; andWHEREAS, According to the 2022 National Survey on Drug Use and Health, alcohol use disorder (AUD) affects an estimated 28.8 million adults in the United States; andWHEREAS, According to the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention, approximately 140,000 people die from alcohol-related causes annually, making alcohol the fourth leading preventable cause of death in the nation; andWHEREAS, Each April since 1987, the National Council on Alcoholism and Drug Dependence, Inc., has sponsored "Alcohol Awareness Month" to increase public awareness and understanding, reduce stigma toward seeking help and encourage local 1234567891011121314151617 communities to focus on alcohol use disorder and alcohol-related issues; andWHEREAS, Adolescent drinking is a serious public health problem in the United States; andWHEREAS, According to the National Survey on Drug Use and Health, an estimated 750,000 boys and girls from 12 to 17 years of age are affected by AUD; andWHEREAS, Alcohol use during the teenage years can interfere with normal adolescent brain development, increase the risk of developing AUD and lead to unintended consequences such as injury, sexual assault, alcohol overdose and death; andWHEREAS, Drug and alcohol addiction treatment providers are essential partners in helping Pennsylvanians through the process of recovery; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the Senate recognize the month of April 2026 as "Alcohol Awareness Month" in Pennsylvania; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the Senate inform Pennsylvanians of the dangers associated with alcohol use and abuse in adults and children.20260SR0270PN1592 - 2 - 12345678910111213141516171819
