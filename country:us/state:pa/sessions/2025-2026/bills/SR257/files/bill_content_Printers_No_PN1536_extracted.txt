2025-2026 Regular Session SR 257 PN 1536 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 1536 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIASENATE RESOLUTION No.257 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY MALONE, PENNYCUICK, HUGHES, FONTANA, SANTARSIERO, COMITTA, HAYWOOD, COSTA, PISCIOTTANO AND VOGEL, MARCH 23, 2026 REFERRED TO RULES AND EXECUTIVE NOMINATIONS, MARCH 23, 2026 A RESOLUTIONDesignating April 4, 2026, as "School Librarian Day" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, School libraries across this Commonwealth exist to provide young people with free access to a wide range of educational resources, including books, digital media and scholarly journals; andWHEREAS, Schools employ librarians to manage these libraries and fulfill this mission; andWHEREAS, School librarians help cultivate a lifelong appreciation for reading, encouraging students to explore knowledge; andWHEREAS, School librarians teach students the media and informational literacy skills that they need to thrive in the digital age; andWHEREAS, Pennsylvania schools with a full-time certified librarian saw more students scoring advanced on State reading assessments; and1234567891011121314151617 WHEREAS, Accomplishing these feats means choosing public service over more lucrative careers requiring similar levels of professional expertise; andWHEREAS, The number of librarians employed in Pennsylvania schools has fallen consistently for every academic year on record; andWHEREAS, Encouraging young people to join this profession represents an act of public service to future generations; andWHEREAS, "National School Librarian Day" is celebrated annually on April 4 to honor school librarians' dedication to their profession; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the Senate designate April 4, 2026, as "School Librarian Day" in Pennsylvania; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the Senate acknowledge the irreplaceable role that school librarians play in the lives of young people across this Commonwealth.20260SR0257PN1536 - 2 - 12345678910111213141516
