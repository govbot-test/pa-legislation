2025-2026 Regular Session HR 571 PN 3654 Bill Text (HTM) PRINTER'S NO. 3654 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.571 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY MAYES, CURRY, TIBURCIO, McNEILL, GUZMAN, RIVERA, HOHENSTEIN, SANCHEZ, WARREN, MERSKI, PIELLI, E. NELSON, CEPEDA-FREYTIZ, STEELE, GALLAGHER, MADDEN, CIRESI AND FLEMING, JUNE 18, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON HEALTH, JUNE 22, 2026 A RESOLUTIONRecognizing the month of September 2026 as "Baby Safety Month" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, Children deserve safe living environments where they can play, learn and grow; andWHEREAS, Making decisions about food, living environment, toys and other hazards can be an overwhelming process for parents; andWHEREAS, When parents are empowered with clear information and knowledge about how to protect their baby, they can make informed choices; andWHEREAS, Many changes to promote the safety of a baby require minimal effort to eliminate hazards; andWHEREAS, Using a crib, bassinet or cradle that has been properly assembled according to instructions is an important way to make sure that a baby sleeps in a safe environment and is the foundation of good infant sleep practices; andWHEREAS, Parents should pay close attention to the age 1234567891011121314151617 ratings for products so that they can choose items that meet their children's developmental needs; andWHEREAS, No matter the products employed, supervision from an adult is essential to making sure that a child is safe; andWHEREAS, Bringing attention to the ways parents across this Commonwealth can keep their babies safe could help prevent tragedy; andWHEREAS, In 1983, the Juvenile Products Manufacturers Association began an annual awareness campaign related to child health, beginning with Expectant Mother's Day in 1983; andWHEREAS, The organization is now called the Baby Safety Alliance, and beginning in 1991, September has been designated as "Baby Safety Month"; andWHEREAS, "Baby Safety Month" offers us the opportunity to bring attention to ways that parents can protect the safety of their children; andWHEREAS, Every child deserves a safe, healthy and vibrant childhood; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives recognize the month of September 2026 as "Baby Safety Month" in Pennsylvania; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives encourage all parents to take action to protect babies from hazards.20260HR0571PN3654 - 2 - 1234567891011121314151617181920212223
