A RESOLUTION
Recognizing the month of September 2026 as "Baby Safety Month"
in Pennsylvania.
WHEREAS, Children deserve safe living environments where they
can play, learn and grow; and
WHEREAS, Making decisions about food, living environment,
toys and other hazards can be an overwhelming process for
parents; and
WHEREAS, When parents are empowered with clear information
and knowledge about how to protect their baby, they can make
informed choices; and
WHEREAS, Many changes to promote the safety of a baby require
minimal effort to eliminate hazards; and
WHEREAS, Using a crib, bassinet or cradle that has been
properly assembled according to instructions is an important way
to make sure that a baby sleeps in a safe environment and is the
foundation of good infant sleep practices; and
WHEREAS, Parents should pay close attention to the age

WHEREAS, No matter the products employed, supervision from an

WHEREAS, Bringing attention to the ways parents across this
Commonwealth can keep their babies safe could help prevent
tragedy; and
WHEREAS, In 1983, the Juvenile Products Manufacturers
Association began an annual awareness campaign related to child
health, beginning with Expectant Mother's Day in 1983; and
WHEREAS, The organization is now called the Baby Safety
Alliance, and beginning in 1991, September has been designated
as "Baby Safety Month"; and
WHEREAS, "Baby Safety Month" offers us the opportunity to
bring attention to ways that parents can protect the safety of
their children; and
WHEREAS, Every child deserves a safe, healthy and vibrant
childhood; therefore be it
RESOLVED, That the House of Representatives recognize the
month of September 2026 as "Baby Safety Month" in Pennsylvania;
and be it further
RESOLVED, That the House of Representatives encourage all
parents to take action to protect babies from hazards.
