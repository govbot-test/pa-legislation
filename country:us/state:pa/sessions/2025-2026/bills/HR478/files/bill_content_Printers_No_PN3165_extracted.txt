2025-2026 Regular Session HR 478 PN 3165 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 3165 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.478 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY KERWIN, APRIL 9, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON LABOR AND INDUSTRY, APRIL 9, 2026 A RESOLUTIONDesignating April 29, 2026, as "Professional Pest Management Appreciation Day" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, Effective pest management in homes, businesses and public places throughout this Commonwealth is an important factor in maintaining the health and well-being of all Pennsylvanians; andWHEREAS, Pest management professionals help protect this Commonwealth's food supply, homes and the environment from disease and pest-related illnesses by practicing environmentally friendly pest control methods; andWHEREAS, The Pennsylvania Pest Management Association provides training, networking and leadership opportunities for Pennsylvania's pest management professionals and is recognized for its efforts to protect our environment as well as to ensure the safe and effective use of pest management materials; andWHEREAS, The National Pest Management Association is committed to educating the public about the dangers posed by pests and the damage they can cause; and123456789101112131415161718 WHEREAS, In April 1975, President Gerald Ford first declared April as "Pest Control Month"; andWHEREAS, "Professional Pest Management Appreciation Day" offers residents of this Commonwealth an opportunity to understand and appreciate the important role that pest management providers play in ensuring the welfare of residents; andWHEREAS, April marks the beginning of spring in many parts of the United States, which means the emergence of pests such as mosquitoes, ticks, flies, ants and rodents that have been dormant during the winter; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives designate April 29, 2026, as "Professional Pest Management Appreciation Day" in Pennsylvania.20260HR0478PN3165 - 2 - 1234567891011121314
