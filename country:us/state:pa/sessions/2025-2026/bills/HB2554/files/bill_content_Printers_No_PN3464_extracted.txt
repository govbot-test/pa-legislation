2025-2026 Regular Session HB 2554 PN 3464 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 3464 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE BILL No.2554 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY HADDOCK, BURNS, HILL-EVANS, KHAN, MADDEN, INGLIS, COOK, PASHINSKI, DALEY AND CIRESI, MAY 28, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON GAME AND FISHERIES, MAY 29, 2026 AN ACTAmending Titles 30 (Fish) and 34 (Game) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes, in Pennsylvania Fish and Boat Commission, further providing for executive director; and, in Pennsylvania Game Commission, further providing for director.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Section 302(a) of Title 30 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is amended and the section is amended by adding subsections to read:§ 302. Executive director.(a) Appointment and compensation.--The commission shall appoint [to serve at its pleasure and, with the approval of the Governor, fix the compensation of], by and with the advice and consent of a majority of the members elected to the Senate, an executive director who shall be the chief executive officer of the commission and attend to its administrative work and have charge of all activities under the jurisdiction of the commission. The executive director shall be the chief waterways patrolman of the commission and shall have charge of, direct, 12345678910111213141516171819 supervise, and control all waterways patrolmen, deputy waterways patrolmen and employees of the commission. No member of the commission nor any person who has served as a member thereof within one year shall be eligible for appointment as executive director. If a vacancy occurs in the office of executive director, the commission shall conduct the following before appointing an executive director:(1) Develop a job description for the position, which shall include the requirement that an individual applying to serve as executive director have executive experience and that the individual be well informed on the subjects of conservation, restoration, budgeting, facilities management, human resource management, fish and fishing and boats and boating.(2) Transmit notice of the vacancy to the Legislative Reference Bureau for publication in the next available issue of the Pennsylvania Bulletin and post notice of the vacancy on the commission's publicly accessible Internet website.(3) Conduct a competitive, national search for qualified candidates.(a.1) Acting executive director.--If a vacancy occurs in the office of executive director, the commission may select an individual to serve at the pleasure of the commission as acting executive director for no more than two consecutive three-month periods before appointing an individual as provided under subsection (a). An individual serving as acting executive director may apply to fill the vacancy in the office of executive director.(a.2) Compensation.--The commission shall, with the approval of the Governor, fix the compensation of the executive director.20260HB2554PN3464 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 * * *Section 2. Section 302(a) of Title 34 is amended and the section is amended by adding a subsection to read:§ 302.
