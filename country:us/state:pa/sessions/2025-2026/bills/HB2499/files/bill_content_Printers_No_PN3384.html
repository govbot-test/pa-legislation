AN ACT
Amending the act of June 2, 1915 (P.L.736, No.338), entitled "An
act defining the liability of an employer to pay damages for
injuries received by an employe in the course of employment;
establishing an elective schedule of compensation; providing
procedure for the determination of liability and compensation
thereunder; and prescribing penalties," in State Workers'
Insurance Fund, further providing for investments by board.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Section 1512 of the act of June 2, 1915 (P.L.736,
No.338), known as the Workers' Compensation Act, is amended to
read:
Section 1512. The board may invest any of the surplus or
reserve belonging to the fund in such securities and investments
as are authorized for investment by savings banks[.] and,
notwithstanding section 1731-A of the act of April 9, 1929
(P.L.343, No.176), known as The Fiscal Code, section 504 of the
act of November 30, 1965 (P.L.847, No.356), known as the Banking
Code of 1965, sections 922 and 922.1 of the former act of
December 14, 1967 (P.L.746, No.345), known as the Savings

Association Code of 1967, and any other provision of law, may
invest up to twenty per centum of any of the fund's assets in

who shall be the custodian thereof. [He] The State Treasurer
shall collect the principal and interest thereof when due and
pay the same into the fund. The State Treasurer shall pay for
all such securities or evidences of indebtedness by check issued
upon requisition of the secretary. All such payments shall be
made only upon delivery of such securities or evidences of
indebtedness to the State Treasurer. To all requisitions calling
upon the State Treasurer to pay for any securities or evidences
of indebtedness there shall be attached a certified copy of the
resolution of the board authorizing the investment. The board
may, upon like resolution, sell any of such securities.
Section 2. This act shall take effect in 60 days.
