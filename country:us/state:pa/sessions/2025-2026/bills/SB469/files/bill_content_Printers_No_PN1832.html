AN ACT
Amending Titles 30 (Fish) and 34 (Game) of the Pennsylvania
Consolidated Statutes, in fishing licenses, providing for law
enforcement; and, in hunting and furtaking licenses, further
providing for resident license and fee exemptions and for
license costs and fees.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Title 30 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated
Statutes is amended by adding a section to read:
§ 2707.3. Law enforcement.
(a) Eligibility.--The commission or a county treasurer shall
issue one annual resident fishing license at a cost of $1 to any
person eligible for the license under section 2701 (relating to
resident fishing licenses) who provides documentation that the
person is employed as an officer in this Commonwealth OR RETIRED
AS AN OFFICER IN THIS COMMONWEALTH .
(b) Regulations.--The commission may promulgate regulations
to implement this section.
(c) Definitions.--As used in this section, the following
