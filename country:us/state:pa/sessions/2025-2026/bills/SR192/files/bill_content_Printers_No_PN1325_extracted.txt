2025-2026 Regular Session SR 192 PN 1325 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 1325 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIASENATE RESOLUTION No.192 Session of 2025 INTRODUCED BY CAPPELLETTI, L. WILLIAMS, KIM, KEARNEY, HUGHES, HAYWOOD, TARTAGLIONE, SCHWANK, CULVER, COSTA, VOGEL, KANE AND COMITTA, NOVEMBER 24, 2025 REFERRED TO RULES AND EXECUTIVE NOMINATIONS, NOVEMBER 24, 2025 A RESOLUTIONRecognizing the week of November 16 through 22, 2025, as "Hunger and Homelessness Awareness Week" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, For more than 25 years, the National Coalition for the Homeless and National Student Campaign Against Hunger and Homelessness have sponsored "National Hunger and Homelessness Awareness Week"; andWHEREAS, In 1975, Villanova University hosted the first "National Hunger and Homelessness Week"; andWHEREAS, Universities, colleges, high schools and organizations across the United States host events in their communities to increase awareness about the daily effects of hunger and homelessness; andWHEREAS, In this Commonwealth, approximately 12% of the entire population lives in poverty, more than 15,000 people are homeless and 1,710,480 people are facing hunger; andWHEREAS, This week focuses on giving and caring the week before Thanksgiving, and the observance serves as a reminder 1234567891011121314151617 that hunger and homelessness do not stop for the holidays but continue all year long; andWHEREAS, Hunger and homelessness are not discerning of whether Pennsylvanians reside in urban or rural communities; andWHEREAS, The COVID-19 pandemic exacerbated many of the factors that lead to hunger and homelessness; andWHEREAS, Hunger and homelessness are grave concerns for this Commonwealth; andWHEREAS, It is essential to eliminate these serious concerns and increase public awareness of the extent to which they exist; andWHEREAS, The lack of affordable housing in this Commonwealth for individuals with low income, the lack of funding and support for homeless services and the lack of affordable, sustainable food in all areas of this Commonwealth are all factors that contribute to homelessness and hunger in this Commonwealth; andWHEREAS, It is imperative that there be greater awareness of this issue and more must be done to increase activity at the Federal, State and local levels; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the Senate recognize the week of November 16 through 22, 2025, as "Hunger and Homelessness Awareness Week" in Pennsylvania.20250SR0192PN1325 - 2 - 12345678910111213141516171819202122
