WILLIAMS, MAY 28, 2026 AS REPORTED FROM COMMITTEE ON CONSUMER PROTECTION, TECHNOLOGY AND UTILITIES, HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, AS AMENDED, JUNE 9, 2026AN ACTAmending the act of April 9, 1929 (P.L.177, No.175), entitled "An act providing for and reorganizing the conduct of the executive and administrative work of the Commonwealth by the Executive Department thereof and the administrative departments, boards, commissions, and officers thereof, including the boards of trustees of State Normal Schools, or Teachers Colleges; abolishing, creating, reorganizing or authorizing the reorganization of certain administrative departments, boards, and commissions; defining the powers and duties of the Governor and other executive and administrative officers, and of the several administrative departments, boards, commissions, and officers; fixing the salaries of the Governor, Lieutenant Governor, and certain other executive and administrative officers; providing for the appointment of certain administrative officers, and of all deputies and other assistants and employes in certain departments, boards, and commissions; providing for judicial administration; and prescribing the manner in which the number and compensation of the deputies and all other assistants and employes of certain departments, boards and commissions shall be determined," in powers and duties in general, providing for certain text-based communications prohibited.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. The act of April 9, 1929 (P.L.177, No.175), known 12345678910111213141516171819202122232425 as The Administrative Code of 1929, is amended by adding a section to read:Section 531. Certain Text-Based Communications Prohibited.--(a) A Commonwealth agency may not use a text-based communication to collect, or notify an individual about, an unpaid fine, fee, toll or other charge payable to the Commonwealth agency . UNLESS THE AGENCY HAS THE EXPLICIT CONSENT OF THE INDIVIDUAL OR THE AGENCY IS PROVIDING FREE INFORMATION TO INFORM THE RECIPIENT OF THE TEXT OF COMMONWEALTH SERVICES THAT CAN BENEFIT THE RECIPIENT.(b) As used in this section, the following words and phrases shall have the meanings given to them in this subsection unless the context clearly indicates otherwise:"Commonwealth agency." As defined under section 102 of the act of October 15, 1980 (P.L.950, No.164), known as the "Commonwealth Attorneys Act.""Interactive mobile device." As defined in 75 Pa.C.S. § 102 (relating to definitions)."Text-based communication." A text message, instant message , SOCIAL MEDIA MESSAGE or other written communication composed or received on an interactive mobile device. The term does not include electronic mail.Section 2. This act shall take effect in 60 days.20260HB2551PN3556 - 2 - <--<--1234567891011121314151617181920212223
