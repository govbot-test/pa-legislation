Brehm.WHEREAS, The Senate mourns the passing of Kevin L. Brehm, a respected member of the Adams County community; andWHEREAS, Mr. Brehm passed away on April 9, 2026, after suffering a medical event while on duty as an emergency medical technician (EMT) with the Dover Area Ambulance Club in Dover Borough; andWHEREAS, Mr. Brehm worked as an EMT with the club, and gave 42 years of service with local volunteer fire departments, including Northeast Adams Fire and EMS, where he had formerly served in multiple roles, including as Deputy Chief; andWHEREAS, Mr. Brehm first volunteered for the Northeast Adams Fire and EMS as a teenager; andWHEREAS, Mr. Brehm was a graduate of Bermudian Springs High School, class of 1984; andWHEREAS, Mr. Brehm was presented a Senate citation in 2009 for 25 years of service to Adams EMS; and123456789101112131415161718 WHEREAS, Mr. Brehm spent countless hours training, served as a mentor to fellow EMTs across the area and saved many lives over the years; andWHEREAS, Mr. Brehm was known as a devoted husband, brother and friend to many and whose deep commitment to family and community was a defining hallmark of his life; andWHEREAS, The Senate recognizes the profound loss felt by the Brehm family and the broader first responder community and offers its deepest sympathy during this difficult time; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the Senate honor the life and legacy of Kevin L. Brehm and extend heartfelt condolences to his family and friends; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That a copy of this resolution be transmitted to the family of Kevin L. Brehm as a lasting expression of the Senate's respect and sympathy.20260SR0284PN1621 - 2 - 12345678910111213141516
