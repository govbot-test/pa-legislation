2025-2026 Regular Session HB 2297 PN 3010 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 3010 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE BILL No.2297 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY KHAN, KOSIEROWSKI, CARROLL, CURRY, KINKEAD, MAYES, GUENST, SANCHEZ, HILL-EVANS, VENKAT, McNEILL, PARKER, WAXMAN, DOUGHERTY, K. HARRIS, CEPEDA-FREYTIZ AND BOYD, MARCH 17, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON JUDICIARY, MARCH 18, 2026 AN ACTAmending Title 61 (Prisons and Parole) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes, in medical services, further providing for Medical Services Program.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Section 3303(b) of Title 61 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is amended to read:§ 3303. Medical Services Program.* * *(b) Fees.--(1) [The] Subject to paragraph (1.1), the department shall develop by regulation a program for inmates which includes fees for certain medical services. The regulations shall provide for consistent medical services guidelines by specifying the medical services which are subject to fees, the fee amounts, payment procedures, medical services which are not subject to fees and fees applicable to medical emergencies, chronic care and preexisting conditions.123456789101112131415161718 (1.1) The fee for nonemergency medical service provided to an inmate at the inmate's request may not exceed $1.(2) In addition to other medical services provided to the inmate, an inmate may be required to pay a fee for medical services provided because of injuries the inmate inflicted upon himself or another inmate.* * *Section 2. Within 180 days after the effective date of this section, the Department of Corrections shall amend all rules and regulations inconsistent with the amendment of 61 Pa.C.S. § 3303(b), including 37 Pa. Code § 93.12 (relating to Prison Medical Services Program).Section 3. This act shall take effect in 60 days.20260HB2297PN3010 - 2 - 12345678910111213
