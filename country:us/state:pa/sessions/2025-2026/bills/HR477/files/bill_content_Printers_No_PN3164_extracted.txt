2025-2026 Regular Session HR 477 PN 3164 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 3164 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.477 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY McANDREW, HARKINS, SANCHEZ, PROKOPIAK, NEILSON, MULLINS, BENHAM, CONKLIN, ABNEY, KINKEAD, DOUGHERTY, CERRATO, KEPHART, OWLETT, RIVERA, STEELE, KLUNK AND MOUL, APRIL 9, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON TRANSPORTATION, APRIL 9, 2026 A RESOLUTIONDesignating the month of May 2026 as "Motorcycle Safety Awareness Month" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, The safety of all Pennsylvania motorists is an issue of enduring consequence; andWHEREAS, Motorcyclists have the same rights and privileges as all other motor vehicle drivers on the roadway; andWHEREAS, During "Motorcycle Safety Awareness Month" and throughout the year, drivers of other vehicles and all road users are reminded to safely share the road with motorcyclists and to be extra alert to keep motorcyclists safe; andWHEREAS, During "Motorcycle Safety Awareness Month," Pennsylvanians are encouraged to spread the word about increasing motorcyclists' safety, support and enhance motorcycle safety awareness programs and enjoy the use of motorcycles while practicing utmost safety; andWHEREAS, Pennsylvania receives significant economic benefits from the more than 350,000 registered motorcycles and nearly 1234567891011121314151617 800,000 licensed motorcycle operators, all of whom are required to keep their motorcycles inspected and obey laws relating to operator safety and equipment; andWHEREAS, In an effort to reduce the number of motorcycle crashes and fatalities, the Department of Transportation encourages all motorcyclists to take advantage of free training courses through the Motorcycle Safety Program pledge to be one less crash statistic by visiting LiveFreeRideAlive on Facebook or penndot.pa.gov/PAMSP; andWHEREAS, Despite inevitable incidents, motorcycle operators continue to promote highway safety and encourage other motor vehicle operators to recognize and participate in motorcycle safety training; andWHEREAS, Throughout the month of May, the Pennsylvania Motorcycle Safety Program, Alliance of Bikers Aimed Toward Education of Pennsylvania, or A.B.A.T.E., and the Pennsylvania Motorcycle Dealers Association, PMDA, are continuing their advocacy of motorcycle safety; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives designate the month of May 2026 as "Motorcycle Safety Awareness Month" in Pennsylvania.20260HR0477PN3164 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718192021
