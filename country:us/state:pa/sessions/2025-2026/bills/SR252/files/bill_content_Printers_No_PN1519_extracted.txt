2025-2026 Regular Session SR 252 PN 1519 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 1519 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIASENATE RESOLUTION No.252 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY CAPPELLETTI, HUGHES, SAVAL, FONTANA, COSTA, HAYWOOD, SANTARSIERO AND VOGEL, MARCH 20, 2026 REFERRED TO RULES AND EXECUTIVE NOMINATIONS, MARCH 20, 2026 A RESOLUTIONRecognizing March 18, 2026, as "Gideon's Day" in Pennsylvania to honor the hard and selfless work of public defenders across this Commonwealth.WHEREAS, March 18, 2026, marks the 63rd anniversary of the United States Supreme Court decision in Gideon v. Wainwright; andWHEREAS, The Court unanimously ruled that the Sixth Amendment to the Constitution of the United States guarantees the right to legal counsel as a fundamental right essential to a fair trial and requires that counsel be provided to individuals who cannot afford to hire an attorney; andWHEREAS, The ruling spurred nationwide efforts to recruit and train criminal defense attorneys for the purpose of providing a public defense; andWHEREAS, The ruling led to the creation of public defender offices nationwide, including in our Commonwealth, whose law firms were specifically created to represent indigent clients charged with crimes; and123456789101112131415161718 WHEREAS, Public defenders represent more than one-half of individuals charged with crimes in this Commonwealth; andWHEREAS, Public defenders fight to protect our constitutional rights, including the presumption of innocence, the right to be free from unreasonable government searches and seizures and the right to be tried in front of a jury of our peers, amongst others; andWHEREAS, Without zealous advocates, our society cannot ensure that these constitutional rights are protected in criminal proceedings and that innocent people do not lose their freedom; andWHEREAS, In order to best serve this purpose, public defenders must be recognized as indispensable to our adversarial system and be given the same resources as the prosecution; andWHEREAS, Pennsylvania ranks 45th in indigent defense funding, with per capita spending far below the national average; andWHEREAS, The lack of resources strains even the best attorneys and negatively impacts the quality of a legal defense, jeopardizing the integrity of the criminal legal system; andWHEREAS, Additional State funding is necessary to alleviate the strain placed on public defenders; andWHEREAS, Despite ongoing challenges, public defenders continue to fight valiantly for their clients' dignity, humanity and right to a quality defense; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the Senate recognize March 18, 2026, as "Gideon's Day" in Pennsylvania to honor the hard and selfless work of public defenders across this Commonwealth.20260SR0252PN1519 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627
