2025-2026 Regular Session HR 560 PN 3571 Bill Text (HTM) PRINTER'S NO. 3571 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.560 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY PICKETT, BURGOS, GREINER, HAMM, KAUFFMAN, NEILSON, STAMBAUGH AND ROWE, JUNE 8, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON COMMUNICATIONS AND TECHNOLOGY, JUNE 9, 2026 A RESOLUTIONRecognizing the week of June 22 through 28, 2026, as "Amateur Radio Week" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, Amateur radio operators, or "hams," are celebrating more than a century of the miracle of the human voice broadcast over the airwaves; andWHEREAS, Amateur radio has continued to provide a bridge between peoples, societies and countries by creating friendships and the sharing of ideas; andWHEREAS, There are 23,473 licensed amateur radio operators living in this Commonwealth; andWHEREAS, The Commonwealth recognizes that these same radio operators have demonstrated their value in public assistance by providing free radio communications for local parades, bike-a-thons, walk-a-thons, fairs and other charitable public events; andWHEREAS, The Commonwealth recognizes the more than 1,500 members of the Amateur Radio Emergency Services and Radio 1234567891011121314151617 Amateur Civil Emergency Services programs who serve this Commonwealth, county and local emergency response organizations; andWHEREAS, The Commonwealth recognizes and appreciates the diligence of the "hams" who also serve as weather spotters in the SKYWARN program of the National Weather Service; andWHEREAS, All of these amateur radio services are provided wholly uncompensated; andWHEREAS, The Amateur Radio Relay League (ARRL) is the body that organizes the annual exercise called "Amateur Radio Field Day"; andWHEREAS, The ARRL "Amateur Radio Field Day" exercise will take place June 27 through 28, 2026, and is a 24-hour emergency preparedness exercise and demonstration of the radio amateurs' skills and readiness to provide self-supporting communications without further infrastructure being required; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives recognize the week of June 22 through 28, 2026, as "Amateur Radio Week" in Pennsylvania.20260HR0560PN3571 - 2 - 12345678910111213141516171819
