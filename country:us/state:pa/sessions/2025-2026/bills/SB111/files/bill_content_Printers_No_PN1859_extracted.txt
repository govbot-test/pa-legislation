WARD, DUSH AND CAPPELLETTI, JANUARY 22, 2025 AS REPORTED FROM COMMITTEE ON JUDICIARY, HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, AS AMENDED, JUNE 26, 2026AN ACTAmending Title 61 (Prisons and Parole) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes, in general administration relating to correctional institutions, providing for residency of corrections officers.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Title 61 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is amended by adding a section to read:§ 1107. Residency of corrections officers.(a) Residency.-- Notwithstanding 71 Pa.C.S. § 2301(b) (relating to examinations requisite for appointment and promotion), the department may hire an applicant or promote an individual as a corrections officer at a State correctional institution who resides outside this Commonwealth.(b) Preference.--A Commonwealth resident shall receive residential preference as an applicant or an individual seeking a promotion as a corrections officer at a State correctional institution. To determine standing on each certified eligibility <--123456789101112131415161718 list, an additional five points shall be applied to the final examination score obtained by a Commonwealth resident who is an applicant or seeking a promotion as a corrections officer at a State correctional institution.(B) PREFERENCE.--WHENEVER THE NAME OF AN APPLICANT WHO MEETS THE RESIDENCY REQUIREMENTS UNDER 71 PA.C.S. § 2301(B)(1) OR (2) APPEARS ON AN ELIGIBLE LIST CERTIFIED AS THE RESULT OF A CIVIL SERVICE EXAMINATION ADMINISTERED IN ACCORDANCE WITH 71 PA.C.S. PT. III (RELATING TO CIVIL SERVICE REFORM) FOR APPOINTMENT TO A CORRECTIONS OFFICER POSITION AT A STATE CORRECTIONAL INSTITUTION, THE APPOINTING AUTHORITY, IN MAKING AN APPOINTMENT TO THE POSITION, SHALL GIVE PREFERENCE TO THE APPLICANT IF THE APPLICANT IS AMONG THE THREE HIGHEST-RANKING AVAILABLE INDIVIDUALS ON THE CERTIFICATION OF ELIGIBLES OR WITHIN THE SPECIFIED ALTERNATIVE NUMBER DESIGNATED IN ACCORDANCE WITH 71 PA.C.S. § 2402(B)(2) (RELATING TO SELECTION AND APPOINTMENT OF ELIGIBLES). THE PREFERENCE UNDER THIS SUBSECTION SHALL NOT APPLY TO PROMOTIONAL OPPORTUNITIES.Section 2. This act shall take effect in 60 days.20250SB0111PN1859 - 2 - <--12345678910111213141516171819
