WARD AND K. WARD, APRIL 21, 2026 INTRODUCED AND ADOPTED, APRIL 21, 2026 A RESOLUTIONNoting with sadness the death of the Honorable Katherine "Katie" True, former eight-term member of the House of Representatives from Lancaster County, who died October 7, 2025.WHEREAS, Katie True was born January 16, 1941, in Baltimore, Maryland, the daughter of George and Henriette Buck; andWHEREAS, Ms. True was a 1958 graduate of J.P. McCaskey High School, Lancaster County; andWHEREAS, In June 2024, Ms. True's husband, Peter True, preceded her in death; andWHEREAS, Six sons, 12 grandchildren and 11 great-grandchildren survive her; andWHEREAS, The Senate extends its sincere condolences to the True family; andWHEREAS, The Senate further commends Ms. True's effectiveness as a public servant in the Commonwealth's legislative and executive branches; andWHEREAS, Ms. True first won election to the 37th District of 123456789101112131415161718 the House of Representatives in 1992; andWHEREAS, Her faithful service for the benefit of Lancaster County, the Lancaster Delegation and all residents of this Commonwealth continued through 2000; andWHEREAS, After a two-year term as Executive Director of the Pennsylvania Commission for Women, Ms. True returned to the General Assembly; andWHEREAS, Committed to accomplishing more as a legislator, Ms. True won election to the newly created 41st District seat in the House of Representatives in 2002; andWHEREAS, Ms. True represented the 41st District with distinction through 2010; andWHEREAS, On June 20, 2011, Ms. True was confirmed by the Senate as Commissioner of Professional and Occupational Affairs; andWHEREAS, In August 2013, former Governor Tom Corbett announced Ms. True's selection as Secretary of Legislative Affairs; andWHEREAS, "You get passionate about something and you jump in," Ms. True said that year; andWHEREAS, For Ms. True, this encompassed matters relating to independent living, human trafficking, penalties and legal substitute addresses relative to domestic abuse, adoption consent, child protective services and substance abuse prevention and recovery; andWHEREAS, Ms. True is remembered as an experienced advocate, a consensus builder and a compassionate, responsible leader among peers; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the Senate note with sadness the death of the Honorable Katherine "Katie" True, former eight-term member of 20260SR0287PN1628 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 the House of Representatives from Lancaster County, who died October 7, 2025; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That a copy of this resolution be transmitted to the True family.20260SR0287PN1628 - 3 - 1234
