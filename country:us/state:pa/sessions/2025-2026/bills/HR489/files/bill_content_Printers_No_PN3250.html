A RESOLUTION
Designating May 17, 2026, as the "25th Anniversary of the
Educational Improvement Tax Credit Program Day" in
Pennsylvania.
WHEREAS, The act of May 17, 2001 (P.L.4, No.4), established
the educational improvement tax credit (EITC) program in this
Commonwealth; and
WHEREAS, The EITC program provides tax credits to business
entities for contributions to scholarship organizations and
educational improvement organizations; and
WHEREAS, Scholarship organizations use these contributions to
provide scholarships to students in prekindergarten through
grade 12; and
WHEREAS, EITC program scholarships are used to pay tuition
and school-related fees for students to attend a school of their
choice in this Commonwealth; and
WHEREAS, The beneficiaries of EITC program scholarships are
students from low and middle-income families; and
WHEREAS, These scholarships have helped families pursue a
