2025-2026 Regular Session HR 489 PN 3250 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 3250 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.489 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY WHITE, PICKETT, MARCELL, ROWE, COOPER, MOUL, ANDERSON, KAUFFMAN, MENTZER, BERNSTINE, NEILSON AND SCHEUREN, APRIL 21, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON EDUCATION, APRIL 22, 2026 A RESOLUTIONDesignating May 17, 2026, as the "25th Anniversary of the Educational Improvement Tax Credit Program Day" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, The act of May 17, 2001 (P.L.4, No.4), established the educational improvement tax credit (EITC) program in this Commonwealth; andWHEREAS, The EITC program provides tax credits to business entities for contributions to scholarship organizations and educational improvement organizations; andWHEREAS, Scholarship organizations use these contributions to provide scholarships to students in prekindergarten through grade 12; andWHEREAS, EITC program scholarships are used to pay tuition and school-related fees for students to attend a school of their choice in this Commonwealth; andWHEREAS, The beneficiaries of EITC program scholarships are students from low and middle-income families; andWHEREAS, These scholarships have helped families pursue a 123456789101112131415161718 wide range of educational options for their children, including faith-based schools, career-focused programs and specialized instruction that would not otherwise be available; andWHEREAS, Since its inception, the EITC program has awarded more than 600,000 scholarships totaling approximately $1.5 billion in scholarship support; andWHEREAS, The EITC program has demonstrated the power of public-private partnership to expand educational opportunity, strengthen communities and help students access learning environments that best meet their individual needs; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives designate May 17, 2026, as the "25th Anniversary of the Educational Improvement Tax Credit Program Day" in Pennsylvania; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives recognize the vital role the EITC program plays in providing educational opportunity for students in this Commonwealth.20260HR0489PN3250 - 2 - 1234567891011121314151617
