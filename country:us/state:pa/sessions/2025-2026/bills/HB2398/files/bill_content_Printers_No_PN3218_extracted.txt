2025-2026 Regular Session HB 2398 PN 3218 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 3218 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE BILL No.2398 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY T. DAVIS, MADDEN, HILL-EVANS, SHUSTERMAN, PROBST, McNEILL, OTTEN, SANCHEZ, HOWARD, D. WILLIAMS, GILLEN, CERRATO, CEPEDA-FREYTIZ AND WARREN, APRIL 16, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON VETERANS AFFAIRS AND EMERGENCY PREPAREDNESS, APRIL 16, 2026 AN ACTAmending Title 53 (Municipalities Generally) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes, in general provisions relating to government and administration, providing for flood evacuation plans for organized camps.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Title 53 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is amended by adding a section to read:§ 1144. Flood evacuation plans for organized camps.(a) Evacuation plans.--The governing body of each municipality shall require the operator of an organized camp located within 500 feet of a special flood hazard area to prepare and maintain a written flood evacuation plan for the camp premises.(b) Filing and review.--The operator of an organized camp shall file a copy of the flood evacuation plan with the municipality and the county emergency management agency and shall review and update the plan annually before the camp opens 123456789101112131415161718 for the camp season.(c) Posting and distribution.--The operator of an organized camp shall have the following duties:(1) Post a diagram or summary of evacuation routes and safe locations in each sleeping or dining facility within the camp premises.(2) Provide a copy or summary of the flood evacuation plan to adult campers or parents or guardians of children and youth campers before each camp season.(d) Enforcement.--A municipality may condition the issuance or renewal of any license, permit or zoning approval for an organized camp on compliance with this section.(e) Definitions.--As used in this section, the following words and phrases shall have the meanings given to them in this subsection unless the context clearly indicates otherwise:"Operator." A person who owns or manages an organized camp."Organized camp." A s defined in section 1 of the act of November 10, 1959 (P.L.1400, No.497), entitled "An act providing for the annual registration of organized camps for children, youth and adults; defining the duties of the Department of Health of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania; and prescribing penalties.""Special flood hazard area." Land in a floodplain having a 1% or greater annual probability of flooding as identified by the Federal Emergency Management Agency on a Flood Insurance Rate Map or Digital Flood Insurance Rate Map.Section 2. This act shall take effect in 60 days.20260HB2398PN3218 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627
