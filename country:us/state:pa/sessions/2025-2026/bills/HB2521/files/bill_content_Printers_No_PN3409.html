AN ACT
Amending Title 3 (Agriculture) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated
Statutes, in agricultural business development center,
further providing for purpose and for limitation on grants.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Section 4804 of Title 3 of the Pennsylvania
Consolidated Statutes is amended by adding a paragraph to read:
§ 4804. Purpose.
The department shall operate the center for the following
purposes:
* * *
(9) To devise, award and administer grants to
agricultural operations impacted by acute market disruptions.
Section 2. Section 4807(b) of Title 3 is amended and the
section is amended by adding a subsection to read:
§ 4807. Limitation on grants.
* * *
(b) Matching.--[Grant] Except as provided under subsection
