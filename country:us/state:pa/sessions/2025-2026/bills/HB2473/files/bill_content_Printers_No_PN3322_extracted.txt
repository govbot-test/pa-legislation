2025-2026 Regular Session HB 2473 PN 3322 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 3322 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE BILL No.2473 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY MAZZOCCO, BRENNAN, HILL-EVANS, KHAN, VENKAT, PARKER, NEILSON, DEASY AND SANCHEZ, APRIL 29, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON INSURANCE, APRIL 29, 2026 AN ACTRepealing obsolete laws related to insurance.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Repeal of obsolete laws.(a) Intent.--The General Assembly declares that the repeals under subsection (b) are necessary to remove obsolete and expired laws related to insurance.(b) Repeals.--The following acts or provisions of law are repealed:(1) The act of July 11, 1996 (2nd Sp.Sess., P.L.1879, No.10), known as the Flood Insurance Education and Information Act.(2) Sections 635.1(d), 635.6(c) and 2194 of the act of May 17, 1921 (P.L.682, No.284), known as The Insurance Company Law of 1921.Section 2. Effective date.This act shall take effect immediately.1234567891011121314151617
