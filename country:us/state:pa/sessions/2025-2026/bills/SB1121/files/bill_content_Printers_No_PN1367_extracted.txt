2025-2026 Regular Session SB 1121 PN 1367 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 1367 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIASENATE BILL No.1121 Session of 2025 INTRODUCED BY COLLETT, SANTARSIERO, KEARNEY, COMITTA, KANE AND COSTA, DECEMBER 22, 2025 REFERRED TO ENVIRONMENTAL RESOURCES AND ENERGY, DECEMBER 22, 2025 AN ACTAmending the act of July 7, 1980 (P.L.380, No.97), entitled "An act providing for the planning and regulation of solid waste storage, collection, transportation, processing, treatment, and disposal; requiring municipalities to submit plans for municipal waste management systems in their jurisdictions; authorizing grants to municipalities; providing regulation of the management of municipal, residual and hazardous waste; requiring permits for operating hazardous waste and solid waste storage, processing, treatment, and disposal facilities; and licenses for transportation of hazardous waste; imposing duties on persons and municipalities; granting powers to municipalities; authorizing the Environmental Quality Board and the Department of Environmental Protection to adopt rules, regulations, standards and procedures; granting powers to and imposing duties upon county health departments; providing remedies; prescribing penalties; and establishing a fund," in enforcement and remedies, further providing for right of citizen to intervene in proceedings.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Section 615 of the act of July 7, 1980 (P.L.380, No.97), known as the Solid Waste Management Act, is amended to read:Section 615. [Right] Rights of citizen to compel compliance and intervene in proceedings.1234567891011121314151617181920212223242526 [Any] (a) In addition to seeking any other remedy available at law or in equity to a citizen of this Commonwealth against the department, or a person alleged to be in violation of this act, any rule or regulation of the department or order of the department or any term or condition of any permit issued by the department, if the department fails to carry out a duty of the department under this act or any regulation, rule, order or permit issued under this act, a citizen of this Commonwealth having an interest that is or may be adversely affected has a right to commence a civil action to compel compliance with this act or any regulation, rule, order or permit issued under this act.(b) An action under subsection (a) may not be commenced if the department has commenced and is diligently prosecuting a directly related civil action in a court of the United States or the Commonwealth to require compliance with this act or any regulation, rule or order adopted or issued under this act or permit issued according to this act.(c) In any action brought under section 604 or 605, any citizen of this Commonwealth having an interest which is or may be adversely affected shall have the right on [his] the citizen's own behalf, without posting bond, to intervene in [any action brought pursuant to section 604 or 605] the action.Section 2. This act shall take effect in 60 days.20250SB1121PN1367 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324
