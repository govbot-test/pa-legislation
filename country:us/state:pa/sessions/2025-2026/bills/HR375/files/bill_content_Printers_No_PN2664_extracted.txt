2025-2026 Regular Session HR 375 PN 2664 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 2664 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.375 Session of 2025 INTRODUCED BY COOPER, NEILSON, VENKAT AND HADDOCK, DECEMBER 1, 2025 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON INTERGOVERNMENTAL AFFAIRS AND OPERATIONS, DECEMBER 3, 2025 A RESOLUTIONRecognizing October 18, 2025, as "Willie Thrower Day" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, Willie Lee Thrower, Sr., was born March 22, 1930, in New Kensington; andWHEREAS, On October 18, 1953, Willie Thrower became the first African-American quarterback to take a snap in modern National Football League history while playing for the Chicago Bears; andWHEREAS, Willie Thrower was a part of the 1952 Michigan State Spartans that won the national championship; andWHEREAS, In 1979, Willie Thrower was elected to the Westmoreland County Sports Hall of Fame, in 1981, he was inducted into the AK Valley Hall of Fame and, in 2011, he was inducted into the WPIAL Hall of Fame; andWHEREAS, In 2003, the Willie Thrower Historical Marker was erected on the road that runs past the high school he attended in New Kensington; andWHEREAS, In 2006, a statue of Willie Thrower was erected near 1234567891011121314151617 Valley High School in New Kensington to honor his accomplishments; andWHEREAS, In 2021, the Willie Thrower Award Foundation debuted an award for the top quarterback in the WPIAL and City League; andWHEREAS, October 18, 2023, was designated Willie Thrower Community Day in New Kensington, as part of a four-day celebration of the 70th anniversary of Thrower's play for the Chicago Bears; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives recognize October 18, 2025, as "Willie Thrower Day" in Pennsylvania.20250HR0375PN2664 - 2 - 1234567891011
