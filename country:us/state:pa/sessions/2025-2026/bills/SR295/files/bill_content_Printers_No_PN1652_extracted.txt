2025-2026 Regular Session SR 295 PN 1652 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 1652 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIASENATE RESOLUTION No.295 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY SANTARSIERO AND FARRY, APRIL 29, 2026 REFERRED TO STATE GOVERNMENT, APRIL 29, 2026 A RESOLUTIONDesignating the Bucks County Playhouse in New Hope as the State Theater of Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, The Bucks County Playhouse is located on the site of a former grist mill on the banks of the Delaware River in New Hope, Pennsylvania, midway between Philadelphia and New York City; andWHEREAS, The original structure was rebuilt in 1790 after a fire destroyed Hope Mills, leading Benjamin Parry to build the newly named New Hope Mills, which inspired the village to change its name from Coryell's Ferry to New Hope; andWHEREAS, In the 1930s, the historic site was preserved from demolition through the efforts of a dedicated group of artists, including renowned Broadway playwright and Bucks County resident Moss Hart; andWHEREAS, The Bucks County Playhouse officially opened July 1, 1939, with a production of "Springtime for Henry," featuring acclaimed actor Edward Everett Horton; andWHEREAS, The Bucks County Playhouse quickly earned national 123456789101112131415161718 prominence as "America's Most Famous Summer Theater," hosting a remarkable array of American theatrical talent, including Grace Kelly, Robert Redford, Neil Simon, Helen Hayes, Angela Lansbury, Dick Van Dyke, Liza Minnelli, Walter Matthau, Audra McDonald and many others who helped shape the American stage; andWHEREAS, Throughout its history, the Bucks County Playhouse has served as a cultural cornerstone of this Commonwealth, contributing to this Commonwealth's rich theatrical tradition and elevating this Commonwealth's national profile in the performing arts; andWHEREAS, Following a period of closure, the Bucks County Playhouse was restored and reopened in 2012, preserving its historic legacy while continuing to provide high-quality productions, educational programming and community engagement; andWHEREAS, Today, the Bucks County Playhouse remains a vibrant and iconic institution that attracts visitors from across this Commonwealth, the region and the nation; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the Senate designate the Bucks County Playhouse in New Hope as the State Theater of Pennsylvania.20260SR0295PN1652 - 2 - 1234567891011121314151617181920
