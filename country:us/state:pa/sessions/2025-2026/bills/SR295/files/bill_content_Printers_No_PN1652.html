A RESOLUTION
Designating the Bucks County Playhouse in New Hope as the State
Theater of Pennsylvania.
WHEREAS, The Bucks County Playhouse is located on the site of
a former grist mill on the banks of the Delaware River in New
Hope, Pennsylvania, midway between Philadelphia and New York
City; and
WHEREAS, The original structure was rebuilt in 1790 after a
fire destroyed Hope Mills, leading Benjamin Parry to build the
newly named New Hope Mills, which inspired the village to change
its name from Coryell's Ferry to New Hope; and
WHEREAS, In the 1930s, the historic site was preserved from
demolition through the efforts of a dedicated group of artists,
including renowned Broadway playwright and Bucks County resident
Moss Hart; and
WHEREAS, The Bucks County Playhouse officially opened July 1,
1939, with a production of "Springtime for Henry," featuring
acclaimed actor Edward Everett Horton; and
WHEREAS, The Bucks County Playhouse quickly earned national

prominence as "America's Most Famous Summer Theater," hosting a
remarkable array of American theatrical talent, including Grace

many others who helped shape the American stage; and
WHEREAS, Throughout its history, the Bucks County Playhouse
has served as a cultural cornerstone of this Commonwealth,
contributing to this Commonwealth's rich theatrical tradition
and elevating this Commonwealth's national profile in the
performing arts; and
WHEREAS, Following a period of closure, the Bucks County
Playhouse was restored and reopened in 2012, preserving its
historic legacy while continuing to provide high-quality
productions, educational programming and community engagement;
and
WHEREAS, Today, the Bucks County Playhouse remains a vibrant
and iconic institution that attracts visitors from across this
Commonwealth, the region and the nation; therefore be it
RESOLVED, That the Senate designate the Bucks County
Playhouse in New Hope as the State Theater of Pennsylvania.
