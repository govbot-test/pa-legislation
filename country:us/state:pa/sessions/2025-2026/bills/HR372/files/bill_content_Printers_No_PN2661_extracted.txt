2025-2026 Regular Session HR 372 PN 2661 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 2661 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.372 Session of 2025 INTRODUCED BY MATZIE, MEHAFFIE, STAMBAUGH, BRENNAN, PIELLI, HARKINS, GIRAL, FIEDLER, HILL-EVANS, VENKAT, KHAN, HADDOCK, SANCHEZ, NEILSON, RIVERA AND ZIMMERMAN, NOVEMBER 20, 2025 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON CONSUMER PROTECTION, TECHNOLOGY AND UTILITIES, DECEMBER 3, 2025 A RESOLUTIONRecognizing December 2, 2025, as "World Nuclear Energy Day" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, On December 2, 1942, Enrico Fermi and his team achieved a historic milestone by demonstrating the first self-sustaining, controlled nuclear chain reaction in the world at Chicago Pile-1; andWHEREAS, On December 2, 1957, the Shippingport Atomic Power Station in Beaver County began operating as the first commercial nuclear power plant in the world dedicated exclusively to peaceful applications; andWHEREAS, Nuclear energy produces nearly 20% of the electricity generated in the United States; andWHEREAS, Nuclear energy produces nearly 50% of the carbon-free electricity generated in the United States; andWHEREAS, The nuclear sector of the United States directly employs nearly 70,000 people in high-quality jobs that last for decades; and1234567891011121314151617 WHEREAS, This sector is responsible for more than 250,000 secondary jobs; andWHEREAS, For every 100 nuclear power plant jobs, another 66 jobs are created in local communities; andWHEREAS, Nuclear energy plays a key role in the national security of the United States; andWHEREAS, The United States Navy, the largest nuclear-powered navy in the world, is supported by the commercial nuclear energy industry and a shared nuclear supply chain; andWHEREAS, Advanced nuclear technologies are vital to powering space exploration; andWHEREAS, Nuclear energy promotes grid security and reliability as one of the least expensive sources of baseload power generation worldwide; andWHEREAS, Nuclear power plants are largely resilient to adverse weather events, providing electricity 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year to distressed areas when other sources of electricity shut down; andWHEREAS, Nuclear energy contributes an estimated $60 billion per year to the gross domestic product of the United States; andWHEREAS, Pennsylvania is the second largest producer of nuclear energy in the United States, behind only the State of Illinois; andWHEREAS, There are more nuclear operators in this Commonwealth than in any other state; andWHEREAS, These nuclear operators include Constellation, Talen and Vistra; andWHEREAS, Nuclear energy comprises 30% of all electricity generated in this Commonwealth; andWHEREAS, Clean energy production by nuclear plants in this 20250HR0372PN2661 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 Commonwealth prevents more than 37 million metric tons of CO2 emissions annually; andWHEREAS, Currently eight reactors operating at four sites in this Commonwealth provide nearly 9,500 megawatts of emission-free, clean electricity; andWHEREAS, These sites are Beaver Valley Power Station, Limerick Generating Station, Peach Bottom Atomic Power Station and Susquehanna Nuclear Plant; andWHEREAS, The Crane Clean Energy Center (formerly TMI Unit 1) is set to resume operations by 2027, adding an additional 837 megawatts of energy to the PJM electric grid; andWHEREAS, Nuclear energy contributes approximately $2 billion to our State gross domestic product; andWHEREAS, Pennsylvania's nuclear energy plants provide approximately 15,900 in-State, full-time jobs, both direct and secondary; andWHEREAS, Pennsylvania's nuclear energy plants generate approximately $70 million in net State tax revenue; andWHEREAS, A strong civilian nuclear sector is essential to United States leadership in global energy diplomacy, allowing our nation to influence and promote the peaceful use of nuclear technologies; andWHEREAS, The United States collaborates with other countries to develop new and innovative opportunities for nuclear technologies; andWHEREAS, The House of Representatives celebrates the contributions of nuclear energy in advancing clean, reliable and sustainable power generation worldwide;
