HARRIS, MARCH 31, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON EDUCATION, MARCH 31, 2026 AN ACTAmending the act of March 10, 1949 (P.L.30, No.14), entitled "An act relating to the public school system, including certain provisions applicable as well to private and parochial schools; amending, revising, consolidating and changing the laws relating thereto," in Student Teacher Support Grant Program, further providing for program establishment and duties of agency.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Section 1203-B(e) of the act of March 10, 1949 (P.L.30, No.14), known as the Public School Code of 1949, is amended by adding a paragraph to read:Section 1203-B. Program establishment and duties of agency.* * *(e) Grant amount.--* * *(2.1) An eligible student teacher who completes required student teaching in a school entity in a subject shortage area in this Commonwealth shall, in addition to the amount awarded under paragraph (1), receive a minimum grant amount 1234567891011121314151617181920 of $5,000. The agency, in consultation with the department, shall utilize data from the department to determine the subject shortage areas.* * *Section 2. This act shall take effect in 60 days.20260HB2352PN3121 - 2 - 12345
