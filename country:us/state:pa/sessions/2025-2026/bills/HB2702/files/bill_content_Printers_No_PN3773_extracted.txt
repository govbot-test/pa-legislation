2025-2026 Regular Session HB 2702 PN 3773 Bill Text (HTM) PRINTER'S NO. 3773 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE BILL No.2702 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY FRANKEL, CUTLER, HANBIDGE, D. WILLIAMS, KINKEAD, SANCHEZ, HILL-EVANS, WAXMAN, HOWARD, SAMUELSON, GUZMAN, BOROWSKI, RIVERA AND GALLAGHER, JULY 14, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON HEALTH, JULY 14, 2026 AN ACTAmending Title 35 (Health and Safety) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes, in health care cost containment, repealing provisions relating to expiration.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Section 3317 of Title 35 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is repealed:[§ 3317. Expiration.This chapter shall expire 10 years after the effective date of this section.]Section 2. This act shall take effect in 60 days.1234567891011
