AN ACT
Amending Title 35 (Health and Safety) of the Pennsylvania
Consolidated Statutes, in health care cost containment,
repealing provisions relating to expiration.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Section 3317 of Title 35 of the Pennsylvania
Consolidated Statutes is repealed:
[§ 3317. Expiration.
This chapter shall expire 10 years after the effective date
of this section.]
Section 2. This act shall take effect in 60 days.
