2025-2026 Regular Session HR 516 PN 3346 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 3346 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.516 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY CEPHAS, McCLINTON, MAYES, HOHENSTEIN, HILL-EVANS, GUZMAN AND WAXMAN, MAY 4, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON TOURISM, RECREATION AND ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT, MAY 4, 2026 A RESOLUTIONDesignating June 1, 2026, as "Links Day" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, The Links, Incorporated was founded in 1946 by Sara Strickland Scott and Margaret Hawkins in Philadelphia; andWHEREAS, The Links, Incorporated has evolved into one of the nation's largest volunteer service organizations, with more than 17,000 members of African descent across 299 Chapters located in 41 states, the District of Columbia and the Bahamas, including Pennsylvania's Bucks County Chapter, Delaware Valley Chapter, Harrisburg Chapter, Montgomery County Chapter, Penn Towne Chapter, Philadelphia Chapter and Pittsburgh Chapter; andWHEREAS, The Links, Incorporated members are extraordinary women who are committed to the cultural and economic advancement of African Americans and persons of African ancestry; andWHEREAS, The Links, Incorporated's core values are friendship, integrity, honesty, service, commitment, family relationships, courage, respect for self and others, legacy, confidentiality, responsibility and accountability; and1234567891011121314151617 WHEREAS, Links members contribute more than 950,000 documented hours of community service annually, strengthening their communities and enhancing the nation; andWHEREAS, The Links, Incorporated is the recipient of awards from the UN Association of New York and the Leon H. Sullivan Foundation for its premier programs; andWHEREAS, The outstanding programming of The Links, Incorporated has five facets which include services to youth, the arts, national trends and services, international trends and services and health and human services; andWHEREAS, The programs are implemented through strategies such as public information and education, economic development and public policy campaigns; andWHEREAS, The members of The Links, Incorporated serve as influential leaders and role models, shaping their communities through advocacy, mentorship and collaboration; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives designate June 1, 2026, as "Links Day" in Pennsylvania; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives recognize and honor the members of The Links, Incorporated's Bucks County Chapter, Delaware Valley Chapter, Harrisburg Chapter, Montgomery County Chapter, Penn Towne Chapter, Philadelphia Chapter and Pittsburgh Chapter for their outstanding contributions to this Commonwealth; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That a copy of this resolution be transmitted to The Links, Incorporated as a token of appreciation from the members of the House of Representatives.20260HR0516PN3346 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627
