AN ACT
Amending Title 18 (Crimes and Offenses) of the Pennsylvania
Consolidated Statutes, in sexual offenses, further providing
for the offense of indecent exposure.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Section 3127(b) of Title 18 of the Pennsylvania
Consolidated Statutes is amended to read:
§ 3127. Indecent exposure.
* * *
(b) Grading.--[If]
(1) Except as otherwise provided in paragraph (2), if
the person knows or should have known that any of the persons
present are less than 16 years of age, indecent exposure
under subsection (a) is:
(i) For a first offense, a misdemeanor of the first
degree. [Otherwise, indecent exposure under subsection
(a) is a misdemeanor of the second degree.]
(ii) For a second or subsequent offense, a felony of
the third degree.
