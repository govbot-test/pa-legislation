2025-2026 Regular Session HB 2122 PN 2745 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 2745 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE BILL No.2122 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY McNEILL, HILL-EVANS AND GIRAL, JANUARY 9, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON JUDICIARY, JANUARY 9, 2026 AN ACTAmending Title 18 (Crimes and Offenses) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes, in sexual offenses, further providing for the offense of indecent exposure.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Section 3127(b) of Title 18 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is amended to read:§ 3127. Indecent exposure.* * *(b) Grading.--[If](1) Except as otherwise provided in paragraph (2), if the person knows or should have known that any of the persons present are less than 16 years of age, indecent exposure under subsection (a) is:(i) For a first offense, a misdemeanor of the first degree. [Otherwise, indecent exposure under subsection (a) is a misdemeanor of the second degree.](ii) For a second or subsequent offense, a felony of the third degree.12345678910111213141516171819 (2) Indecent exposure under subsection (a) is:(i) For a first offense, a misdemeanor of the second degree.(ii) For a second offense, a misdemeanor of the first degree.(iii) For a third or subsequent offense, a felony of the third degree.Section 2. This act shall take effect in 60 days.20260HB2122PN2745 - 2 - 12345678
