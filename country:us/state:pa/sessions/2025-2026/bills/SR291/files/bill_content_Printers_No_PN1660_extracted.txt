2025-2026 Regular Session SR 291 PN 1660 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 1660 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIASENATE RESOLUTION No.291 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY PHILLIPS-HILL, STREET, COMITTA, TARTAGLIONE, BROWN, HAYWOOD, CULVER, VOGEL, PICOZZI AND STEFANO, MAY 1, 2026 REFERRED TO RULES AND EXECUTIVE NOMINATIONS, MAY 1, 2026 A RESOLUTIONDesignating the week of May 17 through 23, 2026, as "Public Works Week" in Pennsylvania to coincide with "National Public Works Week."WHEREAS, The theme for "National Public Works Week" in 2026 is "Rooted in Service, Powered by Community"; andWHEREAS, The theme is fitting since public works professionals focus on infrastructure, facilities and services that are vitally important to sustainable and resilient communities; andWHEREAS, Public works professionals work behind the scenes to make our communities healthier and safer; andWHEREAS, Public works professionals improve the overall quality of life for residents of this Commonwealth and throughout our nation; andWHEREAS, It is important to recognize public works professionals who we may not always see, but who we know are hard at work because public works is evident in everything we do and everywhere we go; and123456789101112131415161718 WHEREAS, Public works professionals consist of individuals who work for State and local agencies, including engineers, consultants, contractors, inspectors and vendors; andWHEREAS, Public works professionals provide core services to residents of this Commonwealth, including planning, building, managing and operating public works systems; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the Senate designate the week of May 17 through 23, 2026, as "Public Works Week" in Pennsylvania to coincide with "National Public Works Week"; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the Senate honor individuals who work in the public works field and recognize the contributions and accomplishments made by these hardworking individuals across this Commonwealth and our nation.20260SR0291PN1660 - 2 - 12345678910111213
