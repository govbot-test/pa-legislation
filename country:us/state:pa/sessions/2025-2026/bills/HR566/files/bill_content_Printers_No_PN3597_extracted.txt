2025-2026 Regular Session HR 566 PN 3597 Bill Text (HTM) PRINTER'S NO. 3597 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.566 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY McNEILL, PIELLI, M. MACKENZIE, VENKAT, JAMES, HOHENSTEIN, BRENNAN, PICKETT, MERSKI, HAMM, KUZMA, VITALI, GUZMAN, NEILSON, ANDERSON, SOLOMON, RIVERA, GALLAGHER, CONKLIN, PASHINSKI, O'MARA, SANCHEZ, STEELE, DEASY, CIRESI AND MENTZER, JUNE 11, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON VETERANS AFFAIRS AND EMERGENCY PREPAREDNESS, JUNE 12, 2026 A RESOLUTIONDesignating September 27, 2026, as "Gold Star Mother's and Family's Day" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, When a military service member dies in the line of duty, the family receives The Gold Star to commemorate the military member's service and life, while recognizing the impact of loss on the family; andWHEREAS, During World War I, families of service members displayed the Service Flag, which featured a blue star for each immediate family member serving in the military; andWHEREAS, When a service member tragically died during combat, the blue star was replaced with a gold star; andWHEREAS, During World War I, Grace Darling Seibold lost her son, George Vaughn Seibold, who died while serving in France; andWHEREAS, Grace Darling Seibold founded American Gold Star Mothers, Inc., to provide comfort and support to mothers as they 12345678910111213141516 processed their grief; andWHEREAS, President Woodrow Wilson used the term "Gold Star Mother" following World War I, representing the importance and honor of those who served and the lives that were sacrificed; andWHEREAS, In 1936, Congress designated the last Sunday of September as "Gold Star Mother's Day" in order to honor grieving mothers; andWHEREAS, In 2011, President Barack Obama amended the title of "Gold Star Mother's Day" to "Gold Star Mother's and Family's Day," which is recognized on the last Sunday in September; andWHEREAS, "Gold Star Mother's and Family's Day" signifies the impact of the loss of a service member on Gold Star family members, including parents, siblings and children; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives designate September 27, 2026, as "Gold Star Mother's and Family's Day" in Pennsylvania.20260HR0566PN3597 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718
