2025-2026 Regular Session HR 532 PN 3422 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 3422 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.532 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY GALLAGHER, GIRAL, WAXMAN, SANCHEZ, KHAN, NEILSON, HOHENSTEIN, MULLINS, DONAHUE, DOUGHERTY, PROKOPIAK, GUZMAN, BRENNAN, RIVERA, CEPEDA-FREYTIZ AND CERRATO, MAY 20, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON HEALTH, MAY 20, 2026 A RESOLUTIONRecognizing October 24, 2026, as "Acupuncture and Herbal Medicine Day" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, Acupuncture and herbal medicine have a long and rich history as components of a comprehensive traditional medical system that has been used for thousands of years to diagnose and treat illness, prevent disease and improve well-being; andWHEREAS, Acupuncture involves inserting fine needles into a patient's skin to stimulate the body and promote changes in health, often to address back or neck pain; andWHEREAS, The National Certification Board for Acupuncture and Herbal Medicine has served since 1982 as the body that certifies qualified practitioners of acupuncture and herbal medicine; andWHEREAS, Millions of Americans have used acupuncture to address health issues; andWHEREAS, This Commonwealth has a variety of practitioners who provide acupuncture, including physicians; andWHEREAS, Practitioners of acupuncture and herbal medicine are 1234567891011121314151617 dedicated to the highest standards of professionalism and maintain these standards through education, credentialing and personal commitment; andWHEREAS, It is vital that individuals in need of medical services understand the full range of available modalities and seek competent and professional care; andWHEREAS, October 24 has been nationally recognized as "Acupuncture and Herbal Medicine Day" since 2002; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives recognize October 24, 2026, as "Acupuncture and Herbal Medicine Day" in Pennsylvania; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives urge all residents of this Commonwealth to learn more about the potential benefits of acupuncture and herbal medicine.20260HR0532PN3422 - 2 - 123456789101112131415
