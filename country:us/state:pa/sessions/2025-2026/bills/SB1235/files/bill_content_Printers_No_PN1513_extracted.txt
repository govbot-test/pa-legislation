2025-2026 Regular Session SB 1235 PN 1513 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 1513 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIASENATE BILL No.1235 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY BAKER, VOGEL, STREET, J. WARD, PENNYCUICK AND DUSH, MARCH 18, 2026 REFERRED TO JUDICIARY, MARCH 18, 2026 AN ACTAmending Title 61 (Prisons and Parole) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes, in Pennsylvania Board of Probation and Parole, further providing for Pennsylvania Parole Board.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Section 6111(b) and (c)(2) of Title 61 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes are amended to read:§ 6111. Pennsylvania Parole Board.* * *(b) Membership.--The board shall consist of nine members who shall be appointed by the Governor, by and with the advice and consent of a majority of the members of the Senate, and each of whom shall hold office for a term of six years or until that person's successor shall have been duly appointed and qualified, but in no event more than [90 days] six months beyond the expiration of that person's appointed term.(c) Vacancies.--* * *123456789101112131415161718 (2) Whenever a board member's term expires, that member's position shall be immediately deemed a vacancy, and the Governor shall nominate a person to fill that membership position on the board [within 90 days of] not later than six months after the date of expiration, even if the member continues to remain on the board.* * *Section 2. This act shall apply to members of the Pennsylvania Parole Board who hold that office on or after the effective date of this section.Section 3. This act shall take effect in 60 days.20260SB1235PN1513 - 2 - 1234567891011
