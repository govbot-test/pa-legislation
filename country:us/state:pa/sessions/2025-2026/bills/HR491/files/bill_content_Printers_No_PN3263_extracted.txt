2025-2026 Regular Session HR 491 PN 3263 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 3263 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.491 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY GUENST, HADDOCK, SANCHEZ, PROBST, McNEILL, HILL-EVANS, KHAN, WEBSTER, HOHENSTEIN, FREEMAN, CONKLIN, CEPEDA-FREYTIZ, HANBIDGE, INGLIS, RIVERA, SHUSTERMAN, CURRY AND FLEMING, APRIL 23, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON VETERANS AFFAIRS AND EMERGENCY PREPAREDNESS, APRIL 23, 2026 A RESOLUTIONUrging the Congress of the United States to enact legislation designating the Green Star Service Banner as a national emblem honoring families of members of the United States Armed Forces and veterans who died by suicide.WHEREAS, Families of members of the United States Armed Forces and veterans who died by suicide bear a distinct and enduring burden of grief and service; andWHEREAS, For too long, this loss has been referenced as a statistic rather than acknowledged by name and symbol; andWHEREAS, The Green Star Service Banner is intended as a voluntary, symbolic emblem to honor and recognize the surviving families of members of the United States Armed Forces and veterans who died by suicide; andWHEREAS, The House of Representatives of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania recognizes and honors Green Star Families within Pennsylvania; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives of the 1234567891011121314151617 Commonwealth of Pennsylvania urge the Congress of the United States to enact legislation designating the Green Star Service Banner as a national emblem honoring families of members of the United States Armed Forces and veterans who died by suicide; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That agencies of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania and political subdivisions permit the voluntary display of the Green Star Service Banner in appropriate venues and on appropriate observances, consistent with applicable policies; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That copies of this resolution be transmitted to the Governor, the Adjutant General of Pennsylvania, the Secretary of Military and Veterans Affairs of Pennsylvania, the presiding officers of each house of Congress and to each member of Congress from Pennsylvania.20260HR0491PN3263 - 2 - 123456789101112131415
