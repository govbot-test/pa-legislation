A RESOLUTION
Urging the Congress of the United States to enact legislation
designating the Green Star Service Banner as a national
emblem honoring families of members of the United States
Armed Forces and veterans who died by suicide.
WHEREAS, Families of members of the United States Armed
Forces and veterans who died by suicide bear a distinct and
enduring burden of grief and service; and
WHEREAS, For too long, this loss has been referenced as a
statistic rather than acknowledged by name and symbol; and
WHEREAS, The Green Star Service Banner is intended as a
voluntary, symbolic emblem to honor and recognize the surviving
families of members of the United States Armed Forces and
veterans who died by suicide; and
WHEREAS, The House of Representatives of the Commonwealth of
Pennsylvania recognizes and honors Green Star Families within
Pennsylvania; therefore be it
RESOLVED, That the House of Representatives of the

RESOLVED, That copies of this resolution be transmitted to
