AN ACT
Amending Title 53 (Municipalities Generally) of the Pennsylvania
Consolidated Statutes, in preemptions, providing for
prohibiting occupancy limits.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Title 53 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated
Statutes is amended by adding a section to read:
§ 307. Prohibiting occupancy limits.
(a) Prohibition.--A local government unit may not limit the
number of individuals who may live together in a single dwelling
based on familial relationship and BUT may limit the number of
individuals who may live together in a single dwelling based
only on one of the following:
(1) Demonstrated ON DEMONSTRATED health and safety
standards, including the International Building Code
standards, ANY APPLICABLE BUILDING OR CONSTRUCTION CODES AND

STANDARDS PROMULGATED BY REGULATION UNDER THE ACT OF NOVEMBER

(2) Federal, State, local or municipal affordable
housing program guidelines.
(b) Definitions.--As used in this section, the following
words and phrases shall have the meanings given to them in this
subsection unless the context clearly indicates otherwise:
"Familial relationship." An individual's connection to one
or more other individuals through blood, marriage, adoption or
foster care.
"Local government unit." A county, city, borough,
incorporated town, township, home rule municipality, optional
plan municipality or optional charter municipality in this
Commonwealth.
Section 2. This act shall take effect in 60 days.
