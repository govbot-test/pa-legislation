HARRIS, WARREN AND CURRY, DECEMBER 22, 2025 AS REPORTED FROM COMMITTEE ON HOUSING AND COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT, HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, AS AMENDED, APRIL 13, 2026AN ACTAmending Title 53 (Municipalities Generally) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes, in preemptions, providing for prohibiting occupancy limits.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Title 53 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is amended by adding a section to read:§ 307. Prohibiting occupancy limits.(a) Prohibition.--A local government unit may not limit the number of individuals who may live together in a single dwelling based on familial relationship and BUT may limit the number of individuals who may live together in a single dwelling based only on one of the following:(1) Demonstrated ON DEMONSTRATED health and safety standards, including the International Building Code standards, ANY APPLICABLE BUILDING OR CONSTRUCTION CODES AND <--<--<--<--<--12345678910111213141516 STANDARDS PROMULGATED BY REGULATION UNDER THE ACT OF NOVEMBER 10, 1999 (P.L.491, NO.45), KNOWN AS THE PENNSYLVANIA CONSTRUCTION CODE ACT, fire code regulations or Department of Environmental Protection wastewater and water quality standards.(2) Federal, State, local or municipal affordable housing program guidelines.(b) Definitions.--As used in this section, the following words and phrases shall have the meanings given to them in this subsection unless the context clearly indicates otherwise:"Familial relationship." An individual's connection to one or more other individuals through blood, marriage, adoption or foster care."Local government unit." A county, city, borough, incorporated town, township, home rule municipality, optional plan municipality or optional charter municipality in this Commonwealth.Section 2. This act shall take effect in 60 days.20250HB2109PN3172 - 2 - <--123456789101112131415161718
