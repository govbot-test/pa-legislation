2025-2026 Regular Session HR 570 PN 3623 Bill Text (HTM) PRINTER'S NO. 3623 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.570 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY MAYES, KHAN, MADDEN, HOHENSTEIN, RIVERA, SANCHEZ AND D. WILLIAMS, JUNE 15, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON HEALTH, JUNE 16, 2026 A RESOLUTIONRecognizing the week of August 2 through 8, 2026, as "National Health Center Week" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, Community health centers are clinics that serve uninsured and underinsured people through low-cost health care, especially primary care, and reduce barriers to that care, such as travel and language; andWHEREAS, Community health centers include federally qualified health centers, look-alike clinics, rural health clinics and other similar providers; andWHEREAS, Originally called neighborhood health centers, community health centers were first created in 1965; andWHEREAS, Today, community health centers still play an essential role in delivering affordable health care to low-income families and those who live in areas that are health care deserts; andWHEREAS, Across the country, community health centers create more than 300,000 jobs, pay more than $2 billion in taxes annually, and save billions in Medicaid costs; and123456789101112131415161718 WHEREAS, Approximately 1 million Pennsylvanians, or 1 in 13, visit a community health center each year; andWHEREAS, One in three people in rural areas in the United States utilizes a community health center; andWHEREAS, This Commonwealth operates a network of 59 state health centers that also serve communities in similar ways to independent community health centers; andWHEREAS, Providing access to affordable, high-quality health care across this Commonwealth makes our communities stronger; andWHEREAS, The theme for National Health Center Week this year is "Building Innovative Care Where It Matters Most," which celebrates how community health centers use creativity and commitment to advance innovative solutions that meet patients where they are and improve primary care delivery, access and outcomes nationwide; andWHEREAS, The health care professionals and staff at community health centers deserve recognition for their essential role in keeping Pennsylvanians healthy; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives recognize the week of August 2 through 8, 2026, as "National Health Center Week" in Pennsylvania.20260HR0570PN3623 - 2 - 12345678910111213141516171819202122
