2025-2026 Regular Session HB 2287 PN 2996 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 2996 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE BILL No.2287 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY BURGOS, GUZMAN, HANBIDGE, GIRAL, HILL-EVANS, RIVERA, SANCHEZ, SCHLOSSBERG, KINKEAD, CIRESI, WARREN AND GREEN, MARCH 13, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON TRANSPORTATION, MARCH 13, 2026 AN ACTAmending Title 75 (Vehicles) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes, in fees, further providing for exemption of persons, entities and vehicles from fees.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Section 1901 of Title 75 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is amended by adding a subsection to read:§ 1901. Exemption of persons, entities and vehicles from fees.* * *(b.2) Initial identification card fees.--(1) No fee shall be charged for an initial identification card, including for a photograph required for an initial identification card, to an individual who affirms in writing that the individual:(i) i s 18 years of age or older; (ii) is a first-time voter;(iii) is eligible to vote in this Commonwealth; and(iv) does not possess a valid Pennsylvania driver's 123456789101112131415161718 license.(2) The department may require additional information to determine an individual's identity and eligibility under this subsection.* * *Section 2. This act shall take effect in 60 days.20260HB2287PN2996 - 2 - 123456
