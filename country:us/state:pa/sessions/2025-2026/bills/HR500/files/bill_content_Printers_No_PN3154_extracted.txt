DAVIS, FREEMAN, GUENST, HARKINS, HOWARD, KHAN, McNEILL, NEILSON, SANCHEZ AND VENKAT, APRIL 8, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON STATE GOVERNMENT, APRIL 9, 2026 A RESOLUTIONDesignating the year of 2026 as the "80th Anniversary Year of the State YMCA of Pennsylvania Youth and Government Program" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, It is self-evident that democracy requires an engaged citizenry and, as Plato famously wrote, "The price good men pay for indifference to public affairs is to be ruled by evil men"; andWHEREAS, It is our shared responsibility to maintain the health of our republic by continually empowering our youth to experience and understand civics; andWHEREAS, This Commonwealth is fortunate to have one of the most impactful programs for bringing democracy to life for young people through the State YMCA of Pennsylvania, located in Harrisburg, Dauphin County; andWHEREAS, In 1946, visionary leaders, including Lieutenant Governor Daniel B. Strickler, in collaboration with the State YMCA of Pennsylvania, launched a transformative program to engage middle and high school students in participatory civic 123456789101112131415161718 education; andWHEREAS, This initiative, known as the Youth and Government Program, has shaped generations of creative thinkers and innovative leaders and is the only hands-on, multibranch government experience of its kind; andWHEREAS, Eighty percent of individuals who have participated in the Youth and Government Program report being actively engaged in civic life as adults; andWHEREAS, Guided by its enduring motto, "Democracy must be learned by each generation," the Youth and Government Program has brought together students from various regions and ethnic and socioeconomic backgrounds to work throughout the year for the opportunity to be selected to participate in the program in the halls of this Commonwealth's Capitol each spring; andWHEREAS, More than 700 Pennsylvania high school students, having earned leadership roles in the 2026 Youth and Government Program, are arriving in Harrisburg on April 16, 2026, to begin a whirlwind and once-in-a-lifetime experience serving in leadership roles in all three branches of government, the Capitol press corps and as lobbyists; andWHEREAS, In the halls of this Commonwealth's Capitol, these remarkable young people, like the thousands who have come before them, will sharpen their leadership skills, improve their problem-solving abilities and build self-esteem as they learn that their ideas matter and that they can make a difference in the world; andWHEREAS, Demonstrating its deep commitment to ensuring that all eligible students may participate in the Youth and Government Program regardless of financial ability, the State YMCA of Pennsylvania provides scholarships to more than 40% of 20260HR0500PN3154 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 participants, totaling nearly $35,000 this year; andWHEREAS, This year, the State YMCA of Pennsylvania's Youth and Government Program celebrates eight decades of impact and empowerment and its critical role in forming the future of informed and ethical leadership in this Commonwealth; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives designate the year of 2026 as the "80th Anniversary Year of the State YMCA of Pennsylvania Youth and Government Program" in Pennsylvania; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives commend the State YMCA of Pennsylvania's Youth and Government Program for its longstanding commitment to civic education, youth leadership development and the future of this Commonwealth.20260HR0500PN3154 - 3 - 1234567891011121314
