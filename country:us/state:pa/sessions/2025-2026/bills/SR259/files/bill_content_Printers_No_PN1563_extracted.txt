2025-2026 Regular Session SR 259 PN 1563 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 1563 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIASENATE RESOLUTION No.259 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY TARTAGLIONE, FONTANA, SCHWANK, HAYWOOD, HUGHES, COSTA, SANTARSIERO, J. WARD, VOGEL, BOSCOLA AND CAPPELLETTI, APRIL 1, 2026 REFERRED TO RULES AND EXECUTIVE NOMINATIONS, APRIL 1, 2026 A RESOLUTIONRecognizing the month of April 2026 as "Limb Loss and Limb Difference Awareness Month" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, More than 2.7 million Americans of all ages, races and genders have had amputations, and another 28 million Americans are at risk for amputation; andWHEREAS, Each day, more than 300 Americans lose a limb; andWHEREAS, When limb difference, defined as the partial or complete absence of or malfunction of limbs, is included, the number of Americans affected more than doubles to 5.6 million; andWHEREAS, In this Commonwealth alone, more than 200,000 individuals are living with limb loss or limb difference; andWHEREAS, Limb loss and limb difference are lifelong conditions that impact physical health, emotional well-being and financial stability, and the general public is largely unaware of the many challenges faced by those affected; andWHEREAS, The leading causes of amputation are vascular 1234567891011121314151617 disease, trauma and cancer; andWHEREAS, Prosthetic and orthotic devices and care are essential components of health care that restore function and independence for people living with limb loss and limb difference, yet access to proper devices and care can be costly and requires ongoing maintenance and periodic replacement over time; andWHEREAS, Individuals with limb loss and limb difference often require more than one prosthetic or orthotic device to restore full function across essential daily activities such as work, physical activity and personal hygiene, yet access is frequently limited to basic ambulation; andWHEREAS, Individuals with limb loss and limb difference must overcome many challenges, including barriers to accessing the care and mobility resources necessary to live active, healthy and independent lives; andWHEREAS, The physical effects of limb loss and limb difference may be the most visible, but the emotional and social impacts are often equally significant; andWHEREAS, Physical activity is a critical component of health and well-being, helping to prevent secondary health conditions such as cardiovascular disease, diabetes and depression; andWHEREAS, Individuals with limb loss and limb difference often face barriers to fully participating in physical activity due to limitations in access to appropriate care and equipment; andWHEREAS, When individuals have access to the care and mobility devices they need, they experience improved health outcomes, increased independence and greater participation in their communities; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the Senate recognize the month of April 2026 20260SR0259PN1563 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 as "Limb Loss and Limb Difference Awareness Month" in Pennsylvania; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the Senate acknowledge the importance of ensuring that individuals with limb loss and limb difference have access to the care and mobility resources necessary to support their health, independence and full participation in society; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the Senate encourage efforts that promote equitable access to mobility and active lifestyles and recognize movement and physical activity as a vital component of health and well-being for all Pennsylvanians; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the Senate encourage all residents of this Commonwealth to recognize the importance of this month, celebrate individuals with limb loss and limb difference who are living full and productive lives and express gratitude to caregivers, clinicians and communities, who are a source of support and motivation.20260SR0259PN1563 - 3 - 1234567891011121314151617
