2025-2026 Regular Session HR 387 PN 2751 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 2751 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.387 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY CEPEDA-FREYTIZ, BURGOS, GUZMAN, VITALI, BRENNAN, HOHENSTEIN, McNEILL, SCHLOSSBERG, GIRAL, HANBIDGE, HILL-EVANS, DONAHUE, NEILSON, SANCHEZ, MADDEN, RIVERA, FRANKEL, CARROLL, SHUSTERMAN, KAZEEM AND PROBST, JANUARY 9, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON TOURISM, RECREATION AND ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT, JANUARY 9, 2026 A RESOLUTIONRecognizing February 27, 2026, as "Dominican Republic Independence Day" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, On February 27, 2026, the Dominican Republic is celebrating the anniversary of its independence from Haiti in 1844; andWHEREAS, Before the Dominican Republic declared its independence, the shared island of Hispaniola had been under Haitian rule for more than two decades after centuries of colonialism, first by the Spanish and then by the French; andWHEREAS, "Dominican Republic Independence Day" coincides with Carnaval, which is celebrated the entire month of February with parades, competitions and spirited events; andWHEREAS, The Dominican Republic is an important partner to the United States in hemispheric affairs due to its standing as the Caribbean's second-largest economy and third-largest country in terms of population and land mass, its large bilateral trade 12345678910111213141516 with the United States and its proximity to the United States and other Caribbean nations; andWHEREAS, The family unit within Dominican society is incredibly important and has formed the basis of stability throughout all of the Dominican Republic's political and economic upheaval; andWHEREAS, Whenever possible, the extended family, which usually encompasses three generations, lives within the same vicinity; andWHEREAS, Family loyalty is a deeply ingrained characteristic of the Dominican people; andWHEREAS, Dominicans are the third-largest population of Hispanic origin living in this Commonwealth; andWHEREAS, The Dominican-American community contributes to our nation's economic vitality and culture while maintaining a deep connection with its Dominican roots; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives recognize February 27, 2026, as "Dominican Republic Independence Day" in Pennsylvania.20260HR0387PN2751 - 2 - 12345678910111213141516171819
