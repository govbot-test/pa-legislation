2025-2026 Regular Session HB 2025 PN 2584 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 2584 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE BILL No.2025 Session of 2025 INTRODUCED BY CURRY, PROBST, WEBSTER, RIVERA, KHAN, SAPPEY, HOHENSTEIN, DONAHUE, HILL-EVANS, SANCHEZ AND HARKINS, NOVEMBER 10, 2025 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON APPROPRIATIONS, NOVEMBER 12, 2025 AN ACTAmending the act of March 10, 1949 (P.L.30, No.14), entitled "An act relating to the public school system, including certain provisions applicable as well to private and parochial schools; amending, revising, consolidating and changing the laws relating thereto," in reimbursements by Commonwealth and between school districts, providing for reimbursements for borrowing costs during budget impasse; and establishing the School District Impasse Recovery Fund.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. The act of March 10, 1949 (P.L.30, No.14), known as the Public School Code of 1949, is amended by adding a section to read:Section 2599.9. Reimbursements for Borrowing Costs During Budget Impasse.--(a) The following shall apply to the School District Impasse Recovery Fund:(1) The School District Impasse Recovery Fund is established.(2) Money in the fund is appropriated on a continuing basis to the department for the purpose of providing reimbursements in 1234567891011121314151617181920 accordance with this section.(3) The fund shall consist of all money appropriated for use under this section and any interest that accrues on the money.(4) Money in the fund:(i) May only be used for the reimbursements authorized under this section.(ii) May not be transferred or diverted to any other purpose by administrative action.(iii) Shall be used to reimburse school entities for eligible expenses incurred during an impasse period in accordance with this section.(b) The department shall reimburse school entities for all eligible expenses incurred during an impasse period in the fiscal year following the fiscal year in which the impasse period occurred.(c) The total amount of reimbursement under this section for a school entity may not exceed the documented eligible expenses submitted by the school entity.(d) The department shall develop an application to enable school entities to apply for reimbursement under this section. The application must include:(1) Itemized documentation of each claimed eligible expense. (2) Proof of payment or financial obligation.(3) Certification by the school entity's business administrator.(4) Other information deemed necessary by the department.(e) The department shall determine the method or manner in which applications under this section are submitted to the department.(f) Applications for reimbursement under this section must 20250HB2025PN2584 -
