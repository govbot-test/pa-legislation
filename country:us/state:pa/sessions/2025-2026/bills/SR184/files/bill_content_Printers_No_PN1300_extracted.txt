2025-2026 Regular Session SR 184 PN 1300 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 1300 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIASENATE RESOLUTION No.184 Session of 2025 INTRODUCED BY YAW, CULVER, BARTOLOTTA, VOGEL AND STEFANO, NOVEMBER 11, 2025 REFERRED TO RULES AND EXECUTIVE NOMINATIONS, NOVEMBER 11, 2025 A RESOLUTIONDesignating October 28, 2025, as "Propane Legislative Day" in Pennsylvania to highlight propane as a fuel source, as well as the industry's important role in assisting the residents of this Commonwealth meet their unique energy needs.WHEREAS, Propane has been helping Americans meet their energy needs for more than 100 years; andWHEREAS, Propane is a clean burning and low-carbon fuel that provides essential energy to residential, commercial, industrial and agricultural customers across Pennsylvania ensuring that a variety of market sectors can thrive; andWHEREAS, Propane is nontoxic and a federally designated clean, alternative fuel that reduces emissions and improves air quality; andWHEREAS, Propane is commonly used as fuel for engine applications, including forklifts, generators, mowers, farm irrigation engines, fleet vehicles and school buses and when used as a vehicle fuel, propane is known as propane autogas; and WHEREAS, Propane autogas is the most widely used alternative fuel for school buses with Pennsylvania ranking fourth in the 12345678910111213141516171819 United States for the number of propane-fueled school buses that help school districts run routes, meet operational needs and significantly cut harmful emissions while saving on costs; andWHEREAS, America is the world's leading propane producer and propane supports energy security, reliability and resilience, and helps consumers meet their unique heating and energy needs; andWHEREAS, Pennsylvania produces more than 245 million gallons of propane yearly, 2.84% of the United States total; andWHEREAS, Pennsylvania's propane industry supports good-paying jobs and employs nearly 3,000 residents working for more than 125 companies covering every county of this Commonwealth and contributes $2.7 billion in State economic activity annually; andWHEREAS, October 28, 2025, has been declared "Propane Legislative Day" in Pennsylvania and this legislative body recognizes and celebrates "Propane Legislative Day" and the important contributions made by this Commonwealth's propane industry; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the Senate designate October 28, 2025, as "Propane Legislative Day" in Pennsylvania to highlight propane as a fuel source, as well as the industry's important role in assisting the residents of this Commonwealth meet their unique energy needs.20250SR0184PN1300 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324
