2025-2026 Regular Session SR 290 PN 1636 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 1636 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIASENATE RESOLUTION No.290 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY CULVER, TARTAGLIONE, COMITTA, MARTIN, BROWN, HUGHES, PHILLIPS-HILL, FONTANA, STREET, HAYWOOD, COSTA, VOGEL AND KANE, APRIL 23, 2026 REFERRED TO RULES AND EXECUTIVE NOMINATIONS, APRIL 23, 2026 A RESOLUTIONRecognizing April 29, 2026, as "World Wish Day" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, The mission of the Make-A-Wish Foundation is "Together, we create life-changing wishes for children with critical illnesses"; andWHEREAS, The Make-A-Wish Foundation has been granting life-changing wishes for children and families for more than 45 years; andWHEREAS, Make-A-Wish International now brings the hope and joy of granting wishes to more than 50 countries and territories on six continents; andWHEREAS, The Make-A-Wish Foundation currently has 57 chapters across the United States; andWHEREAS, The Make-A-Wish Foundation has granted more than 650,000 wishes worldwide with the help of donors and more than 27,000 volunteers; andWHEREAS, In fiscal year 2025, active Make-A-Wish chapters in Pennsylvania granted 686 wishes to children with critical 1234567891011121314151617 illnesses in our community; andWHEREAS, A child is diagnosed with a critical illness every 20 minutes; andWHEREAS, Research suggests that the granting of a wish can give children the strength to fight harder against critical illness; andWHEREAS, A wish provides renewed hope, uplifts the spirits of children and can unite neighbors, friends and communities; andWHEREAS, "World Wish Day" celebrates the anniversary of the fulfillment of the first wish in April 1980 that inspired the creation of the Make-A-Wish Foundation; andWHEREAS, "World Wish Day" was first officially celebrated April 29, 2010; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the Senate recognize April 29, 2026, as "World Wish Day" in Pennsylvania.20260SR0290PN1636 - 2 - 123456789101112131415
