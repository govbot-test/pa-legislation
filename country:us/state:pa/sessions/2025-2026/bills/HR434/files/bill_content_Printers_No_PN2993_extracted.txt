2025-2026 Regular Session HR 434 PN 2993 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 2993 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.434 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY PARKER, BRENNAN, SANCHEZ, GUZMAN, WAXMAN, MALAGARI, HILL-EVANS, HOHENSTEIN, SAMUELSON, KHAN, VENKAT, DOUGHERTY, HANBIDGE, CEPEDA-FREYTIZ, MAYES, RIVERA, GALLAGHER, PASHINSKI, CIRESI, COOPER AND BELLMON, MARCH 12, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON EDUCATION, MARCH 12, 2026 A RESOLUTIONRecognizing the week of March 9 through 13, 2026, as "Civic Learning Week" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, A well-informed and engaged citizenry are essential to sustaining a healthy democracy; andWHEREAS, After local observances in Massachusetts, Rhode Island and New York in 2022, "Civic Learning Week" became a national observance in 2023; andWHEREAS, "Civic Learning Week" is observed across the nation annually as a week designed to highlight the knowledge, skills and dispositions that provide the foundation for an informed and engaged citizenry, and to call for individual, local, state and national action to prioritize civic education; andWHEREAS, It is essential that each new generation is equipped to engage in our democracy for our civic strength, our economy, our national security and more; andWHEREAS, Civic education is vital to the preservation and 12345678910111213141516 improvement of the constitutional government of the United States; andWHEREAS, In 2026, Philadelphia will host this year's Civic Learning Week National Forum, where civic leaders, educators, researchers and other advocates will come together to call for an elevated prioritization of civic education in our classrooms; andWHEREAS, As Pennsylvania prepares to celebrate our nation's semiquincentennial, it is important that the Commonwealth continues to explore ways of strengthening and increasing access to quality civic education programs so that students are equipped to be engaged citizens within our democracy; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives recognize the week of March 9 through 13, 2026, as "Civic Learning Week" in Pennsylvania; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives recognize the vital role civic education plays in preparing the next generation for democratic engagement.20260HR0434PN2993 - 2 - 12345678910111213141516171819
