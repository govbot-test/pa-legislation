AN ACT
Amending Title 75 (Vehicles) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated
Statutes, in licensing of drivers, providing for initiation
of suspension of operating privilege.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Title 75 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated
Statutes is amended by adding a section to read:
§ 1541.1. Initiation of suspension of operating privilege.
(a) Initiation.--Except as otherwise provided in this title,
the department shall initiate a suspension of the operating
privilege required under this title no later than six months
after the date of sentencing for the offense giving rise to the
suspension.
(b) Unenforceable suspension.--If a suspension of the
operating privilege is imposed more than six months after the
date of sentencing for the offense giving rise to the
suspension, the suspension shall be unenforceable by the
department and in the courts of this Commonwealth.
