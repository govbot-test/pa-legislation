2025-2026 Regular Session HB 2101 PN 2701 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 2701 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE BILL No.2101 Session of 2025 INTRODUCED BY MIHALEK, KRUPA, GILLEN, KUZMA AND RIVERA, DECEMBER 15, 2025 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON TRANSPORTATION, DECEMBER 15, 2025 AN ACTAmending Title 75 (Vehicles) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes, in licensing of drivers, providing for initiation of suspension of operating privilege.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Title 75 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is amended by adding a section to read:§ 1541.1. Initiation of suspension of operating privilege.(a) Initiation.--Except as otherwise provided in this title, the department shall initiate a suspension of the operating privilege required under this title no later than six months after the date of sentencing for the offense giving rise to the suspension.(b) Unenforceable suspension.--If a suspension of the operating privilege is imposed more than six months after the date of sentencing for the offense giving rise to the suspension, the suspension shall be unenforceable by the department and in the courts of this Commonwealth.123456789101112131415161718 Section 2. This act shall take effect in 60 days.20250HB2101PN2701 - 2 - 1
