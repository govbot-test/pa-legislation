Amending the act of June 23, 1999 (P.L.159, No.22), entitled "An
act providing for scholarships for persons entering into the
field of science and technology," further providing for
annual report.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Section 9(a) introductory paragraph of the act of
June 23, 1999 (P.L.159, No.22), known as the New Economy
Technology Scholarship Act, is amended to read:
Section 9. Annual report.
(a) Development of report.--The agency shall publish a
report [by September 1, 2000, and every year thereafter.] no
later than September 1 following the end of the fiscal year in
which funds were allocated for the purpose of the program. The
