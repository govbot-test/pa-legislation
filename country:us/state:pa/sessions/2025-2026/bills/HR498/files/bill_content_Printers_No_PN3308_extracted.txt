2025-2026 Regular Session HR 498 PN 3308 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 3308 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.498 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY MALAGARI, FREEMAN, HOHENSTEIN, RIVERA, MADDEN, GALLAGHER, KHAN, CEPEDA-FREYTIZ, CERRATO, HANBIDGE AND DALEY, APRIL 28, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON TOURISM, RECREATION AND ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT, APRIL 29, 2026 A RESOLUTIONDesignating September 25, 2026, as "Chuseok Day" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, Chuseok is South Korea's major annual harvest festival, a three-day celebration observed during the eighth month of the lunar calendar; andWHEREAS, Sometimes referred to as "Korean Thanksgiving," Chuseok is a two-thousand-year-old tradition dedicated to honoring one's ancestors and showing appreciation for a successful harvest; andWHEREAS, Festivities are marked by ritual offerings of fresh food and drink at loved ones' graves, followed by sharing stories and food, playing music and games and dancing, all to celebrate nature's abundance; andWHEREAS, The values reflected in Chuseok - family, gratitude, unity and respect for nature - are embodied by Korean Americans across the nation, enriching the cultural, economic and civic fabric of the United States; and1234567891011121314151617 WHEREAS, Korean Americans have made and continue to make significant contributions in esteemed fields in this Commonwealth and across the country, including the fields of law, medicine, business, education, architecture and the arts; andWHEREAS, The Commonwealth recognized the service of Korean Americans in the Vietnam War through the enactment of the act of June 30, 2025 (P.L.17, No.10); andWHEREAS, The ongoing achievements and contributions of Korean Americans continue to enhance the prosperity, cultural diversity and communal well-being of this Commonwealth and the United States; andWHEREAS, In recognizing Chuseok, the Commonwealth acknowledges and honors these contributions, as well as the values Chuseok highlights, connecting with loved ones, nature and one's heritage; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives designate September 25, 2026, as "Chuseok Day" in Pennsylvania.20260HR0498PN3308 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718
