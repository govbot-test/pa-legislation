2025-2026 Regular Session SR 285 PN 1623 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 1623 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIASENATE RESOLUTION No.285 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY SAVAL, HAYWOOD, HUGHES, COMITTA, FONTANA, TARTAGLIONE, KEARNEY, STEFANO, CAPPELLETTI, SANTARSIERO, COSTA, VOGEL, PICOZZI, MILLER AND STREET, APRIL 20, 2026 INTRODUCED AND ADOPTED, APRIL 20, 2026 A RESOLUTIONHonoring the 100th anniversary of the Curtis Institute of Music.WHEREAS, The Curtis Institute of Music was founded in Philadelphia and celebrated its 100th anniversary on October 13, 2024; andWHEREAS, The Curtis Institute of Music is a world-class conservatory that has cultivated and refined generations of peerless musicians; andWHEREAS, The Curtis Institute of Music reflects the vision of its founder, Mary Louise Curtis Bok, by opening its doors to young musicians across the world and offering quality music education tuition-free since 1928; andWHEREAS, The Curtis Institute of Music alumni, including Leonard Bernstein, Yuja Wang and Samuel Barber, have bestowed on the world a century of music that has touched and enriched innumerable lives, extending the bounds of human connection and capacity; andWHEREAS, Curtis alumni have gone on to lead, found and 1234567891011121314151617 bolster Philadelphia's illustrious musical institutions, including its Orchestra, Chamber Music Society, Opera Company and Woodwind Quintet; andWHEREAS, Beyond the bounds of our city, Commonwealth and country, Curtis alumni grace the world's premier musical ensembles, orchestras and opera houses, forging new frontiers in composition and performance; andWHEREAS, In the century since its founding, more than 4,400 students have called the Curtis Institute of Music home; andWHEREAS, The school continues to embrace Mary Louise Curtis Bok's vision of a musical family that is equitable, worldwide and united in its love for the craft; andWHEREAS, The Curtis Institute of Music's example is a beacon to music lovers everywhere and for those who believe in the possibilities wrought from talent and determination; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the Senate honor the Curtis Institute of Music for its 100th anniversary and recognize the Curtis Institute of Music for its countless contributions to arts and culture in Philadelphia and across the world.20260SR0285PN1623 - 2 - 1234567891011121314151617181920
