2025-2026 Regular Session HB 2626 PN 3590 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 3590 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE BILL No.2626 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY DAVIDSON, WAXMAN, MAZZOCCO, FREEMAN, CEPEDA-FREYTIZ, DONAHUE, SANCHEZ, CURRY, HILL-EVANS, FLEMING AND CIRESI, JUNE 10, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON CONSUMER PROTECTION, TECHNOLOGY AND UTILITIES, JUNE 12, 2026 AN ACTAmending the act of December 17, 1968 (P.L.1224, No.387), entitled "An act prohibiting unfair methods of competition and unfair or deceptive acts or practices in the conduct of any trade or commerce, giving the Attorney General and District Attorneys certain powers and duties and providing penalties," further providing for definitions.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Section 2(4) of the act of December 17, 1968 (P.L.1224, No.387), known as the Unfair Trade Practices and Consumer Protection Law, is amended by adding a subclause and the section is amended by adding a clause to read:Section 2. Definitions.--As used in this act.* * *(4) "Unfair methods of competition" and "unfair or deceptive acts or practices" mean any one or more of the following:* * *(xx.1) Using dynamic pricing to offer or set a price for a good or service in a retail store physically located within this 12345678910111213141516171819 Commonwealth if that price is displayed electronically in a manner which enables a person or automated system to remotely change the price displayed using dynamic pricing.* * *(14) "Dynamic pricing" means pricing that fluctuates dependent on conditions where models retrain or recalibrate on information in near real time, excluding promotional pricing offers, loyalty program benefits or other temporary discounts or changes to pricing related to retention of existing customers.Section 2. This act shall take effect in 60 days.20260HB2626PN3590 - 2 - 12345678910
