andWHEREAS, The university acquired the property in 2001; andWHEREAS, The university has assessed the physical space as part of financial sustainability planning; andWHEREAS, The facility is not in use and is not required for future university needs; andWHEREAS, The property totals approximately 67.517 acres and consists of two buildings totaling 92,373 square feet; andWHEREAS, One building, Founders Hall, was built in 2001 and is 49,163 square feet, and the second building is the Health Annex and consists of 43,210 square feet;
