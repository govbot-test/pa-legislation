2025-2026 Regular Session HR 510 PN 3341 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 3341 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.510 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY WEBSTER, MAKO, VENKAT, PIELLI, MALAGARI, MAYES, HILL-EVANS, GUENST, VITALI, SANCHEZ, NEILSON AND RIVERA, MAY 1, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON LABOR AND INDUSTRY, MAY 4, 2026 A RESOLUTIONDesignating May 4, 2026, as "Aerospace Day" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, Pennsylvania is emerging as a national leader in aerospace innovation; andWHEREAS, This Commonwealth's highly skilled workforce and proximity to key Federal agencies and defense hubs uniquely position Pennsylvania to drive advancements in aerospace; andWHEREAS, Additionally, Pennsylvania's world-class education system is one of our greatest assets in supporting the aerospace sector with research universities and technical training institutions providing exceptional human capital that drives aerospace innovation here in this Commonwealth; andWHEREAS, By attracting high-wage aerospace and advanced manufacturing jobs, revitalizing our industrial regions and investing in a resilient, future-ready workforce, Pennsylvania can secure long-term economic growth and reinforce our State's position as a national hub for innovation; andWHEREAS, Leveraging the Commonwealth's assets can strengthen 1234567891011121314151617 our role in the national aerospace sector and fuel economic growth here in Pennsylvania; andWHEREAS, Celebrating "Aerospace Day" in this Commonwealth provides a valuable opportunity for both residents and lawmakers to better understand the industry and its impact on our Commonwealth; andWHEREAS, Throughout the day, there will be exhibitions of the Commonwealth's aerospace industry in Harrisburg, as well as a public hearing to further educate members of the General Assembly and Pennsylvanians on the opportunity to support and bolster this growing industry; andWHEREAS, It is crucial that we raise awareness of the aerospace industry so that we do not miss the opportunity within our reach; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives designate May 4, 2026, as "Aerospace Day" in Pennsylvania.20260HR0510PN3341 - 2 - 12345678910111213141516
