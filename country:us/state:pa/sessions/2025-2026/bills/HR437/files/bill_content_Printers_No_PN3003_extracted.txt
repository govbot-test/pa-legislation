2025-2026 Regular Session HR 437 PN 3003 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 3003 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.437 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY FREEMAN, JAMES, HILL-EVANS, BRENNAN, VENKAT, HARKINS, VITALI, GUZMAN, BURGOS, KHAN, RIVERA, CEPEDA-FREYTIZ, SAMUELSON, HOWARD, SANCHEZ, COOPER, MAYES, CIRESI AND DALEY, MARCH 13, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON TOURISM, RECREATION AND ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT, MARCH 16, 2026 A RESOLUTIONRecognizing the month of April 2026 as "National Poetry Month" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, The Academy of American Poets established its observance of "National Poetry Month" in 1996; andWHEREAS, As led by the Academy of American Poets, "National Poetry Month" has become the largest literary celebration in the world; andWHEREAS, April 2026 marks the 30th celebration of "National Poetry Month"; andWHEREAS, "National Poetry Month" highlights the extraordinary legacy and ongoing achievement of American poets; andWHEREAS, "National Poetry Month" introduces Americans to the pleasures and benefits of reading poetry; andWHEREAS, "National Poetry Month" reinforces the importance of poetry in children's learning and assists teachers in bringing poetry into their classrooms; and12345678910111213141516 WHEREAS, Poetry has generated some of our nation's leading creative artists; andWHEREAS, Poetry has inspired other artists in fields spanning music, theater, film, dance and the visual arts; andWHEREAS, "Poetry Out Loud" included more than 2,250 high school students who participated in the competition in 61 schools across this Commonwealth in 2025; andWHEREAS, Numerous communities throughout this Commonwealth celebrate their local poets by appointing them as poet laureate of their community; andWHEREAS, Language arts, including poetry, integrate the creative arts and humanities and reflect the present and historic educational, economic and social fabric of our communities; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives recognize the month of April 2026 as "National Poetry Month" in Pennsylvania.20260HR0437PN3003 - 2 - 12345678910111213141516
