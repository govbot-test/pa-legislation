2025-2026 Regular Session HR 400 PN 2797 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 2797 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.400 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY N. NELSON, BURGOS, BELLMON, KHAN, PARKER, WAXMAN AND RIVERA, JANUARY 23, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON INTERGOVERNMENTAL AFFAIRS AND OPERATIONS, JANUARY 26, 2026 A RESOLUTIONUrging the Secretary of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania, in consultation and cooperation with the United States Secretary of State and relevant Federal partners, to compile and publicize information on reputable humanitarian, nonprofit and faith-based organizations through which the residents of Pennsylvania may provide assistance for humanitarian relief in Haiti.WHEREAS, The Republic of Haiti continues to endure a severe and escalating humanitarian crisis marked by widespread violence, displacement, food insecurity and the disruption of basic services; andWHEREAS, According to humanitarian reporting by the United Nations (UN) from December 2025, Haiti's crisis has forced millions into acute need, with an ongoing Humanitarian Response Plan aiming to assist more than 4.2 million vulnerable people; andWHEREAS, High levels of food insecurity, internal displacement and threats to health and education have been clearly documented by UN agencies; andWHEREAS, UN data from 2025 confirm alarmingly high levels of 1234567891011121314151617181920 displacement, violence and deteriorating conditions for civilians, especially children and other vulnerable populations, underscoring the urgent requirement for humanitarian relief and international cooperation; andWHEREAS, The Haiti Criminal Collusion Transparency Act of 2025 (H.R. 2643) was introduced in the Congress of the United States in 2025 to promote accountability by requiring periodic reporting on ties between prominent criminal gangs and political and economic elites in Haiti, and authorizing sanctions on those individuals and entities, reflecting bipartisan concern about corruption and violence and their impact on Haitian stability; andWHEREAS, Pennsylvania is home to a vibrant Haitian and Haitian-American community whose members maintain deep familial, cultural and economic ties to Haiti, including Pennsylvanians currently living or working in Haiti; andWHEREAS, The humanitarian crisis in Haiti has affected families across Pennsylvania, creating heightened needs for accurate information, organized relief pathways and lawful opportunities for Pennsylvanians to contribute meaningfully to assistance efforts; andWHEREAS, Pennsylvania has a longstanding tradition of supporting humanitarian causes and fostering public-private partnerships, including through nonprofit, faith-based, academic and business communities; andWHEREAS, Pennsylvania's World Trade Centers and international business networks can serve as important platforms to identify economic and logistical collaboration opportunities that support Haitian recovery and empower local Haitian enterprises through lawful and ethical partnerships; and20260HR0400PN2797 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 WHEREAS, Coordination between the Pennsylvania Department of State and the United States Department of State can help ensure that Pennsylvania residents, nonprofits and businesses seeking to support legitimate humanitarian efforts are guided to credible, vetted organizations and that efforts are aligned with broader international aid strategies; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania urge the Secretary of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania, in consultation and cooperation with the United States Secretary of State and relevant Federal partners, to compile and publicize information on reputable humanitarian, nonprofit and faith-based organizations through which the residents of Pennsylvania may provide assistance for humanitarian relief in Haiti; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania recognize the humanitarian crisis in Haiti and urge coordinated partnerships to support the Haitian people, Haitian families residing in Pennsylvania and Pennsylvanians living in Haiti; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania encourage collaboration between the Pennsylvania Department of State, Pennsylvania's World Trade Centers and economic development agencies to identify lawful, ethical and impactful opportunities for Pennsylvania-based businesses to partner in humanitarian support and economic development initiatives that aid Haitian communities in need; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania recognize the serious humanitarian findings in recent United Nations reporting and the policy 20260HR0400PN2797 - 3 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 intent reflected in the Haiti Criminal Collusion Transparency Act as reinforcing the need for transparent, accountable and collaborative approaches to supporting peace, stability and humanitarian relief in Haiti; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania affirm its support for the families of Haitian residents of Pennsylvania and Pennsylvanians living in Haiti and encourage dissemination of timely, accurate resources to help families access humanitarian support and community services; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania call upon Pennsylvania's nonprofit, faith-based, academic and business communities to continue to work collaboratively to provide relief, compassion and assistance to those impacted by the crises in Haiti; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania urge Federal authorities to recognize the severity of the ongoing humanitarian crisis in Haiti and, in light of these conditions, to redesignate Temporary Protected Status for Haitians currently residing in the United States; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That a copy of this resolution be transmitted to the Secretary of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania and the United States Secretary of State.20260HR0400PN2797 - 4 - 12345678910111213141516171819202122232425
