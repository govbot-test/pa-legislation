2025-2026 Regular Session HR 564 PN 3595 Bill Text (HTM) PRINTER'S NO. 3595 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.564 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY FREEMAN, VITALI, WEBSTER, SANCHEZ, HILL-EVANS, STEELE, SAPPEY, NEILSON, DONAHUE, BOROWSKI, BOYD, OTTEN, CIRESI, FLEMING, PIELLI, GUZMAN AND MERSKI, JUNE 10, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON ENERGY, JUNE 12, 2026 A RESOLUTIONDirecting the Joint State Government Commission to conduct a study and issue a report on waste heat recovery in this Commonwealth.WHEREAS, Data centers require significant inputs of energy and water to cool heat produced by daily operations; andWHEREAS, The cooling operations of data centers present a challenge to consumers by competing for energy and water resources; andWHEREAS, Waste heat recovery serves as a potential solution to alleviate the negative effects of data centers by repurposing waste heat produced by data centers to meet heating demands, reducing the energy and water input needed for daily operations of data centers; andWHEREAS, Waste heat recovery has been tried and tested successfully in cases domestically and in other countries; andWHEREAS, The House of Representatives shall promote potential solutions to alleviate the concerns of Pennsylvanians regarding the topic of data centers; and123456789101112131415161718 WHEREAS, It is necessary to investigate waste heat recovery technology and how the technology can be implemented within the policy environment in this Commonwealth while keeping in mind economic and infrastructural barriers; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives direct the Joint State Government Commission to conduct a study and issue a report on waste heat recovery in this Commonwealth; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the study include:(1) An investigation into waste heat recovery technology and its possible applications in this Commonwealth, including:(i) Mapping of district energy networks, data centers and planned data center developments in this Commonwealth.(ii) Identifying opportunities for beneficial use of waste heat from data centers.(iii) Identifying barriers to adopting waste heat recovery systems.(iv) Evaluating best practices and policies for using waste heat implemented in other states, counties and localities.(v) Assessing the estimated costs necessary to develop supporting infrastructure and associated programs.(vi) Evaluating the expected economic costs and benefits, environmental impacts and community-level outcomes associated with implementing waste heat recovery systems.(vii) Producing policy recommendations to promote 20260HR0564PN3595 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 the adoption of waste heat recovery in data centers.(viii) The applicability of waste heat recovery to other industries.(ix) Investigating additional information that the Joint State Government Commission deems to be useful to investigate.(2) Public hearings, taking testimony and conducting site visits;and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the Joint State Government Commission establish an advisory committee to receive input on waste heat recovery, including representatives of:(1) The data center industry.(2) The waste heat recovery industry.(3) Gas and electric utilities.(4) Pennsylvania-based institutions of higher education.(5) Local governments.(6) State agencies.(7) Environmental advocacy groups.(8) Organized labor.(9) Potential consumers of waste heat.(10) The public.(11) Any additional stakeholders identified by the Joint State Government Commission;and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the Joint State Government Commission submit a report on waste heat recovery in this Commonwealth to the House of Representatives no later than one year after the adoption of this resolution.20260HR0564PN3595 - 3 - 1234567891011121314151617181920212223242526272829
