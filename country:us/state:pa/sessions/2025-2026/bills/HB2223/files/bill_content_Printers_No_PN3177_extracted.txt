HARRIS, BOYD, CURRY, HANBIDGE AND DOUGHERTY, MARCH 3, 2026 AS REPORTED FROM COMMITTEE ON ENERGY, HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, AS AMENDED, APRIL 13, 2026AN ACTAmending Title 66 (Public Utilities) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes, in service and facilities, providing for advanced transmission technologies.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Title 66 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is amended by adding a section to read:§ 1504.1. Advanced transmission technologies.(a) Submission of evidence.--The following shall apply to each transmission siting application or letter of notification filed with the commission under 52 Pa. Code Ch. 57 Subch. G (relating to commission review of siting and construction of electric transmission lines):(1) An electric distribution company shall provide evidence to the commission of the electric distribution company's evaluation and assessment of implementing advanced transmission technologies on existing transmission <--1234567891011121314151617 infrastructure and all proposed transmission infrastructure within the jurisdiction of the commission identified in the application or letter of notification.(2) The application or letter of notification under paragraph (1) shall not be granted, either as proposed or modified, unless the commission determines, in addition to any other existing criteria, that the proposed transmission lines incorporate all technically feasible and cost-effective advanced transmission technologies to achieve at least two of the following:(i) Avoid construction of new transmission infrastructure in whole or in part.(ii) Increase capacity, efficiency and reliability of the transmission system.(iii) Reduce transmission system congestion.(iv) Reduce environmental or aesthetic impacts.(b) Cost recovery.--Any additional costs borne by the electric distribution company in compliance with this section shall be recovered in accordance with a commission-approved tariff.(A) APPLICABILITY.--THIS SECTION SHALL APPLY TO EACH TRANSMISSION SITING APPLICATION FILED WITH THE COMMISSION UNDER 52 PA.
