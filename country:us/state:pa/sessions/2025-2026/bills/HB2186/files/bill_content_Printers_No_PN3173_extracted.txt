Permitted use by right.An accessory dwelling unit shall be a permitted use by right in every municipality WITH ZONING in which a single-family detached dwelling unit RESIDENTIAL BUILDING is a permitted use, except that the permission may not supersede any deed restriction, covenant or agreement restricting the use of land or any master deed, bylaw or other document applicable to a planned community.§ 6303. Adoption of ordinance.A municipality shall adopt an ordinance to permit the development of at least one accessory dwelling unit for each single-family detached dwelling unit, subject to reasonable regulation that is the least restrictive means of accomplishing the goal of the regulation.§ 6304 6303 . Application. (a) Process.--The following application process shall apply for an accessory dwelling unit:(1) A permit application for an accessory dwelling unit shall be considered and approved without discretionary review or a hearing.(2) The municipality shall approve or deny an application to create or serve an accessory dwelling unit within:(i) 30 days from the date the municipality receives the completed application; or(ii) 60 days if additional review is required.20260HB2186PN3173 - 3 - <--<--<--<--<--<--123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 (3) If the permit application to create or serve an accessory dwelling unit is submitted with a permit application to create or serve a new single-family dwelling unit on the lot, the municipality may delay approving or denying the permit application for the accessory dwelling unit until the municipality approves or denies the permit application to create or serve the new single-family dwelling unit.(4) If the applicant requests a delay, the time period provided under paragraph (2) shall be tolled for the period of the delay.(5) If the municipality has not approved or denied the completed application within the time period provided under paragraph (2), the application shall be deemed approved.(6) A municipality may charge a fee, which shall not exceed $250 in the aggregate, for costs incurred to review the permit application for the creation or service of an accessory dwelling unit.(7) A municipality may not require a renewal for a permit for an accessory dwelling unit.(b) Denial of application.--If a municipality denies an application for an accessory dwelling unit, the municipality shall return in writing a full set of comments to the applicant with a list of items that are defective or deficient and a description of how the applicant may remedy the application.CONSIDERATION OF APPLICATIONS AND INSPECTIONS SHALL BE AS PROVIDED UNDER SECTION 502 OF THE ACT OF NOVEMBER 10, 1999 (P.L.491, NO.45), KNOWN AS THE PENNSYLVANIA CONSTRUCTION CODE ACT.§ 6305 6304 . (12) (11) A municipality may not require a familial relationship between the occupant of an accessory dwelling unit and the occupant of the single-family detached dwelling unit RESIDENTIAL BUILDING . (13) (12) A municipality shall allow an accessory dwelling unit to be rented separate from the single-family detached dwelling unit RESIDENTIAL BUILDING , but may prohibit the sale or other conveyance of an accessory dwelling UNIT separate from the single-family dwelling unit RESIDENTIAL BUILDING .
