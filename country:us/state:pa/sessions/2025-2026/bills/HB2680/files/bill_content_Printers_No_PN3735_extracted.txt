2025-2026 Regular Session HB 2680 PN 3735 Bill Text (HTM) PRINTER'S NO. 3735 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE BILL No.2680 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY M. BROWN, KRUPA, REICHARD, GAYDOS, WARNER, ROWE, SMITH, KULIK, BANTA, ARMANINI, BOYD, TAKAC AND JAMES, JUNE 29, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON LIQUOR CONTROL, JUNE 30, 2026 AN ACTDesignating rye whiskey as the official State spirit of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Findings and declarations.The General Assembly finds and declares as follows:(1) Rye whiskey has deep roots in our Commonwealth's history.(2) Long before Kentucky Bourbon rose to prominence, Pennsylvania distillers were defining the American whiskey tradition with bold, spicy rye.(3) This legacy continues today, as craft distillers across this Commonwealth are once again producing exceptional rye whiskeys using local grain and traditional methods.(4) Distilling rye whiskey supports local agriculture, sustains small businesses and strengthens this Commonwealth's reputation for craftsmanship and authenticity.1234567891011121314151617 Section 2. Official State spirit.Rye whiskey is designated as the official State spirit of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania.Section 3. Effective date.This act shall take effect immediately.20260HB2680PN3735 - 2 - 12345
