2025-2026 Regular Session HB 171 PN 2801 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 2801 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE BILL No.171 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY LAWRENCE, CIRESI, K.HARRIS, PUGH, SCHEUREN AND ZIMMERMAN, JANUARY 28, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON CONSUMER PROTECTION, TECHNOLOGY AND UTILITIES, JANUARY 28, 2026 AN ACTAmending Title 66 (Public Utilities) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes, in rates and distribution systems, providing for wastewater utility deduct meter tariff requirement.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Title 66 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is amended by adding a section to read:§ 1302.1. Wastewater utility deduct meter tariff requirement.(a) Deduct meter program.--Each wastewater utility shall develop a deduct meter program, which shall allow for eligible customers the installation of a deduct meter as a part of any new tariff or tariff supplement.(b) Billing.--A wastewater utility shall provide customers with the option to be billed for wastewater services based on:(1) total water usage; or(2) net sewer outflow, calculated as total water usage minus the volume measured by the deduct meter.123456789101112131415161718 (c) Requirements for eligibility.--An eligible customer electing to participate in the deduct meter program must:(1) have a metered water supply and be current on all wastewater utility payments; and(2) be responsible for:(i) purchasing, installing and maintaining a utility-approved deduct meter;(ii) ensuring that the meter is installed at an approved location and available for inspection as needed by the utility; and(iii) submitting meter readings or allowing utility access for reading and verification.(d) Fee.--A wastewater utility may charge a reasonable administrative or inspection fee, subject to commission approval.(e) Duties.--The commission shall:(1) approve deduct meter models and installation standards;(2) maintain a registration system for deduct meters;(3) provide clear program guidelines and application procedures to customers; and(4) review and approve all deduct meter tariffs to ensure consistency, fairness and customer protection.(f) Regulations.--The commission may adopt regulations to implement this section, including standardizing deduct meter eligibility, calibration and reporting requirements.(g) Definitions.--As used in this section, the following words and phrases shall have the meanings given to them in this subsection unless the context clearly indicates otherwise:"Deduct meter." A secondary water meter installed to measure 20260HB0171PN2801 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 water that is not discharged into the sanitary sewer system, including water used for irrigation, pools and other outdoor uses."Eligible customer." A residential customer with a metered water supply who meets the program requirements as provided under this section."Wastewater utility." A wastewater utility under the jurisdiction of this Commonwealth.Section 2. This act shall take effect in 60 days.20260HB0171PN2801 - 3 - 123456789
