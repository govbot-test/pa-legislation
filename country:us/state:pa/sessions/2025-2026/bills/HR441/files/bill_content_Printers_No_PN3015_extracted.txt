2025-2026 Regular Session HR 441 PN 3015 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 3015 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.441 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY SANCHEZ, FREEMAN, WAXMAN, MALAGARI, MADDEN AND CEPEDA-FREYTIZ, MARCH 17, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON STATE GOVERNMENT, MARCH 18, 2026 A RESOLUTIONRecognizing March 2, 2026, as "Adwa Victory Day" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, In 1895, Italy annexed a large portion of the Ethiopian province of Tigray through their colonial forces in Eritrea, leading to war with Ethiopia; andWHEREAS, At the start of the campaign, the Italian forces were successful, but the victories were not amounting to grand strategic success and by the end of 1895, large Ethiopian armies were threatening Italian outposts; andWHEREAS, By February 7, 1896, both the Italian and Ethiopian armies were running low on supplies and were waiting for the other to strike; andWHEREAS, Despite misgivings, the Italian army attacked the city of Adwa on March 1, 1896, with a force of 14,500; andWHEREAS, The Ethiopian army of 100,000 routed the Italian forces, forcing them to retreat over difficult terrain; andWHEREAS, The battle resulted in the Treaty of Addis Ababa, signed in October 1896, which required Italy to abandon its 123456789101112131415161718 protectorate claim over Ethiopia and massively reduce its colonial presence in Eritrea; andWHEREAS, In an age of relentless European expansion, Ethiopia successfully defended its independence and cast doubt upon an unshakable certainty of the age, that sooner or later all Africans would fall under the rule of Europeans; andWHEREAS, Ethiopia holds the distinction of being one of the only African nations to remain independent and sovereign, never being colonized, and officially recognized by global powers throughout the 19th and 20th centuries; andWHEREAS, "Adwa Victory Day" is celebrated annually on March 2 in Ethiopia, and 2026 marks the 130th anniversary of the victory at Adwa; andWHEREAS, By recognizing "Adwa Victory Day," the Commonwealth honors the heritage of the more than 6,000 Ethiopians who live in Pennsylvania and the struggle against colonialism of the larger African diaspora community; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives recognize March 2, 2026, as "Adwa Victory Day" in Pennsylvania.20260HR0441PN3015 - 2 - 12345678910111213141516171819
