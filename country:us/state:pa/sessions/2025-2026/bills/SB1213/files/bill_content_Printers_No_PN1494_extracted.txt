2025-2026 Regular Session SB 1213 PN 1494 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 1494 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIASENATE BILL No.1213 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY LAUGHLIN AND VOGEL, MARCH 12, 2026 REFERRED TO GAME AND FISHERIES, MARCH 12, 2026 AN ACTAmending Title 30 (Fish) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes, in general provisions relating to fish and fishing, further providing for rules and regulations.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Section 2102 of Title 30 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is amended by adding a subsection to read:§ 2102. Rules and regulations.* * *(e) Snatch fishing, foul hooking and snag fishing.--Rules and regulations may also be promulgated that involve the methods of taking fish by snatching, foul hooking or snagging. Any person violating any rule or regulation promulgated under this subsection commits a summary offense of the first degree.Section 2. This act shall take effect in 60 days.123456789101112131415
