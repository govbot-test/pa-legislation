Section 1402(a) and (a.1) of Title 8 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes are amended to read:§ 1402. A written record of telephonic price quotations must be made and must contain at least the date of the quotation, the name of the contractor and the contractor's representative, the construction, reconstruction, repair, maintenance or work that was the subject of the quotation and the price. Written price quotations, written records of telephonic price quotations and memoranda must be retained for a period of three years. Written price quotations as used throughout this section include electronic mail.* * *Section 2. A copy of the advertisement for contracts or purchases shall be posted in the city office designated by council.§ 11901.2.
