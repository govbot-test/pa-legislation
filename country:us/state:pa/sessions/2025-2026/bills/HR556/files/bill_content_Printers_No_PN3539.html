A RESOLUTION
Recognizing the month of June 2026 as "National Immigrant
Heritage Month" in Pennsylvania.
WHEREAS, During this month, we honor the countless
contributions immigrants and their descendants have made and
continue to make to our shared communities; and
WHEREAS, Immigrants are a significant part of the United
States population and a crucial part of our nation's story; and
WHEREAS, While 5% of the total world population resides in
the United States, close to 20% of all global migrants reside in
the United States; and
WHEREAS, This Commonwealth was home to 1,031,000 immigrant
individuals in 2023, which made up 8% of this Commonwealth's
total population; and
WHEREAS, Immigrants built our country, heroically serve on
both military and health care frontlines, feed our communities
and spark economic growth and innovation; and
WHEREAS, While immigrants make up approximately 14% of the
United States population, they make up 18% of the labor force;

WHEREAS, Approximately 18% of health care workers, including

WHEREAS, Immigrants have also served in the armed forces of

States was founded; and
WHEREAS, Additionally, nearly three-quarters of our farm

WHEREAS, Hundreds of thousands of immigrants performed the
essential work of packing, stocking and shipping goods
throughout this country during the COVID-19 pandemic; and
WHEREAS, Immigrants own approximately one in five businesses,
and almost half of all Fortune 500 companies were started by
immigrants or their children; and
WHEREAS, Immigrants added $4.6 trillion to the gross domestic
product of the United States and $579.1 billion to Federal,
State and local taxes in 2022; and
WHEREAS, Immigrants represent 16% of inventors in the United
States and have produced 25% of patents, patent citations and
the economic value of the patents; and
WHEREAS, Seven municipalities in this Commonwealth have
recognized the tremendous value that immigrants bring to their
communities by joining Welcoming America's global network of
nonprofit organizations and governments committed to being
welcoming places for all residents, including immigrants; and
WHEREAS, This Commonwealth has a long way to go in
recognizing and encouraging the continued contributions of our
immigrant residents; and
