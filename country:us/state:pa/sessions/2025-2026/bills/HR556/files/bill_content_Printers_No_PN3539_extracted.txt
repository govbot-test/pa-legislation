2025-2026 Regular Session HR 556 PN 3539 Bill Text (HTM) PRINTER'S NO. 3539 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.556 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY BELLMON, CEPEDA-FREYTIZ, VENKAT, SCHLOSSBERG, PROBST, McNEILL, WAXMAN AND GIRAL, JUNE 8, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON STATE GOVERNMENT, JUNE 8, 2026 A RESOLUTIONRecognizing the month of June 2026 as "National Immigrant Heritage Month" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, During this month, we honor the countless contributions immigrants and their descendants have made and continue to make to our shared communities; andWHEREAS, Immigrants are a significant part of the United States population and a crucial part of our nation's story; andWHEREAS, While 5% of the total world population resides in the United States, close to 20% of all global migrants reside in the United States; andWHEREAS, This Commonwealth was home to 1,031,000 immigrant individuals in 2023, which made up 8% of this Commonwealth's total population; andWHEREAS, Immigrants built our country, heroically serve on both military and health care frontlines, feed our communities and spark economic growth and innovation; andWHEREAS, While immigrants make up approximately 14% of the United States population, they make up 18% of the labor force; 123456789101112131415161718 andWHEREAS, Approximately 18% of health care workers, including one in five physicians in the United States, are immigrants; andWHEREAS, Immigrants have also served in the armed forces of the United States during every major conflict since the United States was founded; andWHEREAS, Additionally, nearly three-quarters of our farm workers are immigrants; andWHEREAS, Hundreds of thousands of immigrants performed the essential work of packing, stocking and shipping goods throughout this country during the COVID-19 pandemic; andWHEREAS, Immigrants own approximately one in five businesses, and almost half of all Fortune 500 companies were started by immigrants or their children; andWHEREAS, Immigrants added $4.6 trillion to the gross domestic product of the United States and $579.1 billion to Federal, State and local taxes in 2022; andWHEREAS, Immigrants represent 16% of inventors in the United States and have produced 25% of patents, patent citations and the economic value of the patents; andWHEREAS, Seven municipalities in this Commonwealth have recognized the tremendous value that immigrants bring to their communities by joining Welcoming America's global network of nonprofit organizations and governments committed to being welcoming places for all residents, including immigrants; andWHEREAS, This Commonwealth has a long way to go in recognizing and encouraging the continued contributions of our immigrant residents; andWHEREAS, "National Immigrant Heritage Month" was started to give "people across the United States an opportunity to annually 20260HR0556PN3539 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 explore their own heritage and celebrate the shared diversity that forms the unique story of America"; andWHEREAS, Two Presidents of the United States, 48 states and 257 small towns and metropolitan areas across this country have joined together to honor immigrants during the month of June; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives recognize the month of June 2026 as "National Immigrant Heritage Month" in Pennsylvania;
