2025-2026 Regular Session SR 268 PN 1576 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 1576 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIASENATE RESOLUTION No.268 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY KEARNEY, STREET, FONTANA, TARTAGLIONE, HUGHES, BROWN, HAYWOOD, KANE, VOGEL AND COSTA, APRIL 13, 2026 REFERRED TO RULES AND EXECUTIVE NOMINATIONS, APRIL 13, 2026 A RESOLUTIONRecognizing April 12 through 18, 2026, as "Architecture Week" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, "Architecture Week" is an annual public celebration recognized from April 12 through 18, 2026, and hosted by the American Institute of Architects (AIA) Pennsylvania; andWHEREAS, "Architecture Week" brings greater public awareness and appreciation of architecture and showcases the impact and value of design; andWHEREAS, "Architecture Week" is an opportunity to celebrate the rich architectural history of this Commonwealth and an opportunity to advocate for great design going forward; andWHEREAS, AIA Pennsylvania was chartered in 1941 and represents approximately 3,000 individual members, including architects, associates and allied design professionals from the architecture, engineering and construction industries; andWHEREAS, AIA Pennsylvania has promoted design excellence for more than 85 years, championing policies that benefit and empower Pennsylvania's architects; and123456789101112131415161718 WHEREAS, Architecture reflects the richness of the nation's diverse heritage and the vitality of its spirit; andWHEREAS, Pennsylvania's architects have combined advances in building technology with design innovation to enliven our cities and communities and improve our quality of life; andWHEREAS, Thoughtful architecture creates more valuable, healthy, secure and resilient communities; andWHEREAS, During "Architecture Week," we recognize the architects and design professionals whose creativity and dedication enrich our communities and elevate our quality of life; andWHEREAS, This Commonwealth recognizes architects as a force for positive change and appreciates their contributions within our community; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the Senate recognize April 12 through 18, 2026, as "Architecture Week" in Pennsylvania; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the Senate celebrate the positive, long-term contributions of architects and American Institute of Architects Pennsylvania; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the Senate extend congratulations and best wishes for continued success to AIA Pennsylvania, AIA Philadelphia, AIA Pittsburgh, AIA Bucks County, AIA Central PA, AIA Middle PA, AIA Northeastern PA, AIA Northwestern PA and AIA Eastern PA, and recognize architects for their contributions to improving the health, safety and welfare of the residents of this Commonwealth through the built environment;
