2025-2026 Regular Session SB 1363 PN 1778 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 1778 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIASENATE BILL No.1363 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY SAVAL, PISCIOTTANO, STREET, HAYWOOD, TARTAGLIONE, KIM, KEARNEY, COSTA, VOGEL, KANE AND SCHWANK, JUNE 5, 2026 REFERRED TO CONSUMER PROTECTION AND PROFESSIONAL LICENSURE, JUNE 5, 2026 AN ACTProviding for public utility credit reporting; and imposing duties on public utilities.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Short title.This act shall be known and may be cited as the Public Utility Credit Act.Section 2. Definitions.The following words and phrases when used in this act shall have the meanings given to them in this section unless the context clearly indicates otherwise:"Public utility." Any of the following:(1) A person or corporation owning or operating in this Commonwealth equipment or facilities for:(i) Producing, generating, transmitting, distributing or furnishing natural or artificial gas, electricity or steam for the production of light, heat or 1234567891011121314151617 power to or for the public for compensation.(ii) Diverting, developing, pumping, impounding, distributing or furnishing water to or for the public for compensation.(iii) Transporting passengers or property as a common carrier.(iv) Use as a canal, turnpike, tunnel, bridge, wharf and other transportation facilities for the public for compensation.(v) Transporting or conveying natural or artificial gas, crude oil, gasoline or petroleum products, materials for refrigeration, oxygen or nitrogen or other fluid substance, by pipeline or conduit, for the public for compensation.(vi) Conveying or transmitting messages or communications by telephone or telegraph or domestic public land mobile radio service, including, but not limited to, point-to-point microwave radio service for the public for compensation.(vii) Wastewater collection, treatment or disposal for the public for compensation.(viii) Providing limousine service in a county of the second class under 66 Pa.C.S. Ch. 11 Subch. 5 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 to reports of accidents), as those sections or portions relate to safety only, the term includes a municipal authority or transportation authority organized under the laws of this Commonwealth when it owns or operates, for the carriage of passengers or goods by rail, a line of railroad composed of lines formerly owned or operated by the Pennsylvania Railroad, the Penn-Central Transportation Company, the Reading Company or the Consolidated Rail Corporation.Section 3. Public utility payments reported to credit reporting agencies.(a) Option.--A public utility shall provide customers the opportunity to opt into and, at any time, to opt out of having the public utility payments of the customers reported to a nationwide credit reporting agency.(b) New customers.--A person who becomes a customer of a public utility after the effective date of this subsection shall be notified by the public utility of the opportunity to opt into and, at any time, to opt out of having the customer's payments reported to credit reporting agencies when establishing the customer's public utility account with the public utility.(c) Existing customers.--A person who is a customer of a public utility on the effective date of this subsection shall be notified in writing by the public utility of the opportunity to opt into and, at any time, to opt out of having the customer's payments reported to credit reporting agencies within 45 days of the effective date of this subsection.(d) Time limit to opt in.--(1) New and existing public utility customers may register to opt into and opt out of public utility payment 20260SB1363PN1778 - 6 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 credit reporting at any time after notification of the availability of the option.(2) A public utility may not set a time limit or expiration date on the credit reporting option nor may the option be rescinded at any time in the future.(e) Costs and fees.--A public utility may not pass on costs or charge a fee to a consumer who chooses to opt into or opt out of the credit reporting under this section.Section 4. Effective date.This act shall take effect in 60 days.20260SB1363PN1778 - 7 - 12345678910
