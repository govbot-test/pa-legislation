2025-2026 Regular Session HR 445 PN 3021 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 3021 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.445 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY BENNINGHOFF, ANDERSON, ARMANINI, BOROWSKI, CAUSER, CONKLIN, CUTLER, DEASY, FLEMING, FLICK, GILLEN, GREINER, GUENST, HADDOCK, HAMM, HEFFLEY, KEPHART, KRUPA, MADSEN, MARCELL, MENTZER, MOUL, NEILSON, PICKETT, PUGH, RIVERA, ROWE, SCHEUREN, SHAFFER, STAMBAUGH, STENDER, TWARDZIK, VITALI, WARREN, WATRO AND WENTLING, MARCH 17, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON TOURISM, RECREATION AND ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT, MARCH 18, 2026 A RESOLUTIONDesignating April 8, 2026, as "The Pennsylvania State University IFC/Panhellenic Dance Marathon (THON) Day" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, THON is the commonly used name for The Pennsylvania State University Interfraternity Council/Panhellenic Dance Marathon, the largest student-run philanthropy event in the world; andWHEREAS, THON is a yearlong fundraising and awareness campaign to fight pediatric cancer, sustained by more than 16,500 volunteers who donate more than 5 million hours of their own time, that culminates in a 46-hour dance marathon during which dancers are required to be continuously moving and are not permitted to sit or sleep; andWHEREAS, In 1977, THON partnered with Four Diamonds at Penn State Health Golisano Children's Hospital and selected Four Diamonds as the sole beneficiary of THON's fundraising efforts; 123456789101112131415 andWHEREAS, Four Diamonds offsets costs of treatment not covered by insurance, including medical and emotional support, and supports the medical team that cares for children; andWHEREAS, Four Diamonds funds pediatric cancer research through start-up grants and the Four Diamonds Pediatric Cancer Research Center and provides direct support to children with cancer and their families; andWHEREAS, Since 1977, THON has raised more than $200 million to provide emotional and financial support to children, families, researchers and staff associated with Four Diamonds; andWHEREAS, THON 2026, "Love Leads Forward," revealed a total fundraising amount of $18,841,726.53, breaking THON's yearly record for the fifth consecutive year; andWHEREAS, THON has inspired similar events and organizations across the United States, including at high schools and institutions of higher education, and continues to encourage students throughout the nation to volunteer and remain involved in charitable causes in their communities; andWHEREAS, Since its inception, THON has supported more than 4,000 children and families impacted by childhood cancer and continues to enable research and improve treatments, cures and benefits for children with cancer; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives designate April 8, 2026, as "The Pennsylvania State University IFC/Panhellenic Dance Marathon (THON) Day" in Pennsylvania; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives recognize THON for raising more than $18 million for Four Diamonds at Penn State Health Golisano Children's Hospital to help fight 20260HR0445PN3021 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 pediatric cancer; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives acknowledge The Pennsylvania State University students, volunteers and supporting organizations for their hard work in planning and conducting THON.20260HR0445PN3021 - 3 - 12345
