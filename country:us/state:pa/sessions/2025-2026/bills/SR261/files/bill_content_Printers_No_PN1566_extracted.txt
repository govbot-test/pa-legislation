2025-2026 Regular Session SR 261 PN 1566 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 1566 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIASENATE RESOLUTION No.261 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY PHILLIPS-HILL, HUGHES, MARTIN, CULVER, HAYWOOD, COSTA, J. WARD AND VOGEL, APRIL 6, 2026 REFERRED TO RULES AND EXECUTIVE NOMINATIONS, APRIL 6, 2026 A RESOLUTIONRecognizing April 19, 2026, as "Congenital Diaphragmatic Hernia Awareness Day" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, One in every 2,500 pregnancies are diagnosed with a congenital diaphragmatic hernia (CDH); andWHEREAS, CDH occurs when a baby's diaphragm fails to fully form allowing abdominal organs into the chest cavity and preventing lung growth; andWHEREAS, Since 2000, it is estimated that more than 700,000 babies have been born with CDH, however only 50% of those babies survived; andWHEREAS, CDH is as common as spina bifida and cystic fibrosis, however very few people know or are aware of it; andWHEREAS, There are approximately 1,600 babies born with CDH each year in the United States; andWHEREAS, Many people in this Commonwealth have been diagnosed with and survived CDH, although many families have endured the horrible pain and grief associated with the loss of loved ones with CDH; and123456789101112131415161718 WHEREAS, CDH International is a 501(c)(3) charity organization that was created to help families affected by CDH; andWHEREAS, Those with CDH often endure multiple surgeries and possible medical complications beyond their diagnosis, including heart defects, pulmonary complications, gastric and intestinal problems and developmental delays, and may require respiratory and medicinal support for years; andWHEREAS, Raising awareness of this congenital defect will help bring about acceptance and support for those suffering with CDH and will help advocate for urgently needed medical research and advances; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the Senate recognize April 19, 2026, as "Congenital Diaphragmatic Hernia Awareness Day" in Pennsylvania.20260SR0261PN1566 - 2 - 1234567891011121314
