AN ACT
Amending Title 75 (Vehicles) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated
Statutes, in registration of vehicles, providing for
contributions for the deaf and hard of hearing. AND FOR
CONTRIBUTIONS FOR THE SHRINERS HOSPITALS FOR CHILDREN.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Title 75 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated
Statutes is amended by adding a section SECTIONS to read:
§ 1325. Contributions for the deaf and hard of hearing.
(a) Contributions.--Within one year of the effective date of
this subsection, the department shall provide for all of the
following:
(1) The ability of a person renewing a driver's license
or identification card electronically through the
department's publicly accessible Internet website to make a
contribution of $5 to the office.
(2) The ability of a person renewing a vehicle

(b) Implementation.--
(1) A contribution under subsection (a) shall be added,
as appropriate, to the regular fee for a:
(i) renewal of a driver's license or identification

(ii) renewal of a vehicle registration.
(2) One contribution under subsection (a) may be made

(i) renewal of a driver's license or identification
card; and
(ii) renewal of a vehicle registration.
(c) Use.--Contributions made under subsection (a) shall be
used by the office exclusively to provide communication access
for the deaf and hard of hearing.
(d) Deposits.--The department shall determine annually the
total amount designated under this section and shall report the
amount to the State Treasurer. The State Treasurer shall
transfer the amount from the Motor License Fund to the office.
(e) Information.--The department shall provide adequate
information concerning the contribution under subsection (a) in
its instructions for the applicants described under subsection
(a). The information concerning the contribution shall include
the address of the office to which contributions may be sent by
individuals wishing to make additional contributions.

§ 1326. CONTRIBUTIONS FOR THE SHRINERS HOSPITALS FOR CHILDREN.
(A) ELECTRONIC RENEWAL CONTRIBUTION OPTION.-- WITHIN ONE YEAR
OF THE EFFECTIVE DATE OF THIS SUBSECTION, THE DEPARTMENT SHALL
PROVIDE FOR ALL OF THE FOLLOWING:
(1) ABILITY OF A PERSON RENEWING A DRIVER'S LICENSE OR
IDENTIFICATION CARD ELECTRONICALLY THROUGH THE DEPARTMENT'S
PUBLICLY ACCESSIBLE INTERNET WEBSITE TO MAKE A CONTRIBUTION
OF $5 TO SHRINERS HOSPITALS FOR CHILDREN.

ELECTRONICALLY THROUGH THE DEPARTMENT'S PUBLICLY ACCESSIBLE
INTERNET WEBSITE TO MAKE A CONTRIBUTION OF $5 TO SHRINERS
HOSPITALS FOR CHILDREN.

AS APPROPRIATE, TO THE REGULAR FEE FOR:
