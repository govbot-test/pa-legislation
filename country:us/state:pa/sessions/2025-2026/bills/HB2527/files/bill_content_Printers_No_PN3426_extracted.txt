WILLIAMS AND CEPEDA-FREYTIZ, MAY 20, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON CHILDREN AND YOUTH, MAY 21, 2026 AN ACTAmending the act of March 10, 1949 (P.L.30, No.14), entitled "An act relating to the public school system, including certain provisions applicable as well to private and parochial schools; amending, revising, consolidating and changing the laws relating thereto," in early learning programs, further providing for definitions.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. The definition of "eligible student" in section 1511-D of the act of March 10, 1949 (P.L.30, No.14), known as the Public School Code of 1949, is amended to read:Section 1511-D. Definitions.The following words and phrases when used in this subarticle shall have the meanings given to them in this section unless the context clearly indicates otherwise:* * *"Eligible student." A child who [is at least three years of age and is younger than the entry age of kindergarten in the school district of residence.] meets all of the following 12345678910111213141516171819 requirements:(1) Is at least three years of age and is younger than the entry age of kindergarten in the school district of residence.(2) Resides in a household with an annual household income that is not greater than 400% of the Federal poverty level.* * *Section 2. This act shall take effect in 60 days.20260HB2527PN3426 - 2 - 123456789
