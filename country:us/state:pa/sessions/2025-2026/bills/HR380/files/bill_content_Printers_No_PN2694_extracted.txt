2025-2026 Regular Session HR 380 PN 2694 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 2694 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.380 Session of 2025 INTRODUCED BY WAXMAN, GUENST, GIRAL, VITALI, HOWARD, RIVERA, FREEMAN, SANCHEZ, KHAN, COOPER AND HOHENSTEIN, DECEMBER 10, 2025 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON TOURISM, RECREATION AND ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT, DECEMBER 10, 2025 A RESOLUTIONDesignating November 16, 2025, as "Philadelphia Orchestra Day" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, Since the Philadelphia Orchestra's founding in 1900, the Philadelphia Orchestra has been a global symbol of artistic excellence and innovation; andWHEREAS, The Philadelphia Orchestra is celebrating its 125th anniversary in 2025; andWHEREAS, The Philadelphia Orchestra brings people together across generations in the Marian Anderson Hall at the Kimmel Center for the Performing Arts and in communities and venues around the world; andWHEREAS, From the Philadelphia Orchestra's inception, the group has had visionary musicians and leaders who share groundbreaking musical achievements with this Commonwealth; andWHEREAS, The Philadelphia Orchestra has created memorable performances like the soundtrack for Walt Disney's "Fantasia"; and1234567891011121314151617 WHEREAS, The Philadelphia Orchestra was the first symphonic orchestra to make electrical recordings in 1925, perform its own commercially sponsored radio broadcasts in 1929 and the first to appear on a television broadcast in 1948; andWHEREAS, Throughout its history, the Philadelphia Orchestra has cultivated strong relationships with influential composers who have performed and debuted new works with the orchestra; andWHEREAS, The Philadelphia Orchestra is known globally for its unique sounds defined by iconic strings, known as the "Philadelphia Sound"; andWHEREAS, Under the visionary leadership of Music and Artistic Director Yannick Nézet-Séguin, the orchestra continues to push boundaries and inspire audiences around the world; andWHEREAS, Through innovative programming and education, the Philadelphia Orchestra is creating an expansive and inclusive future for classical music; andWHEREAS, As the United States' 250th anniversary is celebrated, we recognize the orchestra's unique place in American music in the birthplace of American democracy; andWHEREAS, As the Philadelphia Orchestra celebrates 125 years of artistic excellence, cultural diplomacy and community service, we honor its legacy as a preeminent musical institution; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives designate November 16, 2025, as "Philadelphia Orchestra Day" in Pennsylvania.20250HR0380PN2694 - 2 - 1234567891011121314151617181920212223242526
