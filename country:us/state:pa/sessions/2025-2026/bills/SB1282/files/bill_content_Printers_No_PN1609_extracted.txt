2025-2026 Regular Session SB 1282 PN 1609 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 1609 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIASENATE BILL No.1282 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY YAW, HAYWOOD, MILLER, PENNYCUICK, VOGEL AND STEFANO, APRIL 17, 2026 REFERRED TO TRANSPORTATION, APRIL 17, 2026 AN ACTAmending Title 75 (Vehicles) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes, in miscellaneous provisions relating to operation of vehicles, providing for protection of children from second-hand smoke in vehicles; and imposing a penalty.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Title 75 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is amended by adding a section to read:§ 3701.2. Protection of children from second-hand smoke in vehicles.(a) Prohibition.--An individual may not smoke in a vehicle that is transporting a child under 12 years of age.(b) Penalty.--(1) An individual who violates subsection (a) commits a summary offense and shall, upon conviction, be sentenced to pay a fine of $100.(2) An individual who commits a second or subsequent offense after being convicted under paragraph (1) commits a summary offense and shall, upon conviction, be sentenced to 12345678910111213141516171819 pay a fine of $250.(c) Limitation.--An individual may not be convicted of an offense under subsection (a) unless the individual is also convicted of another offense under this title that occurred at the same time.(d) Definition.--As used in this section, the term "smoke" means to carry a lighted cigar, cigarette, pipe or other smoking device.Section 2. This act shall take effect in 90 days.20260SB1282PN1609 - 2 - 123456789
