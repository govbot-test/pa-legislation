2025-2026 Regular Session SR 193 PN 1318 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 1318 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIASENATE RESOLUTION No.193 Session of 2025 INTRODUCED BY LANGERHOLC, COLLETT, TARTAGLIONE, CULVER, HUGHES, L. WILLIAMS, VOGEL AND LAUGHLIN, NOVEMBER 17, 2025 REFERRED TO RULES AND EXECUTIVE NOMINATIONS, NOVEMBER 17, 2025 A RESOLUTIONDesignating the month of November 2025 as "Care at Home Month" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, Home care, home health and hospice professionals provide invaluable, life-enhancing services to individuals with disabilities and to this Commonwealth's rapidly aging population, enabling them to remain safely and comfortably in their own homes while staying connected to their families, friends and communities; andWHEREAS, Home care aides and direct care workers perform vital hands-on assistance with essential activities of daily living, including medication management, meal preparation, mobility support, bathing, dressing, light housekeeping and transportation to medical appointments ensuring dignity, independence and quality of life for those they serve; andWHEREAS, Hospice care combines medical expertise, pain and symptom management and compassionate support providing comfort, dignity and peace to terminally ill individuals and their loved ones during life's most tender moments; and123456789101112131415161718 WHEREAS, Home health professionals offer a broad range of skilled medical services, including nursing care and physical, occupational, speech and respiratory therapies, helping patients recover, regain strength and thrive in the place they call home; andWHEREAS, Home care, home health and hospice teams work hand-in-hand with families, offering valuable education, training and respite, empowering caregivers with the tools and confidence to provide the best possible care for their loved ones; andWHEREAS, These dedicated professionals represent more than 375,000 jobs across this Commonwealth, forming a vital part of Pennsylvania's health care workforce, with the growing demand for care projected to create a need for more than 115,000 additional workers by 2030; andWHEREAS, Pennsylvania's aging population is expanding at an unprecedented rate, with an estimated one in three Pennsylvanians expected to be over 60 years of age by 2030; andWHEREAS, Each month, more than 500,000 residents of this Commonwealth receive compassionate, person-centered home-based care that allows them to live fuller, safer and more independent lives; andWHEREAS, Every individual, whether aging, living with a disability or managing chronic illness deserves the right to choose to live independently and with dignity in the comfort of their own home; and WHEREAS, Despite the extraordinary dedication of these professionals, there remains only one home care worker for every eight Pennsylvanians who wish to receive care in their own homes, underscoring the urgent need to strengthen and support this essential workforce; therefore be it20250SR0193PN1318 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 RESOLVED, That the Senate designate the month of November 2025 as "Care at Home Month" to honor, commend and express its deepest gratitude to the compassionate home care, home health and hospice professionals whose tireless service, skill and empathy enhance the lives of countless Pennsylvanians and their families every day.20250SR0193PN1318 - 3 - 123456
