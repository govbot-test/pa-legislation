2025-2026 Regular Session HR 501 PN 3310 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 3310 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.501 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY CEPEDA-FREYTIZ, GUZMAN, VENKAT, FREEMAN, HILL-EVANS, RIVERA, SANCHEZ, PASHINSKI, FLICK, MALAGARI, D. WILLIAMS AND DEASY, APRIL 28, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON TOURISM, RECREATION AND ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT, APRIL 29, 2026 A RESOLUTIONRecognizing the week of May 3 through 9, 2026, as "National Travel and Tourism Week" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, The week of May 3 through 9, 2026, has been selected as "National Travel and Tourism Week" by the U.S. Travel Association; andWHEREAS, "National Travel and Tourism Week" was first established in 1983, when the United States Congress passed a joint resolution designating a week in May 1984 to honor the importance of travel and tourism; andWHEREAS, The 2026 theme, "Postmarked: Essential," emphasizes that travel is fundamental to the nation's economy, workforce and way of life, with each visitor experience contributing to local growth, opportunity and cultural connection; andWHEREAS, Tourism remains a cornerstone of this Commonwealth's economy, generating approximately $83.9 billion in total economic impact in 2024 and demonstrating continued growth across Pennsylvania's regions; and1234567891011121314151617 WHEREAS, Pennsylvania's travel and tourism industry supports more than 514,000 jobs, representing approximately 1 in every 16 jobs in this Commonwealth, and sustains families, small businesses and local economies; andWHEREAS, In 2024, Pennsylvania welcomed more than 201.6 million visitors, whose spending exceeded $49.9 billion and generated approximately $5 billion in State and local tax revenue, helping to fund critical public services and infrastructure; andWHEREAS, This Commonwealth's diverse attractions, including historic landmarks, outdoor recreation, cultural institutions, major sporting events and vibrant local communities, continue to draw visitors from across the nation and around the world; andWHEREAS, As the United States prepares to celebrate its 250th anniversary in 2026, Pennsylvania, home to the signing of the Declaration of Independence, will play a central role in commemorating this historic milestone and welcoming visitors to experience its rich heritage; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives recognize May 3 through 9, 2026, as "National Travel and Tourism Week" in Pennsylvania.20260HR0501PN3310 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718192021
