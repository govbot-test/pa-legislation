2025-2026 Regular Session HR 366 PN 2574 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 2574 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.366 Session of 2025 INTRODUCED BY MADDEN, SANCHEZ, K.HARRIS, HOHENSTEIN, GUENST, MAYES, HILL-EVANS, SCHLOSSBERG, CEPEDA-FREYTIZ, CURRY, NEILSON, SHUSTERMAN, MENTZER, RIVERA AND GALLAGHER, NOVEMBER 5, 2025 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON AGING AND OLDER ADULT SERVICES, NOVEMBER 6, 2025 A RESOLUTIONDesignating the month of November 2025 as "Care at Home Month" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, Home care and hospice professionals provide critical services to individuals with disabilities and this Commonwealth's aging population, allowing them to remain in their homes and stay connected with their communities; andWHEREAS, Home care aides and direct care workers offer essential support with daily living activities, such as managing medications, meal preparation, assisting with mobility, bathing, dressing, light housekeeping and transportation to medical appointments; andWHEREAS, Hospice care integrates medical care, pain management and emotional and spiritual support, offering compassionate end-of-life and palliative care to terminally ill patients and their families; andWHEREAS, Home health services provide skilled care, including 12345678910111213141516 nursing services and physical, occupational, speech and respiratory therapies; andWHEREAS, Home care, home health and hospice professionals collaborate closely with families, offering guidance and training in caregiving techniques and providing needed respite for family caregivers and natural supports; andWHEREAS, These professionals represent more than 375,000 jobs in this Commonwealth at any given time with a growing demand that may require an additional 115,000 or more workers by 2030; andWHEREAS, This Commonwealth's aging population is growing rapidly, with a projected one in three Pennsylvanians being over 60 years of age by 2030; andWHEREAS, More than 500,000 Pennsylvanians of all ages receive home-based care in any given month; andWHEREAS, Individuals of all ages who are aging or physically or intellectually disabled deserve to live independent and full lives in the setting of their choice; andWHEREAS, There is only one worker for every eight people who want and need to have care inside their own homes; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives designate the month of November 2025 as "Care at Home Month" to honor and express gratitude for the dedication and contributions of these professionals in improving the lives of their patients.20250HR0366PN2574 - 2 - 12345678910111213141516171819202122232425
