Section 1. Martin J. Kupchella Memorial Bridge.
(a) Findings.--The General Assembly finds and declares as
follows:
(1) Martin J. Kupchella was born April 14, 1920, in
Nanty Glo, Cambria County.
(2) Mr. Kupchella was employed as a coal miner before
becoming inducted into the United States Army during World
War II.
(3) During his time with the Army, Mr. Kupchella saw
action in Normandy, Northern France, Rhineland, Ardennes,
Northern Apennines and Central Europe.
(4) On December 17, 1944, near Honengen, Belgium, Mr.
Kupchella volunteered to act as leader on a towed anti-tank
gun while under enemy fire and, when his platoon leader was
wounded, he ran into an open field to free a bogged-down
vehicle so that he could get the platoon leader to safety.
