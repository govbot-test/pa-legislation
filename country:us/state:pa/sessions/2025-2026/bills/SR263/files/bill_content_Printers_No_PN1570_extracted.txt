2025-2026 Regular Session SR 263 PN 1570 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 1570 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIASENATE RESOLUTION No.263 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY SANTARSIERO, HUGHES, PICOZZI, CULVER, PHILLIPS-HILL, BROWN, COMITTA AND HAYWOOD, APRIL 8, 2026 REFERRED TO RULES AND EXECUTIVE NOMINATIONS, APRIL 8, 2026 A RESOLUTIONRecognizing the week of April 19 through 25, 2026, as "National Crime Victims' Rights Week" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, Crime in America has a devastating impact on victims and survivors and tears at the social fabric of society as a whole; andWHEREAS, No victim or survivor should have to endure the trauma of victimization and its physical, financial, emotional, spiritual and social effects alone; andWHEREAS, The community of crime victims and survivors, those who serve them and countless allies work together to ensure that victims' needs are identified and met through quality, collaborative and comprehensive services; andWHEREAS, More than 12,000 system-based and community-based victim services programs across the nation help crime survivors and promote community safety, and more than 32,000 laws define and protect victims' rights; andWHEREAS, "National Crime Victims' Rights Week" was first proclaimed in 1981, and 2026 marks the 45th anniversary of this 123456789101112131415161718 important observance; andWHEREAS, This time-honored tradition unites all who care about crime, victimization and survivors to acknowledge and honor survivors, their challenges and their strengths; andWHEREAS, The 2026 "National Crime Victims' Rights Week" theme, "Listen. Act. Advocate. Protect victims. Serve communities," calls on all of us to support victims and strengthen the systems that serve them; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the Senate recognize the week of April 19 through 25, 2026, as "National Crime Victims' Rights Week" in Pennsylvania; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the Senate reaffirm its commitment to ensuring that crime victims' rights are protected and that services for victims are recognized not only during this week but throughout the year.20260SR0263PN1570 - 2 - 123456789101112131415
