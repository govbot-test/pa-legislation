A RESOLUTION
Recognizing the week of April 19 through 25, 2026, as "National
Crime Victims' Rights Week" in Pennsylvania.
WHEREAS, Crime in America has a devastating impact on victims
and survivors and tears at the social fabric of society as a
whole; and
WHEREAS, No victim or survivor should have to endure the
trauma of victimization and its physical, financial, emotional,
spiritual and social effects alone; and
WHEREAS, The community of crime victims and survivors, those
who serve them and countless allies work together to ensure that
victims' needs are identified and met through quality,
collaborative and comprehensive services; and
WHEREAS, More than 12,000 system-based and community-based
victim services programs across the nation help crime survivors
and promote community safety, and more than 32,000 laws define
and protect victims' rights; and
WHEREAS, "National Crime Victims' Rights Week" was first
proclaimed in 1981, and 2026 marks the 45th anniversary of this

WHEREAS, This time-honored tradition unites all who care

RESOLVED, That the Senate recognize the week of April 19
through 25, 2026, as "National Crime Victims' Rights Week" in
Pennsylvania; and be it further
RESOLVED, That the Senate reaffirm its commitment to ensuring
that crime victims' rights are protected and that services for
victims are recognized not only during this week but throughout
the year.
