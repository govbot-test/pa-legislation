OTHER PROHIBITIONS
Sec.
511. Parking minimums.
§ 511. Parking minimums.
Except as required by the Americans with Disabilities Act of
1990 (Public Law 101-336, 104 Stat. 327), a county, city,
borough, incorporated town, township, home rule municipality,
optional plan municipality or optional charter municipality in
this Commonwealth may not require a minimum number of vehicular
parking spaces per land development or structure, as those terms
are defined in section 107 of the act of July 31, 1968 (P.L.805,
No.247), known as the Pennsylvania Municipalities Planning Code,
regardless of occupancy or use.
Section 4. This act shall take effect in one year.
