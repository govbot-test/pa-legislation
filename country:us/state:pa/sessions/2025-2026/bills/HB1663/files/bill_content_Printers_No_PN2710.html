AN ACT
Amending the act of March 10, 1949 (P.L.30, No.14), entitled "An
act relating to the public school system, including certain
provisions applicable as well to private and parochial
schools; amending, revising, consolidating and changing the
laws relating thereto," establishing the Statewide
Imagination Library CHILDREN'S LITERACY Program and the
Imagination Library CHILDREN'S LITERACY PROGRAM of
Pennsylvania Restricted Account; and imposing duties on the
Department of Education.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. The act of March 10, 1949 (P.L.30, No.14), known
as the Public School Code of 1949, is amended by adding an
article to read:
ARTICLE XXIII-B
STATEWIDE IMAGINATION LIBRARY CHILDREN'S LITERACY PROGRAM
Section 2301-B. Definitions.
The following words and phrases when used in this article
shall have the meanings given to them in this section unless the
context clearly indicates otherwise:

Section 2302-B. Imagination Library CHILDREN'S LITERACY PROGRAM
of Pennsylvania Restricted Account.
(a) Establishment.--The Imagination Library CHILDREN'S
LITERACY PROGRAM of Pennsylvania Restricted Account is
established as a restricted account in the General Fund. Money
in the account is appropriated to the department on a continuing
basis for the purposes of this article.
(b) Funding.--The State Treasurer may accept appropriations,
transfers, gifts, grants, donations, legacies or any other
revenues, including allowable Federal funds, for deposit into
the account.
(c) Allocation.--Money from the account shall be allocated
to qualified local entities that agree to a dollar-for-dollar
match for purposes of the program. The department may waive the

Section 2303-B. Statewide Imagination Library CHILDREN'S
LITERACY Program.
(a) Establishment and purpose.--The Statewide Imagination
Library CHILDREN'S LITERACY Program is established in the
department to promote the development of a comprehensive
Statewide initiative to encourage children from birth to five
years of age ELIGIBLE CHILDREN to develop a love of reading and
learning.
(b) Providing books.--Qualified local entities shall use
allocated money to provide one age-appropriate book to each
registered child from birth to five years of age in
participating counties ELIGIBLE CHILD IN THE QUALIFIED LOCAL
ENTITY'S SERVICE AREA . Books shall be sent monthly to each
child's home at no cost to families. A BOOK PROVIDED UNDER THIS
SUBSECTION MAY NOT CONTAIN DEPICTIONS OF SEXUAL ACTIVITY, CHILD
PORNOGRAPHY, PORNOGRAPHY, NUDITY OR SEXUAL CONDUCT, OR MATERIAL
THAT IS OBSCENE AS DEFINED IN 18 PA.C.S. § 5903(B) (RELATING TO
OBSCENE AND OTHER SEXUAL MATERIALS AND PERFORMANCES).
(c) Matching.--In accordance with section 2302-B(c), the
department, if money is available IN THE ACCOUNT , shall match
the amount required of local programs QUALIFIED LOCAL ENTITIES
participating in the program.

Section 2304-B. Duties of department.
(a) Administration.--The department shall administer the
program and shall coordinate with a nonprofit entity, qualified
under 26 U.S.C. § 501(c)(3) (relating to exemption from tax on
corporations, certain trusts, etc.), and organized solely to
promote and encourage reading by the children of this
Commonwealth, CHILDREN'S LITERACY PROGRAM PARTNER for the
purpose of implementing this article.
(b) Oversight.-- The SUBJECT TO AVAILABLE FUNDS IN THE
ACCOUNT, THE department shall provide oversight to the nonprofit
entity in subsection (a) A CHILDREN'S LITERACY PROGRAM PARTNER
to do all of the following:
(1) Manage the daily operations of the program.
(2) Promote the Statewide development of local Dolly
Parton's Imagination Library programs QUALIFIED LOCAL
ENTITIES .
(3) Advance and strengthen local Dolly Parton's
Imagination Library programs QUALIFIED LOCAL ENTITIES .
(4) Develop community engagement initiatives.
(5) Develop, promote and coordinate a public awareness
campaign to make donors aware of the opportunity to donate to
the affiliate programs and make the public aware of the
opportunity to register eligible children to receive books
through the program.
(6) Administer the local match requirement and
coordinate the collection and remittance of local program
QUALIFIED LOCAL ENTITY costs for books and mailing.
(7) Develop Statewide marketing and communication plans.
(8) Solicit donations, gifts and other funding to
financially support local Dolly Parton's Imagination Library

Section 2305-B. Report.
The department shall prepare and submit to the General
Assembly, no later than January 1, 2026, and each January 1
thereafter, THE JANUARY 1 FOLLOWING THE END OF THE FISCAL YEAR
IN WHICH FUNDS WERE ALLOCATED FROM THE ACCOUNT, a report that
includes the following:
(1) The deposits made to, and expenditures made from,
the account.
(2) Whether the department waived any local match
requirements.
(3) How many local programs QUALIFIED LOCAL ENTITIES
exist, where the programs are located and which entity or
organization serves as the local partner THE NAME OF THE
QUALIFIED LOCAL ENTITY .
(4) How many children are enrolled in a program
REGISTERED BY QUALIFIED LOCAL ENTITIES and how many books
those children have received.
