AN ACT
Amending the act of March 10, 1949 (P.L.30, No.14), entitled "An
act relating to the public school system, including certain
provisions applicable as well to private and parochial
schools; amending, revising, consolidating and changing the
laws relating thereto," establishing the Statewide Children's
Literacy Program and the Children's Literacy Program of
Pennsylvania Restricted Account; and imposing duties on the
Department of Education.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. The act of March 10, 1949 (P.L.30, No.14), known
as the Public School Code of 1949, is amended by adding an
article to read:
ARTICLE XXIII-B
STATEWIDE CHILDREN'S LITERACY PROGRAM

"Account." The Children's Literacy Program of Pennsylvania
Restricted Account established under section 2302-B.
"Children's Literacy Program Partner." A nonprofit entity
qualified under 26 U.S.C. § 501(c)(3) (relating to exemption
from tax on corporations, certain trusts, etc.) and organized
solely to promote and encourage reading by children.

"Eligible child." A child who resides within the
Commonwealth and is under five years of age.

"Qualified local entity." An existing or new Pennsylvania-
based partner of a Children's Literacy Program Partner .
Section 2302-B. Children's Literacy Program of Pennsylvania
Restricted Account.
(a) Establishment.--The Children's Literacy Program of
Pennsylvania Restricted Account is established as a restricted
account in the General Fund. Money in the account is
appropriated to the department on a continuing basis for the
purposes of this article.
(b) Funding.--The State Treasurer may accept appropriations,
transfers, gifts, grants, donations, legacies or any other
revenues, including allowable Federal funds, for deposit into
the account.
(c) Allocation.--Money from the account shall be allocated
to qualified local entities that agree to a dollar-for-dollar
match for purposes of the program AND TO THE DEPARTMENT FOR
OVERSIGHT UNDER SECTION 2304-B(B) . The department may waive the
dollar-for-dollar match requirement for a qualified local entity

Section 2303-B. Statewide Children's Literacy Program.
(a) Establishment and purpose.--The Statewide Children's
Literacy Program is established in the department to promote the
development of a comprehensive Statewide initiative to encourage
eligible children to develop a love of reading and learning.
(b) Providing books.--Qualified local entities shall use
(B) PROVIDING BOOKS.--
(1) QUALIFIED LOCAL ENTITIES SHALL USE allocated money
to provide one age-appropriate book to each registered
eligible child in the qualified local entity's service area .
Books shall be sent monthly to each child's home at no cost
to families. A book provided under this subsection may not
contain depictions of sexual activity, child pornography,
pornography, nudity or sexual conduct, or material that is
obscene as defined in 18 Pa.C.S. § 5903(b) (relating to
obscene and other sexual materials and performances).
(2) WHEN AN ELIGIBLE CHILD IS REGISTERED, THE QUALIFIED
LOCAL ENTITY SHALL PROVIDE THE CHILD'S FAMILY WITH
INFORMATION ABOUT HOW TO OBTAIN A LIBRARY CARD FROM THE LOCAL
LIBRARY.

department, if money is available in the account , shall match

Section 2304-B. Duties of department.
(a) Administration.--The department shall administer the
program and shall coordinate with a Children's Literacy Program
Partner for the purpose of implementing this article.
(b) Oversight.-- Subject to available funds in the account,
the department shall provide oversight to a Children's Literacy
Program Partner to do all of the following:
(1) Manage the daily operations of the program.
(2) Promote the Statewide development of qualified local
entities .
(3) Advance and strengthen qualified local entities .
(4) Develop community engagement initiatives.
(5) Develop, promote and coordinate a public awareness
campaign to make donors aware of the opportunity to donate to
the affiliate programs and make the public aware of the
opportunity to register eligible children to receive books
through the program.
(6) Administer the local match requirement and
coordinate the collection and remittance of qualified local
entity costs for books and mailing.
(7) Develop Statewide marketing and communication plans.
(8) Solicit donations, gifts and other funding to
financially support qualified local entities .
(c) Guidelines .--The department may issue guidelines as
needed for the administration of the program.
