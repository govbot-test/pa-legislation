2025-2026 Regular Session HB 1663 PN 2720 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRIOR PRINTER'S NOS. 2031, 2710PRINTER'S NO. 2720THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE BILL No.1663 Session of 2025 INTRODUCED BY HANBIDGE, KHAN, HOWARD, SANCHEZ, HILL-EVANS, FREEMAN, FRANKEL, OTTEN, WEBSTER, CERRATO, GREEN, O'MARA, DAVIDSON, POWELL, SALISBURY, K.HARRIS AND MADSEN, JUNE 25, 2025 AS RE-REPORTED FROM COMMITTEE ON APPROPRIATIONS, HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, AS AMENDED, DECEMBER 17, 2025AN ACTAmending the act of March 10, 1949 (P.L.30, No.14), entitled "An act relating to the public school system, including certain provisions applicable as well to private and parochial schools; amending, revising, consolidating and changing the laws relating thereto," establishing the Statewide Children's Literacy Program and the Children's Literacy Program of Pennsylvania Restricted Account; and imposing duties on the Department of Education.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. The act of March 10, 1949 (P.L.30, No.14), known as the Public School Code of 1949, is amended by adding an article to read:ARTICLE XXIII-BSTATEWIDE CHILDREN'S LITERACY PROGRAMSection 2301-B. Definitions.The following words and phrases when used in this article shall have the meanings given to them in this section unless the context clearly indicates otherwise:12345678910111213141516171819 "Account." The Children's Literacy Program of Pennsylvania Restricted Account established under section 2302-B."Children's Literacy Program Partner." A nonprofit entity qualified under 26 U.S.C. § 501(c)(3) (relating to exemption from tax on corporations, certain trusts, etc.) and organized solely to promote and encourage reading by children."Department." The Department of Education of the Commonwealth."Eligible child." A child who resides within the Commonwealth and is under five years of age."Program." The Statewide Children's Literacy Program established under section 2303-B."Qualified local entity." An existing or new Pennsylvania- based partner of a Children's Literacy Program Partner . Section 2302-B. Children's Literacy Program of Pennsylvania Restricted Account.(a) Establishment.--The Children's Literacy Program of Pennsylvania Restricted Account is established as a restricted account in the General Fund. Money in the account is appropriated to the department on a continuing basis for the purposes of this article.(b) Funding.--The State Treasurer may accept appropriations, transfers, gifts, grants, donations, legacies or any other revenues, including allowable Federal funds, for deposit into the account.(c) Allocation.--Money from the account shall be allocated to qualified local entities that agree to a dollar-for-dollar match for purposes of the program AND TO THE DEPARTMENT FOR OVERSIGHT UNDER SECTION 2304-B(B) . The department may waive the dollar-for-dollar match requirement for a qualified local entity 20250HB1663PN2720 - 2 - <--123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 on a case-by-case basis to prevent undue financial hardship.(d) Money shall not lapse.--Money remaining in the account at the close of the fiscal year shall not lapse but shall be carried forward to the next fiscal year and be continuously appropriated only for the purposes specified in this article.(e) Interest.--Any interest that accrues in the account shall remain in the account.Section 2303-B. Statewide Children's Literacy Program. (a) Establishment and purpose.--The Statewide Children's Literacy Program is established in the department to promote the development of a comprehensive Statewide initiative to encourage eligible children to develop a love of reading and learning. (b) Providing books.--Qualified local entities shall use(B) PROVIDING BOOKS.--(1) QUALIFIED LOCAL ENTITIES SHALL USE allocated money to provide one age-appropriate book to each registered eligible child in the qualified local entity's service area . Books shall be sent monthly to each child's home at no cost to families. A book provided under this subsection may not contain depictions of sexual activity, child pornography, pornography, nudity or sexual conduct, or material that is obscene as defined in 18 Pa.C.S. § 5903(b) (relating to obscene and other sexual materials and performances).(2) WHEN AN ELIGIBLE CHILD IS REGISTERED, THE QUALIFIED LOCAL ENTITY SHALL PROVIDE THE CHILD'S FAMILY WITH INFORMATION ABOUT HOW TO OBTAIN A LIBRARY CARD FROM THE LOCAL LIBRARY.(c) Matching.--In accordance with section 2302-B(c), the department, if money is available in the account , shall match the amount required of qualified local entities participating in 20250HB1663PN2720 - 3 - <--<--<--123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 the program.Section 2304-B. Duties of department.(a) Administration.--The department shall administer the program and shall coordinate with a Children's Literacy Program Partner for the purpose of implementing this article. (b) Oversight.-- Subject to available funds in the account, the department shall provide oversight to a Children's Literacy Program Partner to do all of the following: (1) Manage the daily operations of the program.(2) Promote the Statewide development of qualified local entities . (3) Advance and strengthen qualified local entities . (4) Develop community engagement initiatives.(5) Develop, promote and coordinate a public awareness campaign to make donors aware of the opportunity to donate to the affiliate programs and make the public aware of the opportunity to register eligible children to receive books through the program.(6) Administer the local match requirement and coordinate the collection and remittance of qualified local entity costs for books and mailing. (7) Develop Statewide marketing and communication plans.(8) Solicit donations, gifts and other funding to financially support qualified local entities . (c) Guidelines .--The department may issue guidelines as needed for the administration of the program.Section 2305-B. Report.The department shall prepare and submit to the General Assembly, no later than the January 1 following the end of the fiscal year in which funds were allocated from the account, a 20250HB1663PN2720 - 4 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 report that includes the following:(1) The deposits made to, and expenditures made from, the account.(2) Whether the department waived any local match requirements.(3) How many qualified local entities exist, where the programs are located and the name of the qualified local entity . (4) How many children are registered by qualified local entities and how many books those children have received. Section 2. This act shall take effect in 60 days.20250HB1663PN2720 - 5 - 1234567891011
