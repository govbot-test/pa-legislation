A RESOLUTION
Designating December 17, 2025, as "Sustainable Forestry
Initiative Day" in Pennsylvania to commend the Sustainable
Forestry Initiative and the Pennsylvania SFI Implementation
Committee for its 30 years of leadership, commitment and
impact on the sustainability of this Commonwealth's forests
and forest-based communities.
WHEREAS, This Commonwealth is blessed with nearly 17 million
acres of forestland that provide ecological, economic and
recreational benefits to the citizens of this Commonwealth and
contribute globally through the supply of sustainably sourced
wood products, clean water and wildlife habitats; and
WHEREAS, Pennsylvania's forest products industry remains one
of the Commonwealth's largest manufacturing sectors, employing
tens of thousands of workers and supporting rural economies
across this Commonwealth; and
WHEREAS, In response to growing concerns in the early 1990s
about forest sustainability and industry practices, the forest
products industry launched the Sustainable Forestry Initiative
(SFI) in 1995 to promote responsible environmental stewardship

WHEREAS, The same year, the Pennsylvania SFI Implementation

WHEREAS, Over the past 30 years, the PA SIC has delivered
innovative logger training, landowner outreach and public

best management practices and supported sustainable timber
harvesting across Pennsylvania; and
WHEREAS, The PA SIC has trained more than 8,000 loggers,
foresters and landowners in its Professional Timber Harvester
Training Program and has developed, compiled and distributed
countless resources encouraging responsible forestry on private
lands; and
WHEREAS, The Pennsylvania SFI Implementation Committee
continues to engage with educators, policymakers,
conservationists and industry leaders to build consensus and
foster collaborative forest stewardship that benefits both
people and the environment; therefore be it
