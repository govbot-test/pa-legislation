2025-2026 Regular Session HR 378 PN 3186 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRIOR PRINTER'S NO. 2696PRINTER'S NO. 3186THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.378 Session of 2025 INTRODUCED BY CAUSER, PASHINSKI, PICKETT, RAPP, STENDER, NEILSON, SMITH, ARMANINI, GILLEN AND ZIMMERMAN, DECEMBER 10, 2025 AS REPORTED FROM COMMITTEE ON AGRICULTURE AND RURAL AFFAIRS, HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, AS AMENDED, APRIL 14, 2026A RESOLUTIONDesignating December 17, 2025 2026, as "Sustainable Forestry Initiative Day" in Pennsylvania to commend the Sustainable Forestry Initiative and the Pennsylvania SFI Implementation Committee for its OVER 30 years of leadership, commitment and impact on the sustainability of this Commonwealth's forests and forest-based communities.WHEREAS, This Commonwealth is blessed with nearly 17 million acres of forestland that provide ecological, economic and recreational benefits to the citizens of this Commonwealth and contribute globally through the supply of sustainably sourced wood products, clean water and wildlife habitats; andWHEREAS, Pennsylvania's forest products industry remains one of the Commonwealth's largest manufacturing sectors, employing tens of thousands of workers and supporting rural economies across this Commonwealth; andWHEREAS, In response to growing concerns in the early 1990s about forest sustainability and industry practices, the forest products industry launched the Sustainable Forestry Initiative (SFI) in 1995 to promote responsible environmental stewardship <--<--12345678910111213141516171819 through a commitment to sustainable forestry practices; andWHEREAS, The same year, the Pennsylvania SFI Implementation Committee (PA SIC) was formed to ensure the successful and locally adapted implementation of SFI standards and principles across this Commonwealth, making Pennsylvania one of the original State-level leaders in the program; andWHEREAS, Over the past 30 years, the PA SIC has delivered innovative logger training, landowner outreach and public education programs that have improved forest health, advanced best management practices and supported sustainable timber harvesting across Pennsylvania; andWHEREAS, The PA SIC has trained more than 8,000 loggers, foresters and landowners in its Professional Timber Harvester Training Program and has developed, compiled and distributed countless resources encouraging responsible forestry on private lands; andWHEREAS, The Pennsylvania SFI Implementation Committee continues to engage with educators, policymakers, conservationists and industry leaders to build consensus and foster collaborative forest stewardship that benefits both people and the environment; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives designate December 17, 2025 2026, as "Sustainable Forestry Initiative Day" in Pennsylvania; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives commend the Sustainable Forestry Initiative and the Pennsylvania SFI Implementation Committee for its OVER 30 years of leadership, commitment and impact on the sustainability of this Commonwealth's forests and forest-based communities;
