A RESOLUTION
Designating the month of April 2026 as "Donate Life Month" and
April 10, 2026, as "Blue and Green Day" in Pennsylvania in
support of organ donation awareness.
WHEREAS, Pennsylvania strongly supports organ and tissue
donation because of its lifesaving and life-enhancing
opportunities; and
WHEREAS, Pennsylvania saw strong donation activity in 2024,
with nearly 2,300 transplants, and by mid-2025, more than 7,000
residents were on the transplant waitlist, showing ongoing need;
and
WHEREAS, Nearly half of all Pennsylvanians have demonstrated
their support of donation by registering as organ, tissue and
cornea donors; and
WHEREAS, One organ and tissue donor may save up to eight
lives, restore sight to two people and heal the lives of 75
others; and
WHEREAS, People of all ages are considered potential organ
