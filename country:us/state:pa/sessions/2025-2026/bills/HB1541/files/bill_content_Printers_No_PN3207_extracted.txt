BROWN, HILL-EVANS, SANCHEZ, WEBSTER, OLSOMMER, GALLAGHER, GUENST, WARREN, OTTEN, KENYATTA, KERWIN AND DAVIDSON, JUNE 3, 2025 AS AMENDED ON THIRD CONSIDERATION, HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, APRIL 15, 2026AN ACTAmending the act of July 9, 1970 (P.L.484, No.164), entitled "An act relating to indemnification agreements between architects, engineers or surveyors and owners, contractors, subcontractors or suppliers and indemnification agreements relating to snow removal or ice control services," providing for void and unenforceable provisions or terms in construction contracts; and making editorial changes.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. The title of the act of July 9, 1970 (P.L.484, No.164), entitled "An act relating to indemnification agreements between architects, engineers or surveyors and owners, contractors, subcontractors or suppliers and indemnification agreements relating to snow removal or ice control services," is amended to read:AN ACTRelating to indemnification agreements between architects, engineers or surveyors and owners, contractors, subcontractors or suppliers [and], indemnification agreements 12345678910111213141516171819 relating to snow removal or ice control services[.] and indemnification agreements relating to construction contracts.Section 2. The act is amended by adding a section to read:Section 1.2. The following apply to provisions or terms in a construction contract which are void and unenforceable CONSTRUCTION CONTRACTS : (1) A provision or term in a construction contract in which an indemnitee shall be indemnified, held harmless or insured for damages, claims, losses or expenses arising out of bodily injury to persons, damage to property or economic damage caused by or resulting from the indemnitee's negligence, in whole or in part, shall be void as against public policy and unenforceable.(2) As used in this section:(i) The term "construction contract" means a covenant, promise, agreement or understanding in, or in connection with or collateral to, a nonresidential contract, agreement or purchase order, relative to the design, observation, inspection, planning, surveying, construction, alteration, repair, maintenance, servicing or security of a building, structure, road, highway, railroad, appurtenance or appliance, including moving, demolition, excavating, grading, clearing, site preparation or development of real property connected therewith, purporting to indemnify or hold harmless the indemnitee against liability for damages arising out of bodily injury to persons , damage to property or economic damages caused by or resulting from the negligence, in whole or in part, of the indemnitee.(ii) The term "indemnitee" means an owner, general contractor or agent or employe of an owner or general contractor that presents a provision or term in a construction contract for 20250HB1541PN3207 - 2 - <--<--123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 indemnification.Section 3.
