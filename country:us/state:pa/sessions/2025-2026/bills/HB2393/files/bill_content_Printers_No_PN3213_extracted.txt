2025-2026 Regular Session HB 2393 PN 3213 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 3213 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE BILL No.2393 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY KAUFFMAN, IRVIN AND REICHARD, APRIL 15, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON INSURANCE, APRIL 16, 2026 AN ACTAmending the act of September 30, 1983 (P.L.160, No.39), entitled "An act establishing salaries and compensation of certain public officials including justices and judges of Statewide courts, judges of courts of common pleas, judges of the Philadelphia Municipal Court, judges of the Philadelphia Traffic Court, district justices and the Governor, the Lieutenant Governor, the State Treasurer, the Auditor General, the Attorney General and certain other State officers and the salary and certain expenses of the members of the General Assembly; and repealing certain inconsistent acts," further providing for the compensation of members of the General Assembly.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Section 4 of the act of September 30, 1983 (P.L.160, No.39), known as the Public Official Compensation Law, is amended by adding a subsection to read:Section 4. Members of the General Assembly.* * *(e) Health insurance benefits.--(1) A member of the House of Representatives is eligible to receive health insurance benefits only for such time as the member remains in office and, if the member is reelected 1234567891011121314151617181920212223 to the office of Representative in the General Assembly, during the interim between legislative sessions.(2) As used in this subsection, the term "health insurance benefits" means medical, dental, vision, prescription drug and long-term care insurance coverage, the cost of which is paid by the Commonwealth on behalf of a member of the House of Representatives or any spouse or other qualified adult or dependent of the member.Section 2. The addition of section 4(e) of the act shall apply to all members of the House of Representatives identified under section 4(a) and (b)(2) of the act who are elected to their respective offices after the effective date of this section.Section 3. This act shall take effect in 60 days.20260HB2393PN3213 - 2 - 1234567891011121314
