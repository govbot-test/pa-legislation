2025-2026 Regular Session SR 281 PN 1618 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 1618 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIASENATE RESOLUTION No.281 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY DUSH, ROTHMAN, MASTRIANO, ROBINSON, VOGEL, PHILLIPS-HILL, MARTIN, J. WARD, PENNYCUICK AND BAKER, APRIL 17, 2026 REFERRED TO RULES AND EXECUTIVE NOMINATIONS, APRIL 17, 2026 A RESOLUTIONCommemorating the anniversary of Operation El Dorado Canyon.WHEREAS, On April 14, 1986, the United States launched Operation El Dorado Canyon; andWHEREAS, Operation El Dorado Canyon was a retaliatory United States military mission aimed at terrorist operations led by then Libyan President Muammar Gaddafi; andWHEREAS, Operation El Dorado was carried out in direct response to the bombing of the La Belle Discotheque in West Berlin, a violent attack which ultimately killed two American soldiers and injured at least 230 individuals, which included more than 50 American service members; andWHEREAS, The mission was planned primarily by the United States Air Force's 48th Tactical Fighter Wing based in England; andWHEREAS, Twenty-four United States Air Force F-111s departed Royal Air Force (RAF) Lakenheath along with five EF-111s from RAF Upper Heyford; and1234567891011121314151617 WHEREAS, The F-111s faced a 3,500-mile flight with four aerial refuelings each way due to flight restrictions; andWHEREAS, As the aircraft approached Libya, two United States Navy aircraft carriers, the USS Coral Sea and the USS America, launched 14 A-6E strike aircraft and 12 F/A-18 and A-7 strike support aircraft; andWHEREAS, In close coordination with the United States Navy, aircraft launched from the carriers effectively suppressed Libyan air defenses and electronic countermeasures began shortly before the attacks, neutralizing Libyan radar and missile systems; andWHEREAS, After Libya's air defenses were rendered useless, Navy aircraft struck targets near Benghazi, while Air Force F-111s attacked strategic sites in Tripoli, including military barracks, a terrorist training camp and a military airport; andWHEREAS, Tragically, one F-111 was shot down, resulting in the deaths of both crew members; andWHEREAS, Search and rescue efforts continued for over a day but ultimately failed to recover the pilots; andWHEREAS, Overall, the United States military recognized Operation El Dorado Canyon as a successful operation and one which demonstrated the Air Force's ability to conduct very precise long-range bombing strikes; andWHEREAS, The success of this operation and its show of force resulted in ending international terrorism based in Libya for decades and forced Colonel Muammar Gaddafi to cease his support of international terrorism; andWHEREAS, In the long term, the operation had lasting significance for United States military strategy, exhibiting this country's ability to achieve global reach on the ever-20260SR0281PN1618 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 changing battlefield; andWHEREAS, Equally as important, the lessons learned from technical and operational shortcomings were later applied successfully, particularly during Operation Desert Storm in 1991, where improved F-111 targeting systems proved highly effective; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the Senate commemorate the anniversary of Operation El Dorado Canyon.20260SR0281PN1618 - 3 - 12345678
