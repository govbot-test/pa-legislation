Sections 2.1(i)(1), (3)(e)(1) and 4(d)(1) and (d.1)(1) of the act of September 30, 1983 (P.L.160, No.39), known as the Public Official Compensation Law, are amended to read:Section 2.1. The percentage increase and the salary amounts shall be determined by the Governor prior to the annual effective date of the adjustment and shall be published by the Secretary of the Budget in the Pennsylvania Bulletin within ten days of the date the determination is made.* * *Section 4.
