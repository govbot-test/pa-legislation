2025-2026 Regular Session HR 469 PN 3150 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 3150 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.469 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY CEPHAS, CURRY, MAYES, POWELL, HILL-EVANS, GUZMAN, PROBST, MADDEN AND NEILSON, APRIL 7, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON STATE GOVERNMENT, APRIL 8, 2026 A RESOLUTIONDesignating April 28, 2026, as "Delta Day" in Pennsylvania in honor of the members of the Delta Sigma Theta Sorority for more than a century of commitment to social activism, academic excellence and civic engagement.WHEREAS, Delta Sigma Theta Sorority, Inc., was founded in 1913 by 22 collegiate women on the campus of Howard University in Washington, DC; andWHEREAS, Delta Sigma Theta Sorority, Inc., has since grown into one of the preeminent service-based sororities, with more than 350,000 predominantly African American initiated members; andWHEREAS, Delta Sigma Theta Sorority, Inc., boasts 31 collegiate and alumnae chapters within this Commonwealth, as well as more than 1,050 chartered chapters worldwide; andWHEREAS, For more than a century, the sorority has been guided by its mission of the constructive development of its members and public service, with a primary focus on the African American community; andWHEREAS, Delta Sigma Theta Sorority, Inc., fulfills its 12345678910111213141516171819 mission through an extensive array of public service initiatives aligned with its Five-Point Programmatic Thrust: educational development, economic development, international awareness and involvement, physical and mental health and political awareness and involvement; andWHEREAS, Over the past 113 years, members of Delta Sigma Theta Sorority, Inc., have led countless initiatives addressing critical issues, including education, health, international development and the strengthening of African-American families; andWHEREAS, Delta Sigma Theta Sorority, Inc.'s theme, "One Delta. One Mission: Anchored in Legacy, Evolving with Intention." reflects the sorority's enduring values and guiding principles; andWHEREAS, Since 1989, members of Delta Sigma Theta Sorority, Inc., have participated in "Delta Days" at the Nation's Capital, the United Nations, state capitols, town halls and school boards across the country to increase their involvement in the advocacy process; andWHEREAS, April 28, 2026, marks Delta Sigma Theta Sorority, Inc.'s 23rd Annual "Delta Day" at the State Capitol in Harrisburg, where members from across this Commonwealth convene to engage with legislators, advocate for key issues and serve as a voice for their communities; andWHEREAS, The women of Delta Sigma Theta Sorority, Inc., continue to exemplify leadership through an unwavering commitment to their communities, serving as the driving force behind the mission of "Delta Day" at the State Capitol; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives designate April 20260HR0469PN3150 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 28, 2026, as "Delta Day" in Pennsylvania in honor of the members of the Delta Sigma Theta Sorority for more than a century of commitment to social activism, academic excellence and civic engagement.20260HR0469PN3150 - 3 - 1234
