2025-2026 Regular Session HR 369 PN 2588 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 2588 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.369 Session of 2025 INTRODUCED BY HADDOCK, HARKINS, CONKLIN, GUENST, PROBST, KRUPA, SANCHEZ, HILL-EVANS, McNEILL, BRENNAN, BOROWSKI, COOK, SCHLOSSBERG, NEILSON, MENTZER, GILLEN, GALLAGHER, ANDERSON, SCHWEYER, MARCELL, HOHENSTEIN, GREINER, FREEMAN, VITALI, PUGH, CEPEDA-FREYTIZ AND COOPER, NOVEMBER 10, 2025 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON VETERANS AFFAIRS AND EMERGENCY PREPAREDNESS, NOVEMBER 12, 2025 A RESOLUTIONDesignating December 13, 2025, as "Wreaths Across America Day" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, Having been inspired by a trip to Arlington National Cemetery in his youth, Morrill Worcester donated 5,000 surplus wreaths from his Worcester Wreath Company in Harrington, Maine, to a sparsely visited section of Arlington National Cemetery in December 1992; andWHEREAS, In a community effort, volunteers from the American Legion and Veterans of Foreign Wars posts and the local community tied red bows onto each wreath, and the local trucking company, Blue Bird Ranch, Inc., transported the wreaths to Virginia; andWHEREAS, This initiative became a tradition that continued for more than 10 years when, in 2005, photos of the wreaths circulated and gained national attention, leading to thousands of inquiries to purchase and lay wreaths at Arlington National 12345678910111213141516 Cemetery and thousands more hoping to emulate the wreath-layingat local sites; andWHEREAS, Worcester began sending seven wreaths to each state, one for each branch of the military and an additional wreath to honor prisoners of war and those missing in action; andWHEREAS, With the help of the Civil Air Patrol and other civic organizations, simultaneous wreath-laying ceremonies were held at more than 150 locations in December 2006; andWHEREAS, The Patriot Guard Riders volunteered to escort the convoy of wreaths south along the East Coast to Arlington National Cemetery, now an annual tradition; andWHEREAS, The Worcester family and countless volunteers formed Wreaths Across America, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, in 2007 with the mission to remember the fallen, honor those who serve and teach the next generation the value of freedom; andWHEREAS, During the December 2014 holiday season, Wreaths Across America met its goal to place wreaths at all 226,525 graves at Arlington National Cemetery; andWHEREAS, A veteran's wreath consists of 10 balsam bouquets, representing 10 special qualities that veterans embody, including:(1) a belief in a greater good;(2) love for one another;(3) strength, work ethic and character;(4) honesty and integrity;(5) humility, selflessness and modesty;(6) ambitions and aspirations;(7) optimism for America;(8) concern for the future;(9) pride in duty; and20250HR0369PN2588 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 (10) hopes and dreams that did not always come true but left them with no regrets;andWHEREAS, The evergreen branches of a veteran's wreath symbolize longevity and endurance, and its forest scent represents purity and simplicity; andWHEREAS, The wreath's circular shape symbolizes eternity, and the red bow symbolizes great sacrifice; andWHEREAS, More than 2 million volunteers are expected to participate at more than 5,200 locations across the United States and abroad; andWHEREAS, The theme for Wreaths Across America Day 2025 is "Keep Moving Forward," inspired by the last words of Capt. Joshua Byers, United States Army, who was killed in action in Iraq in 2003; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives designate December 13, 2025, as "Wreaths Across America Day" in Pennsylvania; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives encourage the residents of this Commonwealth to remember, honor and teach the stories of the brave individuals who wore the uniform to protect and defend the United States of America.20250HR0369PN2588 - 3 - 12345678910111213141516171819202122
