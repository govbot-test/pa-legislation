HARRIS, NEILSON, N. NELSON, PARKER, PIELLI, REICHARD, PROBST, MAYES, KHAN AND MEHAFFIE, MARCH 5, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON APPROPRIATIONS, MARCH 5, 2026 A RESOLUTIONDesignating March 25, 2026, as "Prince Hall Mason Day at the Capitol" in Pennsylvania in recognition of The Most Worshipful Prince Hall Grand Lodge of Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, The House of Representatives applauds The Most Worshipful Prince Hall Grand Lodge of Pennsylvania for its endeavors that have served the welfare of the residents of this Commonwealth on issues vital to the history and future of the Commonwealth; andWHEREAS, As we celebrate the 250th anniversary of the United States of America, it is important to recognize the history and tradition of Prince Hall Freemasonry dating back to 1775, when Prince Hall and 14 other free Black men began their journey to become masons, eventually forming African Lodge No. 459, the first African-American Lodge; andWHEREAS, The first warranted Lodge of Free and Accepted Masons among men of color in Pennsylvania was established by the Right Worshipful Grand Master Prince Hall of the African Grand Lodge of Massachusetts on September 22, 1797, in Philadelphia, 123456789101112131415161718 known as African Lodge No. 459 of Philadelphia; andWHEREAS, Mother Bethel AME Church is deeply intertwined with Prince Hall Masonry, as its founder, Richard Allen, was one of the first officers of the African Lodge in Philadelphia, having been installed by Prince Hall himself in 1797; andWHEREAS, On December 27, 1815, the brethren erected the "First African Independent Grand Lodge of Free and Accepted Masons, for and in the Jurisdiction of North America," electing Rev. Absalom Jones as its first Right Worshipful Grand Master; andWHEREAS, Richard Allen, who founded the African Methodist Episcopal Church, and Rev. Absalom Jones, the first African American ordained as an Episcopal priest, were both integral in establishing the Free African Society and other independent Black institutions that provided a blueprint for Black self-determination during the Revolutionary era; andWHEREAS, Over the past 200 years, the legacy of the Prince Hall Grand Lodge of Pennsylvania is cemented by the profound contributions of its members to the American story, including figures such as Octavius V. Catto, a martyr for voting rights and education, the Honorable Raymond Pace Alexander, a legal titan and the first African-American judge on the Pennsylvania Courts of Common Pleas, and countless other jurists, educators, veterans and civic leaders whose names are etched in the bedrock of Pennsylvania's progress; andWHEREAS, Prince Hall Free Masons have served the Commonwealth with lodges in communities spanning from Erie to Pittsburgh, Harrisburg to Coatesville and Philadelphia and Chester; andWHEREAS, The House of Representatives is moved to honor the Prince Hall Masons of Pennsylvania, who have labored 20260HR0424PN2964 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 conscientiously to improve the quality of life for the communities they serve; andWHEREAS, Under the leadership of the Most Worshipful Grand Master Timothy J. Cager, the Most Worshipful Prince Hall Grand Lodge of Pennsylvania continues to perform amazing charitable works and serves as a source of unity, peace, hope and love; andWHEREAS, The Most Worshipful Prince Hall Grand Lodge of Pennsylvania is currently comprised of approximately 4,000 members across the Commonwealth, in addition to its female auxiliary, Deborah Grand Chapter, Order of the Eastern Star, Inc., Prince Hall Adopted, led by Grand Worthy Matron Kelly J. Sheppard; andWHEREAS, Lodges throughout this Commonwealth continue to flourish through their members' commitment to education, community service and philanthropy; andWHEREAS, The House of Representatives is proud to welcome Prince Hall Masons from across this Commonwealth to the State Capitol; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives designate March 25, 2026, as "Prince Hall Mason Day at the Capitol" in Pennsylvania in recognition of The Most Worshipful Prince Hall Grand Lodge of Pennsylvania.20260HR0424PN2964 - 3 - 12345678910111213141516171819202122
