REICHARD, PROBST, MAYES, KHAN AND MEHAFFIE, MARCH 5, 2026
REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON APPROPRIATIONS, MARCH 5, 2026
A RESOLUTION
Designating March 25, 2026, as "Prince Hall Mason Day at the
Capitol" in Pennsylvania in recognition of The Most
Worshipful Prince Hall Grand Lodge of Pennsylvania.
WHEREAS, The House of Representatives applauds The Most
Worshipful Prince Hall Grand Lodge of Pennsylvania for its
endeavors that have served the welfare of the residents of this
Commonwealth on issues vital to the history and future of the
Commonwealth; and
WHEREAS, As we celebrate the 250th anniversary of the United
States of America, it is important to recognize the history and
tradition of Prince Hall Freemasonry dating back to 1775, when
Prince Hall and 14 other free Black men began their journey to
become masons, eventually forming African Lodge No. 459, the
first African-American Lodge; and
WHEREAS, The first warranted Lodge of Free and Accepted
Masons among men of color in Pennsylvania was established by the
Right Worshipful Grand Master Prince Hall of the African Grand
Lodge of Massachusetts on September 22, 1797, in Philadelphia,

WHEREAS, Mother Bethel AME Church is deeply intertwined with
Prince Hall Masonry, as its founder, Richard Allen, was one of
the first officers of the African Lodge in Philadelphia, having
been installed by Prince Hall himself in 1797; and
WHEREAS, On December 27, 1815, the brethren erected the
"First African Independent Grand Lodge of Free and Accepted
Masons, for and in the Jurisdiction of North America," electing
Rev. Absalom Jones as its first Right Worshipful Grand Master;
and
WHEREAS, Richard Allen, who founded the African Methodist
Episcopal Church, and Rev. Absalom Jones, the first African
American ordained as an Episcopal priest, were both integral in
establishing the Free African Society and other independent
Black institutions that provided a blueprint for Black self-
determination during the Revolutionary era; and
WHEREAS, Over the past 200 years, the legacy of the Prince
Hall Grand Lodge of Pennsylvania is cemented by the profound
contributions of its members to the American story, including
figures such as Octavius V. Catto, a martyr for voting rights
and education, the Honorable Raymond Pace Alexander, a legal
titan and the first African-American judge on the Pennsylvania
Courts of Common Pleas, and countless other jurists, educators,
veterans and civic leaders whose names are etched in the bedrock
of Pennsylvania's progress; and
WHEREAS, Prince Hall Free Masons have served the Commonwealth
with lodges in communities spanning from Erie to Pittsburgh,
Harrisburg to Coatesville and Philadelphia and Chester; and
WHEREAS, The House of Representatives is moved to honor the
Prince Hall Masons of Pennsylvania, who have labored

WHEREAS, Under the leadership of the Most Worshipful Grand

Lodge of Pennsylvania continues to perform amazing charitable

WHEREAS, The Most Worshipful Prince Hall Grand Lodge of

Capitol; therefore be it
