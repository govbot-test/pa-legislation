"Residential economic development district." All parcels of

§ 6302. Residential Economic Development District Grant
Program.
(a) Establishment.--The Residential Economic Development
District Grant Program is established within the department for
the purpose of encouraging major workforce housing projects in
areas of this Commonwealth that otherwise would not attract such
developments and to increase home ownership among residents of
this Commonwealth.
(b) Applicants.--A county or municipal corporation that is
fully or partially located within a residential economic
development district may apply for a grant under the program.
(c) Duties of department.-- The department may deny,
withhold, suspend or recoup grant funds that do not adhere to
this chapter.
§ 6303. Submission to department.
(a) Application.--A county or municipal corporation may
submit an application independently or in collaboration with a
housing developer or one or more other counties or municipal
corporations. The application shall, at minimum, include
documentation or other evidence that proves, to the satisfaction
of the department, that the applicant has done or has imminent
plans to do both of the following within the district:
(1) adopt and implement, or demonstrate a documented
commitment to adopt and implement, at least five pro-housing
development policies that apply districtwide or municipality-
wide; and

(b) Award of grants.--The department, in coordination with
local municipalities, shall review applications and award grants
under the program on a rolling basis, to the extent that funds

(c) Determination.--The department shall evaluate
applications and determine the amount of each grant awarded in
accordance with scoring metrics that give preference to counties
and municipal corporations that adopt pro-housing policies as
provided under this chapter.
(d) Limitations.--A grant recipient shall use the funds only
for the following purposes:
(1) Providing capital for housing development through
grants or loans.
(2) Acquiring and readying sites for development.
(3) Providing financial assistance for housing-related
infrastructure projects, including road improvements and
water or sewer connections.
(4) Addressing additional human services or public
safety needs due to increases in population.
(5) Building capacity at the municipal or regional level
to support the planning, application, administration and
implementation of grant-funded housing and infrastructure
projects, including technical assistance, grant writing,
program administration and assistance provided by housing
coalitions, redevelopment authorities or other qualified
entities.

department in keeping with the intent of this chapter,

(e) Use of grant funds.--Grant funds shall be used solely

(f) Other department grants.--Adoption of pro-housing

(g) Eligible housing developers.--Eligible housing

housing developers and nonprofit supportive housing developers.
(h) Eligibility for additional grants.--
(1) Receipt of grant funds under the program shall not

programs.
(2) The department shall encourage projects located
within a residential economic development district that
receive Redevelopment Assistance Capital Program funding and
include a residential component to incorporate workforce
housing units or other workforce housing accommodations.
§ 6304. Residential Economic Development District Fund.
(a) Establishment.--The Residential Economic Development
District Fund is established as a separate fund in the State
Treasury for the purpose of providing grants under section 6302
(relating to Residential Economic Development District Grant
Program).
(b) Continuing appropriation.--Money in the fund shall be
appropriated to the department on a continuing basis for the
purpose under subsection (a).
