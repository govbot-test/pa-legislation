2025-2026 Regular Session HB 2337 PN 3111 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 3111 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE BILL No.2337 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY NEILSON, McNEILL, KULIK, HARKINS, ISAACSON, HILL-EVANS, GUZMAN, MALAGARI, HOHENSTEIN, MENTZER AND CIRESI, MARCH 30, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON TRANSPORTATION, MARCH 30, 2026 AN ACTAmending Title 75 (Vehicles) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes, in miscellaneous provisions relating to operation of vehicles, further providing for the offense of off-road vehicles in urban municipalities.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Section 3722(f) of Title 75 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is amended to read:§ 3722. Off-road vehicles in urban municipalities.* * *(f) Construction.--Nothing contained in this section shall be construed to:(1) allow the operation of an all-terrain vehicle or dirt bike outside of the boundaries of an urban municipality in a manner that is inconsistent with this title or other law of this Commonwealth[.];(2) prevent an urban municipality from enacting or enforcing an ordinance that prohibits, permits or regulates 123456789101112131415161718 the operation of an all-terrain vehicle or dirt bike inside of the boundaries of the urban municipality; or(3) supersede or preempt an ordinance enacted prior to the effective date of this paragraph by an urban municipality that prohibits, permits or regulates the operation of an all-terrain vehicle or dirt bike inside of the boundaries of the urban municipality.* * *Section 2. This act shall take effect in 60 days.20260HB2337PN3111 - 2 - 123456789
