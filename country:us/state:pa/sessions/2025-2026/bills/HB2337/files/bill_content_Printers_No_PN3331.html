AN ACT
Amending Title 75 (Vehicles) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated
Statutes, in miscellaneous provisions relating to operation
of vehicles, further providing for the offense of off-road
vehicles in urban municipalities.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Section 3722(f) of Title 75 of the Pennsylvania
Consolidated Statutes is amended to read:
§ 3722. Off-road vehicles in urban municipalities.
* * *
(f) Construction.--[Nothing] NOTWITHSTANDING SECTION 6301
(RELATING TO PROSECUTIONS UNDER LOCAL ORDINANCES SUPERSEDED BY
TITLE), NOTHING contained in this section shall be construed to:
(1) allow the operation of an all-terrain vehicle or
dirt bike outside of the boundaries of an urban municipality
in a manner that is inconsistent with this title or other law
of this Commonwealth[.];
(2) prevent an urban municipality from enacting or
