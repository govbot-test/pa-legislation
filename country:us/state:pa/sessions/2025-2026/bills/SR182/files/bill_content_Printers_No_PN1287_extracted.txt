2025-2026 Regular Session SR 182 PN 1287 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 1287 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIASENATE RESOLUTION No.182 Session of 2025 INTRODUCED BY ROBINSON, FARRY, BARTOLOTTA, BROWN, BROOKS, BOSCOLA, ROTHMAN, FONTANA, MASTRIANO, MARTIN, PENNYCUICK, HUGHES, TARTAGLIONE, HUTCHINSON, LAUGHLIN, PHILLIPS-HILL, COSTA, KANE, HAYWOOD, J. WARD, CULVER, STEFANO, VOGEL AND DUSH, OCTOBER 28, 2025 INTRODUCED AND ADOPTED, OCTOBER 28, 2025 A RESOLUTIONRecognizing the 250th anniversary of the United States Marine Corps and honoring the generations of Marines whose service and sacrifice have strengthened the Commonwealth and the Nation.WHEREAS, November 10, 2025, marks the 250th anniversary of the United States Marine Corps; andWHEREAS, The United States Marine Corps was established on November 10, 1775, at Tun Tavern in Philadelphia; andWHEREAS, Since its founding, the United States Marine Corps has earned a distinguished reputation as one of the world's premier naval infantry forces, its legacy built upon an unwavering fighting spirit and adherence to the highest standards of excellence; andWHEREAS, This historic milestone stands as a testament to the Corps' skill in battle, distinguished leadership, extraordinary courage and selfless sacrifice in every major American conflict from the Revolution to the Global War on Terrorism, including 1234567891011121314151617 service in the historic engagements of Princeton, Derna, Chapultepec, First Bull Run, Belleau Wood, Guadalcanal, Tarawa, Peleliu, Iwo Jima, Okinawa, the Chosin Reservoir, Khe Sanh, Hue, the liberation of Kuwait, Nasiriyah and Fallujah; andWHEREAS, The United States Marine Corps exemplifies the enduring values of honor, courage and commitment, inspiring generations of Americans to serve and safeguard their country; andWHEREAS, The United States Marine Corps has earned a lasting legacy of readiness and steadfast devotion to duty in both war and peace; andWHEREAS, Marines take great pride in upholding the Corps' motto, Semper Fidelis, which means "always faithful" and reflects their loyalty to country, Corps and fellow Marines; andWHEREAS, The United States Marine Corps has continually demonstrated its ability to adapt to emerging threats and to meet the nation's security needs from its founding to the present day; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the Senate recognize the 250th anniversary of the United States Marine Corps and honor the generations of Marines whose service and sacrifice have strengthened the Commonwealth and the Nation; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the Senate invite the people of Pennsylvania to join in celebrating the 250th anniversary of the United States Marine Corps by participating in commemorative events across the Commonwealth, honoring stories of Marine Corps valor and accomplishment and paying tribute to all who have earned the title of United States Marine over the past 250 years.20250SR0182PN1287 - 2 - 12345678910111213141516171819202122232425262728
