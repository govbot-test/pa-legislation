2025-2026 Regular Session HR 440 PN 3014 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 3014 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.440 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY BELLMON, BARTON, SANCHEZ, DOUGHERTY, STAMBAUGH, HARKINS, KENYATTA, HILL-EVANS, MAYES, CEPEDA-FREYTIZ AND HOHENSTEIN, MARCH 17, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON TOURISM, RECREATION AND ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT, MARCH 18, 2026 A RESOLUTIONDesignating March 8, 2026, as "Fight of the Century Day" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, In what has been deemed the "Fight of the Century," Muhammad Ali and Joe Frazier, two undefeated fighters and former Olympic gold medalists, faced off in a legendary 15-round boxing match at Madison Square Garden on March 8, 1971; andWHEREAS, Ali and Frazier were both offered $1.5 million and a percentage of the total gate for the fight but chose to take the guaranteed $2.5 million, record earnings for a single prizefight at the time; andWHEREAS, Had the fighters taken the $1.5 million and the percentage, they each would have grossed more than $6 million from the fight, which itself grossed more than $30 million in total; andWHEREAS, Ticket sales for the fight grossed $45 million at closed-circuit venues in the United States alone, and it was viewed by more than 300 million people worldwide, with an 1234567891011121314151617 estimated half the population of the United Kingdom watching the replay on BBC after the results were already widely known; andWHEREAS, Frazier won the fight by unanimous decision, a victory secured by a massive left hook to Ali's jaw, making the "Fight of the Century" Ali's first professional loss; andWHEREAS, The two men fought again in a 14-round match in Manila on October 1, 1975, known as the "Thrilla in Manila," which saw Ali defeat Frazier; andWHEREAS, In 1972, Muhammad Ali built a training camp named "Fighter's Heaven" in Deer Lake, where he prepared for numerous fights; andWHEREAS, Joe Frazier, nicknamed "Smokin' Joe," moved to Philadelphia as a teenager and later in his retirement operated a gym on North Broad Street in Philadelphia where he shared his experience with and helped train young people; andWHEREAS, Muhammad Ali suffered through several health ailments in the final years of his life, including Parkinson's disease, spinal stenosis and pneumonia, before passing away in 2016, remembered now for his legacy as a boxer, philanthropist and social activist; andWHEREAS, Joe Frazier passed away from a battle against liver cancer in 2011, leaving behind a significant legacy, including impacting the lives of youths throughout Philadelphia and helping to shape a generation of boxers; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives designate March 8, 2026, as "Fight of the Century Day" in Pennsylvania.20260HR0440PN3014 - 2 - 1234567891011121314151617181920212223242526
