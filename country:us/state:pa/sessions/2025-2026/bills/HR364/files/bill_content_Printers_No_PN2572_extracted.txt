2025-2026 Regular Session HR 364 PN 2572 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 2572 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.364 Session of 2025 INTRODUCED BY SCOTT, GUENST, PROBST, KHAN, SANCHEZ, HILL-EVANS, CEPEDA-FREYTIZ, D. WILLIAMS, MAYES AND FLEMING, NOVEMBER 3, 2025 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON INTERGOVERNMENTAL AFFAIRS AND OPERATIONS, NOVEMBER 6, 2025 A RESOLUTIONUrging the Congress of the United States to expand TRICARE Reserve Select eligibility to include members of reserve components of the Armed Forces of the United States who are enrolled or eligible to enroll in the Federal Employees Health Benefits Program.WHEREAS, TRICARE is the Federal health care program for active duty service members, National Guard members, reserve members and their family members around the world; andWHEREAS, TRICARE provides comprehensive coverage, including health care, dental care, vision care, mental health care, prescriptions and other special programs, to beneficiaries; andWHEREAS, There are three reserve components of the Armed Forces of the United States, including the Ready Reserve, which maintains a pool of trained service members that may be recalled to active duty should the need arise; andWHEREAS, The Ready Reserve is divided into three programs, including the Selected Reserve, whose members are considered active-status members of a United States military Ready Reserve 123456789101112131415161718 unit; andWHEREAS, Federal employees who serve in the National Guard or Armed Forces reserve component are ineligible and prohibited from enrolling in TRICARE Reserve Select (TRS) coverage; andWHEREAS, This prohibition undermines TRS as a recruiting tool; andWHEREAS, This prohibition penalizes reservists serving their country in a civilian capacity and creates a financial incentive to not work for the Federal Government; andWHEREAS, TRICARE enhances the Department of Defense and our nation's security by providing health support for the full range of military operations and sustaining the health of all those entrusted to its care; andWHEREAS, States cannot expand eligibility for TRICARE coverage as TRICARE is a Federal program; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania urge the Congress of the United States to expand TRICARE Reserve Select eligibility to include members of reserve components of the Armed Forces of the United States who are enrolled or eligible to enroll in the Federal Employees Health Benefits Program; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That a copy of this resolution be transmitted to the presiding officers of each house of Congress and each member of Congress from Pennsylvania.20250HR0364PN2572 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324
