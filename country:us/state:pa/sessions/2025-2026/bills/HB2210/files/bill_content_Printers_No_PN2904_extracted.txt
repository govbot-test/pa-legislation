2025-2026 Regular Session HB 2210 PN 2904 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 2904 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE BILL No.2210 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY GUZMAN, RIVERA, HILL-EVANS, WAXMAN, HANBIDGE, ABNEY, VENKAT, BURGOS, GOUGHNOUR, CEPEDA-FREYTIZ, FLEMING, SANCHEZ AND MAYES, FEBRUARY 9, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON TRANSPORTATION, FEBRUARY 11, 2026 AN ACTAmending Title 75 (Vehicles) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes, in general provisions, further providing for definitions; and, in registration of vehicles, further providing for issuance of registration card and for registration card to be signed and exhibited on demand.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Section 102 of Title 75 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is amended by adding definitions to read:§ 102. Definitions.Subject to additional definitions contained in subsequent provisions of this title which are applicable to specific provisions of this title, the following words and phrases when used in this title shall have, unless the context clearly indicates otherwise, the meanings given to them in this section:* * *"Electronic product." An electronic extension of the department-issued physical product that conveys the authority for a vehicle to operate on a highway.12345678910111213141516171819 "Electronic product device." A computer, tablet, smart phone or other electronic personal device used by an electronic product holder to access or display an electronic product."Electronic registration card." An electronic representation of a physical registration card, including a digital photograph of a valid registration card, that is viewable on an electronic product device. An electronic registration card shall be considered the same as a physical registration card issued by the department.* * *Section 2. Sections 1308(a) and 1311 of Title 75 are amended to read:§ 1308. Issuance of registration card.(a) General rule.--The department, upon registering a vehicle, shall issue to the registrant a physical registration card or electronic registration card which shall contain the registration number assigned to the vehicle, the name and address of the registrant and the name of the owner, if other than the registrant, a description of the vehicle including the vehicle identification number, the expiration date and such other information as may be determined by the department.* * *§ 1311. Registration card to be signed and exhibited on demand.(a) Signing card.--Upon receiving the physical registration card or any duplicate, the registrant shall sign his name in the space provided.(b) Carrying and exhibiting card.--Every registration card shall, at all times while the vehicle is being operated upon a highway, be in the possession of the person driving or in control of the vehicle or carried in the vehicle and shall be 20260HB2210PN2904 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 exhibited upon demand of any police officer.(c) Production to avoid penalty.--No person shall be convicted of violating this section or section 1301 (relating to driving unregistered vehicle prohibited) if the person produces at the office of the issuing authority or at the office of the arresting police officer within five days of the violation, a physical registration card or electronic registration card valid in this Commonwealth at the time of the arrest.(d) Surrender of electronic product device not required.--(1) A statute or regulation that requires an individual to surrender a registration card to a police officer shall not apply to an electronic product device.(2) If an individual chooses to surrender an electronic product device to a police officer who inadvertently causes damage to the electronic device, the police officer shall not be liable for the damage to the electronic product device.(e) Privacy of information on electronic product device.-- (1) If a registrant exhibits an electronic registration card upon demand by a police officer under this section, the police officer may review the electronic registration card using the electronic product device.(2) If a police officer inadvertently reviews information on an electronic product device that is not related to an electronic registration card, the inadvertently reviewed information may not be used as evidence in a civil or criminal trial.(f) Immunity.--If a registrant chooses to exhibit an electronic registration card or electronic product device under this title, the individual viewing the electronic registration card in good faith for a purpose under this title shall be 20260HB2210PN2904 - 3 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 immune from any civil or criminal liability that arises from the registrant's choice, including:(1) The inadvertent viewing of information on the electronic product device other than the electronic registration card.(2) The inadvertent deletion of information from the electronic product device.(3) The inadvertent interception of a communication while in possession of the electronic product device.(4) Any breakage that may occur to the electronic product device.Section 3.
