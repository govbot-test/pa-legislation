AN ACT
Amending Title 34 (Game) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated
Statutes, in hunting and furtaking, further providing for
unlawful devices and methods.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Section 2308(b.1) of Title 34 of the Pennsylvania
Consolidated Statutes is amended to read:
§ 2308. Unlawful devices and methods.
* * *
(b.1) Additional [exception.--A] exceptions.--
(1) A semiautomatic rifle may be used to hunt game in
accordance with regulations promulgated by the commission.
(2) Notwithstanding subsection (a)(4), the commission
may direct that persons may lawfully hunt or aid, abet,
assist or conspire to hunt any snow goose during the snow
goose conservation season through the use of a semiautomatic
shotgun or magazine shotgun without regard to the restriction
that the shotgun is plugged to a two-shell capacity in the
