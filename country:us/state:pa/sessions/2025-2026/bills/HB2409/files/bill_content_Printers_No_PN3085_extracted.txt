2025-2026 Regular Session HB 2409 PN 3085 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 3085 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE BILL No.2409 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY J. HARRIS, MARCH 24, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON APPROPRIATIONS, MARCH 24, 2026 AN ACTMaking appropriations from the Philadelphia Taxicab and Limousine Regulatory Fund to the Philadelphia Parking Authority for the fiscal year July 1, 2026, to June 30, 2027.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. The sum of $2,037,000 is hereby appropriated from the Philadelphia Taxicab and Limousine Regulatory Fund to the Philadelphia Parking Authority for the fiscal year July 1, 2026, to June 30, 2027.Section 2. This act shall take effect July 1, 2026, or immediately, whichever is later.1234567891011
