AN ACT
Providing for public utility credit reporting; and imposing
duties on public utilities.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Short title.
This act shall be known and may be cited as the Public
Utility Credit Act.
Section 2. Definitions.
The following words and phrases when used in this act shall
have the meanings given to them in this section unless the
context clearly indicates otherwise:

(1) A person or corporation owning or operating in this
Commonwealth equipment or facilities for:
(i) Producing, generating, transmitting,
distributing or furnishing natural or artificial gas,
electricity or steam for the production of light, heat or

Section 3. Public utility payments reported to credit reporting
agencies.
(a) Option.--A public utility shall provide customers the
opportunity to opt into and, at any time, to opt out of having
the public utility payments of the customers reported to a
nationwide credit reporting agency.
(b) New customers.--A person who becomes a customer of a
public utility after the effective date of this subsection shall
be notified by the public utility of the opportunity to opt into
and, at any time, to opt out of having the customer's payments
reported to credit reporting agencies when establishing the
customer's public utility account with the public utility.
(c) Existing customers.--A person who is a customer of a
public utility on the effective date of this subsection shall be
notified in writing by the public utility of the opportunity to
opt into and, at any time, to opt out of having the customer's
payments reported to credit reporting agencies within 45 days of
the effective date of this subsection.
(d) Time limit to opt in.--
(1) New and existing public utility customers may
register to opt into and opt out of public utility payment

(e) Costs and fees.--A public utility may not pass on costs
or charge a fee to a consumer who chooses to opt into or opt out

Section 4. Effective date.
This act shall take effect in 60 days.
