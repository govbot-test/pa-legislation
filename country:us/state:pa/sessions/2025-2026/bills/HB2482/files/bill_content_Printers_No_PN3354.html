AN ACT
Amending the act of March 10, 1949 (P.L.30, No.14), entitled "An
act relating to the public school system, including certain
provisions applicable as well to private and parochial
schools; amending, revising, consolidating and changing the
laws relating thereto," establishing the Newly Employed
Teacher Grant Program.
The General Assembly finds and declares as follows:
(1) The General Assembly finds that this Commonwealth is
currently experiencing a significant teacher shortage.
(2) Resources should be directed to newly employed
teachers in this Commonwealth in an effort to attract and
retain them in the education workforce.
(3) To alleviate financial burden placed on newly
employed teachers establishing an engaging and effective
experience for their students, the General Assembly is
committed to providing funding for newly employed teacher-
requested classroom supplies.
(4) The purpose of this act is to provide funds to the
Department of Education for newly employed teacher-requested

The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. The act of March 10, 1949 (P.L.30, No.14), known
as the Public School Code of 1949, is amended by adding an
article to read:
ARTICLE XII-D
NEWLY EMPLOYED TEACHER GRANT PROGRAM
Section 1201-D. Scope of article.
This article relates to providing a grant to newly employed
teachers.
Section 1202-D. Definitions.
The following words and phrases when used in this article
shall have the meanings given to them in this section unless the
context clearly indicates otherwise:
"Administrative costs." Operational expenses to facilitate
the implementation of section 1203-D, not to exceed Federal de
minimus rate, as issued by the Office of Management and Budget.
"Classroom supplies." Materials, equipment and tools used to
facilitate teaching and learning.
"Department." The Department of Education of the
Commonwealth.
"Newly employed teacher." A person who holds a valid
Pennsylvania teaching certificate and has been employed by a
school entity for approximately 24 months.
"Online e-commerce platform." A consumer interface for a
vendor that can meet the requirements under section 1205-D and
facilitate a commercial transaction, which shall include the
transmission of funds for data or both, conducted electronically
via the Internet.

"Program." The Newly Employed Teacher Grant Program

"School entity." A school district, intermediate unit, area
career and technical school, charter school, regional charter
school or cyber charter school operating within this
Commonwealth.
Section 1203-D. Establishment.
The Newly Employed Teacher Grant Program is established
within the department to award grants to newly employed teachers
who request classroom supplies to the extent that money is
appropriated for this purpose.
Section 1204-D. Duties of department.
The department shall:
(1) In accordance with 62 Pa.C.S. (relating to
procurement), select and enter into an agreement with one or
more online e-commerce platforms to administer the program.
An online e-commerce platform must demonstrate prior
experience operating a Statewide or multidistrict program
that provides direct, itemized classroom supplies with real-
time public transparency prior to being selected by the
department.
(2) Establish a process to ensure that purchases by
newly employed teachers who have been awarded a grant are
restricted to online purchases of classroom supplies from
department-selected online e-commerce platforms.
(3) Establish a grant application in accordance with
section 1206-D and issue guidelines that advise grant
applicants which classroom supplies are eligible for grant
funding.
(4) Require annual reporting from an online e-commerce

Section 1206-D. Creation and submission of application.
(a) Department.--No later than May 31, 2026, the department
shall:
(1) Establish and publish on the department's publicly
accessible Internet website the application and guidelines
for the program.
(2) Transmit notice of the availability of the
application and guidelines to the Legislative Reference
Bureau for publication in the next available issue of the
Pennsylvania Bulletin.
(b) Submission.--The online e-commerce platform shall accept
applications for funding from newly employed teachers no later
than July 15, 2026, and each July 15 thereafter.

Section 1208-D. Reports to General Assembly.
No later than September 1, 2027, and each September 1
thereafter, the department shall provide a report to the
chairperson and minority chairperson of the Education Committee
of the Senate and the chairperson and minority chairperson of
the Education Committee of the House of Representatives. The
report shall include the following:
(1) The amount of funds requested by newly employed
teachers during the previous school year.
(2) The amount of funds distributed to newly employed
teachers during the previous school year.
(3) A list of approved items purchased with grant funds
during the previous school year.
(4) Information reported to the department by online e-
commerce platforms.
Section 2. This act shall take effect in 60 days.
