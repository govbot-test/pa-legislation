AN ACT
Amending Title 75 (Vehicles) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated
Statutes, in traffic-control devices, further providing for
obedience to traffic-control devices.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Section 3111 of Title 75 of the Pennsylvania
Consolidated Statutes is amended by adding subsections to read:
§ 3111. Obedience to traffic-control devices.
* * *
(e) Liability of GPS navigation provider.--If a commercial
driver violates this section using a GPS navigation system that
did not provide explicit notice of the prohibitions of an
applicable official traffic-control device, in addition to the
penalties under subsection (a.1), a GPS navigation provider of
the GPS navigation system shall be subject to a civil penalty of
$2,000 for each occurrence.
(f) Definitions.--As used in this section, the following
words and phrases shall have the meanings given to them in this
