2025-2026 Regular Session HB 2230 PN 2923 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 2923 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE BILL No.2230 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY FLOOD, RADER AND SAPPEY, FEBRUARY 19, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON COMMUNICATIONS AND TECHNOLOGY, FEBRUARY 20, 2026 AN ACTAmending Title 75 (Vehicles) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes, in traffic-control devices, further providing for obedience to traffic-control devices.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Section 3111 of Title 75 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is amended by adding subsections to read:§ 3111. Obedience to traffic-control devices.* * *(e) Liability of GPS navigation provider.--If a commercial driver violates this section using a GPS navigation system that did not provide explicit notice of the prohibitions of an applicable official traffic-control device, in addition to the penalties under subsection (a.1), a GPS navigation provider of the GPS navigation system shall be subject to a civil penalty of $2,000 for each occurrence.(f) Definitions.--As used in this section, the following words and phrases shall have the meanings given to them in this 123456789101112131415161718 subsection unless the context clearly indicates otherwise:"GPS." The Global Positioning System."GPS navigation provider." A person that offers for sale, subscription or free a GPS navigation system."GPS navigation system." GPS navigation software, satellite navigation software, route planning software, Internet-based route planning or mapping services.Section 2. This act shall take effect in 60 days.20260HB2230PN2923 - 2 - 12345678
