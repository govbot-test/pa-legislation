Definitions.--As used in this article--* * *(17) "Epinephrine delivery system" means a device approved by the United States Food and Drug Administration that contains a premeasured dose of epinephrine and is used to administer 1234567891011121314151617181920 epinephrine into the human body to prevent or treat a life-threatening allergic reaction.(18) "Short-acting asthma medication" means any medication approved by the United States Food and Drug Administration, including a beta-2 agonist or bronchodilator, used for the quick relief of asthma symptoms, including:(i) an orally inhaled medication that contains a premeasured single dose of albuterol or albuterol sulfate delivered by a nebulizer compressor device; and(ii) a pressurized, metered-dose inhaler used to treat asthma or respiratory distress, including wheezing, shortness of breath and difficulty breathing.Section 2.
