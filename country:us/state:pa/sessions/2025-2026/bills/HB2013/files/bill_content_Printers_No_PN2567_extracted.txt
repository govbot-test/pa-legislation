2025-2026 Regular Session HB 2013 PN 2567 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 2567 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE BILL No.2013 Session of 2025 INTRODUCED BY SCOTT, GUENST, GIRAL, HANBIDGE, SANCHEZ, OTTEN, CIRESI AND GREEN, NOVEMBER 3, 2025 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON COMMERCE, NOVEMBER 6, 2025 AN ACTAmending the act of December 22, 1983 (P.L.306, No.84), entitled "An act providing for the State Board of Vehicle Manufacturers, Dealers and Salespersons; and providing penalties," in vehicles, further providing for unlawful acts by manufacturers or distributors.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Section 310(c)(6)(i)(E) of the act of December 22, 1983 (P.L.306, No.84), known as the Board of Vehicles Act, is amended to read:Section 310. Unlawful acts by manufacturers or distributors.* * *(c) Restriction on ownership of dealer.--* * *(6) The following shall apply:(i) A manufacturer or distributor may own, operate or control not more than five new vehicle dealerships trading solely in electric vehicles, as defined in 75 Pa.C.S. § 102 (relating to definitions), that are not 12345678910111213141516171819 sold as new vehicles by a licensed independent new vehicle dealer pursuant to an existing franchise with a manufacturer or distributor, if each of the following conditions are met:* * *[(E) The manufacturer shall have continuously offered electric vehicles for sale for a period of not less than 12 months prior to the effective date of this clause.]* * *Section 2. This act shall take effect in 60 days.20250HB2013PN2567 - 2 - 1234567891011
