2025-2026 Regular Session HR 557 PN 3554 Bill Text (HTM) PRIOR PRINTER'S NO. 3540PRINTER'S NO. 3554THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.557 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY LABS, TOMLINSON, MARCELL AND NEILSON, JUNE 8, 2026 AS REPORTED FROM COMMITTEE ON TOURISM, RECREATION AND ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT, HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, AS AMENDED, JUNE 9, 2026A RESOLUTIONDesignating June 10, 2026, as "Just Be You Day" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, Lynzi Allen, 2025 Miss Pennsylvania Teen, is the author of the book, "Just Be You" and is the founder of the initiative Just Be You: Self Love, Inclusion, and Diversity; andWHEREAS, Ms. Allen travels throughout this Commonwealth with an inspiring mission to encourage young Pennsylvanians of all backgrounds to embrace who they are, respect the uniqueness of others and foster environments where individuals feel seen, valued and empowered; therefore be it ANDWHEREAS, ACROSS THIS COMMONWEALTH MANY YOUNG PEOPLE FACE VARIOUS SOCIAL AND EMOTIONAL CHALLENGES DAILY SUCH AS PRESSURES TO CONFORM TO SOCIETAL EXPECTATIONS AND CHALLENGES RELATED TO SELF-ESTEEM, IDENTITY AND BELONGING; ANDWHEREAS, THE PURPOSE OF "JUST BE YOU DAY" IS TO PROMOTE SELF-CONFIDENCE, CELEBRATE DIVERSITY ACROSS PENNSYLVANIA'S COMMUNITIES, SUPPORT INCLUSIVE ENVIRONMENTS, EMPOWER YOUNG PEOPLE TO USE THEIR VOICES AND REINFORCE THE IMPORTANCE OF <--1234567891011121314151617 REPRESENTATION; ANDWHEREAS, "JUST BE YOU DAY" CREATES A SENSE OF BELONGING, SUPPORTS MENTAL HEALTH AWARENESS AND ALIGNS WITH STATEWIDE EFFORTS TOWARD EQUITY AND INCLUSION; ANDWHEREAS, "JUST BE YOU DAY" ACROSS THIS COMMONWEALTH WILL ACTIVATE A STATEWIDE MOVEMENT; ANDWHEREAS, THE STATEWIDE MOVEMENT WOULD INCLUDE SCHOOL PROGRAMS AND ASSEMBLIES ON CONFIDENCE BUILDING AND STUDENT-LED INSTRUCTIONS, COMMUNITY EVENTS AND PARTNERSHIPS THROUGH COLLABORATIONS WITH ESTABLISHED YOUTH LEADERSHIP ORGANIZATIONS AND SOCIAL MEDIA CAMPAIGNS THAT INCLUDE A STATEWIDE DIGITAL INITIATIVE ENCOURAGING INDIVIDUALS TO SHARE THEIR STORIES USING A UNIFIED HASHTAG OR MESSAGE; ANDWHEREAS, YOUTH LEADERSHIP SPOTLIGHTS WILL RECOGNIZE YOUNG LEADERS ACROSS THIS COMMONWEALTH AS SHINING EXAMPLES OF THE POSITIVE IMPACT OF CONFIDENCE, KINDNESS AND EMPATHY; THEREFORE BE ITRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives designate June 10, 2026, as "Just Be You Day" in Pennsylvania; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives encourage all Pennsylvanians to come together to help create a Commonwealth where all individuals feel they are enough.20260HR0557PN3554 - 2 - 1234567891011121314151617181920212223
