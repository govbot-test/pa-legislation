2025-2026 Regular Session HR 557 PN 3540 Bill Text (HTM) PRINTER'S NO. 3540 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.557 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY LABS, TOMLINSON, MARCELL AND NEILSON, JUNE 8, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON TOURISM, RECREATION AND ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT, JUNE 8, 2026 A RESOLUTIONDesignating June 10, 2026, as "Just Be You Day" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, Lynzi Allen, 2025 Miss Pennsylvania Teen, is the author of the book, "Just Be You" and is the founder of the initiative Just Be You: Self Love, Inclusion, and Diversity; andWHEREAS, Ms. Allen travels throughout this Commonwealth with an inspiring mission to encourage young Pennsylvanians of all backgrounds to embrace who they are, respect the uniqueness of others and foster environments where individuals feel seen, valued and empowered; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives designate June 10, 2026, as "Just Be You Day" in Pennsylvania; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives encourage all Pennsylvanians to come together to help create a Commonwealth where all individuals feel they are enough.123456789101112131415
