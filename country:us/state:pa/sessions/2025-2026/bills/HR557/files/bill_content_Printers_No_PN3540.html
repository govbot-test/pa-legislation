A RESOLUTION
Designating June 10, 2026, as "Just Be You Day" in Pennsylvania.
WHEREAS, Lynzi Allen, 2025 Miss Pennsylvania Teen, is the
author of the book, "Just Be You" and is the founder of the
initiative Just Be You: Self Love, Inclusion, and Diversity; and
WHEREAS, Ms. Allen travels throughout this Commonwealth with
an inspiring mission to encourage young Pennsylvanians of all
backgrounds to embrace who they are, respect the uniqueness of
others and foster environments where individuals feel seen,
valued and empowered; therefore be it
RESOLVED, That the House of Representatives designate June
10, 2026, as "Just Be You Day" in Pennsylvania; and be it
further
RESOLVED, That the House of Representatives encourage all
Pennsylvanians to come together to help create a Commonwealth
where all individuals feel they are enough.
