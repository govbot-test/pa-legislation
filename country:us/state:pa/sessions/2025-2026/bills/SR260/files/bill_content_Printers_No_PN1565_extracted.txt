2025-2026 Regular Session SR 260 PN 1565 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 1565 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIASENATE RESOLUTION No.260 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY CAPPELLETTI, SAVAL, TARTAGLIONE, KANE, KEARNEY, COMITTA, HUGHES, HAYWOOD AND STREET, APRIL 1, 2026 REFERRED TO RULES AND EXECUTIVE NOMINATIONS, APRIL 1, 2026 A RESOLUTIONRecognizing the month of April 2026 as "National Fair Housing Month" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, The Fair Housing Act, enacted April 11, 1968, one of the most important components of our national civil rights policy, prohibits discrimination in housing on the basis of race, national origin, religion, sex, family status and disability; andWHEREAS, This landmark piece of legislation continues to serve as a powerful statement about the values of equality, equity and dignity that we strive to uphold and the places where we still have work to do to fulfill our promise as a nation; andWHEREAS, Access to quality housing is the foundation for achieving better educational, employment and health outcomes, as well as one of the most important ways that families build wealth that can be passed down through generations; andWHEREAS, Acts of housing discrimination and barriers to equal housing opportunity are repugnant to a common sense of decency and fairness; and123456789101112131415161718 WHEREAS, The Pennsylvania Affordable Housing Act offers additional protections beyond the Federal Fair Housing Act and certain local fair housing ordinances expand protections based on sexual orientation, gender identity, marital status, source of income and other protected classes; andWHEREAS, Despite the work of Federal, State and local agencies in reducing housing discrimination using the Fair Housing Act and other important laws, we have a long way to go to ensure safe, decent and affordable housing for all Pennsylvanians; andWHEREAS, People of color are significantly more likely than white people to experience evictions and homelessness, the result of centuries of structural racism that continues today, and we cannot address racial inequities without addressing housing; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the Senate recognize the month of April 2026 as "National Fair Housing Month" in Pennsylvania.20260SR0260PN1565 - 2 - 1234567891011121314151617
