2025-2026 Regular Session HR 374 PN 2663 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 2663 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.374 Session of 2025 INTRODUCED BY KRUPA, ARMANINI, BANTA, COOK, GALLAGHER, HAMM, JAMES, KAUFFMAN, NEILSON, PUGH, RAPP, ROWE, SCHMITT, STAATS, STENDER AND WATRO, NOVEMBER 25, 2025 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON VETERANS AFFAIRS AND EMERGENCY PREPAREDNESS, DECEMBER 3, 2025 A RESOLUTIONDesignating 2025 as "The Year of the Catholic War Veterans of the United States of America" in Pennsylvania in honor of the organization's 90-year history of enduring service to faith, family and country.WHEREAS, Ninety years ago, in 1935, a humble parish priest from New York, Reverend Edward J. Higgins, looked into the hearts of the men returning from the Great War, saw courage and the need for healing, fellowship and faith; andWHEREAS, Guided by a conviction that Catholic veterans deserved an organization grounded in both patriotism and faith, Father Higgins founded what would become the Catholic War Veterans of the United States of America; andWHEREAS, In 1935, Father Higgins carried the organization's American and Papal flags to Rome, where Pope Pius XI personally blessed and bestowed his Apostolic Blessing upon this new union of faith and service, a sacred seal upon what would become a proud tradition of Catholic men and women in uniform; andWHEREAS, The Catholic War Veterans grew quickly, uniting 123456789101112131415161718 those who fought in every branch of the armed forces, in every conflict, under one banner of devotion to God and country; andWHEREAS, From World War II through Korea, Vietnam, the Persian Gulf, Iraq and Afghanistan, Catholic War Veterans have continued to embody the creed of "For God, For Country, For Home," bringing compassion to hospitals, honor to memorials and moral courage to public life; andWHEREAS, For decades the Catholic War Veterans have been members of the Pennsylvania State Veterans Commission, an organization that is responsible for advising the Adjutant General of the Pennsylvania National Guard on veterans benefits and support, recommending veteran-focused legislation to the Governor, managing several veterans assistance programs and aiding veterans and their families in receiving the support and benefits they earned through their service and sacrifice; andWHEREAS, In 1940, the organization earned official recognition from the Veterans Administration and, in 1984, the Catholic War Veterans were granted a Congressional Charter, a testament to their national stature and lasting contribution to America's veteran community; andWHEREAS, Here in Pennsylvania, the Catholic War Veterans established vibrant posts and auxiliaries that have served communities for generations, including visiting the sick, aiding the poor, supporting schools and parishes, mentoring young people and preserving the sacred memory of the fallen; andWHEREAS, Through prayer and action, through faith and fellowship, the Catholic War Veterans have stood at the intersection of devotion and duty, reminding us that patriotism is not only love of country, but also love of neighbor; andWHEREAS, As the Catholic War Veterans celebrate their 90th 20250HR0374PN2663 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 anniversary, this Commonwealth proudly joins in gratitude and reflection, honoring those who have worn the uniform, lifted the cross and carried forward the light of service across nine decades of American history; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives designate 2025 as "The Year of the Catholic War Veterans of the United States of America" in Pennsylvania in honor of the organization's 90-year history of enduring service to faith, family and country; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That a copy of this resolution be transmitted to the Catholic War Veterans Department of Pennsylvania as an expression of high regard and gratitude from the elected representatives of the people of this Commonwealth.20250HR0374PN2663 - 3 - 12345678910111213
