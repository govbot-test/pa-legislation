MACKENZIE, VENKAT, CONKLIN, SANCHEZ, McNEILL, HOWARD, BRENNAN, PICKETT, HANBIDGE, STEELE, ANDERSON, RIVERA, BOROWSKI, CEPEDA-FREYTIZ, GALLAGHER, PASHINSKI, MALAGARI, O'MARA, D. WILLIAMS, PARKER, NEILSON, HOHENSTEIN, GILLEN, ISAACSON, MENTZER AND CIRESI, MAY 12, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON VETERANS AFFAIRS AND EMERGENCY PREPAREDNESS, MAY 13, 2026 A RESOLUTIONRecognizing September 29, 2026, as "VFW Day" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, The Veterans of Foreign Wars of the United States, known as the VFW, is a nonprofit veterans service organization consisting of eligible veterans and military service members from the active, guard and reserve forces; andWHEREAS, The VFW was established on September 29, 1899, by a small group of veterans of different foreign wars who called themselves the American Veterans of Foreign Service and later merged with two other combat war veteran associations during a historic convention near Pittsburgh, becoming the Veterans of Foreign Wars of the United States and opening their membership to veterans from all foreign wars and branches of service; andWHEREAS, The mission of the VFW is to foster camaraderie among United States veterans of overseas conflicts, serve our veterans, the military and their communities, and advocate on 123456789101112131415 behalf of all veterans; andWHEREAS, Since its inception, the VFW has been a leading advocate for veterans' rights and benefits, playing a crucial role in the establishment of the Veterans Administration (VA), the national veterans' cemetery system and the GI Bill of Rights; andWHEREAS, The VFW was instrumental in the passage of the Sergeant First Class Heath Robinson Honoring our Promise to Address Comprehensive Toxics Act of 2022 signed into law by President Biden, which expanded and extended eligibility for VA health care for veterans with toxic exposures and veterans of the Vietnam War, Gulf War and post-9/11 eras, and added more than 20 more presumptive conditions for burn pits, Agent Orange and other toxic exposures, while making other improvements for the health and benefit of our veterans; andWHEREAS, The VFW and the VFW Auxiliary boast a membership of more than 1.4 million individuals, with approximately 6,000 posts worldwide; andWHEREAS, The VFW has provided tens of millions of dollars in financial assistance to military families, scholarships and local community service projects; andWHEREAS, The VFW helps veterans recoup billions of dollars annually in the United States Department of Veterans Affairs disability compensation and pension benefits, ensuring veterans and their families receive the care they earned and deserve; andWHEREAS, The VFW continues to honor its motto, "No One Does More For Veterans," through unwavering devotion and service to veterans, military personnel and communities; andWHEREAS, "VFW Day" is a time to commemorate the hard work and sacrifice of veteran men and women who have selflessly served 20260HR0522PN3405 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 the United States of America, and to recognize the continued service and dedication of the VFW;
