AN ACT
Amending Title 65 (Public Officers) of the Pennsylvania
Consolidated Statutes, in open meetings, further providing
for notification of agency business required and exceptions.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Section 712.1(a), (c) and (e)(1) of Title 65 of
the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes are amended AND THE
SECTION IS AMENDED BY ADDING A SUBSECTION to read:
§ 712.1. Notification of agency business required and
exceptions.
(a) Official action.--Except as provided in subsection (b),
[(c), (d) or (e)] (c) or , (d) OR (D.1) , an agency may not take
official action on a matter of agency business at a meeting if
the matter was not included in the notification required under
section 709(c.1) (relating to public notice).

(c) Business arising within 24 hours before meeting.--An
agency may take official action on a matter of agency business
that is not listed on a meeting agenda if the matter:
(1) [the matter] arises or is brought to the attention
of the agency within the 24-hour period prior to the meeting;
and
(2) [the matter] is de minimis in nature and does not
involve the expenditure of funds or entering into a contract
or agreement [by the agency].
* * *
(D.1) MINOR ADMINISTRATIVE APPROVALS.--
(1) AN AGENCY MAY TAKE OFFICIAL ACTION ON A MINOR
ADMINISTRATIVE APPROVAL NOT LISTED ON A MEETING AGENDA,
INCLUDING A MINOR ADMINISTRATIVE APPROVAL INADVERTENTLY
OMITTED FROM THE AGENDA, THAT WOULD CAUSE A HARDSHIP IF
DELAYED UNTIL THE NEXT MEETING SUCH AS ITEMS DISCUSSED AND
ACTED ON AT A PREVIOUS MEETING. THE EXCEPTION UNDER THIS
SUBSECTION SHALL ONLY INCLUDE THE FOLLOWING:
(I) PERSONNEL ACTIONS AFTER AN EXECUTIVE SESSION
HELD IN ACCORDANCE WITH SECTION 708(A)(1) AND RESTRICTED
TO FIRING, LEAVE, INVESTIGATIONS OR DISCIPLINING.
(II) OFFICIAL ACTION, AFTER AN EXECUTIVE SESSION
HELD IN ACCORDANCE WITH SECTION 708(A)(4), AUTHORIZING
TIME-SENSITIVE LEGAL FILINGS OR OTHER ACTION RELATED TO
LITIGATION BY THE SOLICITOR OR CHIEF LEGAL COUNSEL OR
OTHER PROFESSIONAL REPRESENTATIVES. THE OPINION RENDERED
BY THE SOLICITOR UNDER PARAGRAPH (2) SHALL INCLUDE A
DESCRIPTION OF WHY IMMEDIATE ACTION IS REQUIRED IN ORDER
TO PRESERVE OR PROMOTE THE AGENCY'S LEGAL INTERESTS.
(III) SMALL PURCHASING AND BILL APPROVALS OF LESS
