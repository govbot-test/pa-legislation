AN ACT
Amending the act of October 24, 2018 (P.L.733, No.118), entitled
"An act providing for requirements for documentation of the
need for an assistance animal or service animal in housing
and for the offenses of misrepresentation of entitlement to
assistance animal or service animal and misrepresentation of
animal as assistance animal or service animal," further
providing for definitions; establishing the trained service
dog card; providing for the documentation of service dogs and
for admittance of registered service dog to public
accommodations; establishing a trained service dog card
database; providing for immunity from liability; providing
for the offense of fraudulent misrepresentation of a service
dog; and making editorial changes.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:

Section [3] 302. Documentation of disability and disability-
related need.
(a) Right to request documentation.--A landlord or
association that receives a request from a person to make an
exception to the landlord's or association's policy prohibiting
animals or limiting the size, weight, breed or number of animals
on the landlord's property or within property controlled by the
association because the person requires the use of an assistance
animal or service animal may require the person to produce
documentation of the disability and disability-related need for
the animal only if the disability or disability-related need is
not readily apparent or known to the landlord or, in the case of
an association, the executive board of the association.
(b) Minimum standards.--Any documentation proving that a
person is disabled and requires the use of an assistance animal
or service animal as a reasonable accommodation in housing under
the Fair Housing Act (Public Law 90-284, 42 U.S.C. § 3601 et
seq.), the Rehabilitation Act of 1973 (Public Law 93-112, 29
U.S.C. § 701 et seq.), the Americans with Disabilities Act of
1990 (Public Law 101-336, 104 Stat. 327) or the act of October
27, 1955 (P.L.744, No.222), known as the Pennsylvania Human
Relations Act, or local law shall:
(1) Be in writing.
(2) Be reliable and based on direct knowledge of the
person's disability and disability-related need for the
assistance animal or service animal.
(3) Describe the person's disability-related need for
the assistance animal or service animal.

Section 8. Sections 5 and 6 of the act are amended to read:
Section [5] 304. Misrepresentation of entitlement to assistance
animal or service animal in housing.
(a) Offense defined.--A person commits the offense of
misrepresentation of entitlement to assistance animal or service
animal in housing if the person intentionally:
(1) misrepresents to another that the person has a
disability or disability-related need for the use of an
assistance animal or service animal in housing; or
(2) makes materially false statements for the purpose of
obtaining documentation for the use of an assistance animal
or service animal in housing.
(b) Grading.--A person who violates the provisions of
subsection (a) commits a misdemeanor of the third degree.

Section 501. Definitions.
The following words and phrases when used in this chapter
shall have the meanings given to them in this section unless the

all of the following:

substantially limits one or more major life activity;
(2) has a history or record of an impairment described
under paragraph (1); and
(3) is perceived or known by other people as having an
impairment as described under paragraph (1).
"Public accommodation." The term shall have the same meaning
as "public accommodation, resort or amusement" defined under
section 4(l) of the act of October 27, 1955 (P.L.744, No.222),

"Registered service dog." A service dog that has been issued
a trained service dog card and is listed in the trained service
dog card database.
"Service dog in training." A dog that is being individually
trained to do work or perform tasks for the benefit of a
qualified individual with a disability.
"Trained service dog card." An identification card issued by
the department to indicate that a dog is a registered service
dog that includes:
(1) A specific insignia.
(2) A photograph of the service dog.
(3) A photograph of the owner of the service dog.
"Trained service dog patch." A patch with a specific
insignia inscribed and issued by the department to indicate that
a dog is a registered service dog.
"Trainer of a service dog." An individual who is training a
service dog to do work or perform tasks for the benefit of an
individual with a disability.
Section 502. Trained service dog patch.
(a) Duty of department.--The department shall develop, make
and issue a trained service dog patch.
(b) Eligibility.--For an individual with a disability to
receive a trained service dog patch for their service dog, the
individual with a disability shall provide to the department all
of the following:
(1) A completed registered service dog application form
developed by the department.

Section 505. Immunity from liability.
An owner of a public accommodation shall not be liable for a
personal injury or property damage caused by a registered
service dog or service dog in training so long as the personal
injury or property damage has no causal relationship with the
owner of a public accommodation's own negligence, recklessness
or willful misconduct.
Section 506. Regulations.
The department may promulgate regulations as necessary to
implement this chapter.
CHAPTER 7
MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS
Section 701. Fraudulent misrepresentation of a service dog.
A person is guilty of a summary offense if the person
fraudulently misrepresents a dog as a service dog with the
intention of bringing the dog into a place of public
accommodation or obtaining other benefits.
