The act of March 10, 1949 (P.L.30, No.14), known as the Public School Code of 1949, is amended by adding a section to read:Section 1802.1. Commission for Career and Technical Education Excellence.-- (a) The Commission for Career and Technical Education Excellence is established in the department as an administrative commission. The commission shall have the 1234567891011121314151617181920 authority of the department with all the powers and duties generally vested in and imposed upon the commissions under the act of April 9, 1929 (P.L.177, No.175), known as "The Administrative Code of 1929."(b) The commission shall have the following duties:(1) No later than six months after its establishment, develop guidelines to identify which secondary or postsecondary courses, programs or activities may apply the credit toward the completion of a career and technical education program at the secondary or postsecondary level or a pre-apprenticeship program established under section 1919-A(b).(2) Provide recommendations of revision of the guidelines to the department every five years.(3) By December 31 each year, the commission shall submit a report to the Governor and the chairperson and minority chairperson of the Education Committee of the Senate and the chairperson and minority chairperson of the Education Committee of the House of Representatives on the status of career and technical education in this Commonwealth. The report shall:(i) Outline career and technical education programs and achievements.(ii) Assess the trends and needs in secondary and both formal and informal postsecondary career and technical education and training.(iii) Investigate and assess workforce trends of the building and construction trades industries.(iv) Consider the manner in which money is used to support career and technical education activities.(v) Highlight new initiatives and recommend future program needs.20260HB2461PN3547 - When compiling the annual report, the department may consult with organizations outside of State government, including the Pennsylvania Commission for Community Colleges.(3) The department, in consultation with the State Board of Higher Education and the Department of Labor and Industry, shall develop guidelines to implement this subsection.(b) Each community college in this Commonwealth shall establish and operate pre-apprenticeship programs using the National Association of Building Trade Unions' Multi-Craft Core Curriculum. The following apply:(1) The department shall implement any necessary policy or procedure to administer the program and may consult with:(i) The Department of Labor and Industry.(ii) The Pennsylvania Commission for Community Colleges.(iii) Career and technical high schools located in this 20260HB2461PN3547 -
