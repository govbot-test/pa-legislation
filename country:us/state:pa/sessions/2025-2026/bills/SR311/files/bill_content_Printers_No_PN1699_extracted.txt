2025-2026 Regular Session SR 311 PN 1699 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 1699 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIASENATE RESOLUTION No.311 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY MARTIN, BROWN, COSTA, FARRY, HUTCHINSON, KEEFER, PHILLIPS-HILL, PICOZZI, VOGEL AND J. WARD, MAY 7, 2026 REFERRED TO APPROPRIATIONS, MAY 7, 2026 A RESOLUTIONDesignating June 1, 2026, as "PIAA Wrestling Day of Champions" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, Whether at a young age or an adult of any age, wrestling teaches key life skills, including self-reliance, self-discipline and teamwork, all of which apply to an athlete's growth and professional development; andWHEREAS, Wrestling promotes both a physically healthy lifestyle and mental growth; andWHEREAS, Pennsylvania has a rich history of and unrivaled passion for wrestling as a sport; andWHEREAS, Pennsylvania is home to elite wrestling programs that exist at all age and experience levels of the sport; andWHEREAS, Pennsylvania's wrestlers participate at all stages of the sport from professional, international competitors to collegiate-level competitors under the guidance of the Pennsylvania Interscholastic Athletic Association (PIAA) and even younger with local clubs and associations; andWHEREAS, Participation in wrestling has grown in all age 123456789101112131415161718 groups and levels of the sport; andWHEREAS, According to the National Federation of State High School Associations, participation in wrestling reached a record 374,278 participants nationwide during the 2024-2025 season; andWHEREAS, Boys' wrestling participation grew to a record 300,214 and girls' wrestling participation grew to a record 74,064 during the 2024-2025 season; andWHEREAS, The growth in participation can also be attributed to the increase in female participation at all levels of the sport; andWHEREAS, Girls' wrestling has been the fastest-growing high school sport in the country over the past five years; andWHEREAS, The third PIAA Championship Playoff with both boys and girls wrestling in two distinct championship systems was held March 5 through 7, 2026; andWHEREAS, Participation in wrestling continues to grow and leads to invaluable opportunities to compete and succeed in life; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the Senate designate June 1, 2026, as "PIAA Wrestling Day of Champions" in Pennsylvania.20260SR0311PN1699 - 2 - 1234567891011121314151617181920
