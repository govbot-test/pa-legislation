2025-2026 Regular Session HB 2197 PN 2888 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 2888 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE BILL No.2197 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY KAZEEM, PIELLI, HANBIDGE, SANCHEZ, PROBST, VENKAT, McNEILL, KENYATTA, WAXMAN, MADDEN, CIRESI, OTTEN AND SCHLOSSBERG, FEBRUARY 4, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON EDUCATION, FEBRUARY 5, 2026 AN ACTAmending the act of March 10, 1949 (P.L.30, No.14), entitled "An act relating to the public school system, including certain provisions applicable as well to private and parochial schools; amending, revising, consolidating and changing the laws relating thereto," in pupils and attendance, further providing for attendance policy at charter, regional charter and cyber charter schools.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Section 1327.2 of the act of March 10, 1949 (P.L.30, No.14), known as the Public School Code of 1949, is amended by adding a subsection to read:Section 1327.2. Attendance Policy at Charter, Regional Charter and Cyber Charter Schools.--* * *(d) A board of trustees of a charter, regional charter or cyber charter school shall report to the department each fiscal year the number of students who returned to the school district of residence or were otherwise removed from the charter, regional charter or cyber charter school.Section 2. This act shall take effect in 60 days.1234567891011121314151617181920
