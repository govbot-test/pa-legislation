AN ACT
Amending Title 23 (Domestic Relations) of the Pennsylvania
Consolidated Statutes, in child protective services,
providing for information for school districts.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Title 23 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated
Statutes is amended by adding a section to read:
§ 6334.2. Information for school districts.
(a) Regulation.--The department shall promulgate regulations
and establish procedures for best practices for the county
agency to notify a school building principal, a certified school
guidance counselor or an identified mental health professional
when a student at that school building is engaged in a traumatic
event. A traumatic event shall be defined as an event which
induces trauma as defined under section 102 of the act of March
10, 1949 (P.L.30, No.14), known as the Public School Code of
1949.
(b) Confidential information.--The department and the county
