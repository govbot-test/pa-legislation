2025-2026 Regular Session HR 567 PN 3604 Bill Text (HTM) PRINTER'S NO. 3604 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.567 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY KAZEEM, CEPEDA-FREYTIZ, KINKEAD, TIBURCIO, PIELLI, McNEILL, HOWARD, MADDEN, SHUSTERMAN, MAYES, VENKAT, VITALI, PROBST, SANCHEZ, RIVERA, WAXMAN, HOHENSTEIN, McANDREW, PASHINSKI, O'MARA, D. WILLIAMS AND CIRESI, JUNE 12, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON HEALTH, JUNE 15, 2026 A RESOLUTIONDesignating the month of June 2026 as "Gun Violence Awareness Month" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, Gun violence is a national, State and local problem affecting Americans in cities, suburbs and rural communities; andWHEREAS, Nearly 130 people are killed and more than 200 people are injured with firearms each day in the United States; andWHEREAS, An average of 60 children and teens are shot and killed or injured each day in the United States; andWHEREAS, In 2024, firearms were used in 76% of all homicides in the United States and 57% of all suicides; andWHEREAS, Among American adults, 21% have been personally threatened with a firearm, 19% report that a family member was killed by firearm, 17% have personally witnessed someone being shot with a firearm and 4% have been injured with a firearm; andWHEREAS, Gun violence is the leading cause of death for 1234567891011121314151617 children under 18 years of age; andWHEREAS, In this Commonwealth, an individual is killed with a firearm every five hours; andWHEREAS, A law enforcement officer in this Commonwealth is fatally shot every six months; andWHEREAS, This year, "National Gun Violence Awareness Month" is being observed in June; andWHEREAS, "Gun Violence Awareness Month" is a nationwide and Statewide occasion to recognize the impact of gun violence; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives designate the month of June 2026 as "Gun Violence Awareness Month" in Pennsylvania; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives acknowledge this public safety and public health issue; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives memorialize the lives lost to gun violence in this Commonwealth.20260HR0567PN3604 - 2 - 1234567891011121314151617
