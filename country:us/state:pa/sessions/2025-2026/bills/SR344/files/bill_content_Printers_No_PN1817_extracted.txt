2025-2026 Regular Session SR 344 PN 1817 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 1817 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIASENATE RESOLUTION No.344 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY MASTRIANO, PENNYCUICK, ROBINSON, ROTHMAN, YAW, BROOKS, BROWN, HAYWOOD, KANE, MARTIN, STEFANO, TARTAGLIONE, VOGEL AND J. WARD, JUNE 15, 2026 REFERRED TO RULES AND EXECUTIVE NOMINATIONS, JUNE 15, 2026 A RESOLUTIONDesignating September 25, 2026, as "Disabled American Veterans Day" in Pennsylvania in recognition of the men and women who have borne the wounds of war, and the unwavering advocacy and support of the Disabled American Veterans.WHEREAS, Founded in 1920 and chartered by Congress in 1932, the Disabled American Veterans (DAV) is a nonprofit organization dedicated to delivering comprehensive services on behalf of our nation's veterans with disabilities and their families, ensuring the fulfillment of basic promises; andWHEREAS, September 25, 1920, is considered to be the birth date of the DAV; andWHEREAS, The DAV assists with United States Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) claims and appeals, ensuring access to earned benefits; andWHEREAS, The DAV Transportation Network offers critical no-cost rides to VA medical appointments, ensuring access to health care; andWHEREAS, DAV employment and volunteer programs and outreach 123456789101112131415161718 events foster engagement in rural and metropolitan areas alike, ensuring access to local resources and partnerships; andWHEREAS, DAV volunteers and service officers exemplify the commitment to leaving no veteran behind; andWHEREAS, Their life-changing support reaches more than 1 million veterans nationwide each year; andWHEREAS, Veterans with disabilities who reside in this Commonwealth have made profound sacrifices in service to our nation; andWHEREAS, "Disabled American Veterans Day" honors them, raises public awareness of their challenges and highlights the DAV's impact; andWHEREAS, "Disabled American Veterans Day" permits us to reflect on the contributions, hardships and sacrifices of veterans with disabilities; andWHEREAS, "Disabled American Veterans Day" permits us to renew our commitment to the critical support we owe them; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the Senate designate September 25, 2026, as "Disabled American Veterans Day" in Pennsylvania in recognition of the men and women who have borne the wounds of war, and the unwavering advocacy and support of the Disabled American Veterans.20260SR0344PN1817 - 2 - 1234567891011121314151617181920212223
