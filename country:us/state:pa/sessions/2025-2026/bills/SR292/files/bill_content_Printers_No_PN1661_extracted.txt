2025-2026 Regular Session SR 292 PN 1661 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 1661 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIASENATE RESOLUTION No.292 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY SANTARSIERO, KEARNEY, MILLER, COMITTA, HAYWOOD, L. WILLIAMS, SCHWANK, TARTAGLIONE, BROWN, STREET, COSTA, VOGEL, FLYNN AND MALONE, MAY 1, 2026 REFERRED TO RULES AND EXECUTIVE NOMINATIONS, MAY 1, 2026 A RESOLUTIONDesignating May 8, 2026, as "Provider Appreciation Day" in Pennsylvania to recognize child care providers for their important work and how valuable they are to our communities.WHEREAS, Child Care Aware® of America and other organizations nationwide are recognizing child care providers on May 8; andWHEREAS, The child care system supports children's growth, development and educational advancement while creating positive economic impacts for families and communities; andWHEREAS, Child care providers prove essential to the health of our communities by fostering safe and welcoming spaces, supporting working families and creating enriching environments where children develop lifelong skills; andWHEREAS, The Senate recognizes that child care has been and continues to be a lifeline for families, communities and the economy; andWHEREAS, As such, the Senate has demonstrated its commitment to strengthening the child care and early-learning workforce by providing much-needed support to help sustain the viability of 123456789101112131415161718 child care programs, including the creation of a new Child Care Staff Recruitment and Retention line item and an increase of Pre-K Counts reimbursement rates in the 2025-2026 budget; andWHEREAS, These investments by the Commonwealth enable bonus grant awards for child care staff and allow prekindergarten programs to increase wages for teachers; andWHEREAS, Support for affordable, accessible and high-quality child care represents a worthy commitment to help families work, support children's healthy development and learning and keep our economy growing; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the Senate designate May 8, 2026, as "Provider Appreciation Day" in Pennsylvania to recognize child care providers for their important work and how valuable they are to our communities.20260SR0292PN1661 - 2 - 1234567891011121314
