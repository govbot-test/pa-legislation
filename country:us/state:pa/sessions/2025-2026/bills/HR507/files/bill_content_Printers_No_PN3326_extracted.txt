2025-2026 Regular Session HR 507 PN 3326 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 3326 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.507 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY WENTLING, HARKINS, NEILSON, COOK, MALONEY, WAXMAN, GREINER, KHAN, MERSKI, MAZZOCCO, VITALI, BANTA, RIVERA, ROWE AND HOHENSTEIN, APRIL 29, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON TOURISM, RECREATION AND ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT, APRIL 29, 2026 A RESOLUTIONRecognizing the month of May 2026 as "National Bike Month" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, Cycling is a great form of exercise and sport; andWHEREAS, Cycling is a healthy and practical way for people of all ages to travel between home, school and work; andWHEREAS, Cycling trails cover more than 2,400 miles across this Commonwealth; andWHEREAS, Pennsylvania offers a multitude of mountain-biking trails, gravel riding, bicycle clubs, a world class velodrome and award-winning cycling communities; andWHEREAS, "National Bike Month" promotes the safe use of public roads and trails by cyclists; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives recognize the month of May 2026 as "National Bike Month" in Pennsylvania.1234567891011121314
