A RESOLUTION
Honoring the life, valor and sacrifice of United States Army
Specialist Krystofer Lee Lopez and expressing deepest
condolences and enduring gratitude to his family.
WHEREAS, The Senate of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
pauses in solemn remembrance to honor United States Army Private
First Class Krystofer Lee Lopez, who tragically lost his life on
March 12, 2026, during a training incident at Letterkenny Army
Depot in Chambersburg, Franklin County, while faithfully serving
his nation; and
WHEREAS, Specialist Lopez, a resident of Biglerville, Adams
County, embodied the highest ideals of patriotism and selfless
service, choosing at a young age to dedicate his life to the
defense of the United States by enlisting in the United States
Army during his junior year of high school; and
WHEREAS, During his time at Biglerville High School,
Specialist Lopez distinguished himself as a member of the
school's wrestling program, demonstrating perseverance, strength
of character and determination, qualities that would guide him
throughout his life and military service; and

WHEREAS, After graduation, Specialist Lopez further pursued
his education at Shippensburg University, continuing to prepare
himself for a future defined by responsibility, service and
leadership; and
WHEREAS, Specialist Lopez proudly served as a 31B Military
Police Officer with LEAD Directorate of Emergency Services Law
Enforcement Division, where he was assigned to the 947th
Quartermaster Company and carried out his duties with honor,
vigilance and unwavering commitment to the safety and security
of others; and
WHEREAS, The Letterkenny Army Depot community, led by
Commander Colonel Caleb Lewis, expressed profound sorrow and
heartbreak at the loss of this young soldier, recognizing
Specialist Lopez as a valued member of their team whose
dedication to the mission and to his fellow soldiers reflected
the highest standards of military service; and
WHEREAS, In recognition of his dedicated service and
sacrifice, Private First Class Lopez was posthumously promoted
to the rank of Specialist, ensuring that his service and
commitment to duty are honored and remembered; and
WHEREAS, Specialist Lopez is remembered by his loved ones as
a young man of compassion, loyalty and joy, whose twin sister,
Kiki Lopez, lovingly described him as the "greatest brother
ever," a brother who always made people laugh, who always placed
his family first and whose bright smile brought light to those
around him; and
WHEREAS, The communities of Biglerville, Adams County and
throughout this Commonwealth have come together in grief and
remembrance, offering prayers and support to the Lopez family
while honoring a young man whose life, though tragically brief,

WHEREAS, Though only 18 years of age, Specialist Lopez
