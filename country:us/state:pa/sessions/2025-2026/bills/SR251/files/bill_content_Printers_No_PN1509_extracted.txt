andWHEREAS, Specialist Lopez, a resident of Biglerville, Adams County, embodied the highest ideals of patriotism and selfless service, choosing at a young age to dedicate his life to the defense of the United States by enlisting in the United States Army during his junior year of high school; andWHEREAS, During his time at Biglerville High School, Specialist Lopez distinguished himself as a member of the school's wrestling program, demonstrating perseverance, strength of character and determination, qualities that would guide him throughout his life and military service; and12345678910111213141516171819 WHEREAS, After graduation, Specialist Lopez further pursued his education at Shippensburg University, continuing to prepare himself for a future defined by responsibility, service and leadership; andWHEREAS, Specialist Lopez proudly served as a 31B Military Police Officer with LEAD Directorate of Emergency Services Law Enforcement Division, where he was assigned to the 947th Quartermaster Company and carried out his duties with honor, vigilance and unwavering commitment to the safety and security of others; andWHEREAS, The Letterkenny Army Depot community, led by Commander Colonel Caleb Lewis, expressed profound sorrow and heartbreak at the loss of this young soldier, recognizing Specialist Lopez as a valued member of their team whose dedication to the mission and to his fellow soldiers reflected the highest standards of military service; andWHEREAS, In recognition of his dedicated service and sacrifice, Private First Class Lopez was posthumously promoted to the rank of Specialist, ensuring that his service and commitment to duty are honored and remembered; andWHEREAS, Specialist Lopez is remembered by his loved ones as a young man of compassion, loyalty and joy, whose twin sister, Kiki Lopez, lovingly described him as the "greatest brother ever," a brother who always made people laugh, who always placed his family first and whose bright smile brought light to those around him;
