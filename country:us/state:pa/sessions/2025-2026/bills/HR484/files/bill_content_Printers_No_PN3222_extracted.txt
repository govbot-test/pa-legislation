2025-2026 Regular Session HR 484 PN 3222 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 3222 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.484 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY SOLOMON, MAJOR, HOGAN, K. HARRIS, KHAN, HILL-EVANS, GAYDOS, SANCHEZ, INGLIS AND SMITH-WADE-EL, APRIL 15, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON HOUSING AND COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT, APRIL 16, 2026 A CONCURRENT RESOLUTIONDirecting the Joint State Government Commission to study the creation of a Statewide housing accelerator and to conduct a comprehensive review of State and local housing approval and permitting processes that delay residential construction.WHEREAS, Communities across this Commonwealth are experiencing a shortage of housing that is attainable and sufficient to meet demand; andWHEREAS, Limited housing supply contributes to rising costs, workforce shortages and economic strain across Pennsylvania; andWHEREAS, Many shovel-ready housing projects face long delays due to inconsistent State and local permitting requirements, conservation district backlogs and uneven local land development review processes; andWHEREAS, Prolonged approval timelines increase residential construction costs and discourage the timely development of new housing; andWHEREAS, A coordinated Statewide approach could help identify and reduce regulatory barriers that unnecessarily delay 123456789101112131415161718 residential construction; therefore be itRESOLVED (the Senate concurring), That the General Assembly direct the Joint State Government Commission to study whether the Commonwealth should create a Statewide housing accelerator to assist in advancing shovel-ready housing projects; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the study include a comprehensive review of State and local housing-related policies and procedures, including zoning, conservation district approvals, land development regulations and permitting processes to identify barriers that delay residential construction; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the Joint State Government Commission evaluate best practices from other states to develop recommendations to streamline approvals, reduce unnecessary delays and expand housing attainability across this Commonwealth; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the Joint State Government Commission report its findings and recommendations to the General Assembly no later than one year after the adoption of this resolution.20260HR0484PN3222 - 2 - 12345678910111213141516171819
