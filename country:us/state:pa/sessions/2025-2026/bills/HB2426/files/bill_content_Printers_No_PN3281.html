AN ACT
Amending the act of March 29, 1803 (P.L.542, No.156), entitled
"An act to establish a Board of Wardens for the port of
Philadelphia, and for the regulation of pilots and pilotages,
and for other purposes therein mentioned," further providing
for pilots in the Delaware Bay and River.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Section 17(a) of the act of March 29, 1803
(P.L.542, No.156), entitled "An act to establish a Board of
Wardens for the port of Philadelphia, and for the regulation of
pilots and pilotages, and for other purposes therein mentioned,"
is amended to read:
Section 17. (a) Every person not now a licensed pilot, and
who desires to exercise the profession of pilot in the Delaware
Bay and River, shall apply to the Navigation Commission for the
Delaware River and its navigable tributaries for a license to
entitle the person to follow that occupation; and it shall be
the duty of the Navigation Commission to call to their
