AN ACT
Amending Title 18 (Crimes and Offenses) of the Pennsylvania
Consolidated Statutes, in burglary and other criminal
intrusion, further providing for the offense of unlawful use
of unmanned aircraft.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Section 3505(b)(1) of Title 18 of the
Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is amended, subsections (a)
and (c) are amended by adding paragraphs and subsection (f) is
amended by adding definitions to read:
§ 3505. Unlawful use of unmanned aircraft.
(a) Offense defined.--A person commits the offense of
unlawful use of unmanned aircraft if the person uses an unmanned
aircraft intentionally or knowingly to:
* * *
(2.1) Operate on the grounds or aerial space of a school
entity without written permission from the school entity.
* * *
(b) Grading.--The offense of unlawful use of unmanned

(c) Exceptions for law enforcement officers.--Subsection (a)

(f) Definitions.--As used in this section, the following

subsection unless the context clearly indicates otherwise:
* * *
"School entity." A school district, intermediate unit, area
career and technical school, charter school, cyber charter
school, regional charter school, private school or nonpublic
school.
"School security personnel." As defined in section 1301-C of
the act of March 10, 1949 (P.L.30, No.14), known as the Public
School Code of 1949.
