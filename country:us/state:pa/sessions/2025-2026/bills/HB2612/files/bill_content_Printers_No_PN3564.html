AN ACT
Designating a bridge, identified as Bridge Key 8455, on that
portion of U.S. Route 219 over Palestine Road in Adams
Township, Cambria County, as the Sgt. Jason Dale Kriedler
Memorial Bridge.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Sgt. Jason Dale Kriedler Memorial Bridge.
(a) Findings.--The General Assembly finds and declares as
follows:
(1) Jason Dale Kriedler was born December 23, 1929, in
Cambria County.
(2) Sgt. Kriedler attended South Fork High School in
Cambria County.
(3) Outside of work, Sgt. Kriedler enjoyed working on
cars.
(4) Sgt. Kriedler joined the United States Army as an
automotive mechanic, 2nd Echelon, as a Sergeant with B
Company, 2nd Engineer Battalion, 2nd Infantry Division.
(5) Sgt. Kriedler earned various honors and awards for

(6) Sgt. Jason Dale Kriedler was reported missing-in-

Reservoir in North Korea.
(b) Designation.--The bridge, identified as Bridge Key 8455,
carrying U.S. Route 219 over Palestine Road in Adams Township,
Cambria County, is designated as the Sgt. Jason Dale Kriedler
Memorial Bridge.
(c) Signs.--The Department of Transportation shall erect and
maintain appropriate signs displaying the name of the bridge to
traffic in both directions on the bridge.
Section 2. Effective date.
This act shall take effect in 60 days.
