A RESOLUTION
Urging the Congress of the United States to enact legislation
for the payment of Transportation Security Officers during a
government shutdown.
WHEREAS, Transportation Security Officers (TSOs) are
employees of the Transportation Security Administration and are
responsible for screening passengers, baggage and cargo at
airports across the nation; and
WHEREAS, Operating as the front line of security at more than
400 airports, TSOs ensure safety by detecting prohibited items
and verifying the identification of passengers; and
WHEREAS, As of March 2026, TSOs are working without pay due
to a partial government shutdown, and many are calling off sick
or taking leave; and
WHEREAS, Because of this situation, there are long lines for
security screenings at airports and waits of up to three hours
and longer are common; and
WHEREAS, The Congress of the United States needs to enact
legislation that provides funding for TSOs during the current
