2025-2026 Regular Session HR 459 PN 3095 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 3095 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.459 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY HARKINS, FREEMAN, WAXMAN, MERSKI, JAMES, KHAN, CONKLIN, MALAGARI, STEELE, PASHINSKI, NEILSON, SANCHEZ, D. WILLIAMS AND CIRESI, MARCH 25, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON TRANSPORTATION, MARCH 26, 2026 A RESOLUTIONUrging the Congress of the United States to enact legislation for the payment of Transportation Security Officers during a government shutdown.WHEREAS, Transportation Security Officers (TSOs) are employees of the Transportation Security Administration and are responsible for screening passengers, baggage and cargo at airports across the nation; andWHEREAS, Operating as the front line of security at more than 400 airports, TSOs ensure safety by detecting prohibited items and verifying the identification of passengers; andWHEREAS, As of March 2026, TSOs are working without pay due to a partial government shutdown, and many are calling off sick or taking leave; andWHEREAS, Because of this situation, there are long lines for security screenings at airports and waits of up to three hours and longer are common; andWHEREAS, The Congress of the United States needs to enact legislation that provides funding for TSOs during the current 123456789101112131415161718 government shutdown and provide a means by which this situation does not recur in the future; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania urge the Congress of the United States to enact legislation for the payment of Transportation Security Officers during a government shutdown.20260HR0459PN3095 - 2 - 123456
