2025-2026 Regular Session HR 371 PN 2636 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 2636 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.371 Session of 2025 INTRODUCED BY GROVE, JAMES, PICKETT, ANDERSON, BERNSTINE AND WATRO, NOVEMBER 19, 2025 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON JUDICIARY, NOVEMBER 19, 2025 A RESOLUTIONDirecting the Joint State Government Commission to conduct a thorough and comprehensive study on the feasibility of the establishment by the Pennsylvania National Guard and the Pennsylvania State Police of a joint police and tactical unit training facility at Fort Indiantown Gap and issue a report containing findings and recommendations.WHEREAS, Pennsylvania has more than 25,000 police officers at the State and local levels and more than 1,200 municipal police departments, regional police departments and county detective agencies that serve local communities by investigating crimes and filing criminal charges; andWHEREAS, The Municipal Police Officers' Education and Training Commission sets the Statewide curriculum and minimum standards for basic and in-service police training; andWHEREAS, In addition to basic police training, police officers also need proper tactical training to fulfill aspects of their job; andWHEREAS, Tactical training for police officers includes specialized courses involving high-risk scenarios, including active shooter incidents, hostage rescues and barricaded 1234567891011121314151617181920 suspects; andWHEREAS, For many police officers in this Commonwealth, this type of tactical training is provided by specialized academies, regional facilities and private vendors; andWHEREAS, Tactical units often train as collaborative regional teams rather than on a county-by-county basis; andWHEREAS, Regional tactical training centers in this Commonwealth include the Montgomery County Tactical Response Training Center and the Chester County Tactical Village; andWHEREAS, Recent high-profile cases in this Commonwealth have highlighted the dangerous encounters that police officers face when needing to employ tactical maneuvers and coordination; andWHEREAS, The availability of more tactical unit training centers in this Commonwealth would help ensure that more police officers continue to receive this invaluable training; andWHEREAS, A study on the feasibility of the Pennsylvania National Guard and the Pennsylvania State Police establishing a joint police and tactical unit training facility at Fort Indiantown Gap would provide valuable insight into determining whether the training facility would be a sound investment to help ensure that officers receive necessary tactical unit training; andWHEREAS, By conducting a study on this topic, the Joint State Government Commission could help identify whether the training facility would be beneficial and whether Fort Indiantown Gap would be the proper location; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the study:(1) ascertain the estimated costs necessary for building and constructing a joint police and tactical unit training facility;(2) ascertain any other start-up costs associated with building and constructing a joint police and tactical unit training facility;(3) examine successful models and best practices implemented by other facilities and jurisdictions to help develop a model curriculum;(4) provide recommendations for the development of continuing education courses that can be taken by law enforcement officers on an annual basis;(5) provide recommendations for the development of tactical training and courses that feature different local law enforcement agencies working together with the Pennsylvania State Police in a county;(6) examine the utility of allowing other types of law enforcement officers to train at the joint police and tactical unit training facility, including sheriffs and deputy sheriffs;(7) determine the feasibility of establishing the joint police and tactical unit training facility at Fort Indiantown Gap or at another location in this Commonwealth;
