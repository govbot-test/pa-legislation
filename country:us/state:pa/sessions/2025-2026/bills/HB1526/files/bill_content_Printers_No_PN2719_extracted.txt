HARRIS AND KHAN, MAY 29, 2025 AS AMENDED ON SECOND CONSIDERATION, HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, DECEMBER 17, 2025AN ACTAmending Title 66 (Public Utilities) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes, in powers and duties, providing for degraded natural gas pipeline prevention plans.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Title 66 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is amended by adding a section to read:§ 531. Degraded natural gas pipeline prevention plans.(a) Commission duties.--The commission shall:(1) Establish a system to receive annual reports from natural gas distribution companies PUBLIC UTILITIES required under subsection (b) to issue a degraded natural gas pipeline prevention plan.(2) Prepare guidance for natural gas distribution companies PUBLIC UTILITIES on how to determine the integrity of their natural gas distribution systems.(3) Provide guidance on mitigating threats to existing Aldyl A polyethylene natural gas pipelines and other <--<--<--123456789101112131415161718 potentially compromised plastic pipelines.(4) Prescribe the methodology to be used by natural gas distribution companies PUBLIC UTILITIES to mitigate insufficient records necessary to identify each pipeline segment containing Aldyl A polyethylene piping.(5) Provide guidance to determine the placement of critical valves to ensure that natural gas distribution companies PUBLIC UTILITIES are considering potential risks, emergency response times and any threat to the nearby population.(6) Provide recommendations and best practices on the placement of critical valves.(b) Duty to issue plan.--Within one year after the effective date of this section, and every January 1 thereafter, a natural gas distribution company PUBLIC UTILITY operating in this Commonwealth shall issue to the commission a degraded natural gas pipeline prevention plan that contains:(1) An inventory of all Aldyl A polyethylene natural gas assets recording the following:(i) The age of the Aldyl A polyethylene natural gas asset.(ii) Any sign of degradation of a pipeline or junction.(iii) Potential local environmental threats, including increased room temperatures, other compromised systems in the building, the presence of steam or environmental factors that could degrade the natural gas asset.(iv) Any other information deemed necessary by the commission.20250HB1526PN2719 -
