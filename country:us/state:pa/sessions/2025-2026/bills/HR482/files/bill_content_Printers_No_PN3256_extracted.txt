2025-2026 Regular Session HR 482 PN 3256 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 3256 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.482 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY GILLEN, SOLOMON, CONKLIN, HAMM, HOHENSTEIN, KRUPA, KHAN, COOK, RAPP, KUZMA, GUENST, STAATS, SHAFFER, RIVERA, VENKAT, MEHAFFIE, SAPPEY, PUGH, MALAGARI, NEILSON, MOUL, GALLAGHER, BOROWSKI, BENNINGHOFF, WHITE AND PASHINSKI, APRIL 15, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON VETERANS AFFAIRS AND EMERGENCY PREPAREDNESS, APRIL 22, 2026 A CONCURRENT RESOLUTIONRecognizing July 4, 2026, as "Independence Day" in Pennsylvania and honoring the 250th anniversary of the United States.WHEREAS, On July 4, 1776, in Philadelphia, the members of the Second Continental Congress adopted "The Unanimous Declaration of the Thirteen United States of America," popularly known as the Declaration of Independence, asserting independence from Great Britain; andWHEREAS, The year 2026 marks the 250th anniversary of the adoption of the Declaration of Independence and thereby our nation's birth; andWHEREAS, A celebration of the nation's birth in 2026 will provide a milestone opportunity to commemorate the nation's historic achievements, as well as our history and culture both as a nation and as a Commonwealth; andWHEREAS, This Commonwealth played a pivotal role in the founding of the United States and gained its title as "The 12345678910111213141516 Keystone State" by being the final state to ratify the Declaration of Independence; andWHEREAS, The Greater Philadelphia Area was the site of the meeting of the First and Second Continental Congresses, the drafting and signing of the Declaration of Independence and the Constitution of the United States, seminal battles of the Revolutionary War such as Princeton and Brandywine and the historic encampment at Valley Forge; andWHEREAS, Several foreign governments and individuals from nations such as France and Poland assisted the American colonies in their fight for independence, and several nations such as Morocco were early supporters of the young United States, thereby making the anniversary of the founding of the United States an occasion for both national and international celebration; andWHEREAS, The Greater Philadelphia Area has hosted major celebrations honoring landmark anniversaries of the nation's birth, including the nation's first world's fair at the 1876 Centennial Exhibition, the Sesquicentennial of 1926 and the Bicentennial of 1976; andWHEREAS, This Commonwealth contains numerous historical sites connected to our nation's birth and revolutionary-period history, including Independence Hall, the Liberty Bell, the Graff House site, where Thomas Jefferson drafted the Declaration of Independence, the site of Benjamin Franklin's house, the Betsy Ross house and the site of the President's house; andWHEREAS, This celebration will be held shortly after the 250th anniversary of the United States Navy and Marine Corps on October 13, 2025; andWHEREAS, The 250th anniversary of the United States Navy and 20260HR0482PN3256 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 Marine Corps are to be celebrated with several ships sailing along the Delaware River from Philadelphia to the Camden area; andWHEREAS, The United States Navy was founded in this Commonwealth on October 13, 1775, when the Second Continental Congress at Independence Hall in Philadelphia authorized its first ships; andWHEREAS, Later that year, in this Commonwealth, the Continental Navy commissioned its first ship and granted its first commission to John Barry, the father of the American Navy; andWHEREAS, The Second Continental Congress in this Commonwealth commissioned Lieutenant John Paul Jones who, on the Delaware River, was the first to raise an American national flag, the Grand Union Flag, on a naval vessel; andWHEREAS, The United States Marine Corps was born later that year after its first commissioned officer Captain Samuel Nicholas held a recruiting drive in Tun Tavern in Philadelphia; andWHEREAS, The Tun Tavern was the location where John Adams and the Naval Committee met in 1775 to write the documents that structured what would become the United States Navy; andWHEREAS, The first United States Marine Corps amphibious operation, mounted from this Commonwealth, seized sorely needed gunpowder for the Continental Army; andWHEREAS, The 250th anniversary of the United States Marine Corps coincides with the 100th Marine Corps Birthday Ball to be held on November 10, 2025; andWHEREAS, The United States Marine Corps' 250th birthday should be celebrated alongside the rebuilding of the historic 20260HR0482PN3256 - 3 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 Tun Tavern in Philadelphia by The Tun Legacy Foundation, which is set to open its doors in November 2025, drawing United States Marines from this Commonwealth and beyond; andWHEREAS, Huntington Ingall Industries recently launched their first San-Antonio Class Flight II Amphibious Support Dock known as the "USS Harrisburg" on October 5, 2024; andWHEREAS, The USS Harrisburg was christened on January 11, 2025, by Alexandra Curry and Jennifer Diaz and is set to be fully commissioned in 2026; andWHEREAS, Mrs. Curry is a native of Slatington and is the wife of Jim Curry, the sitting mayor of Middletown; andWHEREAS, Mrs. Curry was recognized on the House Floor as one of the sponsors of the USS Harrisburg on October 10, 2019, by Speaker Michael Turzai; andWHEREAS, Mrs. Diaz is a native of central Pennsylvania who currently serves as the Director of Major Gifts for the Salvation Army of Philadelphia and her husband Chris Diaz is currently serving as the Chief of Staff to the Secretary of the Navy; andWHEREAS, The United States Navy's 250-year history should be celebrated by assembling ships, including the Pennsylvania State Flagship Niagara, from every major war the United States Navy has fought; andWHEREAS, This assemblage would be possible only in this Commonwealth because Philadelphia has preserved USS Olympia, the sole survivor of the Spanish-American War; andWHEREAS, This Commonwealth has continued to design and build ships and aircraft and has contributed sons and daughters to lead the United States Navy and the United States Marine Corps since their birth;
