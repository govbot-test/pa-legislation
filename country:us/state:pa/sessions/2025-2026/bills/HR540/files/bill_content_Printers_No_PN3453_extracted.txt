2025-2026 Regular Session HR 540 PN 3453 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 3453 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.540 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY MENTZER, MADDEN, VENKAT, MAYES, HANBIDGE, GOUGHNOUR, PASHINSKI, CEPEDA-FREYTIZ, NEILSON AND PUGH, MAY 27, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON AGING AND OLDER ADULT SERVICES, MAY 27, 2026 A RESOLUTIONRecognizing June 15, 2026, as "World Elder Abuse Awareness Day" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, In fiscal year 2024-2025, more than 66,000 Statewide reports of elder abuse were received, and more than 15,700 of those cases were substantiated through investigation, with the number of suspicious death reports totaling 154; andWHEREAS, The top three forms of substantiated abuse were self-neglect, caregiver neglect and financial exploitation; andWHEREAS, A total of 1,317 reports of elder abuse in this Commonwealth originated from mandated abuse reports, reflecting a 45% increase over the past five years; andWHEREAS, Risk factors that increase the likelihood of abuse, neglect, exploitation or abandonment include living alone, social isolation, physical or cognitive incapacitation and more; andWHEREAS, The National Center on Elder Abuse is observing "World Elder Abuse Awareness Day" on June 15, 2026; and1234567891011121314151617 WHEREAS, "World Elder Abuse Awareness Day" was launched by the International Network for the Prevention of Elder Abuse in 2006 and recognized as a United Nations Observance in 2011; andWHEREAS, "World Elder Abuse Awareness Day" calls attention to the issue of elder abuse and serves as a reminder for individuals, communities, lawmakers and organizations to do their part in helping to identify and prevent elder abuse; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives recognize June 15, 2026, as "World Elder Abuse Awareness Day" in Pennsylvania; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives continue to support efforts to protect the health, safety and welfare of older adults in this Commonwealth and urge all Pennsylvanians to take an active stand against elder abuse by promoting vigilance, education and community awareness of this critical issue.20260HR0540PN3453 - 2 - 12345678910111213141516
