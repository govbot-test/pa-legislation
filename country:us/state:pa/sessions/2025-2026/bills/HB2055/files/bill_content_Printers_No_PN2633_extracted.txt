2025-2026 Regular Session HB 2055 PN 2633 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 2633 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE BILL No.2055 Session of 2025 INTRODUCED BY GROVE, JAMES, PICKETT, ANDERSON, BERNSTINE AND WATRO, NOVEMBER 19, 2025 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON JUDICIARY, NOVEMBER 19, 2025 AN ACTAmending Title 53 (Municipalities Generally) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes, in employees, providing for basic tactical operations course.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Title 53 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is amended by adding a section to read:§ 2167.1. Basic tactical operations course.(a) Development and implementation.--The commission shall develop and implement a basic tactical operations course as part of the mandatory basic training required under section 2167 (relating to police training).(b) Course content.--The basic tactical operations course under this section shall include instruction and practical exercises in the following areas:(1) Safe building entry and room clearing procedures.(2) Team coordination and communication during tactical operations.123456789101112131415161718 (3) Threat identification and decision-making under stress.(4) Use of cover and concealment in dynamic environments.(5) Prevention of friendly fire and crossfire incidents.(6) Scenario-based training emphasizing police officer safety, civilian protection and lawful use of force.(c) Applicability.--Completion of the basic tactical operations course under this section shall be a prerequisite for certification as a police officer under this chapter.Section 2. This act shall take effect in 60 days.20250HB2055PN2633 - 2 - 1234567891011
