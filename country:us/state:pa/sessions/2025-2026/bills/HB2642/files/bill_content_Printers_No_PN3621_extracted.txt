2025-2026 Regular Session HB 2642 PN 3621 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 3621 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE BILL No.2642 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY SCHEUREN, ROAE, SMITH, KUZMA, MOUL AND STEHR, JUNE 16, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON TRANSPORTATION, JUNE 16, 2026 AN ACTAmending Title 18 (Crimes and Offenses) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes, in nuisances, further providing for the offense of scattering rubbish.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Section 6501(a)(1) of Title 18 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is amended to read:§ 6501. Scattering rubbish.(a) Offense defined.--A person is guilty of an offense if he:(1) causes any waste paper, sweepings, ashes, household waste, grass clippings or other landscaping matter, glass, metal, refuse or rubbish, or any dangerous or detrimental substance to be deposited into or upon any road, street, highway, alley or railroad right-of-way, or upon the land of another or into the waters of this Commonwealth;* * *Section 2. This act shall take effect in 60 days.123456789101112131415161718
