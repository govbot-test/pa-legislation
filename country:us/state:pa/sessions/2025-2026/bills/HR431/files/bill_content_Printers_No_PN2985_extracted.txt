2025-2026 Regular Session HR 431 PN 2985 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 2985 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.431 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY GUZMAN, MARCH 11, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON LOCAL GOVERNMENT, MARCH 11, 2026 A RESOLUTIONDesignating March 11, 2026, as "Berks County 274th Anniversary Day" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, Berks County has a rich cultural, historical and natural heritage, dating back to its founding communities and early settlers; andWHEREAS, Reading, the county seat of Berks County, was founded in 1748 and incorporated as a city on March 16, 1847; andWHEREAS, Reading long served as a center of growth and development assisting in the growth of Berks County; andWHEREAS, Berks County has grown from a modest early settlement to a thriving community with a diverse population and a vibrant economy, including industries such as manufacturing, agriculture, health care, tourism, education and services; andWHEREAS, The county has long honored its history through historical societies, archives, markers and community events, highlighting its contributions to this Commonwealth; andWHEREAS, Berks County will soon celebrate its 275th 123456789101112131415161718 anniversary, marking nearly three centuries since its establishment; andWHEREAS, Berks County formally kicks off its yearlong 275th anniversary celebration on March 11, 2026, continuing throughout the year until 2027, commemorating its heritage, achievements and community spirit; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives designate March 11, 2026, as "Berks County 274th Anniversary Day"; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives commend the residents, leaders and organizations whose dedication and contributions have made Berks County a vibrant and welcoming place to live, work and raise a family.20260HR0431PN2985 - 2 - 12345678910111213
