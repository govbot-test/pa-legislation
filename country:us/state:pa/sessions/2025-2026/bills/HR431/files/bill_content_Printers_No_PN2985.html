A RESOLUTION
Designating March 11, 2026, as "Berks County 274th Anniversary
Day" in Pennsylvania.
WHEREAS, Berks County has a rich cultural, historical and
natural heritage, dating back to its founding communities and
early settlers; and
WHEREAS, Reading, the county seat of Berks County, was
founded in 1748 and incorporated as a city on March 16, 1847;
and
WHEREAS, Reading long served as a center of growth and
development assisting in the growth of Berks County; and
WHEREAS, Berks County has grown from a modest early
settlement to a thriving community with a diverse population and
a vibrant economy, including industries such as manufacturing,
agriculture, health care, tourism, education and services; and
WHEREAS, The county has long honored its history through
historical societies, archives, markers and community events,
highlighting its contributions to this Commonwealth; and
WHEREAS, Berks County will soon celebrate its 275th
