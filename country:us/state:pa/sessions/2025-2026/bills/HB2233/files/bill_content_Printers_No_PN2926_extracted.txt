2025-2026 Regular Session HB 2233 PN 2926 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 2926 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE BILL No.2233 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY BRENNAN, FREEMAN, HAMM, PROBST, HARKINS, SANCHEZ, ZIMMERMAN, HADDOCK, HILL-EVANS, CEPEDA-FREYTIZ, MADDEN AND WARREN, FEBRUARY 19, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON TRANSPORTATION, FEBRUARY 20, 2026 AN ACTAmending Title 75 (Vehicles) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes, in registration of vehicles, further providing for vehicles exempt from registration.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Section 1302(23) of Title 75 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is amended and the section is amended by adding a paragraph to read:§ 1302. Vehicles exempt from registration.The following types of vehicles are exempt from registration:* * *(23) Any all-terrain vehicle exempt from registration under Chapter 77 (relating to snowmobiles and all-terrain vehicles) and operated as an emergency vehicle by a police or fire department or emergency medical services agency when the vehicle does not travel on public highways in excess of [two] ten miles and is primarily for off-highway use and only operated incidentally upon the highway. An all-terrain 123456789101112131415161718 vehicle so operated shall comply with section 4571 (visual and audible signals on emergency vehicles). Notwithstanding the requirements of section 4571, an all-terrain vehicle operated under this paragraph is not required to be equipped with an audible warning system. An operator of an all-terrain vehicle under this paragraph who acts in good faith and within the scope of the operator's official duties shall be entitled to the same protections from liability as apply to operators of authorized emergency vehicles under this title.(24) Any all-terrain vehicle exempt from registration under Chapter 77 and operated as an emergency vehicle by a police or fire department or emergency medical services agency on streets closed or restricted for special events as provided under section 6109(a)(15) (relating to specific powers of department and local authorities). An all-terrain vehicle operated under this paragraph shall comply with section 4571. Notwithstanding the requirements of section 4571, an all-terrain vehicle operated under this paragraph is not required to be equipped with an audible warning system. An operator of an all-terrain vehicle under this paragraph who acts in good faith and within the scope of the operator's official duties shall be entitled to the same protections from liability as apply to operators of authorized emergency vehicles under this title.Section 2. This act shall take effect in 60 days.20260HB2233PN2926 - 2 - 12345678910111213141516171819202122232425
