2025-2026 Regular Session HR 444 PN 3018 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 3018 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.444 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY MADSEN, DAVIDSON, HOHENSTEIN, HILL-EVANS, SANCHEZ, FREEMAN, NEILSON, COOPER, FLEMING, PASHINSKI, CEPEDA-FREYTIZ AND MEHAFFIE, MARCH 17, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON TOURISM, RECREATION AND ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT, MARCH 18, 2026 A RESOLUTIONDesignating September 18, 2026, as "Fútbol Day" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, Fútbol, also known as soccer, is the most popular sport in the world with more than 3.5 billion fans; andWHEREAS, The sport as we know it today has its roots in late 19th century Britain during the period of industrialization and urbanization, where industrial workers, who often had Saturday afternoons off work, played in and spectated games organized by churches, trade unions and schools; andWHEREAS, Once fútbol spread to the rest of Europe, the Federation Internationale de Football Association (FIFA) was founded in Paris in 1904 to serve as the governing body of fútbol, and today consists of 205 member associations, 300,000 clubs and more than 240 million registered players; andWHEREAS, Fútbol is a unique sport that unites people all around the world, crossing origins, social classes and gender, and even stimulating economic development where it is played; 12345678910111213141516 andWHEREAS, Christian Pulisic, born in Hershey on September 18, 1998, is known internationally as one of the greatest fútbol players from the United States; andWHEREAS, Growing up in Hershey, Mr. Pulisic went to Hersheypark every summer and developed his love for fútbol with the encouragement of his parents, Kelley and Mark Pulisic, who he credits with instilling in him at a young age the confidence he has today; andWHEREAS, At age 10, Mr. Pulisic joined the Pennsylvania Classics AC youth soccer club in Lancaster County where he developed his fútbol skill for seven years; andWHEREAS, Mr. Pulisic's professional fútbol career began in 2015 at 17 years of age when he signed to Borussia Dortmund where he played until 2019; andWHEREAS, Mr. Pulisic then moved to Chelsea F.C. until 2023 helping his team win multiple tournaments, including the 2021 FIFA Club World Cup; andWHEREAS, At present, Mr. Pulisic plays for AC Milan where he led his team to victory in the 2024 Italian Super Cup; andWHEREAS, At the age of 20 years and 63 days, Mr. Pulisic became the youngest player to captain the United States Men's National Team, where he is affectionately nicknamed "Captain America"; andWHEREAS, Mr. Pulisic continues to play as a forward in the starting lineup of the United States Men's National Team, boasting a record of 82 appearances and 32 goals, and winning the United States Soccer Male Player of the Year award four times; andWHEREAS, The 2026 FIFA World Cup, in which Mr. Pulisic is 20260HR0444PN3018 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 expected to play, will be jointly co-hosted by Canada, Mexico and the United States in the summer of 2026, with six matches scheduled to be played in Philadelphia; andWHEREAS, The FIFA World Cup is the most watched sporting event in the world, uniting people from all walks of life and parts of the world; andWHEREAS, It is an honor for this Commonwealth to be the birthplace of one of the nation's best fútbol players and the host of six 2026 FIFA World Cup games; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives designate September 18, 2026, as "Fútbol Day" in Pennsylvania.20260HR0444PN3018 - 3 - 1234567891011
