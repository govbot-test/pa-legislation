2025-2026 Regular Session HR 436 PN 2999 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 2999 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.436 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY KINKEAD, REICHARD, FREEMAN, KUTZ, ZIMMERMAN, COOPER, CONKLIN, COOK, GUZMAN, FLEMING, RIVERA AND HILL-EVANS, MARCH 13, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON AGRICULTURE AND RURAL AFFAIRS, MARCH 13, 2026 A RESOLUTIONDesignating the week of March 16 through 20, 2026, as "Pennsylvania Ag Literacy Week" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, The Pennsylvania Friends of Agriculture Foundation was created in 1986 and pursues a mission to preserve and promote agriculture in this Commonwealth; andWHEREAS, The Pennsylvania Friends of Agriculture Foundation and its sponsoring organization, the Pennsylvania Farm Bureau, are the originators of "Pennsylvania Ag Literacy Week"; andWHEREAS, The Pennsylvania Friends of Agriculture Foundation's mission includes the following:(1) enlightening the public about farming and agriculture;(2) creating educational programs for children in elementary and middle schools who may have no other exposure to agriculture;(3) training teachers on agricultural commodities and concepts while helping them meet their own continuing 1234567891011121314151617 education requirements;(4) funding scholarships for postsecondary agricultural studies; and(5) promoting career opportunities in agriculture;andWHEREAS, The Pennsylvania Friends of Agriculture Foundation continues to grow and enhance its innovative programs, including Educator's Ag Institute, Mobile Ag Education Science Lab Program, Mobile Ag Education on the Go Program and Positively 4 Kids! magazine; andWHEREAS, The Pennsylvania Friends of Agriculture Foundation seeks to deliver these programs to schools and educators across this Commonwealth; andWHEREAS, "Pennsylvania Ag Literacy Week" focuses on connecting volunteers from the agricultural community to local classrooms to share the story of agriculture; andWHEREAS, Volunteers will have an opportunity to talk about farming and agriculture by reading the 2026 "Pennsylvania Ag Literacy Week" book, as well as sharing their own stories of farming, agriculture programs with which they are involved and their connections to agriculture; andWHEREAS, The 2026 "Pennsylvania Ag Literacy Week" book is "Cows Can MOO! Can You? All About Farms" by Dr. Seuss; andWHEREAS, Agriculture exists within every county in this Commonwealth, with nearly 53,000 farms and 7.3 million acres of farmland; andWHEREAS, Agriculture is the largest of this Commonwealth's industries, contributing $132.5 billion annually in economic impact and supporting more than 593,000 jobs; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives designate the 20260HR0436PN2999 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 week of March 16 through 20, 2026, as "Pennsylvania Ag Literacy Week" in Pennsylvania; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives congratulate the originators of "Pennsylvania Ag Literacy Week," the Pennsylvania Friends of Agriculture Foundation and the Pennsylvania Farm Bureau for their contribution to this Commonwealth's agricultural industry.20260HR0436PN2999 - 3 - 1234567
