Section 301 of the act of November 10, 1999 (P.L.491, No.45), known as the Pennsylvania Construction Code Act, is amended by adding a subsection to read:Section 301. Adoption by regulations.* * *(e) Electric vehicle charging stations.--The department shall, within 180 days of the effective date of this subsection, promulgate regulations requiring new nonresidential buildings to provide electrical service capacity for electric vehicles in accordance with the following:(1) The electrical service capacity to be provided must 12345678910111213141516171819 serve the number of electric vehicle charging parking spaces as follows:(i) For parking lots with fewer than 25 total parking spaces for the use of hotels, motels, restaurants, offices or health care facilities, at least one level 1 electric vehicle charging parking space is required.(ii) In addition to the requirement under subparagraph (i), for parking lots with fewer than 25 total parking spaces for the use of restaurants, at least one level 2 electric vehicle charging parking space is required.(iii) For parking lots with at least 25 but fewer than 500 total parking spaces for the use of retail facilities, hotels, motels, restaurants or health care facilities, at least 2% of the total number of parking spaces must be level 1 electric vehicle charging parking spaces.(iv) For parking lots with at least 25 but fewer than 500 total parking spaces for the use of offices, schools, including colleges and universities, or industrial facilities, at least 4% of the total number of parking spaces must be level 1 electric vehicle charging parking spaces.(v) In addition to any requirement under subparagraph (iii) or (iv), for parking lots with at least 25 but fewer than 500 total parking spaces for the use of retail facilities, hotels, motels, offices or industrial facilities, at least 1% of the total number of parking spaces must be level 2 electric vehicle charging 20250HB2089PN2725 -
