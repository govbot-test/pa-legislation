AN ACT
Amending Title 42 (Judiciary and Judicial Procedure) of the
Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes, in juvenile matters,
providing for trauma-informed screening for human trafficking
victimization.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Title 42 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated
Statutes is amended by adding a section to read:
§ 6343. Trauma-informed screening for human trafficking
victimization.
(a) Screening development and administration.--The
Department of Human Services, in collaboration with the Juvenile
Court Judges' Commission, shall develop and implement a trauma-
informed screening to identify whether a child is a victim of,
or may be at risk for becoming a victim of, human trafficking
involving sexual servitude as defined in 18 Pa.C.S. § 3001
(relating to definitions). A juvenile probation officer shall
administer the screening to an allegedly delinquent child at the

(b) Connection to specialized services.--If the screening

exploited children).
Section 2. This act shall take effect in 90 days.
