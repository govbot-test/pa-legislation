2025-2026 Regular Session HB 2258 PN 2955 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 2955 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE BILL No.2258 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY WEBSTER, SANCHEZ, KINKEAD, HILL-EVANS, KHAN, FREEMAN, DOUGHERTY, MERSKI, STEELE, HANBIDGE AND DELLOSO, MARCH 2, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON JUDICIARY, MARCH 3, 2026 AN ACTAmending Title 42 (Judiciary and Judicial Procedure) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes, in juvenile matters, providing for trauma-informed screening for human trafficking victimization.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Title 42 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is amended by adding a section to read:§ 6343. Trauma-informed screening for human trafficking victimization.(a) Screening development and administration.--The Department of Human Services, in collaboration with the Juvenile Court Judges' Commission, shall develop and implement a trauma-informed screening to identify whether a child is a victim of, or may be at risk for becoming a victim of, human trafficking involving sexual servitude as defined in 18 Pa.C.S. § 3001 (relating to definitions). A juvenile probation officer shall administer the screening to an allegedly delinquent child at the 123456789101112131415161718 intake conference following the submission of a written allegation.(b) Connection to specialized services.--If the screening under subsection (a) identifies a child as a victim of, or at risk for becoming a victim of, human trafficking involving sexual servitude, a juvenile probation officer shall connect the child with specialized programs and services available under 18 Pa.C.S. § 3062 (relating to specialized services for sexually exploited children).Section 2. This act shall take effect in 90 days.20260HB2258PN2955 - 2 - 12345678910
