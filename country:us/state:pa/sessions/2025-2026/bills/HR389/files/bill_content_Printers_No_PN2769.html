A RESOLUTION
Recognizing the month of February 2026 as "Senior Independence
Month" in Pennsylvania.
WHEREAS, February is National Senior Independence Month, a
time to recognize and honor this Commonwealth's older adults and
encourage community engagement by promoting social interaction,
good health and life satisfaction; and
WHEREAS, Older Pennsylvanians have contributed immeasurably
to the growth, vitality and character of our communities through
their wisdom, experience and accomplishments; and
WHEREAS, Independence is vital to maintaining dignity and
quality of life, enabling all adults to thrive as active
participants in their families, neighborhoods and society; and
WHEREAS, Promoting independence requires collaboration across
all generations and providing access to health care, supportive
services, affordable housing and opportunities for lifelong
learning and engagement; and

WHEREAS, By recognizing the month of February, the
Commonwealth sends a powerful message that the independence of
older Pennsylvanians is a year-round priority, addressing the
evolving needs of all residents; therefore be it
RESOLVED, That the House of Representatives recognize
February 2026 as "Senior Independence Month" in Pennsylvania;
and be it further
RESOLVED, That the House of Representatives call upon all
residents to join in recognizing the contributions of this
Commonwealth's older adults and promoting programs and
activities that foster connections and support for older
Pennsylvanians.
