2025-2026 Regular Session HR 389 PN 2769 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 2769 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.389 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY MADDEN, PROBST, McNEILL, SANCHEZ, GUENST, HILL-EVANS, NEILSON, VITALI, HOHENSTEIN, RIVERA, CIRESI, PARKER, HOWARD, MENTZER, GALLAGHER, CEPEDA-FREYTIZ, K.HARRIS, HARKINS, KHAN, FREEMAN, GIRAL, KAZEEM AND MAYES, JANUARY 13, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON AGING AND OLDER ADULT SERVICES, JANUARY 14, 2026 A RESOLUTIONRecognizing the month of February 2026 as "Senior Independence Month" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, February is National Senior Independence Month, a time to recognize and honor this Commonwealth's older adults and encourage community engagement by promoting social interaction, good health and life satisfaction; andWHEREAS, Older Pennsylvanians have contributed immeasurably to the growth, vitality and character of our communities through their wisdom, experience and accomplishments; andWHEREAS, Independence is vital to maintaining dignity and quality of life, enabling all adults to thrive as active participants in their families, neighborhoods and society; andWHEREAS, Promoting independence requires collaboration across all generations and providing access to health care, supportive services, affordable housing and opportunities for lifelong learning and engagement; and12345678910111213141516 WHEREAS, By recognizing the month of February, the Commonwealth sends a powerful message that the independence of older Pennsylvanians is a year-round priority, addressing the evolving needs of all residents; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives recognize February 2026 as "Senior Independence Month" in Pennsylvania; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives call upon all residents to join in recognizing the contributions of this Commonwealth's older adults and promoting programs and activities that foster connections and support for older Pennsylvanians.20260HR0389PN2769 - 2 - 123456789101112
