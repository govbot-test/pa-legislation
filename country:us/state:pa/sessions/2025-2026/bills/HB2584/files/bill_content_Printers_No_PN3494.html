AN ACT
Amending Title 66 (Public Utilities) of the Pennsylvania
Consolidated Statutes, in Public Utility Commission, further
providing for consumer protection and information.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Section 308.1 of Title 66 of the Pennsylvania
Consolidated Statutes is amended by adding a subsection to read:
§ 308.1. Consumer protection and information.
* * *
(c) Database of rate increases.--
(1) The commission shall, not later than 120 days after
the effective date of this paragraph, create and maintain a
centralized public database of all rates the commission
oversees. All information available must be searchable, at
minimum, by company, entity, type of utility, year or docket
number. The commission shall make the database available on
the commission's publicly accessible Internet website and,

(2) The commission may contract with a third party to

Section 2. This act shall take effect immediately.
